6The descent of the likeness

The likeness of 1586 — the sitting behind the Frederiksborg oil and the woodcut — fed the print campaign. The first Amsterdam engraving of 1590 arrived overloaded with chains against his instructions; he ordered a corrected replacement within days, and that second engraving, produced from the surviving Copenhagen drawing and in copyable form by mid-1595, became the image Europe reproduced in his lifetime and after it. The gilt binding die ran on a separate track: in use by March 1588, carrying the loaded costume before the first engraving existed, and never re-cut. The exile portrait of 1597 and the Mechanica watercolours are fresh sittings, independent of the 1586 face.

The table gives every derivation this chapter asserts, with its kind and its basis. Documented means a letter, a signed plate, a direct copy or a physical transfer mark settles it. Reconstructed means it is the leading reading where no document settles the path. Disputed means the direction of dependence is itself the open question. The figure draws the same edges on a time axis; it is a picture of the table, not a second source, and where the two could disagree the table governs.
