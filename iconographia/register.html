3Register of the images

The same thirty-two records in full, in the order of the three series and chronologically within each. Each record carries its plate at reading size where a public reproduction exists and says in words where none does; its maker and date; the description; the original-or-copy verdict; the dated bounds with their arguments; the whereabouts with their evidence and the holding institution’s own record; the versions and exemplars known; the copy-level carriers where the image lives inside books; the disagreements between the sources, with their status; the questions settled and the questions open; the relations to other records and to the other pillars; and the sources.
