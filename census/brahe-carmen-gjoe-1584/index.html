Methodology

Inclusion: The late-1584 Uraniborg Carmen ad Falconem Gøye (Falk Gjøe), one of the occasional poems Tycho printed to keep his new press busy. PRINTED but no copy survives. Excluded: The surviving companion poems (Ulfeldt §426, Niels Kaas §427, Rantzau §425 — their own keys). The lost Carmen ad Erik Lange (its own key). Counted as a copy: Any surviving exemplar would count. None is known. Prior census base: Known from Christoffer Rothmann's letter of 21 Sept 1587 describing the printed leaves he received at the Frankfurt fair from Hans Aalborg, with the two emblem-woodcuts on the verso; Tycho's reply (17 Aug 1588) confirms 'written four years ago'.
