Methodology

Inclusion: The single 1584 Uraniborg folio Carmen ad Jacobum Ulfeldium (dated VRANIBVRGI 7 Kal. Decemb. = 25 Nov 1584), 2 leaves (Nielsen §426). In the poem Tycho notes he had it printed partly to give his new press work. Excluded: The companion lost poems to Falk Gjøe and Erik Lange (their own stub census keys). Reprints, Opera Omnia text, facsimiles, MS copies. Counted as a copy: Any surviving exemplar; any identifiable fragment counts. Open questions

  • Capture KB shelfmark.
  • Resolved (2026-06-04): Confirmed from Nielsen (reference/Brahe_Bibliography_Nielsen.md, §426 + the lost-occasional-prints layer): Tycho's own Ulfeldt poem (§426) states that companion poems went to Falk Gjøe and Erik Lange the same 1584 season; both are LOST (no copy survives). The Carmen ad Falk Gjøe (Uraniborg, late 1584) is known only from Christoffer Rothmann's letter of 21 Sept 1587 describing the printed leaves he received at the Frankfurt fair (with two emblem-woodcuts on the verso); the Carmen ad Erik Lange (late 1584; Lange later married Tycho's sister Sophie) survives in no copy. These two lost companions fall outside this Ulfeldt-carmen census and are appropriately treated as separate lost-work stubs. Basis: Nielsen §426 + lost-prints layer.
