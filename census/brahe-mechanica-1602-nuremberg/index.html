Methodology

Inclusion: The 1602 Nuremberg re-issue of Astronomiae instaurata mechanica — a COMPLETE NEW REPRINT (new typesetting) using the original Uraniborg woodblocks and copperplates, published by Levinus Hulsius and printed by Christoph Lochner, 2o. This is Norman 320, a distinct edition from the 1598 Wandsbek first edition. Routinely mis-catalogued by libraries as '1598' when the title-leaf is missing (the classic Frauenfeld Kantonsbibliothek + Royal Society London cases). Excluded: The 1598 Wandsbek first edition (its own census key brahe-mechanica-1598-wandsbek, the new typesetting being the discriminator — confirm by typeface, NOT just title-page year). Facsimiles, the modern translations, MS copies. Counted as a copy: Any physical exemplar with >=50% of the original leaves, OR an identifiable fragment (>=1 leaf) with documented provenance. A title-page-less copy is judged by its typesetting (the 1602 reset) not by a presumed 1598 date. Prior census base: Norman 320; Nielsen depth layer §1.6 (confirms it is a complete new reprint, not a sheet re-issue, distinguishing it from the Epistolae/Progymnasmata posthumous re-issues). No dedicated census; the 1598 Wandsbek census explicitly excludes it. Confidence grading: Pearson A-D Copy-ID scheme: stable institutional-slug; never renumbered on sort

Bibliography of prior censuses

No dedicated census of the 1602 edition exists. Ruth Sargent Noyes's ongoing digital census covers the 1598 first edition (she had located ~55 copies as of 2019); it is not confirmed to register the 1602 separately. our own brahe-mechanica-1598-wandsbek census (61 copies) explicitly excludes the 1602 — this census fills that gap.

What the 1602 is

A complete new reprint (new typesetting throughout) of the 1598 Wandsbek Astronomiae instauratae mechanica, using the original Uraniborg woodblocks and the four 1598 copperplates, sold to the Nuremberg publisher Levinus Hülsius by Tycho's heirs (via Tengnagel) after Tycho's death (24 Oct 1601). Hülsius was a publisher, not a printer; the actual printer was Christoph Lochner of Nuremberg (Norlind's identification, settling the "Noribergae imprint but Hülsius at Frankfurt by April 1602" contradiction). This is the "first trade edition" — the 1598 was a private presentation printing of perhaps 60–100 copies.

Bibliographic discriminators (1602 vs 1598)

  • Title-page: 1602 has the engraved portrait of Tycho beneath an arch with the Brahe + Bille arms; 1598 has the Astronomien/Kemien vignette (a few 1598 copies have a pasted watercolour portrait).
  • Decoration: 1598 has the rich de Ohr Hamburg roeskenrammer border on every page; the 1602 (Lochner, Nuremberg) lacks these borders, on cheaper paper with smaller margins.
  • Imprint: 1602 = "NORIBERGAE apud L. HVLSIVM"; 1598 = "PROPRIA AUTHORIS TYPOGRAPHIA opera Philippi de Ohr Chalcographi Hamburgensis".
  • Collation: 54 unnumbered leaves, folio.
  • The misidentification trap: a 1602 copy lacking its title-leaf has repeatedly been catalogued as "1598" (Nielsen flags the Frauenfeld Kantonsbibliothek and Royal Society London cases; both are confirmed 1602 by body-typography).

Downstream

After Hülsius's death the residual stock passed to Gottfried Tampach (Frankfurt), who in 1610 re-issued the three Tycho titles with new Frankfurt title-leaves. For the Mechanica the 1610 Tampach is a title re-issue on remaining Hülsius/Lochner stock — a candidate issue-state for a future refresh, not a separate edition.
