Methodology

Inclusion: All physical copies of the 1573 Copenhagen first edition of De nova et nullius aevi memoria prius visa stella (Tycho Brahe's report on the new star / supernova of 1572), printed in 4to by Lorentz Benedicht (Laurentz Benedict), 54 leaves. The complete work comprises the dedicatory letter to Johannes Pratensis (3 May 1573), Pratensis's reply (5 May 1573), the prefatory poem by Andreas Velleius, and the main astronomical treatise. All in scope. Excluded: The 1602 Prague (Schumann) Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata, which later absorbs and supersedes the De nova stella material as its Liber I — a separate edition with its own census key (brahe-progymnasmata-1602). The 1901 Copenhagen facsimile (Regia Societas Scientiarum Danica, Hauniae), all later photographic reproductions, modern editions (Opera Omnia I), translations, and manuscript copies are excluded. Counted as a copy: Any physical exemplar with >=50% of the original leaves preserved, OR an identifiable fragment (>=1 original leaf) with documented institutional or named-private provenance. Defective copies (the several Nielsen flags as 'defekt') are counted with a condition note in physical.notes. Prior census base: Lauritz Nielsen, Dansk Bibliografi 1551-1600 (Copenhagen, 1931-33) §429 — a ~20-library copy-list (KB Cph 2 copies + Univ-Bibl Cph defekt + BL London + Leiden + Kiel + Konigsberg + Lund + Marburg defekt + Oslo + Prague Univ-Bibl + Rostock + Stockholm Kgl + KVA + Edinburgh Obs. + Gent + Goteborg Stadsbibl defekt + Helsingor Kronborg-museum + Odense Karen-Brahes-Bibl). Dreyer's Tycho Brahe (1890) and the Opera Omnia embed further copy references. No dedicated standalone De nova stella census exists. Confidence grading: Pearson A-D Copy-ID scheme: stable institutional-slug; never renumbered on sort
