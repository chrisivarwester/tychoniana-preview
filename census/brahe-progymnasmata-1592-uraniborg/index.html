Methodology

Inclusion: The 1592 Uraniborg ADVANCE distribution of Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata Liber I (Nielsen §433): the dated collective title-leaf (for the projected 3-volume work + correspondence volume) + woodcut Tycho portrait on verso + Rudolf II's 13 June 1590 privilege, with the partially-printed text (388 leaves, with gaps). Tycho sent these advance copies to scholarly contacts in 1592 (Landgrave of Hessen 20 Feb 1592 (3 copies); Hugo Blotius 20 March; Hagecius 22 March (6 copies)). What the 1592 advance issue is

Tycho printed Volume I of the Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata at Uraniborg with gaps (388 leaves printed, the Moon section pending). The 1592-dated leaf was a collective title-leaf for the projected three-volume work + the correspondence volume, with a woodcut Tycho portrait on the verso and Rudolf II's 13 June 1590 privilege on the last leaves. Tycho sent advance copies to scholarly contacts:

  • Landgrave of Hessen, 20 Feb 1592 (3 copies — preserved at Marburg Staatsarchiv)
  • Hugo Blotius, 20 March 1592 (3 copies)
  • Hagecius, 22 March 1592 (6 copies)

Surviving copies (Nielsen §433: 3 known incomplete)

  1. Berlin, Staatsbibliothek — an advance copy (confirm WW2 survival).
  2. Königsberg — the Johannes Müller working copy — the bibliographically richest: Tycho's own working copy, gifted to his assistant Johannes Müller March 1598 (gift note on the title leaf), with 12 blank leaves bound in where the Moon section was still pending, several carrying Tycho's autograph corrections and observations, the text systematically corrected throughout. This is the canonical witness for what was printed on Hveen vs. what was completed later in Prague. Königsberg collections were dispersed/destroyed 1944–45 — post-WW2 fate uncertain (a HIGH-priority trace target).
  3. Marburg, Hessisches Staatsarchiv — title-leaf fragment only (one of the 3 Landgrave-of-Hessen advance copies).

Significance

The 1592 advance issue is the documentary anchor for the Progymnasmata's decade-long, three-city printing history (Uraniborg 1592 → Wittenberg 1598-9 scrapped Moon section → Prague 1602 Schumann completion). The Königsberg-Müller copy is the single object that lets scholars draw the Hveen/Prague printing boundary.
