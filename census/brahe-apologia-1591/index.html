Methodology

Inclusion: The 1591 Uraniborg Apologetica responsio ad cujusdam Peripatetici in Scotia dubia de parallaxi cometarum — Tycho's printed response to the Scotsman John Craig's critical letters about the 1588 De mundi. PRINTED but no copy survives. Excluded: The enlarged 1598 Wandsbeck reissue (its own key brahe-apologia-1598-wandsbeck). The surviving manuscript fragment in Berlin Staatsbibliothek (transcribed from the print) and Longomontanus's MS copy — manuscripts, not the print. Counted as a copy: Any surviving exemplar of the 1591 PRINT would count. None is known. The Berlin MS fragment is recorded as evidence-of-existence, not as a copy. Prior census base: Existence disputed by Friis (Tyge Brahe 265) and Dreyer (Tycho Brahe 209) on Gassendi's claim it survived only in MS; but Rasmus Bartholin, De cometis (1665) p.49, states he OWNED a printed copy ('quam impressam habeo Uraniburgi 1591') — refuting Gassendi. Listed by Lalande 1803 + Houzeau & Lancaster 1887 ('très rare'). PRINTED, lost, all copies dispersed by mid-17th c. Confidence grading: Pearson A-D Copy-ID scheme: stable institutional-slug; never renumbered on sort
