Methodology

Inclusion: All physical copies of the 1591 Danish-language first edition of Peder Jacobsen Flemloes's En Elementisch oc Jordisch Astrologia, printed at Tycho Brahe's Uraniborg press by Hans Gaschitz (June 1591), 8vo, 80 leaves — the ONLY Danish-language book from the Uraniborg press and Tycho's first use of Fraktur. The dedication was written by Longomontanus (Christopher Sorensen) in Flemloes's name from dictation. Printed under Tycho's imprimatur expressly to keep his printer occupied. Excluded: The parallel 1591 German edition (Ein Elementisch und Jrdische Astrologia, September 1591 — its own census key brahe-flemloes-astrologia-1591-de). The 1865 F. R. Friis reprint (Copenhagen), all facsimiles, modern editions, and manuscript copies. Counted as a copy: Any physical exemplar with >=50% of the original leaves preserved, OR an identifiable fragment (>=1 original leaf) with documented institutional or named-private provenance. Given the extreme rarity (2 recorded copies), any provenanced fragment is significant and counts. Prior census base: Lauritz Nielsen, Bogtrykkeri paa Hveen (1921) and Dansk Bibliografi 1551-1600 (1931, unnumbered) record only 2 surviving copies: KB Copenhagen + Universitetsbiblioteket Copenhagen. Nielsen records two surviving copies only — KB Copenhagen and the University Library, Copenhagen.

  • Author: Peder Jacobsen Flemløs (Tycho's assistant 1578–1588); dedication written by Longomontanus in Flemløs's name from dictation; printed under Tycho's imprimatur expressly to keep his printer occupied.
  • Later editions (1644, 1745; F. R. Friis reprint 1865) confirm 1591 as the first — all OUT of scope.
  • J.R. Christianson, "Tycho Brahe's Cosmology from the Astrologia of 1591", Isis 59 (1968), 312–318 — the standard study of the text.
  • The exhaustive external sweep this run (USTC, WorldCat, KVK, Bibliotheca Danica, Bruun Rasmussen, Lynge, RBH) surfaced no third copy. Groningen holds the German edition, not the Danish.
