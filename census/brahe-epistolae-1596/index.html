Methodology

Inclusion: All physical copies of the single 1596 Uraniborg impression of Epistolarum astronomicarum libri (Liber I) — Tycho's published correspondence with Landgrave Wilhelm IV of Hessen-Kassel and his court astronomer Christopher Rothmann, dedicated to Landgrave Moritz, printed at Tycho's own Uraniborg press (Nielsen §430), 4to, 176 leaves, dated at the vernal equinox (20 March) 1596. Because the 1602 Nuremberg (Levinus Hulsius) and 1610 Frankfurt (Gottfried Tampach) issues are the SAME 1596 Uraniborg sheets sold later under a cancel title-leaf — not new typesettings — copies bearing a Hulsius or Tampach title-leaf ARE in scope (one impression, three issues). Each copy records issue_state (uraniborg-1596 / hulsius-1602 / tampach-1610). Copies with the de Gheyn 1590 copper-portrait pasted to the title-leaf verso, and either setting of the reset quires LL and MM (pp. 265-280), are all in scope. Excluded: Genuinely reset / newly-typeset later editions, if any are identified (none is recorded by Nielsen — the posthumous issues are sheet re-issues, not reprints, distinguishing Epistolae from the Mechanica whose 1602 Hulsius issue WAS a full reprint). Manuscript copies, facsimiles, the Opera Omnia VI modern text, and translations are excluded. Counted as a copy: Any physical exemplar with >=50% of the original leaves preserved, OR an identifiable fragment (>=1 original leaf) with documented institutional or named-private provenance. Issue-state (which cancel title-leaf) is recorded but does not affect whether a copy counts. Prior census base: Lauritz Nielsen, Dansk Bibliografi 1551-1600 (Copenhagen, 1931-33) §430 (~30 libraries cited). Dreyer's Tycho Brahe (1890) and the Opera Omnia VI apparatus embed further copy references. No dedicated standalone Epistolae census exists. Confidence grading: Pearson A-D Copy-ID scheme: stable institutional-slug; never renumbered on sort

Manuscript witness behind the print — the Marburg/Kassel autograph convolute (collation source)

Finding (2026-06-04). The manuscript source behind this printed book has been identified and digitised: the Kassel-retained autograph originals of the Tycho Brahe ⇄ Landgrave Wilhelm IV ⇄ Christopher Rothmann correspondence that the 1596 Epistolae prints. These survive as a single convolute in the Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg —

  • Current signature: HStAM Best. 3 II, Nr. 1070
  • Former signature: 4 a 31 Nr. 14 (= the "4a.31.14" cited in Mosley, Bearing the Heavens, 2007, p. 57 n. 62)
  • Title: "Korrespondenz mit Tycho Brahe …, 1585–1592"
  • Arcinsys archivalDescriptionId: 10465709 (125 images)
  • Surrogate: Sophia holds a complete digital surrogate (125 images).

Status: primary COLLATION SOURCE against the print, not a copy of it. This MS is explicitly excluded from the census's copy count (see census.toml[scope] excluded — manuscript copies are out of scope), but it is the most consequential external witness to the edition. Tycho edited the letters for print: e.g. he suppressed the narrative opening of his 1 March 1586 letter to Wilhelm IV. The autographs therefore preserve un-edited, pre-print readings that the 1596 typesetting does not. Dreyer collated these Marburg originals for the Opera Omnia VI (1919) apparatus, which remains the standard text witness; the digital surrogate now allows direct re-collation against the printed copies enumerated in this census.
