Census: brahe-orthographia-uranienburg-1585 · Copy: marburg-staatsarchiv-only · Confidence: A · Status: extant

The Orthographia arcis Vranienburg (1585) is the single transverse-folio broadsheet on which Tycho Brahe published his observatory: a perspective elevation of Uraniborg (Præcipuæ et maioris domus delineatio), the ground plan (Ichnographia), and the diamond-shaped rampart/garden plan (Totius ambitus … delineatio), with three columns of Latin explanation. It is the earliest graphic representation of Uraniborg and the source of the derived Uraniborg view in Braun & Hogenberg's Civitates orbis terrarum.

The only known exemplar survives at the Hessisches Staatsarchiv Marburg, Best. 3 II, Nr. 1070 (former signature 4 a 31 Nr. 14) — the unit "Korrespondenz mit Tycho Brahe in astronomischen, astrologischen und mathematischen Angelegenheiten sowie über Instrumente," 1585–1592. It is not a free-standing print but a printed enclosure to Tycho's autograph letter to Landgrave Wilhelm IV of Hesse-Kassel (Uraniborg, 1 March 1586; received at Kassel 10 April 1586, carried by Peter Jacobsen Flemløse), bound at the front of the convolute at images 0001 (verso, carrying the former pencil signature "4a 31 14") – 0002 (recto), alongside the Sextans (0012) and Armillae (0013) broadsides. The covering letter is edited and translated in full as brahe_to_wilhelm_1586 (Desktop). Dreyer reproduced it from this original in Opera Omnia VI, p. 349 (n. to 37.11: "Pictura aedificii … Marpurgi asservatur"); Mosley (2007, p. 57 n. 62) cites the unit under its former signature 4a.31.14.
