Methodology

Inclusion: All physical copies of the first edition of Tycho Brahe's Astronomiae Instauratae Progymnasmata (Liber I — on the Sun, Moon, and the new star of 1572): the edition begun on the Uraniborg press in 1592 and COMPLETED posthumously at the Schumann press in Prague in 1602 (title-leaf 'TYPIS INCHOATA VRANIBVRGI DANIAE. ABSOLVTA PRAGAE BOHEMIAE. M.DC.II.'). The ~388 Uraniborg-printed leaves + the ~58 Prague-completed leaves (the two title-quires, the Moon section P-Q-R, the conclusion, Kepler's appendix, index, errata) together constitute one copy. Copies dated 1603 (with the 'Praemissa est Authoris Vita' tag, though no Vita is actually present) are the same edition and ARE in scope. Both the Mueller-Koenigsberg working copy and ordinary copies count. Excluded: (1) The 1592 Uraniborg ADVANCE issue — the collective title-leaf + woodcut portrait + Rudolf II privilege sent to scholarly contacts (Landgrave of Hessen, Blotius, Hagecius) before the work was finished; only ~3 incomplete survivors (Berlin, Koenigsberg, Marburg fragment). A distinct bibliographic state: record in the borderline register / flag per copy, do NOT merge into the 1602 count. (2) The 1610 Frankfurt (Tampach) re-issue — new title-leaf + reset B-C-Kk-Ll quires on otherwise-1602 sheets; a separate issue. CAVEAT: the 1610 Tampach is mostly 1602 sheets with a cancel title, so gate every candidate by quire signatures and title-leaf wording, not the title-page year alone. Facsimiles/photographic/MS copies excluded. Counted as a copy: Any physical exemplar with >=50% of the original leaves preserved, OR an identifiable fragment (>=1 original leaf) with documented institutional or named-private provenance. The Mueller-Koenigsberg working copy (autograph corrections + 12 blank Moon-section leaves) counts as one copy with its special status recorded in physical.marginalia. Copies dated 1603 are in scope (same edition). Prior census base: No dedicated Progymnasmata census exists. Baseline: Lauritz Nielsen, Dansk Bibliografi 1551-1600 (Copenhagen, 1931-33) §433 (the 1592-advance survivors + the completed edition); the Koenigsberg-Mueller copy is the canonical Hveen-vs-Prague printing-boundary witness (Nielsen, Bogtrykkeri paa Hveen). Confidence grading: Pearson A-D Copy-ID scheme: stable institution-slug + shelfmark/barcode-slug; never renumbered on sort

Bibliography of prior censuses

Status

No dedicated copy-census of the Progymnasmata exists. This is the first. Baseline = Lauritz Nielsen §433 + the Bogtrykkeri-paa-Hveen analysis of the printing boundary.

Graded sources

Lauritz Nielsen, Dansk Bibliografi 1551–1600 (Copenhagen, 1931–33), §433 — BASELINE

  • Records the work and its 1592 advance issue survivors (Berlin, Königsberg, Marburg fragment) plus the completed edition.
  • Held: distilled in Brahe_Bibliography_Nielsen.md §433.

Nielsen, Tycho Brahes Bogtrykkeri paa Hveen (1921) — the printing-boundary witness

  • The Königsberg–Müller working copy is the canonical evidence for what was printed at Uraniborg (1592) vs completed at Prague (1602): Tycho's autograph corrections throughout + 12 blank leaves bound in where the Moon section was still pending, gifted to Johannes Müller March 1598.

The three bibliographic states (scope discriminator)

  1. 1592 Uraniborg advance issue — collective title-leaf + woodcut portrait + Rudolf II privilege; sent incomplete to scholarly contacts. ~3 survivors. OUT of scope (→ the borderline register); a distinct state.
  2. 1602 Prague-completed first edition — the standard edition; title 'TYPIS INCHOATA VRANIBVRGI DANIAE. ABSOLVTA PRAGAE BOHEMIAE. M.DC.II.'. Some copies dated 1603. IN scope.
  3. 1610 Frankfurt (Tampach) re-issue — cancel title + reset B-C-Kk-Ll quires on 1602 sheets. OUT of scope. Gate by quire signatures, not the title-page year.

Located in this census

KB Copenhagen ×5 (four 1602 + one 1603), Munich BSB ×2 (digitised), Marburg HStAM ×2 (state TBD), Königsberg-Müller (survival TBD). The KB 1592-advance copy (barcode 130023736617, bought Vienna 1979) is recorded in the borderline register as the excluded advance state.

P3 worklist (→ acquisition-queue)

Nielsen + the standard distribution imply many more: the Landgrave-of-Hessen / Blotius / Hagecius advance copies; institutional copies across VD17 (German), Library Hub (UK), BnF, ÖNB, Uppsala, Prague (the 1602 was completed there). Each candidate gated by title-leaf state + quire signatures.

Bibliographic gate for every candidate

Confirm 1602 Prague-completed sheets (or the 1603 issue) — NOT the 1592 advance issue and NOT the 1610 Tampach re-issue — by the title-leaf wording and quire signatures (especially B, C, Kk, Ll, and the Moon section P-Q-R).
