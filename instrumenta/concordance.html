5Concordance

The same instrument is numbered and named differently in each of the witnesses: by its engraved Latin caption in the 1598 book, by figure and page in the 1946 translation, by page in Dreyer’s edition of the Latin text (Opera Omnia V) and by chapter in the Scripta reading edition, by page in Wennberg’s plate appendix, by page in Blaeu’s atlas, and by Thoren’s short names in his genealogical chart of 1990. The first table walks each record across all of them. The basis of each identification in Thoren’s chart is given in the row’s note, because four of them are editorial. The second table lists the entries of Thoren’s chart, and the instruments of Tycho’s own unengraved list, that have no record here.

Tycho's own list of instruments not yet engraved

Chapter 25 of the Mechanica (TBOO V, p. 96) names the instruments he had not engraved and promises to add them; none has a record here.
