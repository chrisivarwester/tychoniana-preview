6Where they stood

Where each instrument stood, by site and room, as the Mechanica and Blaeu’s account place them and as the room registry of the Uraniborg and Stjerneborg reconstructions identifies the spaces. Each room links to its record in Loca, §5, where the plans and the reconstruction are given; the five places carry their coordinates below. The instruments recorded per stay of Tycho’s life — what he built or bought at Augsburg, at Herrevad, on Hven — are listed in Loca, §6.
