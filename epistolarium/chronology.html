Calendars

Denmark, Hesse-Kassel and the Lutheran north kept the Julian calendar until 1700; the Catholic lands adopted the Gregorian reform in 1582 and Bohemia in 1584, so from 1599 Tycho himself dated in New Style. The two calendars were ten days apart. This chapter marks the style where the dateline or the editors give it — [O.S.] and [N.S.] in the headings here — and the table below shows how the marks fall by place of writing. A reply from Prague can therefore appear to precede the letter from Hven it answers; the register keeps the dates as given and does not convert them.
