3Register of the documents

Every document as a record card, in date order under year headings, with everything known about it short of the text itself: the parties (linked to Familia), the place of writing (linked to Loca where Tycho’s own itinerary has the place), the date and its calendar, the abstract, the first words of the original, the editorial note where the letter needed one, the edition of record with Dreyer’s volume and pages and a link to the facsimile page, the manuscript witnesses by kind with a catalogue link where one is verified, the document’s place in the exchange, and the grade and collation status of the English. The text, the apparatus and the audit’s warrant are on the letter’s own page.
