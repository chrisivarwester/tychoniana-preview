The original

DEM WOLGEBORNEN VNND EDLEN HERRN GOTTFFRYD VON BERBISTORFF, AUFF GRUSCHOW VND STRUCK, RÖMISCH. KEY. MAY. RAADT, MEINEM GUNSTIGEN LIEBEN HERRN, FREUND VND NACHPAREN

MEIN willige dienst mit wunschunge alles gudts beuor. Wollgeborner vnd edler herr, herr Gottfryd von Berbistorff, gunstiger herr vnd freundt. Des herrn schreiben ist mir heute durch dero abgefertigten behendigt, worin ich des herrn freundtlichen guten willen kegen mir erspure, in deme das err mich in diesen nechstkunftigen pfingstagen, nebenst andern seinen freunden zu sich so freundtlich geladen, wor fur ich auch dem herrn, das er mich alss ein frembden vnd vnbekanten dieser örter solche freundtschafft selbstwillig erzeiget, gar dienstlich bedanck, vnd wolte gerne bey dem herrn auf angedeuteten tagk mich einstellen, wen nicht zur selbigen zeit ich anderswo mich hette versprochen. Den wie ich fur 14 tage zu Prage auf des herrn haubtmans von Brandeiss hochzeit gewesen, hab ich dem wolgebornen herren Carll von Wartenberch mussen angeloben, das ich in nebenst dem wollg. herrn Ehrenfridt von Minckwitz kunftigen pfingstage wollte besuchen, vnd ist genanter herr von Wartenbergk seidthero selber alhie bey mir gewesen vnd weiteren beschlus darauf haben wollen, in welchen ich auch consentiert. Es hat auch der obgedachte herr Minkwitz gistern von Prag mir zugeschrieben, das er eigentlich beschlossen, auf zukunftigen donnerstagk oder freitagk aufs lengste alhie (ob Godt will) bey mir zu sein, das wir vns folgendes miteinander konten zu dem herrn von Wartenbergk, wie zugesagt, begeben. Nach dieser gelegenheit, versihe ich, werdt der herr mich entschuldigt haben, das ich auf dissmall, wie ich gerne wolte, nicht kan zu ime kommen. Was aber dissmall, wegen gemelter vhrsachen, nich gescheen kan, gelobe ich dieweill auf einander maell mit Gottes heulffe wiedereinzubringen, vnd will gerne dem herrn, besunders weil wir so nahent nachbar, nach sein wille vnd gelegenheit besuchen; verhoffe, das der herr in demselben widervmb vnbeschwert sich wirt finden lassen. Bitte auch hieneben gantz dienstlich, er wolle alles zum besten ausdeuten vnd aufnemen. Thue hiemit vns beiderseits in gnedigen schutz des Allmechtigen treulich befehlen.

Datirt auf dem keiserlichen hause Bennatick den 15. maj: a:o 1600.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the well-born and noble Lord Gottfried von Berbisdorf, [lord] of Gruschow and Struck, councillor of the Roman Imperial Majesty, my favourable dear lord, friend and neighbour.

My willing service, with the wish of all good, beforehand. Well-born and noble lord, Lord Gottfried von Berbisdorf, favourable lord and friend: the gentleman's letter was handed to me today through his dispatched [messenger], wherein I perceive the gentleman's friendly good will toward me, in that he has so kindly invited me, together with his other friends, to him on these coming Whitsun days; for which I also very serviceably thank the gentleman — that he, to me, a stranger and unknown in these parts, has of his own accord shown such friendship — and I would gladly present myself at the gentleman's on the day indicated, were I not at that same time engaged elsewhere. For, as I was a fortnight ago at Prague at the wedding of the lord captain of Brandeis, I had to pledge to the well-born Lord Carl von Wartenberg that I would visit him, together with the well-born Lord Ehrenfried von Minckwitz, next Whitsun; and the said Lord von Wartenberg has since been here with me himself and wished a further arrangement upon it, to which I also consented. The aforesaid Lord Minckwitz, too, wrote to me yesterday from Prague that he has firmly resolved to be here with me (God willing) next Thursday, or Friday at the latest, so that we might thereafter betake ourselves together to the Lord von Wartenberg, as promised. In view of this circumstance, I trust the gentleman will have excused me, that this time, much as I would wish it, I cannot come to him. But what this time, for the reasons stated, cannot take place, I promise meanwhile to make good on another occasion, with God's help; and I will gladly visit the gentleman — especially since we are such near neighbours — according to his will and convenience; and I hope the gentleman will in the same matter again show himself unburdened [i.e. agreeable]. I also ask herewith, quite serviceably, that he be pleased to construe and take everything for the best. Herewith faithfully commending us both to the gracious protection of the Almighty.

Dated at the imperial house of Benátky, the 15th of May, in the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Tycho Brahe at the imperial house of Benátky nad Jizerou; a social note declining a Whitsun invitation on account of a prior engagement — a glimpse of Tycho's integration into the Bohemian nobility around Benátky.
  • Recipient. Gottfried von Berbisdorf, auf Gruschow und Struck, Röm. Kais. Maj. Rat = Gottfried von Berbisdorf, hereditary lord of Gruschow and Struck and a councillor of the Imperial Majesty (Rudolf II), addressed as Tycho's Herr, Freund und Nachbar ("lord, friend and neighbour"). Tycho calls himself, with courteous self-deprecation, ein Fremder und Unbekannter dieser Örter — "a stranger and unknown in these parts."
  • Persons named.
  • der Hauptmann von Brandeis = the captain (governor) of Brandýs nad Labem (Brandeis), at whose Prague wedding (a fortnight earlier) Tycho had made the prior engagement.
  • Carl von Wartenberg = Karl von Wartenberg, a Bohemian nobleman whom Tycho is bound to visit at Whitsun.
  • Ehrenfried von Minckwitz = Ehrenfried von Minckwitz, who is to come to Benátky first ("Thursday, or Friday at the latest") so that he and Tycho may go on together to Wartenberg.
  • Place. das kaiserliche Haus Bennatick = the imperial house of Benátky; Prag = Prague; Brandeis = Brandýs nad Labem.
  • Register & formulae. Noble-courteous chancery German, the recipient addressed in the third person as der Herr ("the gentleman") throughout. Opening mein williger Dienst mit Wünschung alles Guten zuvor → "my willing service, with the wish of all good, beforehand"; datirt → "dated." nechstkünftige Pfingsttage = the coming days of Whitsun / Pentecost (the fixed point around which the social engagements turn).
  • Witness note. The Basel manuscript is a Kladde — a retained draft / office copy; the subscription is accordingly not written out.
  • Calendar. Dateline den 15. Maij a:o 1600; the edition marks n.s. (New Style), consistent with Tycho's Imperial-service dating. The movable feast Pfingsten (Whitsun) is to be read on the same New-Style reckoning. Reproduced, not converted.
