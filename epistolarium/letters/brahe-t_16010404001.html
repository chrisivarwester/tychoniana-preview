The original

ERLIG OCH WELBYRDIG MANDT HOLGER ROSENKRANTZ TIL ROSENHOLM, MIN KIERRE SWOGER, SLECHT OCH BØRDT, OCH SØNDERLIGE GODE WENN, GANDSKE WENLIGEN TILLSKREFFVITT.

MINN gandske venlig hilsen nu och altiidt med Vor Herre. Kierre Holger Rossenkrantz, slecht och byr, swoger och sønderlig gode ven, Gudt almechtigste verre nu och altijdt hoß eder och beuare eder lenge och well frae allt det, eder kan werre skadelligt enten till sæll eller liff, och nest megenn tacksigelse for allt bewijst gode, hvilcket ygien aff formuen att forskylde ieg altijdt will findiß willig och redebon.

Giffuendiß eder herhoß gandske wenligen tillkiende, att epthersom jeg nest Guds hielp achter engang emellumb paske och pinsdag nu nestkommendis her udj Prag paa dette Curtianiske huß, som jeg nu bor, att giørre min kierre datters erlig och welbyrdig iomfru Elisabet Brahes brøldup med en herremandt, født udj Westfalen, som tiener her y röm: key: may: gaardt, vedt nauffn Frantz Tegnagel till Campe, saa er derfor min gandske wenlig bøn till eder, att y wille well giørre och till forneffnte tijdt komme hidt, och giørre eder løstig och gladt med andre fornemme herrer och gott folck aff høibemelte key: may: raadt och anden y dette loufflig kongeriige Bohemen, som jeg forhaabiß daa till samme tijdt her hoss mig att forsamblis. Kierre Holger och kierre swoger, giører nu her udj, epthersom jeg mig till eder forlader, saa viit eder nogenlunde mueligt kan werre; eder och eders att tiene vdigien, will jeg altijdt findis willig. Och will jeg nu icke lenger opholde eder med denne min skriffuelse, menß nu och altijdt haffue eder Gudt almechtigste bode till sæll och liff befallidt.

Daterit vdj Prag paa dett Curtianske huß, som ehr yderst vedt bøen, som jeg nu bor, den 4. aprilis anno 1601. Stylo novo.
Tyghe Brahe
egenh.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Honourable and well-born man Holger Rosenkrantz to Rosenholm, my dear brother-in-law, kin and blood, and especial good friend, written to in all friendliness.

My quite friendly greeting, now and always, with Our Lord. Dear Holger Rosenkrantz — kin and blood, brother-in-law and especial good friend — may God Almighty be now and always with you, and preserve you long and well from all that can be harmful to you, whether to soul or to life; and next, much thanksgiving for all the good [you have] shown, which to requite in turn, of [my] ability, I will always be found willing and ready.

Giving you herewith, in all friendliness, to know: that, since I intend — next, with God's help — at some time between Easter and Pentecost now coming, here at Prague, at this Curtius house where I now dwell, to make the wedding of the honourable and well-born maiden Elisabeth Brahe, my dear daughter, with a nobleman born in Westphalia who serves here in the Roman Imperial Majesty's court, by name Frans Tengnagel to Camp — so is, therefore, my quite friendly request to you, that you would do well, and come hither at the aforesaid time, and make yourself merry and glad with other distinguished lords and good folk of the high-mentioned Imperial Majesty's Council, and others in this lawful kingdom of Bohemia, who, I hope, will then at the same time be gathered here with me. Dear Holger, and dear brother-in-law, do now herein — as I rely upon you — so far as may be at all possible for you; to serve you and yours in return, I will always be found willing. And I will not now detain you longer with this my writing, but, now and always, have you commended to God Almighty, both to soul and to life.

Dated at Prague, at the Curtius house — which is at the outermost edge of the city — where I now dwell, the 4th of April, in the year 1601. New Style.
Tyge Brahe,
[his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • The wedding invitation. A document of high biographical value: Tycho invites Holger Rosenkrantz (his kinsman) to the wedding of his daughter Elisabeth Brahe to Frans Tengnagel, to be held at Prague between Easter and Pentecost 1601. The marriage of Elisabeth (Elisabet) Brahe to Frans Gansneb Tengnagel van de Camp (Frantz Tegnagel till Campe) — Tycho's long-serving noble assistant (the "living letter" of the 1600 letters), here "a nobleman born in Westphalia who serves at the Imperial court" — took place at Prague in June 1601, a few months before Tycho's death (October 1601).
  • The Curtius house — a notable identification. dette Curtianiske hus … som er yderst ved byen = "this Curtius house, which is at the outermost edge of the city, where I now dwell." This is the Prague mansion of the late Imperial Vice-Chancellor Jakob Kurz von Senftenau (Jacob Curtius, d. 1594) — the very Curtius commended in brahe-t_15980626002 and mourned by Herwart in brahe-t_16000908001 — which Tycho occupied as his Prague residence and observatory after the move from Benátky.
  • Recipient. Holger Rosenkrantz "the Learned," addressed as svoger, slægt og byrd ("brother-in-law, kin and blood") — the kinsman of the Cramer/Liddel and 1598 letters (brahe-t_15980331001, …0524001, …0914001); asked to come and to bring the festal company of imperial councillors.
  • Orthography. Late Danish chancery: erlig/welbyrdig, swoger (brother-in-law/kinsman), brøldup (bryllup, wedding), iomfru (maiden), herremandt (nobleman), till saell eller liff = til sjæl eller liv ("to soul or to life"), forsamblis (forsamles, be gathered), egenh. = egen hånd ("own hand"). Reading-verified as Danish.
  • Calendar. Prag … den 4. aprilis anno 1601. Stylo novo = 4 April 1601, New Style (explicit; Prague, imperial). Header agrees with the label. The wedding itself is set "between Easter and Pentecost" (so c. April–May/June 1601).
