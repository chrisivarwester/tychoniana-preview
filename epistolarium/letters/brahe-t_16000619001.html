The original

DEM EDLEN EHRNVESTEN VND HOCHGELARTEN HERRN TYCHO BRAHEN AUFF KNUTHSTORFF, S. RÖMISCH KEYSERLICHEN MAYT.ASTROΝΟΜΟAUFF BENATHEN, MEYNEM ... GÜNSTIGEN HERRN.CITISSIME.

S.

GENEROSE et doctiss. D. TYCHO, vehementer expectant nostri Canonici tuæ Astronomicæ suppellectilis avectionem, et pecuniam promtam ad omnia offerunt. Accessit quoque me GRUTZMACERUS Dresdensis nauta, qui promittit se navibus suis Leutmeritium usque omnia recte advecturum. En alius quidam ex nostris, qui idem rogat, ut sibi committatur. Duabus enim navibus se omnia rite repositurum esse. Reliqua enim suppellex nuper Hamburgo quoque advecta est. Quid impediat, scripsi ante Pentecosten, et quia nihil impetro responsi, existimo non esse redditas meas. Quare rogavi hunc meum vicinum, ut has aut ipse redderet, aut conducto Pragæ nuncio, cui tu satisfaceres, de tua voluntate sciscitaretur. Est autem hæc mora, quod inventarium prioris vecturæ ANDREAS vester secum abstulit, ut sine insigni errore nos committere eam nautis prorsus nequeamus. Omnino ergo oportet ANDREAM ipsum redire ad nos, aut aliquem ex domesticis, cui nota sunt externo ab intuitu omnia, et qui ex navi reponat in littore, contra pluvias muniat, tibique postea deferenda curet. Quod peregrinus nec vult nec potest recte curare. Mittas ergo, quam primum potes, qui comes et inspector sit omnium. De me præstabo fidelissime omnia, quæ volueris et quæ potero.

B. V.
Magdæburgi 9 Iunij 1600.
Tuus
GEORGIUS ROLLENHAGEN.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the noble, honourable, and highly-learned Master Tycho Brahe at Knudstrup, His Roman Imperial Majesty's Astronomer at Benátky, my … favourable lord. With all speed [CITISSIME].

Greeting.

Generous and most learned Master Tycho, our Canons vehemently await the carrying-off of your astronomical equipment [suppellex], and offer ready money for everything. There also approached me Grützmacher, a Dresden boatman, who promises that he will convey everything rightly by his ships as far as Leitmeritz [Litoměřice]. And behold, another of our [townsmen], who asks the same — that it be entrusted to him, [saying] that with two ships he would duly stow everything. For the rest of the equipment has lately been conveyed from Hamburg too. What is the impediment, I wrote before Pentecost; and because I obtain no answer, I judge that my [letter] was not delivered. Wherefore I have asked this neighbour of mine either to deliver these [present lines] himself, or — a messenger being hired at Prague (whom you would pay) — to inquire about your will. But this is the [cause of] delay: that the inventory of the earlier carriage your Andreas took away with him, so that we cannot at all entrust it to the boatmen without notable error. Altogether, then, it is necessary that Andreas himself return to us, or someone of [your] household to whom everything is known by outward inspection, and who may restow [it] from ship onto the shore, protect [it] against rains, and afterward see to its conveyance to you. Which a stranger neither wishes nor is able rightly to attend to. Send, therefore, as soon as you can, someone to be the companion and overseer of everything. For my part, I will most faithfully perform everything you wish and that I can.

Fare well.
Magdeburg, 9 June 1600.
Yours,
Georg Rollenhagen.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Georg Rollenhagen (1542–1609), rector of the Magdeburg cathedral school and a celebrated German poet (the Froschmeuseler); here acting as Tycho's logistics agent on the Elbe transport route for his instruments.
  • The instrument transport. A vivid document of the shipping of Tycho's astronomical equipment (suppellex Astronomica) up the Elbe to Bohemia: the Magdeburg cathedral canons are eager for it to move on (and offer cash); the Dresden boatman Grützmacher (and a rival local) offer to carry it by ship as far as Leitmeritz (Litoměřice); "the rest of the equipment has lately been conveyed from Hamburg too." The hold-up is that Andreas, Tycho's man, carried off the inventory (inventarium) of the first consignment, so no one can check the freight; Rollenhagen begs Tycho to send Andreas back, or another servant who knows the instruments by sight, to escort and protect them.
  • CITISSIME ("with all speed") on the address marks urgent dispatch. Canonici = the Magdeburg cathedral canons. The German address-line's ASTRONOMO is printed with Greek letters (ΑΣΤΡΟΝΟΜΟ).
  • ⚠ Date. The dateline reads Magdaeburgi 9 Iunij 1600 = 9 June 1600; the edition (identifier …0619… / label "19. juni") gives 19 June. The header follows the as-written 9 June and notes the catalogue's 19 June; the internal "I wrote before Pentecost" is consistent with an early-June date. Magdeburg (Lutheran) = Old Style.
