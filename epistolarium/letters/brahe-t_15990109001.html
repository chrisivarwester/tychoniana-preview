The original

DEM EHRNUESTENN VNND ACHTBARNN ANDREASS MEYERN, FURSTLICHEN MECKELBURGISCHENN RENTMEISTERN VND RATH, MEINEM JNSONDERS GUNSTIGEN VNNDT GUTENN FREUNDT. WISMAR.

MEIN freundlichenn grueß mit wunschung alles guthen zuuor, ehrnuester vnndt achtbar, gunstiger herr renttmeister, besonder guter freundt. Jch kann euch freundtlich nicht bergenn, welchergestalt mir meine elteste tochter anhero zugeschriebenn vnnd zuuernemen gegebenn, daß sie ahnn euch schreibenn vnnd vonn denn baldt betagtenn zinßenn ein zwey hundert taller auffnehmenn lassenn wolte, zweiffell derowegenn nicht, denn das solches beschehenn sein werde, vnnd jhr dargegenn gedachter meiner tochter handtschrifft zuhandenn empfangenn habenn, welche eben souill gelttenn alß wann jchs selbst gethan hette, sollenn auch hernechst wider geburlich gekurtzet werdenn. Jch habe vor etzlichenn wochenn einen eigenen bottenn inn Dennemarckenn abgeferttiget, der meinung, daß mir etzlich geldt von meinen doselbst noch außstehendenn haubtsummen vbersendet werdenn solte. Dieweil es aber itzo vielleicht der winter oder anders was verhindert, kann jch nicht wißenn, wie balt jch dasselbe geldt vberkommenn könne, vnndt jch gleichwoll itziger zeit inn diesenn frembdenn örternn geldes wolbenöttiget, sintemalln jch mich mit denn meinen inn die zwölff personnen starck vnndt souil pferde inn die funff wochen zu Dreßdenn aufgehaltenn, zugeschweigen was mir sonsten auf dem wege gekostet, worvber mein zehrgeldt, welchs jch zur Pragischen reise mitgenommen, aussgangenn, alß ist derowegenn ahn euch mein freundliches bittenn, jhr wollenn mir die freundtschafft beweisenn, vnndt noch zwey hundert thaller mit diesenn wittembergischen fuhrmann Dauidt gegenn vberantworttung meiner quitantz, welche jch ihme zu handen zugestellet, vberschickenn, vnndt jhme dieselbenn, wenn ehr vonn Rostock wider zu ruck auf Gustrow kumpt, empfangenn lassenn, daß ihr es mitlerzeit alda bestellen wollet.

Die victualienn, welche jch vonn euch empfangenn, belangend, bitte jch mir vnschwer zuerke[nnen] zugebenn, was sie kostenn, sollenn in den zinßen nichtweniger auch abgezogenn vnnd gerechnet werdenn. Jnn diesenn erzeiget ihr mir ahnitzo einen sonderlichenn angenehmen dienst, vnndt jch bin es vmb euch mit wunschung eines glucksehligenn neuwen jahrs jedertzeit hinwider zubeschuldenn geflissenn vnnd bereitwillig, vnndt thue euch göttlicher almacht befehlenn.

Datum Wittemberg denn 9. januarij a:o 99.
Eur a[ller]f[r]w[ndlichster]
Tycho Brahe
eigenhd.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and worshipful Andreas Meier, princely Mecklenburg rent-master and councillor, my especially favourable and good friend. Wismar.

My friendly greeting, with the wish of all good, beforehand. Honourable and worshipful, favourable Lord rent-master, especial good friend: I cannot, in friendship, conceal from you in what manner my eldest daughter has written to me here, and given me to understand, that she meant to write to you and have a couple of hundred thalers taken up from the soon-falling-due interest. I do not doubt, therefore, that this will have been done, and that you have received in return my said daughter's bond to hand — which is worth just as much as if I had given it myself — and these [thalers] shall hereafter be duly deducted [from the account]. Some weeks ago I dispatched a special messenger into Denmark, in the expectation that some money should be sent me from my principal sums still outstanding there. But since now perhaps the winter, or something else, hinders it, I cannot know how soon I may obtain that money; and I am withal, at this time, in these foreign parts, in good need of money — seeing that I have stayed some five weeks at Dresden with my people, some twelve persons strong and as many horses, not to mention what it otherwise cost me on the road, whereby my travel-money, which I took along for the Prague journey, has run out. So my friendly request to you, therefore, is that you would show me the kindness and send me another two hundred thalers by this Wittenberg carter David, against delivery of my receipt (which I have handed to him); and have the same paid to him when he comes back from Rostock to Güstrow — so that you would meanwhile have it made ready there.

As for the victuals which I received from you, I ask you to let me know, without difficulty, what they cost; they shall, no less, be likewise deducted and reckoned against the interest. In this you now render me an especially welcome service; and I am, with the wish of a happy New Year, at all times zealous and ready to deserve it of you in return. And I commend you to divine omnipotence.

Given at Wittenberg, the 9th of January, in the year '99.
Your most friendly,
Tycho Brahe
in his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender & moment. Tycho Brahe at Wittenberg, January 1599 — mid-migration, between his departure from Denmark (1597) and his establishment in Bohemia (1599). The letter is the earliest of the Tycho↔Meier series and the financial backstory to the later debt crisis: it shows the normal working of the relationship — Tycho drawing cash advances against the interest on capital he had lent the Mecklenburg house, through its rent-master, while travelling.
  • Recipient. Andreas Meier, fürstlich-mecklenburgischer Rentmeister und Rat at Wismar — here in routine, cordial dealings, two years before the acrimony of 1601 (brahe-t_16010527001, brahe-t_16010718001).
  • The transaction.
  • Tycho's eldest daughter had arranged to draw c. 200 thalers against the bald betagte Zinsen ("soon-falling-due interest"), giving Meier her own bond (Handschrift) — which Tycho ratifies as binding as his own.
  • Tycho asks for a further 200 thalers by the Wittenberg carter David, against a receipt, payable when David returns from Rostock to Güstrow — his travel-funds (Zehrgeld) for the Prague journey having run out after a five-week stay at Dresden with a party of "twelve persons and as many horses."
  • He had sent a messenger to Denmark to draw on capital still outstanding there, but feared winter would delay it.
  • The victuals (Victualien) Meier had supplied are to be costed and set against the interest.
  • Persons named.
  • meine älteste Tochter = Tycho's eldest daughter (Magdalene Brahe), acting on his account.
  • der wittenbergische Fuhrmann David = the Wittenberg carter David, the courier/cash-bearer.
  • Places. Wittemberg (place of issue); Wismar (Meier's seat); Dresden (the five-week stop); Rostock, Güstrow (the Mecklenburg payment-points); Dänemark (outstanding capital); the Pragische Reise (the Prague journey ahead).
  • Formulae & abbreviations. Burgher-courteous register; subscription Eur allerfreundlichster ("Your most friendly") + eigenhändig ("in his own hand," autograph). Datum → "Given."
  • Calendar. Dateline den 9. Januarij a:o 99 at Wittenberg; no style marker. Lutheran Saxony kept the Julian (Old Style) calendar in 1599; reproduced as written, not converted. (The year is given as "99.")
  • Correction. Restored the approximative hedge of the German 'in die zwölf... in die fünf Wochen' ('some twelve... some five weeks'), confirmed idiomatic (not exact) by Dreyer's print (TBOO XIV p.149); the translation had previously stated both as precise counts.
