EXEMPLUM LITERARUM ELECTORIS
BRANDENBURGENSIS AD REGEM
DANIÆ.

DURCHLEUCHTIGER könig, E:K:W: seindt Vnsere freundtwillige dienste vndt waß Wir
der vatterlichen verwandtnuß nach viel mehr ehr, liebs vndt guts vermögen,
allzeit zuuor. Freundtlicher hertzvielgeliebter herr vetter vndt sohn, E:K:W:
geruhen freundtlich, jhr den einschluß vorlesßen zue laßen, wes gestalt Wir von
den ehrenuehsten Vnsern lieben besondern Tycho Brahen vmb intercessionschrifft
an E:K:W: bittlich angelanget vndt ersuchet worden.

Ob Wir nuhn wohl nicht gemeinet E:K:W: mitt etwas, so derselben zue
wieder sein möchte, verdrisßlich zue sein, dahero Wir denn auch fest hierundter
(sintemahln was ausser seinen Tycho Brahens bericht, wie es vmb seine sachen
gewandt, eigenlich nichts bewust, zue dem auch E:K:W: Vns dermasßen bekandt, das
sie auch ohne vnsere vorschrifft einen jeden was billich wiederfahren zue lasßen
geneigt) bedenckens getragen, dannoch aber vndt weil Wir sein suchen, bitten
vndt erbietten gleichwohl nicht vnbillich vermergken, zue dem auch bey E:K:W:
nicht vernommen, das sie ihme mitt vngnaden gewogen sein soltten, so haben Wir
endtlich seinen vndterthenigen suchen statt geben, vndt die gebetene
vorbittschrifft fuglich nicht verweigern mögen. Ersuchen dem nach E:K:W: hier
mitt ganz dienst- vndt freundtlich bittendt, E:K:W: solches nicht allein von Vnß
nicht vbel aufnehmen, sondern auch sein Tycho Brahens wegkziehen auß Dennemargk
im keinen vngnaden vermergken vndt sein gnedigster
könig vndt herr sein vndt bleiben, auch nach dero gelegenheitt die Verordnung
thuen wollen, daß ihme, seinen ferner vndterthenigst suchen vndt bitten nach,
sowoll der hienwegnehmung seiner vff der insul zue Vranenburgk noch habenden
jnstrument vndt waß ihm aldar mehr zueständig sein möchte, vndt dann erstettung
seines angezogenen Verlages, als auch der perpetuirten fundation halben zue
nutzlicher werkstellung deß astronomischen studij, gewelfahrt, vndt er also
hierinn, inn einem vndt dem andern, so viel muglich dieser Vnser, ihm zwar zue
gnaden aber E:K:W: gar nicht zue einigen vorgrifft, gemeinten intercession
seiner geschöpften hofnung nach empfindtlich geniessen möge. Das wirdt
er sich vngezweiffelt nach höchsten seinem vermögen zuuordienen bevleisßigen,
vndt Wir seindt E:K:W: zu allen behaglichen freundtwilligen diensten jederzeitt
geflisßen.

Datum Cölln an der Sprew, den 25. januarij anno 98.

V:G:g: Jochim Friederich
churfurst zue Brandenburgk.

Mutatis mutandis an die königin, dasß jhr k: w: bey derselben herrn gemahl
deß beste inn gueten andtwort verwenden wolle.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Copy of a letter of the Elector of Brandenburg to the King of Denmark.

Most Serene King, [be assured of] our amicable services beforehand at all times, and of whatever, by reason of our paternal kinship, we are able [to do] far more in honour, love, and good. Most amicable, dearly-beloved lord cousin and son: may it please Your Royal Majesty graciously to have the enclosure read out to her, [as to] in what manner we have been petitioned and entreated by the honourable, our trusty and well-beloved Tycho Brahe for a letter of intercession to Your Royal Majesty.

Now, although we were indeed not minded to be irksome to Your Royal Majesty with anything that might be contrary to her — wherefore we also bore firm hesitation in this matter (inasmuch as, apart from his, Tycho Brahe's, [own] report of how things stand with his affairs, [we] are in truth aware of nothing; and besides, Your Royal Majesty is so [well] known to us that she is inclined, even without our recommendation, to let everyone have done to him what is fair) — yet nonetheless, and because we for our part do not deem his application, request, and offer unfair, and because we have moreover not heard from Your Royal Majesty that she should be disposed toward him with disfavour, we have at length given place to his humble application, and could not in propriety refuse the requested letter of intercession. Accordingly we entreat Your Royal Majesty herewith, requesting in all dutiful and amicable wise, that Your Royal Majesty not only would not take such a thing ill of us, but would also not regard his — Tycho Brahe's — departure out of Denmark with any disfavour, and would be and remain his most gracious king and lord, and would also, as her opportunity allows, make the ordinance that he — according to his further most-humble application and request — may, both as regards the removal of his instruments still remaining on the island at Uraniborg, and whatever else there might further belong to him there, and then [as regards] the reimbursement of his alleged outlay [Verlag], as well as on account of the perpetuated foundation for the profitable carrying-out of the astronomical study, be furthered; and that he thus herein, in the one matter and the other, may, so far as possible, palpably enjoy, according to the hope he has conceived, this our intended intercession — [made] indeed to his favour, but in no way to any prejudice of Your Royal Majesty. This he will undoubtedly strive to deserve to the utmost of his ability; and we are at all times zealous for Your Royal Majesty in every agreeable and amicable service.

Given at Cölln an der Spree, the 25th of January, in the year [15]98.
By the grace of God, Joachim Friedrich,
Elector of Brandenburg. With the necessary changes, [the like was written] to the Queen: that Her Royal Highness would be pleased to employ her best [endeavour] with her lord consort toward a favourable answer.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Joachim Friedrich, Elector (Kurfürst) of Brandenburg (1546–1608; Elector from January 1598). He addresses the young Danish king as herr vetter vndt sohn ("lord cousin and son"), the standard inter-dynastic courtesy register; the vatterlichen verwandtnuß ("paternal kinship") reflects the Brandenburg–Denmark marriage ties (Joachim Friedrich's family was allied to the Danish house; Christian IV's sister Anna would later marry into the line).
  • Recipient. Christian IV of Denmark (r. 1588–1648, ruling in his own right from 1596). The accompanying same-day letter from the same Elector to the Danish Hofmeister and Kanzler (Christoffer Valkendorf and Christian Friis) survives in the same correspondence series; this is the parallel petition to the king himself.
  • Document type & rubric. The Latin heading EXEMPLUM LITERARUM ELECTORIS BRANDENBURGENSIS AD REGEM DANIAe ("Copy of a letter of the Elector of Brandenburg to the King of Denmark") marks the text as a register/file copy (the witness is Langebek's Diplomatarium, an 18th-c. transcription, hence "Afskrift"). The code-switch into Latin for the rubric over a German body is preserved by the edition's convention
  • Historical context. Tycho left Denmark in the spring of 1597 and by January 1598 was at Wandsbek (in the territory of Heinrich Rantzau), where he was completing the Astronomiae instauratae mechanica (1598). This letter is an intercession seeking three concessions from the crown: (1) the removal of the instruments still on Hven (hienwegnehmung seiner vff der insul zue Vranenburgk noch habenden jnstrument); (2) the reimbursement of his outlay (erstettung seines angezogenen Verlages); and (3) the honouring of the perpetuated foundation for his astronomical work (perpetuirten fundation … deß astronomischen studij).
  • Honorific abbreviations. E:K:W: = Eurer Königlichen Würden ("Your Royal Majesty / Dignity"), the courtesy title used throughout; rendered "Your Royal Majesty." V:G:g: = Von Gottes gnaden ("By the grace of God"), the standard subscription style for a sovereign prince. The princely Wir and the address forms are kept in the elevated register.
  • Negations (flagged for legal/courtesy force). The central period turns on a chain of deliberate litotes, all reproduced exactly: nicht gemeinet … verdrisßlich zue sein ("not minded to be irksome"); nicht vnbillich vermergken ("do not deem unfair"); nicht vernommen, das sie ihme mitt vngnaden gewogen ("not heard that she should be disposed toward him with disfavour"); fuglich nicht verweigern mögen ("could not in propriety refuse"); and the paired nicht allein … nicht vbel aufnehmen, sondern auch … im keinen vngnaden vermergken ("not only would not take it ill … but would also not regard [it] with any disfavour"). Six distinct negating turns in all; none added, none dropped. The closing qualifier ihm zwar zue gnaden aber E:K:W: gar nicht zue einigen vorgrifft ("indeed to his favour, but in no way to any prejudice of Your Royal Majesty") is the diplomatic safeguard and is rendered with its full hedge.
  • Lexical notes.
  • intercessionschrifft / vorbittschrifft — "letter of intercession" (a formal letter of recommendation on a third party's behalf); kept consistent with the rubric and body.
  • angezogenen Verlages — Verlag here = "outlay, expenditure advanced" (the money Tycho laid out, not the modern "publishing house"); angezogen = "alleged, cited, brought forward." Rendered "his alleged outlay [Verlag]," with the German kept in brackets, since the elector reports Tycho's claim without himself vouching for the amount (he has just said he knows the affair only ausser seinen Tycho Brahens bericht — "apart from Tycho Brahe's [own] report").
  • perpetuirten fundation — "the perpetuated foundation": the endowment/foundation (Latinate fundatio) for the support of Tycho's astronomical studies, which Tycho sought to have continued ("perpetuated"); rendered literally, the institutional sense flagged rather than narrowed.
  • werkstellung (= Werkstelligung) — "carrying-out, execution"; here zue nutzlicher werkstellung deß astronomischen studij = "for the profitable carrying-out of the astronomical study."
  • empfindtlich geniessen — "palpably / perceptibly enjoy [the benefit of]," i.e. that Tycho actually feel the good effect of the intercession; rendered "palpably enjoy."
  • geruhen — "may it please / deign"; einschluß — "the enclosure" (the appended document Tycho's case rested on).
  • Orthography (chancery German, 1598). Characteristic v/u and w spellings and chancery doublings: vndt = und; zuuor = zuvor; vermergken = vermerken; vnbillich = unbillig; vff = auf; jnstrument = Instrument(e); wegkziehen = wegziehen; hienwegnehmung = Hinwegnehmung; erstettung = Erstattung; vorgrifff (vorgrifft) = Vorgriff ("prejudice, encroachment"); Sprew = Spree. Cölln an der Spree is the twin town of Berlin (the electoral residence), not Cologne on the Rhine.
  • Calendar / place. Datum Cölln an der Sprew, den 25. januarij anno 98 = "Given at Cölln an der Spree, 25 January [15]98." Denmark and Brandenburg both kept the Julian calendar (Old Style) at this date, so the dateline is O.S.; no conversion is made. The header date matches the dateline as written; the edition label ("1598. 25. januar. Cölln an der Spree") agrees, so no discrepancy.
  • A parallel letter to the Queen. The docket confirms that a second, adapted copy of this same intercession ('mutatis mutandis') was sent to 'the Queen' — Anne Catherine of Brandenburg, the Elector's own daughter, married to King Christian IV at Haderslevhus Castle in November 1597 — asking her to use her influence with 'her lord consort' (Christian IV) toward a favourable reply to Tycho's petition.
  • Corroboration. John Robert Christianson, Tycho Brahe and the Measure of the Heavens (2020), records that after the Brandenburg-Danish wedding at Haderslevhus, Elector Joachim Friedrich and Electress Catherine met Tycho, received him warmly, and 'agreed to write to Queen Anna Catherine and King Christian IV on his behalf' — independently confirming the two-track intercession (King and Queen) this recovered docket describes.
