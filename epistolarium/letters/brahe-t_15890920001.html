English translation machine translation, Secure

Tyge Brahe received a letter,

that he should hereafter keep the beacon on Kullen better maintained than has been done hitherto, so that no complaint from seafaring folk arises over it.

Done at Copenhagen, 20 September, in the year 1589.

Apparatus

  • Document type. Not a letter text but a chancery-register entry (Sjællandske Tegnelser — the "Zealand registers" of outgoing crown letters): the register records, in the third person, that "Tyge Brahe received a letter [to the effect] that…". The translation keeps that register form rather than recasting it as direct address.
  • Issuer. Entered as from Christian IV, but in 1588–89 the king was a child (b. 1577); the realm was governed by the Regency Council (rigsråd) until 1596. The letter therefore issues in the young king's name through the chancery — hence the impersonal register form. Rendered "[In the name of] King Christian IV" in the header.
  • thend løckte paa Kulden — "the beacon [lantern] on Kullen": the light on the Kullen headland (Skåne), whose upkeep was a duty attached to Tycho's crown holdings. løckte = lygte (lantern/light); paa Kulden = "on Kullen." This is the documentary basis for the well-known friction over Tycho's neglect of the Kullen light.
  • Orthography / language note. The text is Danish, in late-16th-c. chancery spelling whose German-style features (sch-, w-, -ck-, theß) caused the edition's language tagging to mis-tag it "German." Verified by reading: hand = han (he), her epter = herefter, schulde = skulle, wed mackt = ved magt ("in repair/maintained"), klagemaall = klagemål (complaint), aff søefarende folck = af søfarende folk (by seafaring folk). Recorded as a Phase-C correction.
  • Date crux — RESOLVED. Collated against Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV p. 50 (item 77; Sjællandske Tegnelser XVII, 208), who dates and datelines the letter 1589; the chancery register sequence (items 77–79, all 1589) confirms it. The working transcription's a:o 1588 was a misreading of the register's 1589, corrected here in the Source block and translation. (7bris = September, the seventh month counting from March.)
