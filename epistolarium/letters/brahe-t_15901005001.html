The original

TYCHO BRAHE AD ANDREAM SEVERINI VELLEJUM. S.

MIROR [te] dilecte M. ANDREA, in tanto tempore mihi nihil scribere: existimo vos Lillebergenses de Lillehuenensibus nihil amplius cogitare, ac si essent remoti extra Solis cœlique vias; fac ut per hunc ministrum meum cognoscam quid rerum agas et quomodo cum tuis vivas et valeas. Typographia mea iam feriatur ob papyri impressoriæ defectum. Misi hisce diebus Typographum meum in Germaniam pro conquirendo papyro et conducendo idoneo Cartopæo, nam moletrina mea, papyro conficiendæ destinata, iam parata est. Si tibi de tali artifice alicubi quicquam constiterit, da operam ut eum mature habeam, et tuos etiam labores urge, nec nimium procrastina.

Vale. Saluta uxorem unâ cum Parentibus et liberis.
Datæ Uraniburgi 5. Octob. 1590.
Tuus TYCHO BRAHE.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Tycho Brahe to Anders Sørensen Vedel. Greeting.

I am astonished, dear Master Anders, that in so long a time you write nothing to me: I suppose that you people of Liljeberg [vos Lillebergenses] give no further thought to us people of little-Hven [Lillehuenensibus], as if we were set apart, beyond the paths of the Sun and the heavens. See to it that, through this servant of mine, I may learn what you are about, and how you live and fare with your household. My printing-press now stands idle for want of printing-paper. I have these last days sent my printer into Germany to procure paper and to engage a suitable papermaker [Cartopaeus], for my mill, intended for the making of paper, is now ready. If anything has come to your knowledge anywhere about such a craftsman, take pains that I may have him in good time; and press on your own labours too, and do not put them off too long.

Farewell. Greet your wife together with your parents and children.
Given at Uraniborg, 5 October 1590.
Yours, TYCHO BRAHE.

Apparatus

  • Sender / recipient. Tycho Brahe writing from Uraniborg on Hven to Andreas Severini Vellejus = Anders Sørensen Vedel (1542–1616), the Danish historian and royal historiographer, then resident at Ribe; an old friend of Tycho's (the addressee, too, of the 1571 letter on his father's death). The edition's recipient field confirms the identification.
  • Salutation / address. S. = salutem [dicit], "[sends] greeting". M. ANDREA — M. = Magister (academic "Master"), rendered "Master Anders."
  • The wordplay Lillebergenses … Lillehuenensibus. The letter's one rhetorical flourish: Tycho coins two mock-ethnic demonyms. Lillebergenses — "the people of Lilleberg/Liljeberg," i.e. Vedel and his circle (Liljeberg was Vedel's house/printing establishment at Ribe). Lillehuenenses — "the people of Lille-Hven," i.e. "little Hven," Tycho and his household on the island of Hven. The jest plays the two place-names against each other ("you Liljeberg-folk" vs. "us little-Hven-folk"); the coinages are kept transliterated in the translation and the sense glossed, rather than flattened into plain "you" and "us." [te] in the opening clause (MIROR [te] … nihil scribere) is an editorial supplement in the source, retained in brackets.
  • Hyperbole. ac si essent remoti extra Solis coelique vias — "as if we [lit. they] were set apart, beyond the paths of the Sun and the heavens": a playful astronomer's hyperbole for being forgotten, not a literal claim; kept as figure.
  • The Uraniborg press and paper-mill. Typographia mea … feriatur ob papyri impressoriae defectum — Tycho's own printing-house (the press he set up on Hven) "stands idle for want of printing-paper." moletrina mea, papyro conficiendae destinata, iam parata est — "my mill, intended for the making of paper, is now ready": the paper-mill Tycho built on Hven, here reported newly completed. Cartopaeus (Greek-derived coinage, χάρτης "paper" + -ποιός "maker") = a papermaker / paper-craftsman; rendered "papermaker," the term flagged. Typographus meus = "my printer," sent to Germany to buy paper and recruit such a craftsman. This passage is a documentary datum for the Hven press/paper-mill chronology (cf. Bogtrykkeri paa Hveen).
  • Closing. Vale = "Farewell". Saluta uxorem unâ cum Parentibus et liberis — "Greet your wife together with your parents and children." Tuus = "Yours."
  • Calendar. Datae Uraniburgi 5. Octob. 1590 = "Given at Uraniborg, 5 October 1590," Old Style (Denmark kept the Julian calendar until 1700; this chapter's convention). The as-written dateline (5 October 1590) agrees with the edition label ("1590. 5. oktober. Uraniborg") — no discrepancy. The header carries the as-written date; no conversion is made.
  • MS witness. A = Copenhagen, Rigsarkivet, Tycho Brahes privatarkiv, a copy (Afskrift), per the source header.
