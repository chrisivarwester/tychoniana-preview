The original

TYCHO BRAHE AD CHRISTIANUM LONGOMONTANUM.

EGO TYCHO BRAHE de Knudstrup, ex Dania oriundus, Sacræ Cæs. Mai. arcem Benaticam in Regno Bohemiæ inhabitans, testor omnibus, cuiuscunque conditionis et dignitatis sint, hisce meis literis, earundem exhibitorem, eruditissimum et honestissimum virum, Magistrum CHRISTIANUM SEVERINI LONGOMONTANUM, natione Cimbrum, mihi per octennium in Dania, et postea aliquamdiu in Germania et Bohemia fuisse domesticum, atque in exercitijs Astronomicis diligentem et fidelem navasse operam. Nunc vero cum res eius non ferant, ut diutius mecum commaneat, vel accrescente ætate cogitet aliam vitæ rationem inire, ideoque in patriam ad suos reverti: ego conatus eius nullatenus impedire volui, sed eum libere et cum bona gratia dimisi. Quare omnes generosos et nobiles viros, præsertim in Dania mihi sanguine iunctos, et alios quocunque locorum claros et eruditos viros officiose et peramanter rogatos habeo, ut prænominatum Magistrum CHRISTIANUM sibi diligenter commendatum habeant, atque ad optima quæque ipsum promovere non degraventur: invenient me vicissim ad quævis officia rursum in eorum gratiam, quantum per me licuerit, præstanda quam paratissimum.

Dabantur Pragæ in metropoli Bohemiæ die 4 Augusti stilo novo Anno Christi vulgari 1600.
TYCHO BRAHE,
manu propria.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Tycho Brahe to [/ for] Christian Longomontanus.

I, Tycho Brahe of Knudstrup, sprung from Denmark, dwelling in His Sacred Imperial Majesty's castle of Benátky in the Kingdom of Bohemia, testify to all — of whatsoever condition and dignity they may be — by these my letters, that the bearer of the same, the most learned and most honourable man, Master Christian Severin Longomontanus, a Cimbrian [Jutlander] by nation, was my domestic [assistant] for eight years in Denmark, and afterward for some while in Germany and Bohemia, and rendered diligent and faithful service in astronomical exercises. But now, since his affairs do not permit that he stay longer with me — or [since], as his years increase, he is minded to enter upon another manner of life, and therefore to return into his homeland to his own people — I have in no way wished to hinder his endeavours, but have dismissed him freely and with good grace. Wherefore I have dutifully and most lovingly entreated all generous and noble men — especially those joined to me by blood in Denmark — and other distinguished and learned men wheresoever, that they hold the aforenamed Master Christian diligently commended to them, and not find it burdensome to promote him to every best [advantage]: they will find me, in turn, most ready to render whatever good offices, again, in their favour, as far as shall be permitted me.

Given at Prague, in the metropolis of Bohemia, the 4th day of August, new style, in the common year of Christ 1600.
Tycho Brahe,
with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Document type. An open letter of recommendation (a testimonial to all whom it may concern), given to Longomontanus on his departure from Tycho's service — not a private letter "to" him, despite the edition's ad Christianum Longomontanum; the ad here means it is the testimonial he carries (he is its exhibitor).
  • Subject. Christen Sørensen Longomontanus (Christian Severin, 1562–1647), natione Cimbrum (a Jutlander, from Lomborg / Longomontanum), Tycho's ablest assistant. Tycho certifies eight years' service in Denmark and further service "in Germany and Bohemia," and dismisses him "freely and with good grace" to return home (with advancing years) — the amicable end of the long collaboration Tycho had tried to prolong eighteen months earlier (cf. brahe-t_15990111001, the Wittenberg letter luring him back). Longomontanus would become professor at Copenhagen and write the Astronomia Danica (1622).
  • ad optima quaeque … promovere — to promote him "to every best [advantage / post]"; Tycho asks his Danish kinsmen and learned men everywhere to advance him, pledging reciprocal good offices.
  • Calendar. Pragae … die 4 Augusti stilo novo … 1600 = 4 August 1600, New Style (stilo novo explicit), Anno Christi vulgari ("in the common [civil] year of Christ"). Header agrees with the label.
