The original

[TYCHO BRAHE AD ANDREAM MEYERUM].

MEIN freundtlichen gruß mit wunschunge alles guten zuuor. Ehrenuester vndt vornemmer herr rentmeister, ich kan dem herrn freundtlicher meinung nicht bergen, wie ich auß des herrn doctoris Lucae Backmeisterj, superintendentis zue Rostochk, neulich an mich gethonen schreiben vernommenn, das er die 600 thaller, so ich im anfang aprilis dießes lauffenden 1600 jahrs solte bej euch loco interesse von wegen der bewusten summa haben gehabt, von meinetwegen empfangen haben, wiewohl solchs nicht zur rechter zeit vber verhoffen geschehen ist. Das ich darumb noch bißhero daßelbige geldt durch gütte gelegenheit nicht habe könnenn erlangen, sondern allereerst durch dem herrn doctorem Johannem Gödelmannum, der gen Rostoch von hinnen zue verreisen in willens ist, alleine laßen abfodern, vndt dieweil er kaum furn neuenjahrstagk dahin gelangen kan, vndt hernacher nur drey monat restiren zu den termin, auf welchen ich die andern 600 thaller fur dießes jahrs interesse haben soll, so ist an dem herrn mein gantz fleißiges bitten vndt begeren, er wolle obgedachten h. doctor Gödellman die andern 600 thaller auf lieferung meiner quitantze zustehen laßen, dan ich sunsten keine so gewiße gelegenheit weis, dieselbe abholen zu laßen. Vndt wen schon die zeit, da dieses interesse sol bezahlt werden, nicht gentzlich alßdan verfloßen ist, sundern 2 oder 3 monat restiren, so ist hiergegen zue erachten, das ich in dießes jahrs zinse lenger alß ein halb jahr, nicht ohne meine schaden, bin aufgehalten vndt verhindert worden, vndt nun nicht vnbillig ist, das mir ein solche leidliche compensation hierinne wiederumb geschehen. Vndt nachdeme ich vor lengst inn Dennemarck geschrieben zue ein edelman von meinem blutßuerwandten mit nahmen Eske Bilde, das er von meinetwegen solte auf nechstverschienen Michaelis die gantze hauptsumme, alß 10000 thaller, aufsagen vndt resigniren, auf das ich dieselbige auf zukumpfftige ostern oder anfange des aprilis köntte zue Lübeck oder Hamburgk, durch ainen darzue gedeputirten treuen man, wiederbekommen, so versihe ich mich, das er daßelbe verrichtet hat, vndt bitte darumbe den herrn rentmeister, das er woll die versehunge thuen vndt eingedenck sein, das ich auf dieselbige zeit die gantze hauptsum an guetten reichsthaller, so in Ober-Teutschlandt gangkbar sein, köntte wiederbekommen. So ferne es aber nicht geschehen ist, entweder durch genanten Eske Bilde oder andern von meinetwegen, so will ich hiemit, krafft dieses brieffes, dieselbe hauptsumme auf nechstkommenden Michaëlis des 1601 Jahrs geresignirt vndt aufgesagt haben, dan mir nicht zutreglich, das dieselbe lenger alda stehen bleibe, vndt weis alhier in kunigreich Behemen besser nutz vndt profit damit zueschaffen. Welchs ich den herrn zeitlich zuuerstehen zue geben nicht habe sollen vnterlaßen, vndt wirt der obgedachte herr doctor Gödellman alda weiter bescheidt, nachdeme vonnothten, mundtlich oder schrifftlich, wißen zuberichten. Bitte der herr wolle hierinne thuen wie es vor sich selbst billich, vndt ich mich zue ihme gentzlich verlaße. Hiemit dem allmechtigen Gott beuehlen.

Datum Prag denn 15 dag nouembris anno 1600.
Des herren allzeit freundtwilliger
Tycho Brahe

English translation machine translation, Secure

[TYCHO BRAHE TO ANDREAS MEIER].

My friendly greeting, with the wish of all good, beforehand. Honourable and distinguished Lord rent-master, I cannot, in friendly intent, conceal from you that I have learned, from the letter lately written to me by Lord Doctor Lucas Backmeister, Superintendent at Rostock, that he received on my behalf the 600 thalers which I was to have had from you at the beginning of April of this current year 1600, in lieu of interest on the known sum — although this happened, contrary to expectation, not at the right time. Hence I have hitherto not been able to obtain that money through a good opportunity, but have only now let it be fetched solely through Lord Doctor Johann Gödelmann, who intends to travel from here to Rostock; and since he can scarcely reach there before New Year's Day, and thereafter only three months remain to the term at which I am to have the other 600 thalers for this year's interest, my wholly diligent request and desire of you is that you would let the aforesaid Lord Doctor Gödelmann have the other 600 thalers upon delivery of my receipt; for otherwise I know no such sure opportunity to have it fetched. And even though the time when this interest is to be paid is not then wholly elapsed, but 2 or 3 months remain, yet against this it is to be considered that I have been held up and hindered in this year's interest for more than half a year, not without my loss, and it is now not unfair that such a tolerable compensation be made me in return.

And since I long ago wrote into Denmark to a nobleman of my blood-relations named Eske Bille, that he should on my behalf, at the last past Michaelmas, give notice on and resign the whole principal sum — namely 10,000 thalers — so that I might recover the same at the coming Easter, or the beginning of April, at Lübeck or Hamburg, through a trusty man deputed thereto: I assume that he has done so, and therefore ask you, Lord rent-master, that you would make the provision, and be mindful, that I might at that same time recover the whole principal sum in good Reichsthalers, such as are current in Upper Germany. But insofar as it has not been done — either through the said Eske Bille or another on my behalf — I will herewith, by force of this letter, have the same principal sum resigned and given notice on for the coming Michaelmas of the year 1601; for it is not advantageous to me that it remain standing there any longer, and I know how to make better use and profit with it here in the kingdom of Bohemia. Which I ought not to have omitted to give you to understand in good time; and the aforesaid Lord Doctor Gödelmann will know how to report further information there, as needed, orally or in writing. I ask that you would do herein as is in itself fair, and I rely on you entirely. Herewith [I] commend [you] to Almighty God.

Given at Prague, the 15th day of November, the year 1600.
Your ever friend-willing
Tycho Brahe,
with his own hand.

In case the Lord Receiver [Rentmeister] should perhaps not be entirely satisfied with this document and receipt, which I herewith send, then Lord Doctor Gödelmann shall, on my behalf, let another [document] follow to you, under his own hand and seal, concerning these six hundred thalers which will fall due on the coming first of April of the year 1601 — [a document] which shall be worth exactly as much as if it had been done by myself. In witness whereof I have signed once again. Done on the day and in the place as above.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Andreas Meier, ducal Mecklenburg rent-master (Rentmeister) at Wismar (cf. brahe-t_16010527001, brahe-t_16010718001). The MS is an autograph draft (Kladde) in the Basel Autographensammlung.
  • Significance — the earliest letter in the Mecklenburg 10,000-thaler debt thread. This 15 November 1600 letter opens (chronologically) the documented exchange that the 1601 letters continue (Tycho→Meier 27 May & 18 July 1601; Tycho→Duke Ulrich 4 June 1601; the ducal replies of 24 July & 9 August 1601). It already lays out the whole financial structure:
  • The loan carries semi-annual interest of 600 thalers (loco interesse): the April-1600 tranche, paid late, was collected for Tycho by Lucas Backmeister, Superintendent of Rostock; the next 600 (due ≈ February 1601) Tycho asks Meier to release to Johann Georg Gödelmann against a receipt, since Gödelmann is travelling to Rostock and is Tycho's only sure carrier.
  • Tycho presses for a small "tolerable compensation," since he has been kept out of this year's interest "for more than half a year."
  • The principal (10,000 thalers). Tycho has instructed his kinsman Eske Bille to give formal notice (aufsagen und resignieren) on the whole principal at the last Michaelmas (29 Sept 1600), so he can recover it "at the coming Easter or beginning of April" at Lübeck or Hamburg, in good Reichsthalers current in Upper Germany. Should that notice have failed, Tycho here, "by force of this letter," resigns the principal for Michaelmas 1601 — "for I know how to make better use and profit with it here in the kingdom of Bohemia."
  • Persons named. Andreas Meier; Lucas Backmeister (Bacmeister, 1530–1608, Superintendent of Rostock; cf. brahe-t_16000919001), who collected the April interest; Johann Georg Gödelmann (the Mecklenburg jurist-diplomat carrier; cf. brahe-t_16010412001); Eske Bille, Tycho's Danish kinsman-agent.
  • Register / formulae. Chancery German in the courteous third person (der Herr, "the Lord [= you]"); subscription mit eigen Hand (= manu propria). The Latin business terms (loco interesse, quittance, resignieren, Termin) embedded in the German by this chapter's convention
  • Calendar. Dateline Prag den 15 dag Novembris anno 1600; New Style (Prague/imperial). Header agrees with the label.
  • Language. This segment is Early New High German, continuous with the letter's published body (also German).
  • What it is. A recovered closing clause of a business letter Tycho sent from Prague (15 November 1600) to Andreas Meier, evidently a Rentmeister (receiver/treasurer) at or near Rostock — the surviving published tail already shows Tycho withdrawing capital from Rostock to redeploy it "here in the kingdom of Bohemia."
  • The 600 thalers. Very likely the same capital sum documented by Dreyer: an interest-bearing loan of Tycho's on which 6% annual interest was paid to his old friend Professor Lucas Backmeister at Rostock [Dreyer, Tycho Brahe (1890), citing Breve og Aktstykker, p. 145] — the published tail of this very letter names "Doctor Lucæ Backmeisterj, superintendentis zue Rostochk" directly.
  • Doctor Gödelmann. Johann Georg Gödelmann appears elsewhere in Tycho's correspondence as early as 12 November 1598 (a letter to Tycho from Prague) [Tycho Brahes Breve, transcribing TBOO]. A Rostock-trained jurist, he is shown here acting as Tycho's legal proxy, empowered to issue a substitute, equally binding receipt for the loan if the original is not accepted — a concrete instance of Tycho managing financial affairs in his former territories by power of attorney while resident in Bohemia.
  • Legal formula. The closing Latin tag "Actum die et loco ut supra" ("Done on the day and in the place as above") is standard notarial shorthand, appended even within an otherwise German business letter — evidence of the document's semi-legal, quasi-notarial character.
