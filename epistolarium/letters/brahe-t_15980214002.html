The original

DEM ERNUESTEN VNNSERM BESONDERN LIEBEN TYCHO BRAHEN VFF KNUDTTSTRUP VNNDT WRANENNBURGK.

VANN Gottes gnadenn Katharina, geborne vndt vermehlte marggreffin, auch churfurstin zue Brandenburgk, jnn Preusen hertzogin. Wnsern gnedigenn grues zuuorn. Ernuester besonder lieber, Wir thuen euch auff eur begehrn hiermitt Vnserm diener vndt lieben getrewenn Johann Mullern zu dem ende vbersendenn, das ehr ein zeittlang mitt euch conuersiren, vnnd sich in mathematico studio aldar exerciren muegte. Begehrn demnach an euch hiermitt ganntz gnedigst, jhr wollett euch nichtt allein jnn dem kegenn jhnen willfehrigk erweisenn, sonndern auch in spagiricis medicamentorum præparationibus, vnnd was jhr sonnstenn auss erfahrung in pyronomicis habtt, jhnen guettwillig vnterrichtenn, vnndt euch inn diesem allenn dermassen erzeigen, wie vff eurre Vnns durch jhnen, Vnnsernn diener, gethene zuesage erbietenn, zu euch Vnser vngezweifeltes gnedigstes vertrawenn gerichtett. Das wollenn Wir jnn allenn gnadenn, darmitt Wir euch sonnds woll gewogenn, hinwieder mitt danck zu erkennen vnuorgessenn halltenn. Vnnd ob Wir jhn woll gherne nebenn eurem sohne ettwass ehe wieder abgeferttiget haben wollten, so seindt doch Vnnserm freundtlichenn hertzlieben herrn gemahl bißhero andere wichtiche sachenn vorgefallenn, das man darzue fueglich nichtt ehe kommenn konnenn. Werdet der wegenn euren sohn, der es zwartt wie auch gedachter Johann Muller an vleissigsten vnndterthenigstenn sollicitiren nicht manglen lassen, im besten enttschuldigett haltenn. Wir seindt der vnzweifeligen zuversichtt, es werden bey der könn: wd:, Vnnserm freunthlichen hertzvielgeliebten herrn vettern vnnd sohne, die euch mittgetheilte intercession schrifftenn ohne frucht nicht abgehen, sonndernn dardurch eure sachenn wiederumb zu selbst wunschendenn gueten standt gelangenn, wie Wir euch denn gnediglich wunschenn, vnnd mochtens euch gnediger meinunge nicht verhaltenn, deme Wir mitt gnadenn gneigtt.

Datum Cöln an der Sprew, denn 14. februarij anno 98
Katarina korforsten, m: h:

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the Right Honourable, Our particular dear Tycho Brahe of Knudstrup and Uraniborg.

By the grace of God, Katharina, born and married Margravine, also Electress of Brandenburg, Duchess in Prussia. Our gracious greeting beforehand. Right Honourable, particular dear [friend], We herewith send you, at your request, Our servant and dear faithful Johann Müller, to the end that he may for a time converse with you and there train himself in the study of mathematics. We therefore desire of you herewith, most graciously, that you will show yourself compliant toward him not only in this, but also instruct him willingly in the spagyric preparations of medicaments, and in whatever else you have from experience in the pyronomic [arts]; and that you will conduct yourself in all this in such a manner as the promise made to Us through him, Our servant, on your behalf gives undertaking — toward you Our undoubted most gracious trust being directed. This We shall, in all the grace wherewith We are indeed well disposed toward you, in turn acknowledge with thanks and hold unforgotten. And although We should indeed gladly have had him dispatched back, together with your son, somewhat sooner, yet other weighty matters have hitherto befallen Our friendly, heartily dear lord and husband, such that one could not fittingly come to it sooner. You will therefore hold your son excused in the best [sense] — who indeed, like the said Johann Müller, will not let it be wanting in most diligent, most humble solicitation. We are of the undoubted confidence that, with the Royal Majesty, Our friendly, most heartily beloved lord cousin and son, the letters of intercession imparted to you will not go off without fruit, but that thereby your affairs will again attain the good standing you yourself wish for — as We do graciously wish you; and We would not, of gracious intent, withhold this from you, to whom We are inclined with grace.

Given at Cölln an der Spree, the 14th of February, year [15]98.
Katharina, Electress, with my own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Katharina of Brandenburg-Küstrin (1549–1602), Electress of Brandenburg, wife of the Elector Johann Georg of Brandenburg (r. 1571–98 — the "freundtlichen hertzlieben herrn gemahl", "friendly heartily dear lord and husband," named below). She titles herself "born and married Margravine, also Electress of Brandenburg, Duchess in Prussia" — the standard Hohenzollern intitulatio (the Elector of Brandenburg held the Duchy of Prussia in fief).
  • Recipient. Tycho Brahe, addressed "of Knudstrup and Uraniborg" (VFF KNUDTTSTRUP VNNDT WRANENNBURGK) — his ancestral seat at Knudstrup in Scania and his observatory-castle on Hven. By the dateline (14 Feb. 1598) Tycho had in fact already left Hven (he departed for the Continent in 1597); the address preserves his styled titles, not his current residence.
  • Johann Müller. Katharina's diener (servant/court attendant), sent to study with Tycho. Not securely identified beyond the letter; not to be conflated with the astronomer Johannes Müller (Regiomontanus, †1476). The literature shows Tycho, in a related letter (Dreyer, Opera Omnia VIII), agreeing to receive "the boy" the Illustrious Lady had decided to send, "and a spagyric laborator [worker] too" — consistent with Müller's training brief here.
  • "eurem sohne" / "euren sohn" — Tycho's son (the text does not name which; Tycho's sons Tycho the younger and Jørgen were of an age to act as suitors at court). He is at the Brandenburg court soliciting Tycho's affairs alongside Müller.
  • "der könn: wd:" = der königlichen Würde ("the Royal Majesty / royal dignity"), named "Our friendly, most heartily beloved lord cousin and son." This points to Christian IV of Denmark (r. 1588–1648), to whom intercession on Tycho's behalf would be sought; the vetter und sohn register reflects the dynastic kinship between the Hohenzollern and Oldenburg houses. Identification flagged: the source gives only the abbreviated style, not a name; "Christian IV" is the most natural referent but is supplied editorially.
  • intercession schrifftenn — "letters of intercession," i.e. recommendatory letters interceding on Tycho's behalf (regarding his loss of the Hven living and royal favour). Kept as "letters of intercession."
  • Technical / Latin code-switch. The chancery German switches into Latin for the learned matter: mathematico studio = "the study of mathematics"; spagiricis medicamentorum praeparationibus = "spagyric preparations of medicaments" (Paracelsian chemical pharmacy — spagyric kept as a period term, not modernised to "chemical"); in pyronomicis = "in pyronomics," the art of the regulated fire/furnace (assaying and distillation by controlled heat) — rendered "in the pyronomic [arts]," the bracketed noun supplied. conuersiren, exerciren, sollicitiren, intercession are Latinate chancery verbs, rendered to match the learned register.
  • Negations (verified count, 5). nichtt allein … sonndern auch = "not only … but also"; nichtt ehe kommenn konnenn = "could not come to it sooner"; nicht manglen lassen = "will not let it be wanting"; ohne frucht nicht abgehen = "will not go off without fruit"; nicht verhaltenn = "would not withhold." All five negations are reproduced; none added or dropped. The double construction ohne frucht nicht abgehen ("not go off without fruit") is a litotes affirming success, kept as the period idiom.
  • Orthography (chancery German, c. 1598). VANN/VANN Gottes gnadenn = von Gottes Gnaden ("by the grace of God"); Wnsern/Vnsern = unsern; grues zuuorn = Gruß zuvor ("greeting beforehand," the address-formula, the edition's convention); thuen = tun; muegte = möchte; willfehrigk = willfährig ("compliant"); guettwillig = gutwillig; vnuorgessenn = unvergessen; abgeferttiget = abgefertigt ("dispatched"); fueglich = füglich ("fittingly"); vleissigsten vnndterthenigstenn = fleißigsten untertänigsten ("most diligent, most humble"); vettern = Vetter ("cousin/kinsman"). The princely Wir/euch register and full titulature are preserved.
  • Subscription. Katarina korforsten, m: h: — "Katharina, Electress" (Kurfürstin, the spelling phonetic/Low-German-influenced), with m: h: = mit (eigener) Hand / manu propria, "with my own hand." Rendered "with my own hand."
  • Place / calendar. Cöln an der Sprew = Cölln an der Spree, the twin town of Berlin and seat of the Brandenburg court (the Cöllnisches Schloss); not Cologne on the Rhine. 14. februarij anno 98 = 14 February 1598; Brandenburg and Denmark were both on the Julian calendar (O.S.) in 1598. The dateline as written governs the header; the edition label ("1598. 14. februar. Cölln an der Spree") agrees.
