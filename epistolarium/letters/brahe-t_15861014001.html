The original

DEM VESTEN VNSERM LIEBEN BESONDERN TYCHONI BRAHE, HERRN ZU KNUDSTRUP.
WILHELM VON GOTTES GNADEN LANDTGRAUE ZU HESSEN/ GRAUE ZU CATZENELNBOGEN/ DIETZ/ ZIEGENHAIN VND NIDDA/ ETC.

UNSERN gnedigen Gruß zuuor/ Vester lieber besonder/ Wir haben euch nun zu zweyen vnderschiedenen mahlen/ wol bey ewrem Diener/ den 14 Aprilis, als auch hernacher den 26 Augusti geschrieben/ vnd vnsere Obseruationes Stellarum fixarum vberschickt/ vnd darneben in gnaden begeret/ nicht allein ewer Iudicium darauff vns zuzuschreiben/ sondern auch ewre Obseruationes dargegen zuuberschicken. Wann vns aber biß noch von euch kein antwort einkommen/ welches vns gleichwol verwundert/ Als haben wir nicht vnderlassen wollen/ bey dieser gelegenen Botschafft/ euch nachmahls zuerinnern/ Vnd ist an euch vnser gnediges begeren/ Ihr wollet vns nicht allein auff solche vnsere schreiben hinwieder beantworten/ Sondern auch ewre Obseruationes vbersenden/ damit wir sie mit den vnsern zu conferiren haben. Das versehen wir vns/ vnd seind Euch mit gnaden geneigt/

Datum in vnser Stadt vnd Vestung Cassell am 14 Octob. Anno 86.
WILHELM LANDTGRAFF ZU HESSEN.

English translation machine translation, Probable

To the trusty, our dear and especial [friend] Tycho Brahe, Lord of Knudstrup.
Wilhelm, by the grace of God Landgrave of Hesse, Count of Katzenelnbogen, Dietz, Ziegenhain, and Nidda, etc.

Our gracious greeting beforehand. Trusty, dear, and especial [friend]: we have now written to you on two separate occasions — duly by your servant, on 14 April, and again afterward on 26 August — and have sent over our observations of the fixed stars, and therewith graciously requested that you not only write us your judgement upon them, but also send over your own observations in return. But since to this hour no answer has come to us from you — at which we are, indeed, not a little surprised — we would not omit, by this convenient messenger, to remind you once more. And it is our gracious desire of you that you would not only reply again to these our letters, but also transmit your observations, so that we may have them to compare with our own. This we look for of you, and we remain graciously inclined toward you.

Given in our city and fortress of Kassel, on 14 October, in the year [15]86.
Wilhelm, Landgrave of Hesse.

Apparatus

  • Correspondent. Wilhelm IV "the Wise," Landgrave of Hesse-Kassel (1532–1592), the princely founder of the Kassel observatory and Tycho's chief astronomical peer-patron; his mathematician Christoph Rothmann (see brahe-t_15870812001) handled the technical detail.
  • Titulature. The full style — Landgrave of Hesse, Count of Katzenelnbogen, Dietz, Ziegenhain, and Nidda — is reproduced; these are the constituent lordships of the Hessian title and are kept in their German forms.
  • Register. Chancery letter in the princely first-person plural (Wir … euch … unser gnädiges Begehren); the deferential-yet-commanding tone of a prince to a foreign noble savant is preserved ("it is our gracious desire of you…"). Vester (= fest/getreu) "trusty"; lieber besonder "dear and especial," the standard chancery address.
  • Code-switching (kept and italicised). The Latin technical and bureaucratic nouns embedded in the German — Observationes Stellarum fixarum ("observations of the fixed stars"), Iudicium ("judgement / expert opinion"), Observationes, and the verb conferiren ("to compare/collate") — are rendered into English but flagged as the Landgrave's own Latin within the German, by the edition's convention
  • Substance. A reminder that the Landgrave's two earlier letters (14 April, 26 August 1586) enclosing Kassel's fixed-star observations have gone unanswered; he presses again for Tycho's Iudicium and a reciprocal set of Hven observations to collate — the data-exchange that underlies the printed correspondence.
  • Calendar. O.S. (Lutheran Hesse kept Julian).
