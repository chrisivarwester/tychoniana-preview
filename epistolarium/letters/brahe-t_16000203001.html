The original

DEM EDLEN VNND GESTRENGEN HERRN TYCHO BRAHAe, HERRN AUF KNUDTSTRUPP VNND VRANIBURGKH, MEINEM SONNDERSLIEBEN HERRN VNNDT GUETTEN FREUNDT.

S.

LITTERAS tuas, Vir nobilissime, quibus me tam splendide per egregios iuvenes, internunctios tuos, salutas, mei amore et commendatione plenas, imo in excultioribus meae tenuitatis nimiam paene laudem continentes, accepi, cum voluptate profecto, dubius tamen, tot inter gratulandi occasiones palmam cui concederem. Ecce autem animi tandem occurrebat sinceritas, omnibus alijs non immerito praeferenda. Caetera, tanquam illius effectus et signa civilitati ac humanitati Tuae adscribens, pari, si non calamo, mentis tamen integritate et studio compensanda.

Quod D. FRANCISCI TENGNAGELIJ, familiaris tui, virtutem ac valorem, quem ipsa facies et mores exprimunt, etiam litterarum tuarum testimonio apud me augere tibi placuerit, nil miror. Est tamen mihi quod gratulor et tibi, qui tantum Adolescentem cognoscendum dederis, gratiam ut referam. Rationem consilij eius de Bohemica hac ora tui procul dubio causa non tam brevi relinquenda plurimum etiam nostro ex interesse approbamus et laetamur, ac ipsi et filio tuo quam valemus et habemus exilitatem in hac aula nostram fideliter et cum libertate offero.

Magistrum KEPLERUM, omni praeconio maiorem, favoreque tuo et patrocinio multis rationibus dignum, tibi iam commendatum amplius nunc non commendo, sed ipsum se spectandum et fruendum potius praemitto, apud te, mihi, ut spero, multarum rerum praeconem et conciliatorem. Intermediatoris tamen quod officium ita amanter et benigne cum gratiarum actione susceperis, ad me pertinet, Domine TYCHO, ac facis in eo, te certe ac humanitate tua, quin nostra, ad te perpetuo amandum et colendum benevolentia haud indignum.

Addo idipsum, nostri gratia quicquid porro accesserit, id omne non tam ad cumulum tuorum in nos meritorum esse referendum, quam alta et grata memoria prosequendum. In specie vero, quae et ipsius KEPLERI ratione conferenda essent aliqua, in occasionem nostri conventus differre cogimur, quem nobis efficiat Deus propediem, ut spero et opto.

Vale ac reliqua ex ipso KEPLERO et tuis.
Pragae 3 Februarij A. D. 1600.
T. D. ex animo
IOHANNES FRIDERICUS HOFFMANN,
L. Baro.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the noble and gallant Lord Tycho Brahe, lord of Knudstrup and Uraniborg, my especially dear lord and good friend.

Greeting.

Your letter, most noble man — in which you so splendidly greet me through those excellent young men, your messengers, [a letter] full of love and commendation of me, indeed containing in its more polished phrases almost excessive praise of my slender worth — I received with pleasure, in truth, yet at a loss to which among so many occasions for thankfulness I should award the palm. But behold, the sincerity of your mind presented itself at last, not undeservedly to be preferred to all the rest. The other [qualities], ascribing them as the effects and tokens of that [sincerity] to your courtesy and humanity, are to be repaid with an integrity and zeal of mind equal [to yours], if not of pen.

That it pleased you to enhance with me — by the testimony of your letter too — the virtue and worth of Master Franz Tengnagel, your familiar (which his very face and bearing express), I do not wonder at. Yet I have cause to congratulate, and to render you thanks, who have given me so fine a young man to know. The reasoning of his plan to leave this Bohemian shore — doubtless not so soon, [and] for your sake — we likewise much approve and rejoice at, out of our own interest as well; and to him and to your son I faithfully and freely offer our slender [standing] in this court, so far as we are able and have it.

Master Kepler — greater than all proclamation, and worthy of your favour and patronage on many grounds — already commended to you, I now commend no further, but rather send him on ahead to be beheld and enjoyed in person; with you he will be, as I hope, the herald and reconciler of many matters for me. Yet that you have so lovingly and kindly, and with thanksgiving, taken up the office of intermediary — that, Lord Tycho, falls to me [to acknowledge]; and in it you do a thing assuredly not unworthy of being perpetually loved and revered by me with goodwill — by you and your humanity, nay ours.

I add this very thing: whatever shall further accrue for our sake, all of it is to be set down not so much to the heap of your services toward us, as to be attended with deep and grateful memory. But in particular, certain matters that should be conferred upon by reason of Kepler himself, we are forced to defer to the occasion of our meeting — which may God bring about for us soon, as I hope and wish.

Farewell, and the rest from Kepler himself and from your people.
Prague, the 3rd of February, in the Year of the Lord 1600.
Yours from the heart,
Johann Friedrich Hoffmann,
Free Baron.

Apparatus

  • Direction & sender. An inbound letter to Tycho. Johann Friedrich Hoffmann von Grünbüchel und Strechau, liber baro (Free Baron), imperial councillor and a patron of Kepler's — the same magnate at whose Prague house Kepler lodged and whose greeting Tycho sends in the famous summons-letter (brahe-t_16000126002, where he is Baro Iohannes Fridericus Hoffmannus). The mixed presentation (German address-line over a Latin body) is preserved.
  • Occasion. Dated 3 February 1600 — the day before Kepler's first arrival at Benátky (4 February). Hoffmann, who had brought Kepler from Graz to Prague, here sends Kepler on to Tycho (ipsum … praemitto) and acknowledges Tycho's letter of welcome carried by Tycho's "young messengers." It is thus a hinge document of the Tycho–Kepler meeting, the patron's side of the introduction.
  • Persons named.
  • Magister Keplerus = Johannes Kepler, omni praeconio maior ("greater than all proclamation"), commended by Hoffmann as a "herald and reconciler of many matters" at Tycho's.
  • D. Franciscus Tengnagelius, familiaris tuus = Franz Gansneb Tengnagel von Camp, Tycho's noble assistant, whose worth Tycho had praised and whose intended departure from Bohemia (to visit Westphalia — cf. brahe-t_16000306002) is referred to here.
  • filius tuus = one of Tycho's sons.
  • Epistolary formulae & titles. S. = salutem ("Greeting"); Vale → "Farewell"; subscription T. D. ex animo = Tuae Dominationi ex animo ("Yours from the heart") + L. Baro = liber baro ("Free Baron"). The German address meinem sonderslieben Herrn und guten Freund → "my especially dear lord and good friend."
  • Register. A studied, almost mannered courtier's Latin — heavy with the humility topos (meae tenuitatis … exilitatem nostram, "my slender worth … our slender standing"), antithesis (si non calamo, mentis tamen integritate), and deferred clauses. The convolution is preserved rather than smoothed.
  • Calendar. Dateline Pragae 3 Februarij A. D. 1600; the edition marks n.s. (New Style) — correct for an Imperial-court letter from Prague. Reproduced, not converted.
