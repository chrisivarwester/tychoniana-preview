The original

NOBILISSIMO ET AMPLISSIMO VIRO, DOMINO TICHONI BRAHE, VIRO INCOMPARABILE IN ARCE CÆSAREA BENATICA.

NOBILISSIME ET AMPLISSIME DOMINE.

TRES iam septimanæ abierunt, ex quo redditæ mihi sunt litteræ, quas ad me dedisti IX Martij. Sed neque eas accepi, quas memoras datas sub finem anni superioris, neque FRANCISCUM TENGNAGLIUM vidi, quem a te tanto viro commendatum, omnibus fuissem officijs prosecutus. Non gravaberis itaque repetere, et quæ illis litteris erant explicata, et quæ huius fidei commiseras. Me certe, magno tuo merito, habebis ad omnia, quæ quidem erunt meæ potestatis, semper paratissimum. Nec addo plura: mandata tua expecto cupide. Tuas in posterum ad me tuto poteris committere bibliopolæ ei, qui CLAUDIJ MARNIJ Wecheliani officinæ præest Pragæ.

Vir Nobiliss. Deus te servet incolumem.
Francofurto ipsis Kal. Maijs 1600.
Nominis tui studiosissimus
BONGARSIUS.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most noble and most ample man, Master Tycho Brahe, an incomparable man, at the Imperial castle of Benátky.

Most noble and most ample Master.

Three weeks have now passed since the letters were delivered to me which you sent me on 9 March. But I have neither received those which you mention as sent toward the end of last year, nor have I seen Frans Tengnagel, whom — commended by you, so great a man — I would have pursued with every good office. You will not, therefore, find it burdensome to repeat both what was set out in those letters, and what you had entrusted to his good faith. Me certainly, by your great merit, you shall always have most ready for everything that shall indeed be within my power. Nor do I add more: I eagerly await your instructions. Your own [letters] to me you can, in future, safely entrust to that bookseller who is in charge, at Prague, of the office of Claude de Marne the Wechelian.

Most noble Sir, may God keep you unharmed.
From Frankfurt, on the very Kalends of May, 1600.
Most zealous for your name,
Bongars.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Jacques Bongars (see brahe-t_15990501001).
  • A re-sent letter. This is, almost word for word, the same text Bongars had sent a year earlier (brahe-t_15990501001, Frankfurt, 1 May 1599) — the same complaint that the year-end letters never arrived and that Tengnagel had not appeared, the same offer of service, the same instruction to route replies through the Wechel/Marne agent at Prague. Bongars evidently repeated the unanswered letter; the differences are trivial (sunt → erunt, proferebas → fuissem … prosecutus, plus the added closing benediction and subscription). The address now places Tycho at the Imperial castle of Benátky (in arce Caesarea Benatica), where he was installed from 1599.
  • Franciscus Tengnaglius = Frans Tengnagel (see prior letter). Claudij Marnij Wecheliani officina = the Wechel/Marne–Aubry house at Frankfurt, with its Prague agent.
  • ipsis Kal. Maijs = "on the very Kalends of May" = 1 May (Roman-calendar idiom, glossed).
  • Calendar. Frankfurt (Protestant-majority Imperial city) = Julian / Old Style until 1700; taken as O.S. (no n.s. tag in the edition), as in the 1599 instance.
