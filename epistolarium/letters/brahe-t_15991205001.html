The original

ERLICH OCH WELBYRDIGH MANNDTT THYGE BRAHE THILL KNUDTTSTRUP, HØFFUIDTZMANND OFFUER WRANIEBORGH OCH PAA DETT KEYSERLIGE SLOTT BENACHIA, MYNN GUNSTIG OCH SØNNDERLIG GODE WENN GANNDSCHE WENNLIGEN.

MYNN ganndsche ydmygh och wennlig helsenn nu och altid forsenndtt med Gud vor herre. Kierre Thyge Brahe, bethacher jeg Ethers Welb. gandsche storligenn for møgitt beuist gode och wenschaff, som j mig och mine giortt och beuist haffuer, huilkett igien att forschylde jeg gierne vill finndis redebonn och willigh.

Ethers Welb. schriffuelse haffuer jeg entpfanget, huorudaff jeg erfarer, att j och hues ether kiertt er lider well, Gud vere loffuett. Saa haffuer jeg epther ethers schriffuelse hiulpett ethers kierre sønn thill rette, och forschaffed hannom her en skude, som schall føre ethers instrumenter her fran Huenn och thill Lubech. Jeg haffde først fragtett itt schiff der thill med offuerlob paa; samme schipper beuilgett selff att reyse thill Huenn att besee instrumenterne, beretted att verre wmueligtt att faa y nogett stortt schiff for deris storhedts schyld, huorfor jeg endelig motte fragte en aabenn skude, som før er rørtt, med ethers sønns beuilninngh. Nyett aff denne landtz ende haffuer andre ethers wenner well schreffuitt ether thill om. Jeg ønscher Ethers Welb. med alle dj ether kiertt er altidtt att lide vell, dett schall verre mig ganndsche kiertt att høre. Och j hues maade jeg aff min ringe formuffue kand thiene Ethers Fromhed thill gode, skall j altid finnde mig willigh. Will hermed haffue Ethers Welb. med hues ether kiertt er Gud almectigste euindelig befalenndis. :

Aff Kiøbenhaffnn den 5 decembris a:o 99.
E:W: w: t:
Michell Vibe
egen handt.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Honourable and well-born man Tyge Brahe to Knudstrup, Captain over Uraniborg and at the Imperial castle Benátky [Benachia], my favourable and especial good friend — [written] quite friendlily.

My quite humble and friendly greeting, now and always foresent with God our Lord. Dear Tyge Brahe, I thank Your Well-born[ness] quite greatly for the much-shown good and friendship which you have done and shown to me and mine — which to requite in turn I will gladly be found ready and willing.

I have received Your Well-born[ness]'s writing, from which I learn that you, and what is dear to you, fare well, God be praised. So, according to your writing, I have helped your dear son to rights, and procured him here a boat [skude], which shall carry your instruments from here, from Hven, to Lübeck. I had first chartered a ship for it, with an upper deck; the same skipper himself agreed to travel to Hven to inspect the instruments, [but] reported it to be impossible to get [them] into any large ship, on account of their size; wherefore I finally had to charter an open boat, as before mentioned, with your son's approval. News from this end of the land your other friends have surely written to you about. I wish Your Well-born[ness], with all that is dear to you, ever to fare well — that shall be quite dear to me to hear. And in whatever way I, of my slight means, can serve Your Piety for good, you shall always find me willing. Herewith [I] will have Your Well-born[ness], with what is dear to you, eternally commended to God Almighty.

From Copenhagen, the 5th of December, in the year [15]99.
Your Well-born[ness]'s willing servant,
Michel Vibe,
[his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Mikkel Vibe (Michel Vibe, 1561–1624), Copenhagen merchant (later burgomaster); here a practical agent for Tycho in Denmark.
  • The Hven → Lübeck shipment. A key document in the removal of Tycho's instruments from Hven: at Tycho's instruction, Vibe helped Tycho's son charter a boat (skude) to carry the instruments from Hven to Lübeck. He first hired a decked ship (med overløb) whose skipper went to Hven to inspect them, but found it impossible to fit them into any large ship "on account of their size," so an open boat was chartered, with the son's approval. (The Baltic leg — Hven → Lübeck — complementary to the later Elbe leg, Hamburg/Magdeburg → Leitmeritz → Prague: cf. brahe-t_16000619001, …1009001.)
  • Titulature. The address still styles Tycho Høvedsmand over Uraniborg ("Captain/governor over Uraniborg") and "at the Imperial castle Benátky [Benachia]" — registering his new Bohemian seat alongside the old Danish title.
  • Orthography. Late-16th-c. Danish: erlich (ærlig), welbyrdigh (velbyrdig), høffuidtzmannd (høvedsmand), helsenn (hilsen), skude (small boat), offuerlob (overløb, deck), skipper, forschylde (forskylde, requite), Fromhed (Piety, an honorific). E:W: w: t: = Eders Velbyrdigheds villige tjener ("Your Well-born[ness]'s willing servant"). Reading-verified as Danish.
  • Calendar. Kiøbenhaffnn den 5 decembris a:o 99 = 5 December 1599; Denmark = Old Style. Header agrees with the label.
