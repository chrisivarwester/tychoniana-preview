The original

DEM EHRNUESTEN VND ACHTPARN HERRN ANDRESS MAIERN, FÜRSTLICHEM MECKLE[N]BURGISCHEN RENTMEISTERN, MEINEM GUETEN FREUNDTE. WISSMAR.

MEINEN freundtlichen grueß sampt wünschung alles gueten iederzeit bevor. Ehrnuester achtpar, insonders gönstiger lieber her rentmeister. Was sider der auffkündigung dero 10000 thaller hauptsummae sampt dem interesse, damit jj: ff: gg: die junge herschafft zue Mecklenburgk mir verhafftet, vorgelauffen vnd was vngelegenheit mir auß verzugerung der zahlung (welcher termin (meiner auffkündigung nach) verfloßen gewesen den 1. aprilis dieses lauffenden jhars) entstanden, werdet ihr euch auß meinen hiebevor ahn euch ergangenen schreiben nochmahls zur genüege wißen zue entsinn[en]. Dieweiln dan ihr abermahl gegen gleichßfals verlauffenen s. Joannis thagk obangereugte summa vnd interesse richtich zu erlegen erpotten, hab ichs dabej, wiewohl ich dadurch in keinen geringen schaden gerhaten, j: f: g: hertzog Vldrichen etc., meinem gnedichsten herren, zue vnterthenigsten gefallen (wiewohl die bürgern laut vnseres contracts einzuemahnen ich guete vnd pilliche vrsacch gehabt) biß dato beruhen laßen. Vnd nehme mir in kainen zweiffel, ihr werdet nuhnmehr (seitenmahl lenger mitt dem gelde zue warten ich durchauß nitt kan oder bedacht bin) auff die mittel vnd wege gedacht, auch dazu gethon haben, das mehrgenante hauptsumma vnd interesse nuhnmaehr richtich verhanden vnd strax par außgezehlt werden solle vnd könne. Jnmittelst sehe ich gerne, das es bej ainem gewißen man zu Hamburgk oder Lübeck, bis es abgeholet wirt, auffgehoben oder verwahrt würde. Wie dan alßpaldt aine gewiße pottschafft ich derer orter zu senden daßselbige geldt abzuholen bedacht bin. Vnd hab mit zaigern dieses euch söllichs zuvor zu verstendigen, damit er alßpaldt damit gefürdert vnd nitt auffgehalten werde (worauß dan vns baiderseits, so wohl auch den bürgen, allerhande vngelegenheit entstehen würde), vor guet angesehen. Vns hiemit götlicher almacht entpfhelendt.

Prag den 18 julij st:novo a:o 1601.
EE: dienst- vnd freundtwilliger
Tycho Brahe
man: ppj:

[Postscript — restored from Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, p. 226; dropped by the working transcription. See Apparatus.]
Gelanget hieneben an den herren rentmeistern mein freundtliches pitten vnd söcchen, er wolle kainem, der nitt entweder von mir handtschrifft vnd sigell, von heut dato abzurechnen, oder auch von meinem vettern Esche Bilden, zaigen kan, dan auß etzlichen handlen söllichs vonnötten, jnsonderheit wolle er ainem holsteinischen jubilierer mit nahmen Bernhardt Fersten oder seinen dienern, welchen ich zwar vorhin Vollmacht, iedoch in anderer mainung, gegeben, itzo gar nichts folgen laßen. Datum ut [supra].

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and worshipful Lord Andreas Meier, princely Mecklenburg rent-master, my good friend. Wismar.

My friendly greeting, together with the wish of all good, at all times beforehand. Honourable, worshipful, especially favourable, dear Lord rent-master: what has passed since the calling-in of those 10,000 thalers of principal sum, together with the interest, by which Their Princely Graces, the young rulership of Mecklenburg, stand bound to me, and what inconvenience has arisen for me from the delay of payment (the term for which, according to my notice of recall, fell due the 1st of April of this current year) — of this you will know well enough to remind yourself once more from my earlier letters sent to you. Since, then, you have once again offered to pay the above-mentioned sum and interest duly against St. John's Day (which has likewise now passed), I have — although I have thereby come to no small loss — let it rest until now, to the most humble pleasure of His Princely Grace Duke Ulrich etc., my most gracious Lord (although, according to our contract, I had good and fair cause to dun the sureties). And I have no doubt that you will by now (seeing that I altogether cannot, nor am minded to, wait any longer for the money) have thought upon the ways and means, and have so acted, that the aforesaid principal sum and interest shall and may now be duly to hand and at once paid out in cash. In the meantime I should be glad if it were deposited or kept safe with some reliable man at Hamburg or Lübeck until it is fetched; for I intend forthwith to send a trusty messenger to those parts to collect that same money. And I have thought it good to apprise you of this beforehand by the bearer of this [letter], so that he may at once be furthered with it and not held up (whence all manner of inconvenience would arise for us both, and for the sureties as well). Herewith commending us to divine omnipotence.

Prague, the 18th of July, New Style, in the year 1601.
Your Honour's service- and friend-willing
Tycho Brahe
[written] with his own hand.

[Postscript:] Herewith reaches the Lord rent-master my friendly request and entreaty, that he let nothing be paid out to anyone who cannot show either a bond and seal from me — to be reckoned from today's date — or [one] from my cousin Eske Bille, for, from certain dealings, this is necessary; in particular, that he let nothing at all now be paid out to a Holstein jeweller named Bernhard Ferst, or to his servants, to whom I had indeed earlier given a mandate, but with another intent. Dated as above.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Tycho Brahe at Prague, less than four months before his death (24 October 1601). The letter is one of several late documents on his outstanding loans and finances.
  • Recipient. Andreas Meier (Andress Maiern), fürstlich-mecklenburgischer Rentmeister, Wismar = Andreas Meier, the ducal Mecklenburg rent-master (treasurer / financial officer) at Wismar — the official charged with discharging the ducal debt.
  • The transaction. A 10,000-thaler loan (the Hauptsumme, principal) plus Interesse (interest) which the young Mecklenburg ruling house owed Tycho. Tycho had given formal Aufkündigung (notice of recall) with the principal falling due 1 April 1601; when that lapsed, repayment was re-promised for St. John's Day (24 June), which had also now passed. Tycho, though entitled by contract to proceed against the Bürgen (citizen-sureties), had forborne out of deference to Duke Ulrich. He now presses for immediate cash payment, to be lodged meanwhile with a trustworthy agent at Hamburg or Lübeck pending collection by his messenger.
  • Persons named.
  • J. F. G. Herzog Ulrich = Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg(-Güstrow) (1527–1603), Tycho's "most gracious Lord" in the matter.
  • die junge Herrschaft zu Mecklenburg = the young rulers of Mecklenburg (the junior line for whom the debt stood) — Their Princely Graces (Jhr Fürstlich Gnaden).
  • Place. Wismar — the Hanseatic port and Mecklenburg residence-town where the rent-master sat; Hamburg / Lübeck named as safe deposit-points for the funds.
  • Formulae & abbreviations. Burgher-courteous register (ehrnvest und achtbar, "honourable and worshipful"). The subscription EE: dienst- vnd freundtwilliger = Euer Ehren dienst- und freundwilliger ("Your Honour's service- and friend-willing"); man: ppj: = manu propria ("with his own hand," autograph attestation). J. F. G. = Jhr Fürstlich(e) Gnad(en); J. J. F. F. G. G. = the plural ("Their Princely Graces").
  • Calendar. Dateline den 18 Julij st:novo — explicitly New Style, as in all Tycho's Prague letters. The internal deadlines (1. Aprilis; S. Joannis Tag = 24 June) are likewise to be read consistently with the document's stated New-Style usage; reproduced, not converted.
  • §1 Dreyer collation. Closing postscript revoking the Holstein jeweller Bernhard Ferst's mandate, restored from Dreyer XIV p.226 (dropped by the working transcription).
  • Correction. Latin: 'mist nahmen' corrected to 'mit nahmen' in the postscript, per Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, p. 227 ('mit nahmen Bernhardt Ferften').
