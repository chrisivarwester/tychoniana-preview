The original

TILL TYGE BRAHE HELLIGE THREE KONGERS CAPELLIS BYGNING WDI ROSCHILDE DOMBKIRCKE AHNRØRENDIS VT SEQUITUR.

Christian 4..

WOR gunst thilfornn. Wiid, eptersom Wij nogen thiid siden forleeden naadigst haffue schreffuit thig thill om Hellige tre kongers capell wdi Roschilde dombkircke, som wor møgit bygfeldig och brøstholden, attu thend med thet allerførste schulle lade igien forferdige och forbygge, och du nu wnderdanigst lader giffue thilkiende, att samme capell beßuerligen kand igien forferdigis och siiden holdis wed heffd och magt enten met thag eller wdi andre maade, med mindre att thend øffuerste huelning motte affthagis och igien leggis med bielcker och loffte. Tha paa thet samme capell med thet første motte igien bliffue forferdigit och siiden theß bedre kunde holdis med thag och anden nottorfft wed heffd och bygning, bede Wij thig och wille, attu lader samme øffuerste huelling affthage och thet igien lader legge met bielcker och loffte paa the beste maade forwarit, och for:ne bygning paa for:ne capell med thet allerførste schee kand lader ferdig giørre.

Ther met etc. Befallendis etc.
Hafniæ 8. 9bris a:o 94.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To Tyge Brahe, concerning the building of the Chapel of the Three Holy Kings in Roskilde Cathedral, as follows.

Christian IV.

Our favour beforehand. Know that, whereas we did some time ago now graciously write to you concerning the Chapel of the Three Holy Kings in Roskilde Cathedral, which was much in disrepair and decayed, that you should at the very first have it repaired and rebuilt again — and you now most humbly give us to understand that the same chapel can but with difficulty be repaired again and thereafter kept in good repair and condition, whether by roofing or in any other manner, unless the uppermost vaulting were to be taken down and laid again with beams and a ceiling: therefore, so that the same chapel may at the first be repaired again and thereafter be the better kept in repair and building with roofing and other necessaries, we ask of you and will [it], that you have the same uppermost vaulting taken down, and have it laid again with beams and a ceiling secured in the best manner, and have the aforesaid building-work upon the aforesaid chapel made ready at the very first that may be done.

Therewith, etc. Commending [you to God], etc.
At Copenhagen, 8 November, in the year [15]94.

Apparatus

  • Issuer. Christian IV of Denmark (r. 1588–1648), then ruling under the regency council in his minority (he was crowned in his own right in 1596). The letter is a chancery instruction issued in his name.
  • Recipient and capacity. Tyge (Tycho) Brahe, here addressed not as royal astronomer but as a canon (prebendary) of Roskilde Cathedral: from 1579 Tycho held the prebend (canonry) of the Chapel of the Three Holy Kings (Helligtrekongers kapel), a substantial Roskilde benefice whose income was charged with the upkeep of the chapel fabric. The crown is calling on him to discharge that maintenance obligation.
  • Document type. A chancery-register entry (Sjællandske Tegnelser XVIII, 368). The Latin rubric "… AHNRØRENDIS ut sequitur" ("concerning … as follows") heads the Danish body — a code-switch preserved by this chapter's convention AHNRØRENDIS = anrørendes ("concerning"); cf. the parallel formula in the 1584 Frederik II compass-letter.
  • Subject. Hellige tre kongers capell = the Chapel of the Three Holy Kings (the Magi), a late-Gothic chapel adjoining Roskilde Cathedral, the royal burial church of Denmark. bygfeldig och brøstholden — a chancery doublet, "in disrepair and decayed / dilapidated."
  • The structural crux of the order. The substance turns on a single conditional: med mindre att thend øffuerste huelning motte affthagis och igien leggis med bielcker och loffte = "unless the uppermost vaulting were to be taken down and laid again with beams and a ceiling." Tycho had reported (the king relays) that the chapel could not be durably repaired by re-roofing alone unless the top vault were removed and replaced by a flat timber beam-and-board ceiling; the king accordingly commands exactly that course. The translation preserves the report → exception → command chain without smoothing it; huelning/huelling = "vaulting" (the masonry vault), bielcker och loffte = "beams and a [timber] ceiling/loft."
  • Negation / conditional check. The body contains exactly one negating/limiting operator — med mindre ("unless"), governing the vaulting clause — and one near-negative adverb, beßuerligen ("with difficulty / hardly"); both are reproduced and neither added nor dropped. enten … eller … wdi andre maade = "whether [by roofing] … or in any other manner" is a disjunction, not a negation. The directive bede Wij thig och wille is a polite-but-binding royal command ("we ask of you and will it"); rendered with its commanding force intact.
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1594). WOR gunst thilfornn = Vor gunst tilforn ("Our favour beforehand," the royal address-formula, this chapter's convention); Wiid = Vid ("Know"); eptersom = eftersom ("whereas, inasmuch as"); attu = at du; schreffuit = skrevet; forferdige / forferdigis = forfærdige(s) ("repair, put in order"); forbygge = "build up/rebuild"; wnderdanigst = underdanigst ("most humbly/submissively"); thilkiende = til kende ("give to understand"); beßuerligen = besværligen ("with difficulty"); holdis wed heffd och magt = holdes ved hævd og magt ("kept in repair and condition"); thag = tag ("roof"); øffuerste huelning/huelling = øverste hvælving ("uppermost vaulting"); affthagis / affthage = aftages / aftage ("be taken down / take down"); bielcker = bjælker ("beams"); loffte = lofte ("ceiling, loft"); nottorfft = nødtørft ("necessaries, what is needful"); for:ne = fornævnte ("aforesaid," the standard chancery abbreviation); schee kand = ske kan ("may be done").
  • Closing formula. Ther met etc. Befallendis etc. — the abbreviated chancery sign-off; Befallendis = Befalendes ("Commending [you to God / to God's protection]"), the conventional epistolary commendation, here truncated with etc. and so completed in brackets.
  • Calendar / place. Hafniae 8. 9bris a:o 94 = "At Copenhagen (Hafniae), 8 November ( 9bris = November, the ninth month of the Roman year reckoned from March) [15]94" (O.S.). The Latin place-name and month-abbreviation in an otherwise Danish letter are preserved by this chapter's convention; 9bris is the standard early-modern shorthand for Novembris, not "September." The dateline (8 November 1594) and the catalogue label (8 November 1594, Copenhagen) agree; no discrepancy.
