The original

TYCHO BRAHE AD D. PETRUM SEVERINI.

...... hæc ego ad te perscribere volui, ut et tu huius Cometæ si non instrumentalem saltem ocularem et physicam observationem instituere possis, et apud Regem efficere, ne forte ego hinc avocer interim, dum Cometa fulget, ut plenam eius observationem per instrumenta, quæ hîc solum in promtu habeo, perficere possim. Si vero, antequam servus meus has tibi reddiderit, id quod caveri vellem, factum est, da operam, ut Regis consensu per alias literas infectum fieri possit.

Vale et saluta amicos.
Datæ Haffniæ 14. Nov. 1577.
T.
TYCHO BRAHE.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Tycho Brahe to Master Peder Sørensen [Petrus Severinus].

...... this I wished to write out to you, so that you too may set up an observation of this comet — if not an instrumental one, then at least a visual and physical one — and may bring it about with the King that I not perchance be called away from here in the meantime, while the comet shines, so that I may carry through a full observation of it by means of the instruments which I have ready to hand only here. But if, before my servant shall have delivered this [letter] to you, the very thing I should wish to be guarded against has [already] been done, then take pains that, with the King's consent, it may be undone by another letter.

Farewell, and greet our friends.
Given at Copenhagen, 14 November 1577.
T.
TYCHO BRAHE.

Apparatus

  • Sender / dateline. Tycho Brahe, writing from Copenhagen (Haffnia) on 14 November 1577 (O.S.). The edition label and the dateline agree; the header date is the as-written date. Tycho subscribes first with the initial "T." and then his full name, both reproduced.
  • Recipient. D. Petrum Severini = Peder Sørensen, Latinized Petrus Severinus (1542–1602), the Danish Paracelsian physician and theorist (Idea medicinae philosophicae, Basel 1571), at this date a royal physician (medicus regius) close to Frederik II — which is precisely why Tycho asks him to intercede apud Regem ("with the King"). D. = Dominus, rendered "Master" by the edition's convention
  • Fragment. The source is a fragment: it begins mid-sentence after a row of points ("...... haec ego"), so the lost antecedent of haec ("these things / this") — evidently Tycho's preceding report of his own observations of the comet — is not in the text of record. The leading ellipsis is preserved; no opening salutation survives. No MS witness is recorded in the source; the Nye Samlinger printing is the text of record (later reprinted by Dreyer).
  • Occasion — the comet of 1577. huius Cometae = "this comet" is the Great Comet of 1577 (C/1577 V1), Tycho's observations of which (showing it to lie beyond the Moon, i.e. in the celestial, supralunary region) became the foundation of his 1588 De mundi aetherei recentioribus phaenomenis. The letter is thus contemporaneous with the comet's apparition (it was conspicuous in November 1577).
  • Technical terms. instrumentalem … observationem / per instrumenta → "an instrumental observation" / "by means of the instruments" (instrumenta → instruments,); ocularem et physicam observationem → "a visual and physical observation," i.e. a naked-eye observation of the comet's appearance and physical character (tail, colour, motion) as opposed to a measured instrumental one. physica is rendered "physical" (its period sense: of the body's nature/appearance), not "in physics"; flagged so the contrast with instrumentalem is not flattened.
  • Negations / legal force (checked). Two negations in the source, both reproduced exactly: (1) si non instrumentalem saltem … = "if not an instrumental one, then at least …" (the non … saltem concessive pair); (2) ne forte ego hinc avocer = "that I not perchance be called away from here" — a negative purpose clause (ne) governing Tycho's request, not an assertion that he will be called away. No negation added or dropped.
  • Conditional close. Si vero … id quod caveri vellem, factum est, da operam, ut … infectum fieri possit — a real (indicative) condition: "But if … the very thing I should wish to be guarded against has [already] been done, then take pains that … it may be undone." caveri vellem keeps Tycho's own hedged optative ("the thing I should wish guarded against") rather than naming the feared event, which the fragment leaves implicit (apparently an order summoning him away from Copenhagen/Hven while the comet was visible). infectum fieri = "to be made undone / annulled" (legal-administrative idiom: to rescind by a further letter, per alias literas, with the King's consent). The referent of the feared "thing" is not stated in the surviving text; it is left implicit rather than supplied.
  • Closing formula. Vale et saluta amicos = "Farewell, and greet our friends". Datae Haffniae = "Given at Copenhagen."
