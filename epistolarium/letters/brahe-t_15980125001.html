The original

AN DEN HOFMEISTER VNDT CANTZLER.

VNSERN gunstigen gruss zu uor. Ehrenuehster besonder lieber. Es hatt der ehrenuehste Vnser lieber besonder Tycho Brahe Vns vmb intercession an die königliche w: zue Dennemarcken, Vnsern freundtlichen hertzvielgeliebten hern vattern vndt sohn, wie auch an Vnsere freundtliche vielgeliebte tochter die könnigin, in seinen sachen vndterthenigst angehaltten. Weil Wir ihm denn füglich solches nicht haben abschlagen können, so haben Wir ihm wie beyliegendt an jhr k: w: allerseits geschrieben, vndt weil Wir wissen, daß jhr viel gutes bey den sachen ttuen könnet, so begehren Wir gantz gnedigst, ihr wollet so viel sich jmmer fugen wil deß beste vorwenden helfen, damitt er vonn jhr k:w: mitt guetter resolution möge versehen werden, wie Wir denn inn gnaden nicht zweiffeltt, jhr werdet Vnsern halben den vertrawen nach an euch nichts erwinden laßen. Deßselbe wollen Wir mitt allen gnedigen vndt gönstigen willen, darmitt Wir euch ohne deß gewogen, hienwiederumb verschulden.

Datum Cölln an der Sprew, den 25. januarij anno 98.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the Steward and the Chancellor.

Our gracious greeting beforehand. Honourable [and] particularly dear [one]. The honourable, Our dear and particular Tycho Brahe has most humbly applied to Us for [Our] intercession with the Royal Majesties of Denmark — Our friendly, heartily-beloved lord father and son, and likewise with Our friendly, dearly-beloved daughter the Queen — in his affairs. Since We could not in fairness refuse him this, We have accordingly written, as enclosed, to their Royal Majesties one and all; and since We know that you can do much good in these affairs, We most graciously desire that you, so far as ever may be fitting, help to put forward [his] best [interest], so that he may be furnished by their Royal Majesties with a good resolution — even as We do not, in [Our] grace, doubt that you, for Our sake [and] in keeping with the trust [reposed in you], will let nothing be wanting on your part. The same We will requite again with all the gracious and favourable goodwill wherewith We are in any case well-disposed toward you.

Given at Cölln an der Spree, the 25th of January, in the year [15]98.

Apparatus

  • Issuer. Joachim Friedrich = Joachim Friedrich, Elector of Brandenburg (1546–1608; Elector from 1598, in the year of this letter). The royal "We" (Wir / Vns) and the gracious-superior register (gnedigst, in gnaden) are those of a reigning prince addressing officers of another crown. Title rendered "Elector of Brandenburg" in the header; the text itself names no title, opening directly with the address-line.
  • Addressees. Der Hofmeister vndt Cantzler = the two senior officers of the Danish crown: the Hofmeister (Rigshofmester / Lord High Steward), at this date Christoffer Valkendorf (1525–1601), and the Cantzler (Chancellor), Christian Friis af Borreby (1556–1616). The edition names both as recipients; the letter is a single missive addressed jointly to the two offices (the singular Ehrenuehster besonder lieber, "particularly dear one," is the chancery address-formula and does not by itself decide whether it was issued in one copy or two — flagged, not resolved). The surviving witness is a later copy in Langebek's Diplomatarium.
  • Subject — Tycho. Tycho Brahe … vmb intercession … in seinen sachen vndterthenigst angehaltten — Tycho, by then estranged from the Danish court and on the point of leaving Denmark (he quit Hven in 1597 and was abroad — Rostock, then Wandsbek — in 1597–98), had asked the Elector to intercede with the Danish crown "in his affairs." This is one of the intercession letters Tycho solicited from German princes during his self-imposed exile, seeking a favourable settlement (restoration of favour / his estates / pension) before his eventual move to the Imperial court.
  • The kinship formulae. die königliche w: zue Dennemarcken … Vnsern freundtlichen hertzvielgeliebten hern vattern vndt sohn … Vnsere freundtliche vielgeliebte tochter die könnigin — "the Royal Majesties of Denmark … Our friendly, heartily-beloved lord father and son … Our dearly-beloved daughter the Queen." These are conventional princely courtesy-kinship terms ("father," "son," "daughter") expressing the dynastic web binding the Brandenburg and Danish-Oldenburg houses, not literal immediate-family relations; the plural die königliche w: … allerseits ("their Royal Majesties … one and all") shows the addressees are collective. The translation keeps the courtesy terms as written and flags their conventional force here rather than asserting a specific genealogy. königliche w: = königliche Würden (Royal Majesties / Royal Dignities), the abbreviation expanded in sense.
  • Latin / chancery code-switches. intercession (intercession, mediation), resolution (a formal resolution / decision — mitt guetter resolution möge versehen werden, "be furnished with a good resolution"), and Datum (Given) are Latin code-switches embedded in the German, by this chapter's convention; kept and glossed.
  • Negations (counted, per the legal-force rule). Three negations stand in the source and exactly three in the translation: (1) nicht … abschlagen können — "could not refuse"; (2) nicht zweiffeltt — "do not doubt"; (3) nichts erwinden laßen — "let nothing be wanting." None reverses a command; all are courtesy-register assurances. The clause jhr werdet … an euch nichts erwinden laßen is the standard chancery litotes "you will let nothing be lacking on your part" (i.e. you will do your utmost) — rendered as such, not sharpened.
  • Orthography (chancery German, c. 1598). zu uor = zuvor; Ehrenuehster = Ehrenfester ("Honourable," the formal address-epithet); besonder lieber = besonderer lieber; vmb = um; ttuen = tun (with the scribe's doubled initial); vorwenden = vorwenden ("put forward, employ on [someone's] behalf"); fugen = fügen ("be fitting"); hienwiederumb = hinwiederum ("in return"); verschulden = verschulden (here "requite, deserve [your goodwill] in return," not "incur guilt/debt"); gewogen = "well-disposed." Sprew / Spree; Cölln an der Spree is the Berlin sister-town and seat of the Brandenburg residence (today part of Berlin).
  • Calendar / place. Datum Cölln an der Sprew, den 25. januarij anno 98 = "Given at Cölln an der Spree, 25 January [15]98." Brandenburg, as a Lutheran territory, kept the Julian calendar (Old Style) in 1598 (the Protestant estates of the Empire did not adopt the Gregorian reform until 1700); the date is therefore O.S., consistent with the Danish addressees, who likewise used Old Style. No in-letter date discrepancy. The edition label gives the same date (25 January 1598, Cölln an der Spree); header and label agree.
