The original

DEM VESTEN VNSERM LIEBEN BESONDERN TYCHONI BRAHE, HERRN ZU KNUDSTRUP.
WILHELM VON GOTTES GNADEN LANDTGRAUE ZU HESSEN/ GRAUE ZU CATZENELNBOGEN/ DIETZ/ ZIEGENHAIN VND NIDDA/ ETC.

UNSERN günstigen Gruß zuuor/ Vester Lieber Besonder/ Wir machen vns keinen zweiffel/ jhr werdet vnser Antwortlich schreiben/ so wir den 14 verschienes Monats Aprilis, aus vnserer Vestung allhier an Euch gethan/ numehr beneben den vberschickten Obseruationibus vor lengst empfangen/ vnd vnsere meinung darab vernohmen haben. Dieweil vns aber biß noch vnserm gnedigen begeren nach/ darauff kein antwort zukommen/ Vnd vns dann diese gelegene Bottschafft zu euch vorgestossen/ So haben wir nicht vmbgehen können/ Euch hiermit voriges vnsers schreibens gnediglich zu erinnern/ Vnd ist demnach an Euch vnser gnediges begeren/ weil Vnsere vnd Ewere Obseruationes, so viel den negst erschienen Cometam, auch Stellam nouam ad Cassiopeiam antrifft/ gar nahe zusammen treffen/ Ihr wollet vns nicht allein Ewer Iudicium vber vnsere Obseruationes Stellarum fixarum hinwider zuschicken/ Sondern auch Ewere Obseruationes, was jhr so woll dabeuor/ als auch darsieder von Stellis fixis obseruit, communiciren, darmit wir sie gegen den vnsern zu conferiren, Sonderlich aber wollet vns Altitudinem vestri Poli/ wie jhr die bey Euch habt/ zuschicken/ Vnd werdet jhr aus beygefügten vnsers Mathematici schreiben/ vnsere meinung weiter vernehmen. Wolten wir Euch also gnediglich nicht verhalten/ vnnd seind Euch mit gnedigen guten Willen wollgeneigt/

Datum in vnser Stadt vnd Vestung Cassell am 26 Augusti, Anno 1586.
WILHELM LANDTGRAFF ZU HESSEN.

English translation machine translation, Probable

To our trusty and well-beloved Tycho Brahe, Lord of Knudstrup.
Wilhelm, by the grace of God Landgrave of Hesse, Count of Katzenelnbogen, Dietz, Ziegenhain, and Nidda, etc.

Our gracious greeting beforehand. Trusty and well-beloved: we make no doubt that you have by now long since received our letter in reply, which we sent to you from our fortress here on the 14th of the past month of April, together with the observations [Observationibus] forwarded [with it], and have learned our opinion thereon. But since, in accordance with our gracious desire, no answer to it has as yet come to us, and since this convenient messenger to you now presents itself, we could not forbear graciously to remind you herewith of our previous letter. And it is accordingly our gracious desire of you that — since our and your observations, so far as concerns the lately-appeared comet [Cometam], and also the new star toward Cassiopeia [Stellam novam ad Cassiopeiam], agree very closely together — you would not only send us back your judgment [Iudicium] upon our observations of the fixed stars [Observationes Stellarum fixarum], but also communicate to us your observations [Observationes] — whatever you have observed of the fixed stars [Stellis fixis] both before and since — so that we [may compare them] [conferiren] against our own; but above all, that you would send us the altitude of your pole [Altitudinem vestri Poli] as you have it with you. And you will learn our opinion further from the enclosed letter of our mathematician [Mathematici]. This we would graciously not withhold from you, and are well disposed toward you with gracious good will.

Given in our city and fortress of Kassel, the 26th of August, in the year 1586.
WILHELM, LANDGRAVE OF HESSE.

Apparatus

  • Correspondent. Wilhelm = Wilhelm IV, Landgrave of Hesse-Kassel (1532–1592), "Wilhelm the Wise," a practising astronomer who maintained an observatory at Kassel staffed by the mathematician Christoph Rothmann and the instrument-maker Jost Bürgi. This is an early item in the Hven–Kassel correspondence that Tycho printed himself as the Epistolarum astronomicarum liber primus (Uraniborg, 1596). The princely Wir/Euch register and the full titulature are preserved by the edition's convention
  • Recipient address. DEM VESTEN VNSERM LIEBEN BESONDERN … HERRN ZU KNUDSTRUP — "to our trusty and well-beloved … Lord of Knudstrup." Vester (= fester) is the chancery honorific for a nobleman ("steadfast, trusty"); Lieber Besonder is rendered per the glossary formula "trusty and well-beloved." Knudstrup = the Brahe family seat in Scania.
  • Salutation. Unsern günstigen Gruß zuvor — a variant of the princely address-formula; rendered "Our gracious greeting beforehand" (cf. Unsern gnedigen Gruß zuvor). günstig ("favourable, gracious") is here treated as equivalent to gnedig.
  • The April letter. Wilhelm refers to his own reply of 14 April 1586, sent with a set of observations, to which Tycho had not yet responded. The date is reproduced as written ("the 14th of the past month of April"); not converted (O.S.).
  • Latin code-switch (technical nouns). The German body switches into Latin for the scientific lexicon, a feature kept visible by the edition's convention: Observationibus / Observationes → "observations"; Cometam → "comet" (the comet of 1585); Stellam novam ad Cassiopeiam → "new star toward Cassiopeia" (the supernova of 1572, SN 1572 — stella nova, here rendered literally "new star," not modernised); Iudicium → "judgment" (an opinion/assessment); Observationes Stellarum fixarum / Stellis fixis → "observations of the fixed stars" / "fixed stars"; Altitudinem vestri Poli → "the altitude of your pole" (the elevation of the celestial pole at Hven = its geographical latitude; altitudo → "altitude" by the edition's convention); Mathematici → "mathematician" (Wilhelm's court mathematician, Christoph Rothmann,, whose own accompanying letter is the "enclosed letter"). These Latin forms are italicised and the source word given in brackets on first occurrence by the edition's convention.
  • Verbs of comparison. conferiren (Latinate German infinitive, from conferre) → "compare"; rendered in brackets "[may compare them]" because the German clause darmit wir sie gegen den vnsern zu conferiren lacks an explicit finite verb of obtaining (literally "so that we, to compare them against ours"). The supplied "[may compare]" is bracketed as an editorial completion of the elliptical purpose-clause; cf. Mosley's reading ("so that we may compare them with ours," Bearing the Heavens, p. citing Wilhelm to Tycho, 26 August 1586). communiciren → "communicate."
  • dabevor … darsieder — "before … since" (dabevor = zuvor/davor; darsieder = seither/seitdem), i.e. observations made both prior to and subsequent to some prior reference point.
  • Calendar / place. Datum … am 26 Augusti, Anno 1586 = "Given … the 26th of August, in the year 1586." Hesse-Kassel, Protestant, kept the Julian calendar in 1586 (the Protestant German states did not adopt the Gregorian reform until 1700); the dateline is therefore Old Style. The edition label "Kassel" is the place, not a date; the as-written dateline 26 August 1586 governs the header, with no discrepancy to flag. Datum ("Given") corresponds to the Danish Actum formula.
