The original

AN DIE RÖMISKE KAY: MAYTT:.

ALLERGNEDIGSTER herr vnd vetter. E: Röm: Kay: Maytt: kann jch in vnterthenigen vertrawen nicht verhalten, wie das mir der ansehenliche vnd hochberumbte Tycho Brahe gehorsamblich zuerkennen geben, wie daß ehr auß etlichen hochwichtigen vrsachen sein vaterlandt das königreich Dennemarck zuuerlassen, vnd sich mit allen seinen jnstrumentis mathematicis vnter E: Kay: Maytt: allergnedigste protection in dem Teutschlandt niderzuthun, gentzlichen endschlossen sey. Vnd wie woll sein doctrina vnd berumbter löblicher nahme dermasßen jn vnd ausser Teutschlandt nicht allein, sondern auch E: Kay: Maytt: zuuorderst gnedigst woll bekandt, so hat ehr doch auß dem hohen vertrawen, so ehr propter conformia studia zu mir gesetzt, ahn mich gehorsamblich begert, sein personn vnd löbliche intention E: Kay: Maytt: vnterthenigst zu recommendiren. Weil mir dan vorhin wolbewust, dass E: Kay: Maytt: summus fautor vnnd protector aller gelehrter leut, vnndt insonderheit vnter andern dem mathematico studio geneigt sein, so hab jch desto eher diese stoltzigkeit auf mich genommen, dem ehrlichen vnd berumbten man in seinem begeren zuwilfahrenn. Belanget der halben ahn E: Kay: Maytt: mein vnterthenigste bitt, sie wollen jhnen Tycho Brahe sambt seinen studijs in jhren kayserlichen schutz vnnd promotion auf- vnd annehmen, auch sich auf seine weitere vorschleg vnd gehorsamblich begeren dermassen allergnedigst resoluiren, damit ehr Tycho Brahe sehenn vnnd spuhrenn möge, das seine löbliche, hochberumbtte vnd indefessi labores ein gnedigsten mæcenatem bekommen haben. Solche ertzeigte gnadt will nit allein jch, sondern tota Germanica patria wirdt gleichsfalß gegen E: Kay: Maytt: gehorsambst zuuerdienen alletzeit geflisßen sein. Weil auch seine merita dermassen geschaffen, alßo das ehr billich pater astronomorum et unicus hujus laudatissimæ scientiæ restaurator mag genendt werden, so halt jch weitere recommendation vor vnnöttig, sondern das jch vielmehr E: Kay: Maytt: vnterthenigst deßhalben zu congratuliren schuldig mich erkenne, weil derhalben mit meinen weitern schreiben E: Kay: Maytt: nit auffhalten, sondern thue mich derselben zu kayserlichen hulden vnnd gnaden vnterthenigst beuehlenn.

Luttich d. 26. iunij 98.
E: Kay: Maytt: gehorsamer churfurst vnnd vetter
Ernst, ertzbischoff zu Cöllen.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the Roman Imperial Majesty.

Most gracious lord and cousin. I cannot, in humble confidence, withhold from Your Roman Imperial Majesty how the distinguished and highly renowned Tycho Brahe has obediently made known to me that he, for several highly weighty reasons, is wholly resolved to leave his fatherland the Kingdom of Denmark, and to settle in Germany, with all his mathematical instruments, under Your Imperial Majesty's most gracious protection. And although his learning and renowned, laudable name are well known not only within and beyond Germany, but also, and above all, most graciously to Your Imperial Majesty, he has nonetheless — out of the high trust he has placed in me on account of our kindred studies — obediently desired of me that I commend his person and laudable intention most humbly to Your Imperial Majesty. Since, then, it was well known to me beforehand that Your Imperial Majesty is the supreme patron and protector of all learned men, and especially inclined, among other [studies], to the mathematical study, I have the more readily taken this boldness upon myself, to comply with the honest and renowned man in his request. There pertains, therefore, to Your Imperial Majesty my most humble petition: that you would take up and receive Tycho Brahe, together with his studies, into your imperial protection and advancement, and would resolve so most graciously upon his further proposals and obedient request, that he, Tycho Brahe, may see and feel that his laudable, most renowned and indefatigable labours have obtained a most gracious Maecenas. Such shown grace not I alone, but the whole German fatherland, will likewise be ever zealous to deserve, most obediently, toward Your Imperial Majesty. And since his merits are of such a kind that he may rightly be called the father of astronomers and the sole restorer of this most praiseworthy science, I hold further recommendation needless; rather I acknowledge myself bound, much more, to congratulate Your Imperial Majesty most humbly on that account. Wherefore I will not detain Your Imperial Majesty with further writing of mine, but commend myself most humbly to the same's imperial favour and graces.

Liège, the 26th of June [15]98.
Your Imperial Majesty's obedient Elector and cousin,
Ernst, Archbishop of Cologne.

Apparatus

  • Direction & significance. This is the actual letter of recommendation by which the Elector-Archbishop of Cologne commended Tycho to Emperor Rudolf II — the document Tycho refers to, and circulated copies of, across his patronage-seeking correspondence of 1598 (Quae Coloniensis mea de causa Imperatori scripsit in brahe-t_15980905001; cf. brahe-t_16000420001). It is a key instrument in Tycho's transition from exile to imperial service.
  • Sender. Ernst of Bavaria (1554–1612), Elector-Archbishop of Cologne and Prince-Bishop of Liège (hence writing from Liège / Lüttich), brother of Duke Wilhelm V of Bavaria — a leading Catholic prince and himself a patron of mathematics and astrology. He addresses the Emperor as Herr und Vetter ("lord and cousin"), the conventional form among Imperial princes.
  • Recipient. Emperor Rudolf II (Römische Kaiserliche Majestät).
  • The macaronic register. The letter is chancery German studded with Latin technical and rhetorical phrases, preserved verbatim and rendered in place: instrumenta mathematica ("mathematical instruments"), propter conformia studia ("on account of kindred studies"), summus fautor ("supreme patron"), indefessi labores ("indefatigable labours"), Maecenas (the type of the patron), tota Germanica patria ("the whole German fatherland"), and — the rhetorical climax — pater astronomorum et unicus huius laudatissimae scientiae restaurator, "the father of astronomers and the sole restorer of this most praiseworthy science." The restaurator/restitutio language is Tycho's own programme-term (astronomia instaurata,), here deployed by his advocate.
  • Place. Lüttich = Liège, seat of Ernst's prince-bishopric.
  • Epistolary formulae & abbreviations. E: Röm: Kay: Maytt: = Eure Römische Kaiserliche Majestät ("Your Roman Imperial Majesty," declined throughout); subscription gehorsamer Churfürst und Vetter → "obedient Elector and cousin."
  • Calendar. Dateline d. 26. Iunij 98 at Liège; the edition marks n.s. (New Style) — correct, the Catholic Rhineland and prince-bishoprics having adopted the Gregorian calendar. Reproduced as written; the year is given in the source as "98."
  • Witness note. Survives as an afskrift (copy) in Langebek's Diplomatarium at the Copenhagen Rigsarkiv — fittingly, since Tycho himself kept and distributed copies of it.
