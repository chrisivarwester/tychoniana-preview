The original

ANN DEN KÖNIG ZU DENNEMARCK.

DURCHLEUCHTIGSTER, grosmechtiger könig. E: K: M: seindt Vnsere freundtliche willige dienste, vnd was Wir den grosvaterlichen verwantnus nach jederzeitt mehr liebs vnnd gutts vermagen zuvor. Freundtlicher hertzviellgeliebter sohnn, Vns hatt der ehrnuester Vnser lieber besonder Tycho Brahe vnderthenig zuerkennen gegeben, welcher masen er aus hochwichtigen vrsagen, welche Vns doch allerdinge nicht bekant, mit groser seiner vngelegenheitt vndt beschwerung sich aus E: K: M: königreiche Dennemarcken, seinen lieben vatterlande, begeben, vnd sich eine zeittlangk in Vnser stadt Rostogk niederlasen musen, mitt ferner vndertheniger fleisiger bitte, Wir wolthen Vns daselbige nitt zu wieder, zondern viellmer jhnn zu gnaden Vns befolen sein lasen. Wann Wir nun aber bey Vns leichtlich abnehmen konnen, das durch solche seine verenderung, die von jhme nicht alleinn woll angefangene, sondern auch bereitts so viel jahre hero getriebene vnd befurderte löbliche astronomische sachen, dermasen nicht vollends continuiret werden könen, wie ers jhme woll furgenommen, vnd Wir aber solches vor Vnsere persohn, weill Wir es dafur achten, das auch dardurch E: K: M: königreiche ann dem grosen ruhm vndt nahmen, so es bisshero solcher herlichen kunst halben gehabt, ettwas abgehen muchte nicht gernne schehen; alß haben Wir dem nach E: K: W: hier ann freundtlicher gutter wollmeinung erjneren wollen, mitt gandske freundtlicher bitte, E: K: W: wolte nicht geschehen lasen, das gedachtes werck so plötzlich in ein abnehmen kommen, sondern vielmher dero in Gott ruhenden herren vatters, lobsehliger vndt christmilder gedechtnus, vndt aller reichs ratte beschlus ferner nachsetzen, vndt gedacten Tychonem Brahe alls einem furtreffentlichen vndt ihn der astronomia der masen erfaren vndt wolluerdienten mann, daß seines gleichen nictt leichtlich zu finden, do sonsten E: K: W: keinne erhebliche vrsachen hetten, worumb es nicht geschehen köntte, ann voriger ordt gnedigst restituiren. Welchs dann ohne zweiffell E: K: W: und dero gandzen königereich zu mher ruhm vnd ehrenn, wie es auch zu dem ende von ihm angefangen, vnd biß dahero mit groser muhe vndt arbeitt so weitt getrieben, das es keiner ihm baldt nachthun wirt, gereichen wurde. Wir geschweigen, das ermeltes Tychonis studium in astronomia auch fast in allen landen also bekandt vndt berhumet ist, das es pilligk gutter befurderung woll wirdigk, vndt weill Wir ihme dann auch sonsten mit gnaden vnd aller gutter wollgewogen, so bitten Wir dahero vmb so viellmher gandz freundtlich E: K: W: wolle diese Vnsere commendationn vndt intercession nicht anders als ihm besten vermercken vndt auffnehmen. Vnd Wir seindt E:K:W: jederzeit zu aller angenehmen freundtlichen willfahrung vndt grossvatterlichen dienst ertzeigung geneigt vnd gandz willigk.

Datum Gustruoe den 4. augusti anno 97.
Vonn Gottis gnaden Vlrich hertzog
zu Meckelnburgk, furst zu Wenden,
graffe zu Schwerin,
der lande Rostogk
vnd Stargard herr.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the King of Denmark.

Most Serene, mighty King. Our friendly, willing service to Your Royal Majesty beforehand, and whatever more of love and good we are at all times able [to render], in keeping with our grandfatherly kinship. Friendly, heartily well-beloved son: the honourable man, Our trusty and well-beloved Tycho Brahe, has humbly made known to Us how, for weighty reasons — which, however, are not at all known to Us — he has, with great inconvenience and hardship to himself, departed from Your Royal Majesty's kingdom of Denmark, his dear fatherland, and has had to settle himself for a time in Our city of Rostock; with the further humble, earnest petition that We would not take this amiss of him, but rather hold him commended to Us in [Our] grace. Now since We can readily judge for Ourselves that, by such a removal of his, the praiseworthy astronomical works — which he has not only well begun, but has now for so many years pursued and advanced — cannot be brought to full completion in the way he had well purposed; and since, for Our own part, We should not gladly see this happen, because We hold it that thereby Your Royal Majesty's kingdom might lose something of the great renown and name which it has hitherto had on account of that splendid art: therefore We have wished herein to remind Your Royal Worship in friendly, good intent, with quite friendly petition, that Your Royal Worship would not let it come to pass that the said work should so suddenly fall into decline, but rather should further give effect to the resolve of your lord father — of laudable and Christian-gracious memory, now resting in God — and of the whole Council of the Realm, and most graciously restore the said Tycho Brahe — as an excellent man, so experienced and so well-deserving in astronomy that his like is not easily to be found — to his former place, provided Your Royal Worship have not otherwise weighty reasons why it might not be done. This would then, without doubt, redound to the greater renown and honour of Your Royal Worship and of your whole kingdom — even as it was begun by him to that end, and has hitherto, with great toil and labour, been carried so far that none will soon match him in it. We say nothing of the fact that the said Tycho's pursuit of astronomy is so well known and renowned in well-nigh all lands that it is rightly well worthy of good furtherance; and since We are besides graciously and in all good will disposed towards him, We therefore beg all the more, quite in friendship, that Your Royal Worship would mark and receive this Our commendation and intercession in no other way than for the best. And We are at all times inclined and quite willing to show Your Royal Worship every agreeable, friendly compliance and grandfatherly rendering of service.

Given at Güstrow, the 4th of August in the year [15]97.
By the grace of God, Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of [the] Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg[-Güstrow] (1527–1603), reigning from Güstrow. He was the maternal grandfather of the recipient: his daughter Sophie of Mecklenburg was the queen consort of Frederik II and the mother of Christian IV. Hence the repeated grosvaterlich / grossvatterlich ("grandfatherly") register and the address of the King as sohnn ("son," i.e. grandson) throughout — the literal kinship governs the letter's tone.
  • Recipient. Christian IV of Denmark (r. 1588–1648; assumed personal rule 1596). The herren vatter … in Gott ruhenden whose resolve Ulrich invokes is his late father Frederik II (d. 1588), who had granted Tycho the island of Hven in 1576.
  • Subject. Tycho Brahe (1546–1601), who had left Denmark in spring 1597 and settled for a time at Rostock (the Mecklenburg city, named in the text as Vnser stadt Rostogk). This is the intercessory cover-letter Ulrich enclosed when forwarding to his grandson a petition from Tycho, requesting that the King restore Tycho to his "former place" (Hven and his Danish appointments). It matches the companion piece in the edition, Tycho → Duke Ulrich, Rostock, 29 July 1597 (brahe-t_15970729001), which Ulrich is here answering and relaying.
  • Document type. A surviving copy (Afskrift) in Langebek's Diplomatarium; the heading ANN DEN KÖNIG ZU DENNEMARCK ("To the King of Denmark") is the chancery docket, not part of the salutation.
  • Honorific abbreviations. E: K: M: = Eurer Königlichen Majestät ("Your Royal Majesty"); E: K: W: = Eurer Königlichen Würde(n) ("Your Royal Worship/Dignity"). The letter switches register from the higher Majestät in the opening address to the slightly more familiar Würde in the body; the distinction is preserved in the translation ("Your Royal Majesty" vs. "Your Royal Worship") rather than levelled.
  • Chancery formulae. Vnser lieber besonder (= unser lieber besonder[er]) = "Our trusty and well-beloved" (the princely address-formula for a favoured subject/client). commendationn vndt intercession — the two Latin technical nouns commendatio and intercessio are code-switched into the German, the standard diplomatic terms for a letter of recommendation/formal plea on another's behalf; kept and rendered "commendation and intercession."
  • Orthography (Early New High German chancery, c. 1597). seindt = sind; vnd/vndt/vnnd = und; Wir/Vns/Vnser (majuscule) = the princely first-person; jhnn/jhme/ihm = ihn/ihm; zondern = sondern; gandske / gandz / gandzen = ganz(e) ("quite, whole"); muchte / köntte = möchte / könnte (subjunctive); vrsagen = Ursachen ("reasons"); schehen / geschehen = "happen / come to pass"; abnehmen (noun) = "decline, falling-off"; restituiren = restore (Latinate); furtreffentlich = vortrefflich ("excellent"); wolluerdient = wohlverdient ("well-deserving"); berhumet = berühmt ("renowned"); pilligk / wirdigk / willigk = billig / würdig / willig.
  • Negation check. The two double-negative courtesy constructions are rendered with their full logic preserved, not simplified: (1) Wir wolthen Vns daselbige nitt zu wieder … lasen = "We would not take this amiss" (Tycho's plea that the Duke not resent his settling at Rostock); (2) the petition that the King wolte nicht geschehen lasen, das gedachtes werck so plötzlich in ein abnehmen kommen = "would not let it come to pass that the said work should so suddenly fall into decline." The conditional escape-clause do sonsten E: K: W: keinne erhebliche vrsachen hetten, worumb es nicht geschehen köntte is a deliberately deferential double negative — "provided Your Royal Worship have not otherwise weighty reasons why it might not be done" — leaving the King's discretion intact; its mood and polarity are reproduced exactly.
  • Calendar / dateline. Datum Gustruoe … den 4. augusti anno 97 = "Given at Güstrow [the Mecklenburg ducal seat], the 4th of August in the year [15]97" (O.S.; Denmark and Mecklenburg both still on the Julian calendar). The edition label date (1597. 4. august. Güstrow) agrees with the dateline; no header/label discrepancy. The intrusive "35" between "Gustruoe" and "den 4. augusti" is unexplained in the source text — almost certainly a register/folio number or copyist's marginal numeral absorbed into the line of the Langebek Afskrift, not a calendar element. It is reproduced verbatim in the Source block and bracketed "[35,]" in the translation rather than silently dropped or "corrected" into the date; this is the one flagged crux.
  • Titulature. The subscription styles Ulrich furst zu Wenden, graffe zu Schwerin, der lande Rostogk vnd Stargard herr — "Prince of [the] Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard," the standard secondary titles of the Mecklenburg dukes; rendered in full per the edition's princely-titulature rule.
  • Correction. Removed a stray "35" from the closing dateline: Dreyer's printed text reads simply "Datum Guftrue den 4. augufti anno 97" (TBOO XIV p.114); the digit was Dreyer's own marginal line-count, wrongly absorbed into the transcription and then into the translation as a bracketed "[35,]".
