The original

[TYCHO BRAHE AD ESCHILLUM BILDE].

MINN gandske wenlig hilsen nu och altidt forsendt med Vor Herre. Kierre Eske Bilde, broder och sønderlige gode wen, nest min wenlig och store tacksigelße for allt bewist gode, huilcket att forskylde ieg stedze will findis aff formuen willig yhues maade ieg kan giørre nogit, som du kanst haffue ære och gott aff. Giffuendis dig derhos ganske wenligen tillkiende, att epthersom ieg tillforen haffuer skreffuit dig till, att du willde fly mig det bref jndløst, som ieg haffde stoendis vdi Lantheholstein, och ieg forseer mig till, att du det till forgangen ombslag giortt haffuer, och att pengene erre vdi tiide bleffne opsagt vdi Lanthemeckelburg, epthersom ieg ocsaa tillforn haffuer skreffuit dig till och sent dig skrifftlig beskeen om, saa haffuer ieg nu skreffuit die vnge herre[r] aff Meckellburg deris rentmester till, vedt nauffn Andreas Meyer, att handt willde lade mig bekomme hoffuidtstollen vdigen, som er 10000 thaller, till den første dag aprilis eller 14 dage derepther y det seniste vdj dette tillkommendis aar. Er derfor min gandske wenlig bøn till dig, at du wilde well giørre och forschaffe det saa, att handt kunde bekomme det breff vdigien, som mig er giffuit paa samme penge, inden den termin kommer, att pengene kunde bliffue leuerit och oppeborrit vdj Lubeck eller Hamburgk hoß en wiß man, och siden hidt forskreffuen, epthersom ieg, nest Gudz hielp, inden den tidt kommer, will skriffue dig wijdre beskening till hervdom, och saa ieg megit gierne, att du medlertiidt met første muellighedt wilde skriffue mig till, huis du hervdj haffuer vdrettidt. Dersom det icke er skedt (huilcket ieg dog icke will forhoffue), daa beder ieg dig ganske gierne, att du willde flii det saa, att det wist skier met første muelighedt, och wille det bliffue well langt, om mandt skulle tøffue dermedt till omslag kommer vdigien. Giør flux derfor dit beste, som ieg tig fulkommendis tilltroer, att alting kan gange for sig met første muelighedt.

Kierre Eske, kan ieg dig ocsaa wenligen icke forholde, att siden mit gotz met minne jnstrumenter ere alltsammen kommen hidt op formedelst Elben, saa wijt den er skiffrig, och epther rømisch. keys. mayt. naadigst befalling och forordnung bleffet ført hidt till Prag, hor ieg nu er, daa sauner ieg allehonde dervdjblant, som icke er metkommenn. Och kan icke wijde, om det er bleffuen tillbage vdj min gaar y Kiøbenhauffn eller och till Lybech, thi till Hamburg eller Magdeburg tror ieg icke lettelligen att nogit endnu skall findis deraff. Beder ieg dig derfor ganske gierne, att du wilde well giørre och lade grandgiffuellig forfare, hor det seder sammen dermedt, och hor det kan findis. Jeg sender dig herhoß en sedell, paa huilcken ieg haffuer ladit antegnit saa megit, som ieg kan saa snart komme yhu att werre bortte deraff, endog det kan well endaa werre mere endt ieg saa snart kan sauffne. Jeg forseer mig till dig, att du giørrer din beste flidt hervdj, och haffuer ieg ocsaa skreffuit min factor vdj Lubeck till dervdom. Kierre Eske och kierre broder, fortenck mig icke, att ieg saa bemøder dig met mine sager, kan ieg giørre nogit vdjgien, som dig kan werre tient met, daa skallt du alltidt finde mig ganske willig och redebon dertill, och achter ieg, nest Gudz hielp, ydremere at skriffue dig och mine andre wenner till, naar Peder Bradis karll kommer hidt igien, som er dragen indt for hannom y lanthe Vngern. Will derfor nu och alltidt haffue dig met allt huis du well wilt Gudt allmechtigste befallidt.

Datum Prag den 16 dag nouembris anno 1600.

[TYCHO BRAHE TO ESKE BILLE].

My most friendly greeting, now and always, conveyed with [the blessing of] Our Lord. Dear Eske Bille, brother and especial good friend — next [after] my friendly and great thanks for all the kindness shown, which to repay I shall always be found willing according to my means, in whatever way I can do anything from which you may have honour and good. Giving you herewith quite amicably to know that, since I have previously written to you that you would redeem for me the bond I had standing in the land of Holstein, and I trust that you have done it by the past Umschlag, and that the money has been duly given notice on in the land of Mecklenburg (as I have also previously written and sent you written information about): I have now written to the rent-master of the young lords of Mecklenburg, by name Andreas Meier, that he would let me recover the principal — which is 10,000 thalers — by the first day of April, or 14 days thereafter at the latest, in this coming year. It is therefore my quite friendly request to you that you would be so good as to arrange it that he may recover the bond given to me on this money before the term comes, [and] that the money may be delivered and received at Lübeck or Hamburg with a sure man, and afterward transferred up hither — since I, with God's help, before that time comes, will write you further instruction hereon; and I should be very glad if you, meanwhile, at the first opportunity, would write to me what you have accomplished herein. If it has not been done (which, however, I will not expect), then I ask you quite gladly to arrange that it surely happens at the first opportunity; and it would become quite long [delayed] if one were to wait until the Umschlag comes round again. Do therefore quickly your best — as I fully trust you — that everything may proceed at the first opportunity.

Dear Eske, I cannot amicably withhold from you that, since my goods, with my instruments, have all come up here by the Elbe — as far as it is navigable — and by the Roman Imperial Majesty's gracious command and ordinance been brought hither to Prague, where I now am, I miss all sorts of things among them, which have not come along. And I cannot know whether it has been left behind in my house in Copenhagen, or at Lübeck; for at Hamburg or Magdeburg I do not easily believe anything of it should still be found. I ask you therefore quite gladly that you would be so good as to have it carefully investigated how matters stand with it, and where it may be found. I send you herewith a note, on which I have had set down so much as I can so quickly recall to be missing of it — although there may well be even more than I can so quickly miss. I trust you to do your best diligence herein; and I have also written to my factor at Lübeck about it. Dear Eske, and dear brother, do not think ill of me that I thus trouble you with my affairs; [if] I can do anything in return that may serve you, then you shall always find me quite willing and ready thereto. And I intend, with God's help, to write further to you and my other friends when Peder Brahe's man comes hither again, who has gone in on his behalf into the land of Hungary. I will therefore, now and always, commend you, with all that you wish well, to God Almighty.

Given at Prague, the 16th day of November, the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Eske Bille, Tycho's Danish kinsman (frænde/"brother") and financial agent (the Eske Bille of the German debt-letters; cf. brahe-t_16010527001, brahe-t_16010604001). The MS is an autograph draft (Kladde).
  • Significance — the Danish hub of the 16 Nov 1600 debt cluster. This letter is the Danish companion to the same-day German letter to rent-master Andreas Meier (brahe-t_16001115002) and the Latin one to Jacob Bording (brahe-t_16001115001): the three coordinate (a) redeeming the Holstein bond at the Kiel Umschlag and (b) recovering the 10,000-thaler Mecklenburg principal by 1 April 1601 at Lübeck or Hamburg, then transferring it to Bohemia. Eske Bille, "plenipotentiary in those parts," is the linchpin (cf. brahe-t_16010410001, where Tycho calls him "my cousin and plenipotentiary").
  • The missing instruments/goods (notable material-history content). A vivid second matter: now that Tycho's goods and instruments have all come up the Elbe to Prague, he finds he is missing "all sorts of things … which did not come along." He cannot tell whether they were left in his house at Copenhagen or at Lübeck (doubting Hamburg or Magdeburg still hold any), and encloses a list of what he can recall missing ("though there may well be more than I can so quickly miss"), asking Eske and his Lübeck factor (Simon von Petkum; cf. brahe-t_16000422001) to trace them. Direct evidence of losses/gaps in the transplantation of the Uraniborg apparatus to Bohemia.
  • Persons named. Eske Bille; Andreas Meier (Mecklenburg rent-master); Tycho's Lübeck factor; Peder Brahe (Tycho's kinsman; cf. brahe-t_16000323001), whose man "has gone into Hungary" on his behalf; Rudolf II.
  • Terms. Umschlag = the Kiel payment-fair; hovedstol = principal; gård i København = Tycho's Copenhagen house/estate.
  • Calendar. Dateline Prag den 16 dag Novembris anno 1600; New Style (Prague). Header agrees with the label.
