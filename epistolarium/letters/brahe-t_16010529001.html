The original

JCH Iohannes Muller, churfurstlicher Brandenburgischer mathematicus, bekenne mitt dieser meiner eigenen handt, das vff der römischen keyß: m:statt, vnsers allergnedigsten herrn, allergnedigst begehren vnndt schreiben an den churfursten zu Brandenburgk, meinen gnedigsten herrn, jch vff ein jahrlang hieher nach Prag abgefoedert worden, das ich jhr maiestet zu allergnedigsten gefallen bei den edlen vnndt wohlgebornen herrn Tycho Brahen vff Knudtstrup vnndt Vraniburgk mich aufhaltten vnndt allda, was er in nahmen j: m: mier befehlen werde, gehorsamlich vorrichten soltte, welchem ich denn in allervnterthenigsten gehorsam nachkommen. Als aber nun die zeitt verfloßen, vnd jch vber dieselb (weil ich nicht lenger verlaub gehabt) mich nicht hab seumen dorffen, so habe an die hochermeltte römische k: maiestett ich vnterthenigst suppliciret, das dieselben mich mitt einer vorehrung fur meine aufgewandte vnkosten vnndt gehabte mühe allergnedigst bedencken woltten, vnndt wiewohl i: m: am ersten hierin jhe kein bedencken gemacht, sondern dem Francken befohlen, meine supplication dem Heiden zu lieffern, das er j: m: daran erinnern soltte, so woltte j: m: allergnedigst geruhen mier eine vorehrung zustellen zulaßen, so ist doch solchs baldt hernacher, vieleicht durch anderer angeben, mir wieder abgeschlagen worden. Auf das ich aber nicht ledig zu hauß ziehen möchte, vnndt es bei meiner gnedigsten herrschafft vnndt andern nicht das ansehen hette, als wan ich vmbsonst ausgewehsen wehr, so hatt der herr Tycho neben der kost vnndt herbergk, weil er mich vnndt mein weib die gantze zeitt vber vnterhaltten, mier 200 f: am gelde zugestellet, die ich dan auch zu meinen handen wohl empfangen, vnndt thue auch krafft dieses brieffs ermeltten herrn Tychonem vollstendig quittiren. Deßen zu mehrer vhrkundt habe ich diesen brieff mitt eigner handt geschrieben vnndt mitt meinem gewöhnlichen petzschafft besiegeltt.

Geschehen zu Prag den 29. maij a:o 1601.
Johannes Muller
manu pp:.

English translation machine translation, Secure

I, Johannes Müller, Electoral Brandenburg mathematician, acknowledge with this my own hand, that — upon the Roman Imperial Majesty's, our most gracious lord's, most gracious desire and letter to the Elector of Brandenburg, my most gracious lord — I was summoned hither to Prague for [the space of] a year, [so] that I should, to His Majesty's most gracious pleasure, stay with the noble and well-born lord Tycho Brahe of Knudstrup and Uraniborg, and there obediently perform what he should command me in His Majesty's name; which I then complied with, in most submissive obedience. But now, the time having elapsed, and [I] not being permitted to delay beyond it (since I had no longer leave), I most humbly petitioned the highly-mentioned Roman Imperial Majesty that the same would most graciously bethink me of an honorarium for my expended costs and the trouble I had had; and although His Majesty at first made no objection at all herein, but commanded "the Franck" to deliver my supplication to "the Heyden," that he should remind His Majesty thereof, [and] that His Majesty would most graciously deign to have an honorarium delivered to me — yet such was, soon afterward (perhaps by others' instigation), again refused me. But so that I might not travel home empty[-handed], and that it should not have the appearance, with my most gracious lordship [the Elector] and with others, as though I had been [away] for nothing, the lord Tycho — besides board and lodging, since he maintained me and my wife the whole time over — delivered to me 200 florins in money, which I have then also well received into my hands; and I do also, by force of this letter, fully quit the said lord Tycho. In further witness whereof I have written this letter with my own hand, and sealed [it] with my usual seal.

Done at Prague, the 29th of May, in the year 1601.
Johannes Müller,
with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender — the "Brandenburg mathematician Müller." This resolves the thread of brahe-t_16000322003: Johannes Müller, Electoral Brandenburg mathematician, summoned (at the Emperor's request to the Elector of Brandenburg) to Prague for a year to serve Tycho "in the Emperor's name." His quittance (receipt-and-release) on departure, 29 May 1601.
  • Content. Müller's year up, he petitioned Rudolf II for an honorarium (Verehrung) for his costs and trouble; though the Emperor initially ordered it (commanding "the Franck" to pass the supplication to "the Heyden" to remind him), the grant was "soon afterward, perhaps by others' instigation, again refused." So that he should not go home empty-handed and seem to have served "for nothing," Tycho himself paid him 200 florins (besides a year's board and lodging for Müller and his wife). Müller fully quits Tycho. A candid window onto the Rudolfine court's failure to fund even its own commissions — leaving Tycho to pay out of pocket.
  • "dem Francken … dem Heiden" — flagged: two court officials named only by surname, Franck and Hayd/Heyden, through whom the supplication was to pass; not securely re-identified. Rendered "the Franck" / "the Heyden."
  • 200 f. = 200 florins (Gulden). manu pp. = manu propria.
  • Calendar. Prag den 29. maij a:o 1601 = 29 May 1601; Prague (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
