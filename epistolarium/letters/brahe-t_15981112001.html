The original

IOH. GEORG GŒDELMANN AD TYCHONEM BRAHE.

MAGNIFICE Domine TYCHO. Gunstiger Herr, vnndt vornehmer freundt, Ich habe alhier zu Prag mit freudenn vernommen, daß Ihr, Gottlob, frisch vnndt gesundt zu Dreßdenn ankommen, vnndt weil Ihr daneben zu wissen begeret, ob Ihr euch zu der Kay. Maytt. begebenn, oder euch in der nehe, wegen der eingefallenen sterbens leufft, derowegen die Kay. Maytt. von Prag hinweg getzogenn, aufhaltenn sollet, so kann ich euch darauf nicht bergenn, daß obwoll die Kay. Maytt. ein großes verlangenn (alß die in Astronomischen Sachen, gleich wie ihr Großvater CAROLUS V hoch auch erfahrenn) nach dem Herrn, alß nach dem vornemsten Mathematico totius Mundi, dessen Nomen auch vnsterblichen sein wirdt, hatt, auch den Herrn nit weit vonn sich habenn will, dieweil aber ihr Maytt. wegen der vergifften Seuchenn, welche fast alle örter im Königreich Böheim inficiret, bey sich noch nicht entschlossen, wo sie Ihr Hofflager anstellenn wollenn, alß wirdt der Herr biß auff Ihr Maytt. endtliche weitere anordnung, zu Dreßden, oder Wittemberg, mitlerweil sich auffhaltenn. Thue hiemit vnß Göttlicher Almacht beuehlenn.

Geben Prag, 12. Novembris. Anno 98.
Deß Herrn dienstvilliger
JOHAN GEORG GÖDELMAN. D.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Johann Georg Gödelmann to Tycho Brahe.

Magnificent Master Tycho, favourable Sir and distinguished friend. I have learned here at Prague, with joy, that you have, praise God, arrived fresh and well at Dresden. And since you besides desire to know whether you should betake yourself to His Imperial Majesty, or hold yourself in the vicinity — on account of the outbreak of mortality [plague], for the sake of which His Imperial Majesty has withdrawn from Prague — I cannot, thereupon, conceal from you that, although His Imperial Majesty has a great longing (being, just like his grandfather Charles V, also highly versed in astronomical matters) for you, as for the foremost Mathematician of the whole world, whose name too will be immortal, and does not wish to have you far from himself: nevertheless, since His Majesty — on account of the venomous pestilence which has infected almost all places in the Kingdom of Bohemia — has not yet resolved with himself where he will set up his court-residence [Hoflager], the Sir [you] should, until His Majesty's final further arrangement, meanwhile stay at Dresden or Wittenberg. I herewith commend us to God's Almighty [protection].

Given at Prague, the 12th of November, in the year [15]98.
Your service-ready,
Johann Georg Gödelmann, D[octor].

Apparatus

  • Sender. Johann Georg Gödelmann (1559–1611), jurist, Saxon privy councillor and diplomat (and author of a celebrated treatise on witchcraft); here writing from Prague as a court-contact, advising Tycho on the timing of his approach to the Emperor.
  • Occasion. Tycho, having reached Dresden on his way south in the autumn of 1598, asked whether to proceed to the Emperor or wait. Gödelmann reports that plague (sterbens leufft / vergiffte Seuche) had driven Rudolf II from Prague and left the court without a fixed seat; he advises Tycho to stay at Dresden or Wittenberg until the Emperor settles his Hoflager (court-residence). (Tycho did winter at Wittenberg — cf. brahe-t_15990110001, …0111001, dated there Jan. 1599 — before reaching Bohemia in 1599.)
  • ihr Großvater Carolus V — "his grandfather Charles V": genealogically correct, not a slip — Rudolf II's mother was Maria of Austria, daughter of Emperor Charles V, so Charles was Rudolf's maternal grandfather. Gödelmann flatters the Emperor's astronomical bent as inherited.
  • Code-switching (kept and italicised). Latin technical/honorific nouns embedded in the German: Mathematico totius Mundi ("Mathematician of the whole world"), Nomen ("name"), inficiret ("infected"). Rendered into English but flagged as the writer's own Latin within the German. Princely titulature (Kay. Maytt., Ihr Hofflager) preserved.
  • Calendar. Geben Prag, 12. Novembris. Anno 98 = 12 November 1598; Prague (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
  • Correction. Two single-word transcription corrections against Dreyer, TBOO VIII p.118: 'uber'→'aber' and 'sic'→'sie'. Neither affected the published English, which already carried the correct sense.
