The original

DEM WOLGEBORNEN HERRN, HERRN EHRENFRIDT VON MINCKWITZ, FREYHERRN ZU MINKWITZBURG AUF HOLESSHAU VND DREHNA, ROM. KAY. MA:T. APPELLATION-RATH, MEINEM IMBESONDERN GUNSTIGEN HERRNN VNND GUETTEN FREUNDT.

WOLGEBORNER, jnbesonder gunstiger herr vnd freundt. Nach wunschung freindtlichenn gruesses, auch erböttung meiner gantz willigenn vnnd geflissenen dienstenn gebe jch dem herrn hiemit freindtlichenn zuuerstehenn, wie das der wolgeborene herr Carolus freyherr von Wartenberg gestrigen tages mich alhie auf dem kaiserlichenn schloß Bennatig besuchet, vnnd vnter anderem vermeldet, das der herr auf die pfingstfeyertage bey jhme zu sein gentzlichen bey sich beschlossenn vnd zugesagt habe. Gelanget demnach an den herrnn mein freindtliches biettenn, derselbige wolle mich gunstchtiglichenn wiessenn lassenn, welchen tag der herr jhm es proponiret habe, damit jch mich vnd die meinigen auf die reise præpariren möge. Vnd nachdem der her auch hatt mir gunstchtiglichenn gehlobet, das er diesen weg ziehen will, vnd mich alhie zu besuchen ..., damit wir mit einander hinziehen, dan ich jhm auch haben zusagen mussen, bey jhme zu sein. Versehe mich gennzlich, dass der herr dis nit abschlagen werde. Jn den sachen, davon ich mit dem herrn vertreulich geredet habe, bitte jch den herrn ganz freindtlichenn, derselbige wolle di mitlerzeit wol erwegenn, vnd wan wir, ob Gott wil, zusammen kommen, mir mit einem gutten rat darin erscheinen, dan ich gentzlich muß darzu thun was sich geburet, ut meum et meorum honorem, pro ut decet, legitime tuear. Welchs jch nach vermögenn vmb den herrn vnnd all den seinigenn zuuerschuldenn jederzeitt gantz willig wiel erfundenn werdenn. Vnd thue hiemit den herrnn jch jn schutz des Almechtigenn treulichenn befehlen.

Gebenn auf dem kayserlichenn schloß Bennatig am 7 tag maij a:o 1600.
Des herrn altzeit dinst- und freindtwilliger
Tycho Brahe
Eign h.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the well-born Lord, Lord Ehrenfried von Minckwitz, Freiherr zu Minckwitzburg, [lord] of Holešov and Drehna, Roman Imperial Majesty's Appellation-Councillor, my especially favourable lord and good friend.

Well-born, especially favourable lord and friend. After the wishing of a friendly greeting, and the offering of my quite willing and assiduous services, I give the lord herewith friendlily to understand how the well-born lord Carl, Freiherr von Wartenberg, visited me yesterday here at the Imperial castle Benátky, and, among other things, reported that the lord [you] have fully resolved with himself, and promised, to be with him on the Pentecost holidays. There accordingly reaches the lord my friendly request: that the same would favourably let me know which day the lord [Wartenberg] has proposed it to him [you], so that I may prepare myself and mine for the journey. And since the lord [Wartenberg] has also favourably promised me that he will travel this way and visit me here … so that we may travel on together (for I, too, have had to promise him to be with him), I fully trust that the lord [you] will not decline this. In the matters of which I have spoken confidentially with the lord, I ask the lord quite friendlily that the same would, meanwhile, well consider them, and, when we — God willing — come together, present me with good counsel therein; for I must altogether do what is fitting, so as lawfully to protect my own and my family's honour, as is proper [ut meum et meorum honorem, pro ut decet, legitime tuear]. Which I will, at all times, be found quite willing to requite, according to my ability, toward the lord and all his. And I herewith faithfully commend the lord into the protection of the Almighty.

Given at the Imperial castle Benátky, the 7th day of May, in the year 1600.
The lord's ever service- and friendship-willing,
Tycho Brahe,
[his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Ehrenfried, Freiherr von Minckwitz, of Minckwitzburg, [lord] of Holešov (Holesshau) and Drehna, an Imperial Appellation-Councillor (Appellationsrat of the Bohemian appellate court) — an influential Prague-court figure whose counsel Tycho seeks.
  • Content. A planning letter: Carl, Freiherr von Wartenberg, had visited Tycho at Benátky and reported that Minckwitz intended to be with Wartenberg over Pentecost; Tycho, who is also to travel with Wartenberg, asks Minckwitz to fix the day so he can prepare his household for the journey.
  • The confidential honour-matter (Latin tag). Tycho refers to "the matters of which I have spoken confidentially" with Minckwitz, and asks for his counsel, "for I must altogether do what is fitting, ut meum et meorum honorem, pro ut decet, legitime tuear" — "so as lawfully to protect my own and my family's honour, as is proper." The Latin code-switch (kept and italicised by the edition's convention) marks the gravity of the matter — almost certainly the defence of Tycho's honour/priority in the Ursus affair or a related legal-reputational concern then preoccupying him (cf. the Ursus suppression, brahe-t_16000727001). The exact matter is not named in the source; flagged, not over-specified.
  • Eign h. = eigene Hand ("own hand"). The lacuna (…) in "visit me here …" is a gap in the draft (Kladde), left as printed.
  • Calendar. kayserlichenn schloß Bennatig am 7 tag maij a:o 1600 = 7 May 1600; Bohemia (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label. (Written the same day as the Latin Barvitius letter brahe-t_16000507001.)
