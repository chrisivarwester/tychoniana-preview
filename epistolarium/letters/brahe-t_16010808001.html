The original

DEM EDLEN VNNDT ERNUESTEN IOACHIM STRALENDORPFF, MECHLENBURGISCHE FURSTLICHE GNADEN VLRICI CAMMERJONKER, MEINEM FREUNDTLICHEN LIEBEN BRUDER ZU HANDEN, F[REUNDTLICH] G[ESCHRIEBEN].

MEINEN freundtlichen vndt gantz willigen diennst neben wunschungh alles guedtes beuorn, freundtlicher lieber Joachim Stralendorpf, bruder vnd insonders guedter freundt. Freundtlicher meinungh kan ich dihr nicht vorhaldten, daß mihr mein blutuorwandter vnd guedter freundt Tycho Brahen sein schreiben vor 9 oder 10 thage ist behandigtt worden, wor auß ich woll vornhommen, daß er nicht zwifelt (schreibt er) mihr die zehen thausendt thaler (dar ich dihr lestmals in mein schreiben vmme vormeldet habe) ja auff vorlauffenen Johanni seindt erlegtt worden. Vnndt weinig thagen zuuorn ist mihr Andreas Meyer sein schreiben auch behandigt worden, wohrinne ehr mich erinnertt, das einer meinendtwegen soldt zu Dobbran in meines g: f: vnd hernn, hertzog Vlrich, hertzog zu Mechelborig etc., f: f: g: vorgemach vmme zwe thausend thaler angehaldten haben vnd keine beweiß gehabtt, wohrauff ich ihme widderumme zugeschrieben habe, daß ich von keine 2000 thaler wuste, whe ich auch nicht weiß, sondern ehr wiste sich wol zuerinneren von wegen die 10000 thaler, so ich hochgedachte mein g: f: vnd hern auff vorgängen Michelj habe aufkundigen lasßen, worauff ich auch Meyer sein skrifftlich andtwordtt habe, daß solche summa midt sampt die zinse auff vorgangen ostern (whe billich) soldte gewis erlegt werden, vndt alß daß nicht geschehen ist, hadt ehr mich dan widderumme zugeschrieben, daß daß geldt gewisße auff Johannj soldte erlegt werden. Vndt weiln ich nuhn auf de rechte bestimbte zeidtt, alß nemlich auff ostern, habe mein diener hin ghenn Rostock geschicket midt deß furstlichen obligation vorgeschrieben summa zu empfhangen vnndtt nu widderumme auff vorgangen Johanni deßglichen midt hochgedachtes m: g: h: ihre f: g: obligation, vndt had mein diene[r] hir zu Rostock in Joachim Loherman sein hausß gelegen itzliche thage vor, auff vndt nach Johannj vnd sich in doctor Lucas Bachmester sein hauß angeben, weiln daß geldt dahr zuerlegen auffkekundigtt wahr, seindt doch keiner nicht da gewesen, der solche summa außzelen woldte. Vnnd gleichwol schreibt der rentemester inn bemeldte sein leste schreiben, daß die gantze summa vor Johannj ist beisamen gewesen vndt begierdt (weiln dahr keiner nicht meinentwegen auff Johannj ist gewesen, derß entfhangen woldte, schreibt er), daß ich doch wil her auß schicken vndt die 2000 thaler entfhangen lasßen, damidt Tyche daß vbrige [kon]te hinaußgeschicket werden, auff welchen sein skreiben ich mich nicht gnugsam vorwundren mach. Vnd habe ihme drauff schrifftlich geandtwortett, daß weiln ich nun zwey vnterschiedtliche mall, alß nemlich auff ostern vnnd Johannj, mein gewisße potschafft mitt hochgedachtes meines gnedigen fursten vnnd hern, j: f: g: obligation hir zu Rostock gehabtt, so wil ich nun zum vberfluß selbst zum dritten mal midt dem ersten kommen.

So bin ich nuhn, lieber bruder, hir zu Rostock heudt dato angekhommen, vnd auff den abent ist einer (deß namen oder pershon mihr vnbekandtt) bey mihr gewesen vndt auf Meyer sein wegen angegeben, das er habe 5000 thaler vormacht auff Prag, vndt dabey berichtet, daß ich mihr erkleren woldte, ob ich daß vbrige hir entfhangen woldte oder ghen Wißmer darnach zihen, worauff ich ihnen vor andtwordt gegeben habe, daß ich nu hergekommen ware die gantze summa midt sampt allen nachstendigen zinsen, auffgelauffenen skaeden vndt vnkost [zu empfangen], oder woferner solches nicht geschege, worde ich hochvorursachet andre mittel zu gebrauchen, welches Gott weiß ich vor mein pershon so wol alß mein bruede[r] Tycho vngerne thadten. Bitte dich derhalben freundtlich, lieber bruder, du woldtest doch meinem genedigen fursten vnndt hern anmelden, daß ich hir wardte, vndt daß sein f: g: sich gnedigst woldte erkleren, ob ich die summa mochte nuhn bekommen, weiln seiner f: g: schreiben sub dato Dobbran 17. junij anlanget, das ich ein kleine weile erwardten woldte, vorhoffe s: f: g: wirdt mihr ein gnedigen andtwordtt hir auf geben. Jch bitte dihr ghar freundtlich, du woldtest mihr vnuordecht haben, daß ich dihr so offte midt meinen sachen bemuhe; wohr ich dihr mein dage widderumme dienen kan, findest du mihr alletzeidt willich vndt erpottigh, vnd thue vnß beiderseidtz in den schudtz deß Almechtigen emptfhelen.

Datum Rostock den 8. augustj anno 1601.
Eske Bilde

To the noble and honourable Joachim Stralendorff, chamber-junker of His Princely Grace of Mecklenburg, Ulrich, to the hands of my friendly dear brother, amicably written.

My friendly and wholly willing service, with the wish of all good, beforehand, friendly dear Joachim Stralendorff, brother and especial good friend. In friendly intent I cannot conceal from you that my blood-relation and good friend Tycho Brahe's letter was handed to me 9 or 10 days ago, from which I well perceived that he does not doubt (he writes) that the ten thousand thalers (about which I last wrote to you) were indeed paid out to me on the past St. John's Day. And a few days before, Andreas Meier's letter was also handed to me, wherein he reminds me that someone, on my behalf, was [said] to have demanded two thousand thalers in the antechamber of my gracious prince and lord, Duke Ulrich of Mecklenburg, at Doberan, and had no proof; whereupon I wrote back to him that I knew of no 2,000 thalers (as indeed I do not), but he should well recall, concerning the 10,000 thalers which I had given notice on to my aforesaid gracious lord at the past Michaelmas — on which I also have Meier's written answer, that such sum, together with the interest, should certainly be paid at the past Easter (as was fair); and when that did not happen, he wrote to me again that the money should certainly be paid on St. John's Day. And since I have now, at the right appointed time — namely at Easter — sent my servant to Rostock with the princely bond to receive the aforesaid sum, and now again on the past St. John's Day likewise with my gracious lord's bond — and my servant lay here at Rostock in Joachim Loherman's house several days before, on, and after St. John's Day, and presented himself at Doctor Lucas Backmeister's house (since the money was given notice to be paid there) — yet no one was there who would pay out such sum. And yet the rent-master writes, in his last letter, that the whole sum was together before St. John's Day, and desires (since no one was there on my behalf to receive it) that I would send out here and have the 2,000 thalers received, so the rest could be sent on to Tycho — at which letter of his I cannot wonder enough. And I wrote back that, since I have now twice — at Easter and St. John's Day — had my sure messenger with my gracious lord's bond here at Rostock, I will now, for good measure, come myself, a third time, at the first [opportunity].

So I have now, dear brother, arrived here at Rostock this very day; and in the evening someone (whose name and person are unknown to me) was with me and stated, on Meier's behalf, that he had transferred 5,000 thalers to Prague [by bill], and asked therewith that I declare whether I would receive the rest here, or travel to Wismar for it; whereupon I answered that I had now come here [to receive] the whole sum, together with all outstanding interest, accrued damages, and costs — or, failing that, I would be highly compelled to use other means, which (God knows) I, for my person, as well as my brother Tycho, would unwillingly do. I therefore ask you amicably, dear brother, that you would announce to my gracious prince and lord that I am waiting here, and that His Princely Grace would graciously declare whether I might now receive the sum — since His Princely Grace's letter, dated Doberan 17 June, asks that I wait a little while; I hope His Princely Grace will give me a gracious answer hereupon. I ask you quite amicably to hold me unblamed, that I trouble you so often with my affairs; wherever I can serve you in return in my days, you find me always willing and ready; and I commend us both to the protection of the Almighty.

Given at Rostock, the 8th of August, the year 1601.
Eske Bille.

Apparatus

  • Sender & recipient. Eske Bille, Tycho's Danish kinsman and plenipotentiary in the Mecklenburg-debt affair (cf. brahe-t_16010604001, brahe-t_16001116001), writing to Joachim Stralendorff, chamber-junker (Kammerjunker) of Duke Ulrich III of Mecklenburg — the duke's man through whom Eske seeks an answer. The MS is the original letter, filed at Schwerin under Debita passiva principum.
  • Significance — the climax (and impasse) of the Mecklenburg 10,000-thaler debt, August 1601. This first-hand account from the creditor's agent, two months before Tycho's death, shows the collection in deadlock:
  • The muddle. Rent-master Andreas Meier keeps writing of a 2,000-thaler claim "demanded in the antechamber at Doberan without proof"; Eske replies he knows nothing of any 2,000 — only the 10,000-thaler principal he gave notice on at Michaelmas [1600], due (per Meier's own letters) first at Easter, then St. John's Day [24 June 1601].
  • The failed collections. Eske's servant came to Rostock with the ducal bond at both terms, lodging at Joachim Lohmann's and presenting himself at Backmeister's house (the appointed pay-place) — but no one paid. Meier nonetheless claims the sum "was ready," and asks Eske to take 2,000 so "the rest could be sent to Tycho at Prague."
  • Eske's third trip & ultimatum. Disbelieving, Eske has now come himself, a third time; an unnamed man (for Meier) reports 5,000 thalers transferred to Prague by bill and offers the rest at Rostock or Wismar. Eske demands the whole sum with all interest, damages, and costs — or "other means" (legal action), "which I and my brother Tycho would unwillingly do." He asks Stralendorff to get Duke Ulrich's answer (the duke's letter of 17 June, Doberan, had asked him to "wait a little while").
  • Persons named. Eske Bille; Joachim Stralendorff (ducal chamber-junker); Tycho Brahe ("my blood-relation … my brother"); Andreas Meier (rent-master); Duke Ulrich III; Joachim Lohmann (the Rostock host); Lucas Backmeister (Superintendent of Rostock, the pay-place). Places: Doberan, Wismar, Rostock, Prague.
  • Calendar. Dateline Rostock den 8. Augustj anno 1601; Lutheran Rostock kept the Julian (Old Style) calendar; the Doberan 17. Junij ducal letter likewise O.S. Header marked [O.S.].
  • Correction. English corrected the house-owner's surname from 'Lohmann' to 'Loherman', matching both the transcribed original and Dreyer (XIV p.230); the companion letter no.225 (XIV p.229) spells the same man 'Lorman'.

1 defect found:
- mistranslation (low). The house-owner's surname is printed 'Loherman' in both our transcribed original and Dreyer (a companion letter on the facing page, no.225, spells the same man 'Lorman'); the translation silently substitutes the distinct, more common modern surname 'Lohmann', misidentifying a named person in the financial narrative. At: “Joachim Lohmann's house”.

Repaired. Verified against the Dreyer page image (TBOO XIV p.230, letter 226), which prints the house owner's name as 'Loherman'; the facing letter no.225 (p.229) spells the same person 'Lorman', confirming this is a named individual and not the modern surname 'Lohmann' the translation had silently substituted.
