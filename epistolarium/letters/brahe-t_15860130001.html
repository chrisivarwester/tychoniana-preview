The original

ERLIG OCH WELBYRDIG FRUE BERITTE BØLLE THIL GUNDERSLØFFHOLM, MIN KIERRE FADERSØSTER GANDTSKE WENLIGEN THILSCHREFFUIT.

MIN gandske wenlig helsen etther nu och althidt forsendt medt Gudt vor herre. Kierre fru Beritte och kierre fadersøster, betacker jeg etther saa gandske gierne for aldt ehre och gott, y mig althidt giortt och beuist haffuer, huilckett att forschylde jeg alle min dage will findes willig och redebon thil medt altt hues vdi min ringe formu kandt werre. Giffuendis etther wenligen thilkiende, att then byggemestere, som y schriffuer mig thil om, wedt naffn mester Hans, handt icke nu er her hoeß mig, men jeg wenntter hannom hidt att skulde komme om en dag tre eller fire. Och tror jeg icke andett, indt handt io well skulde kunde giørre samme arbedt, att thett schulle werre vstraffeligt, thersom handt icke haffuer tagett naagett andett arbedt ahn att bygge vdi sommer, men bliffuer medt etther om etthers arbedt forehnett. Huorfor jeg gierne will, naar handt hidt kommer thil mig, giffue hannom etthers arbedt ahn, och sige hannom ther om hues leylighedt y haffuer schreffuett mig thil, att handt fanger etthers wille her vdi att wide, och hues beschen handt giffuer fran sich, will jeg entten schrifftlich lade etther wide, eller handt och selff skall komme thil etther, och giffue etther nerwerendis thilkiende. Kierre fru Beritte och kierre fadersøster, will jeg icke lenger oppeholde etther medt thenne min schriffuelfe, men giør althidt gierne, hues jeg wedt etther thil ehre och tieniste kandt werre, och will her medt nu och althidt haffue etther then alsommechtigste Gudt thil siel och liiff befalendis.

DatumVraniborg then 30. januarii anno 1586.
Tyge Brahe
Egen hand.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Written most kindly to the honourable and well-born lady Birgitte Bølle of Gunderslevholm, my dear father's sister.

My wholly friendly greeting sent to you now and always with God our Lord. Dear Lady Birgitte and dear father's sister, I thank you so very gladly for all the honour and kindness you have always done and shown me, which to repay I shall all my days be found willing and ready, with all that may be within my poor means. Making known to you in friendly wise that the builder you write to me about, by name Master Hans, is not now here with me, but I expect him to come hither in a day or three or four. And I do not doubt but that he should indeed be able to do the same work, so that it would be beyond reproach, provided he has not taken on some other work to build this summer, but comes to an agreement with you about your work. Wherefore I am glad, when he comes hither to me, to put your work to him, and to tell him concerning it whatever circumstances you have written to me of, so that he may learn your wishes herein; and whatever answer he gives, I will either let you know in writing, or he shall himself come to you and make it known to you in person. Dear Lady Birgitte and dear father's sister, I will not detain you longer with this my writing, but [will] always gladly do whatever I know may be to your honour and service, and herewith will now and always have you commended to the most Almighty God in soul and body.

Given at Uraniborg, the 30th of January in the year 1586.
Tyge Brahe
[in his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Tycho Brahe (1546–1601), writing from his seat on Hven; the closing Egen hand ("[in his] own hand") marks the letter as an autograph — consistent with the witness being the surviving original (not a register copy).
  • Recipient. Frue Beritte Bølle thil Gundersløffholm = Birgitte Bølle (Berthe/Berete Bølle, d. 1592), of Gunderslevholm in Zealand, widow of the councillor Christoffer Gøye. Tycho addresses her as min kierre fadersøster — "my dear father's sister" (paternal aunt). The address-leaf style Erlig och welbyrdig frue … thil Gundersløffholm ("honourable and well-born lady … of Gunderslevholm") is the standard noble titulature; the seat-name thil Gunderslevholm denotes her as holder of that estate.
  • Place. Vraniborg = Uraniborg, Tycho's house on the island of Hven.
  • Master Hans, the builder. then byggemestere … wedt naffn mester Hans = "the builder … by name Master Hans." On the testimony of Friis (Bidrag til dansk Kunsthistorie), the "Bygmester Hans" recommended in this very letter is most probably Hans van Steenwinckel the Elder (c. 1545–1601), the Netherlandish-Danish architect-builder then active in Denmark (engaged in 1585 on the restoration of St. Petri church in Copenhagen, and afterwards on the architectural work of Tycho's mural-quadrant decoration at Uraniborg). The identification is Friis's, offered as a probability; the letter itself names him only as mester Hans, so the surname is supplied in the apparatus and not inserted in the body.
  • byggemestere / arbedt — "builder" / "work" (i.e. a building commission); Birgitte Bølle had evidently written to ask Tycho to engage Master Hans for building work on her estate, and Tycho here acts as intermediary.
  • Register. A private kinship-and-patronage letter in the polite-familial Danish epistolary register, not chancery: the doubled courtesy formulae (gandske wenlig helsen, kierre fru Beritte och kierre fadersøster), the service-and-honour protestations, and the pious commendation-formula (haffue etther … Gudt thil siel och liiff befalendis, "have you commended to God in soul and body") are the conventional opening and closing topoi of the genre, rendered in matching English period courtesy rather than modernised.
  • Epistemic register preserved. Tycho hedges throughout and these hedges are kept: jeg wenntter hannom … att skulde komme ("I expect him … to come"); tror jeg icke andett, indt handt io well skulde kunde giørre ("I do not doubt but that he should indeed be able to do"); and the conditional thersom handt icke haffuer tagett naagett andett arbedt ahn … men bliffuer … forehnett ("provided he has not taken on some other work … but comes to an agreement"). The single negation in that conditional (icke haffuer tagett … ahn, "has not taken on") is reproduced exactly once; the clause is a genuine condition on Hans's availability, not a statement that he has so taken on other work.
  • Orthography (early-modern Danish, c. 1586). thilschreffuit = tilskrevet ("written to"); etther = eder ("you," dat./acc.); althidt = altid ("always"); betacker = betakker ("thank"); forschylde = forskylde ("repay, requite"); redebon = redebon ("ready"); ringe formu = ringe formue ("poor means/ability"); thilkiende … giffuendis = give til kende ("make known"); hannom = hannem ("him"); vstraffeligt = ustraffeligt ("beyond reproach, blameless"); forehnett = forenet ("agreed, united"); beschen = besked ("answer, information"); nerwerendis = nærværende(s) ("in person, present"); oppeholde = opholde ("detain"); alsommechtigste = allermægtigste ("most almighty"); siel och liiff = sjæl og liv ("soul and body/life").
  • Calendar / dateline. Datum Vraniborg then 30. januarii anno 1586 = "Given at Uraniborg, 30 January 1586" (O.S.; Denmark kept the Julian calendar). The dateline date agrees with the catalogue label, so no discrepancy arises. Datum ("Given") kept by this chapter's convention (cf. Actum); in the source it is run together with the place (DatumVraniborg) — printed thus and not re-spaced in the source block.
  • Language note. The edition heuristic tags this da, which reading confirms: it is early-modern Danish throughout (no German body, no Latin code-switch beyond Datum and the title-style thil [Gundersløffholm]). This is the German-orthography-Danish risk-class only insofar as forms like schriffuer, schulle, wforsømmit-type w-/sch- spellings appear — but the morphology and lexicon are Danish.
