The original

ERLIG OCH WELLBYRDIG MAND CHRISTOFFER WALLCKENDROP THILL GLOERUP, DANMARKIS RIGES HOFFMESTER OCH RAAD, MIN SYNDERLIGE GODE WENN, GANDSCHE WENLIGENN THILLSCHREFFUITT

MIN gandsche vennlig hillsen nu och allthiid forsendt medt Vor Herre. Kierre her hoffmester, sønderlige gode ven, nest min vennlig thacksiigellse for allt beviist gode, hvillcket igen att forskyllde ieg vill findes aff formuen villig. Giffuendiß eder derhoes gandske wennligen thillkiende, att epthersom min fougidt, ieg haffuer betroidt att haffue opsyn paa Hvæn, haffuer nogle gange schreffuitt mig thill, att hand bekommer allthiid god biistand och raad hoes eder, naar hand haffuer nogit att giffue fore, som angaar bønderne derpaa landit, och att i allthiid svarer hannom vell, och hiellper hannom formedellst eders authoritheet och myndighedt derudi thill rette, saa vill ieg haffue eder paa dett wennligste bethackit for denne eders gode villge imod mig, alligevell att ieg icke nu sellff der er thillstede. Dersom mand rett wiste, hordan mottvillige och vlydige bønder der er paa samme lille landt, och hvad ieg haffuer liid aff dennom all denn stundt ieg bode der, och alligevell haffdt gedullt medt dennom, och giort dennom thill gode wiidere end de forskylldede, da skulle mandt kandtskee mene anderlediß derom, end thill diß skeed er. Kierre her hoffmester, epthersom eder witterligt er, att ieg bleff aff medt Nordtfiords forlening vdi Norge om Michaelisthiide strax epther krøningen, saa formener ieg, att mig burde att haffue hvis rente och indtkomst der gick aff y dett hallffue aar thillforn, horudaff ieg dog thill diß en føye thing haffuer bekommit, alligevell att ieg nogle gange haffuer schreffuitt did op derudom. Wille derfore haffue eder paa dett wennligste ombedit, att i ville vell giørre och medtdeele mig eders gode schriffuellse thill slotzherren paa Bergenhues, att hand ville forskaffe dett saa, att ieg eller min fulldtmegthige maatte medt dett første thill gode rede bekomme hvis mig der rester och bøer att haffue medt rette. Kierre her hoffmester, forthencker mig icke, att ieg bemøder eder hermedt, ieg forseer mig endnu allt gott thill eder och throer eder saa vell, att i baade vdi denne och andre maader bethencker leyligheden och verrer mig thill dett beste behiellpelig vdi allt dett, som billigt och rett er, och eder mueligt kand verre att giørre; [y] hvis maade ieg kan giørre nogit vdigen, som i kan haffue ære och gott aff, vill ieg stedtze [findes] medt all thacknemmelighedt ganndsche [villig och] redebon derthill. Och vill icke nu lenger ophollde eder, mens nu och allthiid haffue eder Gud allmegthigste befalidt.

Datum Wandeßbeck widt Hamborg denn 28 dag maij aar 1598.
Tyghe Brahe.
Egen hand.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and well-born man Christoffer Valkendorf of Glorup, Lord High Steward and Councillor of the Realm of Denmark, my especial good friend, [this] most amicably written.

My most friendly greeting, now and always, conveyed with [the blessing of] Our Lord. Dear Lord Steward, especial good friend — next [after] my friendly thanks for all the kindness shown me, which to repay in turn I shall be found willing according to my means. Giving you herewith most amicably to know that, since my bailiff, whom I have entrusted with the oversight on Hven, has several times written to me that he always receives good assistance and counsel from you whenever he has anything to bring forward that concerns the peasants there on the land [the island], and that you always answer him well, and by your authority and power help him to his right in the matter — so I would most amicably thank you for this your good will toward me, even though I am not now myself present there. If one rightly knew how wilful and disobedient the peasants are on that same little land, and what I have suffered from them the whole while I dwelt there — and yet had borne with them, and done them good further than they deserved — then one would perhaps think otherwise about it than has hitherto been the case. Dear Lord Steward, since it is known to you that I was deprived of the Nordfjord fief in Norway about Michaelmas-time, just after the coronation, I therefore consider that I ought to have whatever rent and income accrued from it in the half-year before — of which, however, I have to this day received [but] a trifling thing, although I have several times written up there about it. I would therefore most amicably have asked you to be so good as to grant me your good letter to the castellan of Bergenhus, that he would so arrange it that I, or my attorney, might at the first [opportunity] receive in good order whatever remains [owing] to me there and ought by right to be mine. Dear Lord Steward, think no ill of me that I trouble you herewith; I still expect all good of you, and trust you so well that, both in this and in other ways, you will consider the circumstance and be helpful to me for the best in all that is fair and right, and that may be possible for you to do. In whatever way I can do anything in return from which you may have honour and good, I will always [be found], with all gratitude, wholly [willing and] ready thereto. And I will not now detain you any longer, but, now and always, [will have] commended you to God Almighty.

Given at Wandsbek near Hamburg, the 28th day of May, the year 1598.
Tyge Brahe.
[His] own hand.

Apparatus

- Recipient. Christoffer Valkendorf til Glorup (1525–1601), Rigshofmester (Lord High Steward of Denmark) and member of the Rigsraad (Council of the Realm) — the senior crown officer Tycho also approached (jointly with Chancellor Christian Friis) in the intercession letters of early 1598 (cf. brahe-t_15980125001). The MS is the original letter in Valkendorf's own private archive at the Rigsarkivet. Tycho addresses him in warm terms (min synderlige gode ven) — notable, since Valkendorf had earlier been counted among Tycho's adversaries at court; here he is the helpful patron of Tycho's Hven bailiff.
- Setting. Written from Wandsbek (the Rantzau estate near Hamburg) in May 1598, during Tycho's post-Hven exile, the year he printed the Mechanica and the Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata there. Tycho has left Hven (1597) but still holds the island and its tenants in absentia, his foged (bailiff) managing it.
- Two requests.
1. Thanks for backing the bailiff. Valkendorf has repeatedly given the Hven bailiff "good assistance and counsel" in disputes with the island's bønder (peasant tenants); Tycho thanks him and vents about the tenants' mootvillige och ulydige ("wilful and disobedient") conduct during his own residence — a candid grievance over the long-running friction between Tycho and the Hven peasantry (the corvée and tenancy disputes that dog his lordship of the island).
2. The Nordfjord arrears. Tycho had been stripped of the Nordfjord fief (forlening) in Norway about Michaelmas (29 Sept) 1596, "just after the coronation" — i.e. just after Christian IV's coronation (29 August 1596), one of the deprivations that marked the new reign's withdrawal of favour. He claims the rent and income for the preceding half-year, of which he has had only "a trifling thing," and asks Valkendorf for a letter to the castellan of Bergenhus (the royal slotsherre at Bergen) so that he or his attorney may collect the arrears.
- Persons & places named. Christoffer Valkendorf; Tycho's (unnamed) Hven bailiff; the peasants of Hven; the castellan of Bergenhus; the Nordfjord fief; krøningen = the coronation of Christian IV (1596).
- Register / orthography. Chancery Early Modern Danish in Tycho's own warm-but-deferential register; period spellings (hillsen, wennligen, fougidt, forlening, schriffuellse), German-influenced sch-/w-/-ck (note this orthography elsewhere causes mis-tagging of Danish letters as German — the edition's convention). Subscription Egen hand ("[his] own hand"), the Danish equivalent of manu propria.
- Editorial brackets. The square brackets in the source — [y], [findes], [villig och] — are the edition's restorations of small gaps/abrasions in the original, supplying obvious words; they do not affect the sense and are reproduced as printed.
- Calendar. Dateline 28 dag maij aar 1598 at Wandsbek; Old Style (Julian), as Denmark and Tycho kept it. The edition label agrees (1598. 28. maj) — no discrepancy.
