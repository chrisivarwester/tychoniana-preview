The original

JEG Tygge Brahe thill Knudstrup kienndiß och giør witterligt for alle med thette mitt obnne breff, att effter som jeg nu haffuer soldt och skiødt till min kierre broder, erllig och welbyrdig manndt Steenn Brahe till Knudstrup, min halffpartt aff Knudstrups hoffuittgaardt och grund, och jeg mig dog ther hos forbeholldett att maa och skall ald min liffstiid schriffuis och kalldis aff samme Knudstrup gaardt, liige som jeg giorde, førenndt jeg thennd affhenndt och soldt haffuer, eptherthi thennd hafuer verett min federnne gaardt och mig arffueligenn thillfaldenn som min rette sedegaardt, saa beplichter jeg mig dog med thette breff, att enndog jeg baade selff schriffuer och kaller, och aff anndre, huem som held[s]t thed vere kannd, mig lader schriffue och kalle aff for:ne Knudstrups gaardt, att thed alligeuell icke skall komme for:ne min kierre broder Steenn Brahe eller hanns arffuinge thill hinder eller skade i nogen maade. This till ydermere vidnisbyrtt haffuer jeg trøgtt min signete her neden for thette mit obne breff, och mig med egenn hannd vnnderschreffuit, och wenligen ombedett erllig och welbiurdige menndt, Dethleff Holck thill Høygaardt och Eske Biillde til Ellinnge, thette breff med mig thil widnisbyrtt att besegle.

Giffuit vdi Helsingøer thennd x augustj anno Mdlxxxxiiij

English translation machine translation, Secure

I, Tyge Brahe of Knudstrup, acknowledge and make known to all by this my open letter that, inasmuch as I have now sold and conveyed to my dear brother, the honourable and well-born man Steen Brahe of Knudstrup, my half-share of the principal estate and land of Knudstrup — and have nonetheless reserved to myself therewith that I may and shall, for all my lifetime, be written and styled "of" that same estate of Knudstrup, just as I did before I parted with and sold it, inasmuch as it has been my paternal estate and has fallen to me by inheritance as my rightful ancestral seat — I do nevertheless bind myself by this letter that, although both I myself write and style [myself thus], and have myself written and styled by others, whosoever they may be, "of" the aforesaid estate of Knudstrup, it shall nevertheless not be to the hindrance or harm, in any manner, of my aforesaid dear brother Steen Brahe or his heirs. In further witness whereof I have impressed my seal here below this my open letter, and have signed it with my own hand, and have in friendship requested the honourable and well-born men Detlev Holck of Højgård and Eske Bille of Ellinge to seal this letter, together with me, in witness.

Given at Helsingør, the 10th [day] of August, in the year 1594.
Tyge
Brahe (place of the seal)
[in his] own hand

Detlev
Holck (place of the seal)
[in his] own hand

Apparatus

  • Document type. Not an ordinary letter but an open letter / deed of declaration (åbent brev) issued by Tycho in his own name — a self-binding instrument annexed to the sale of his half-share of Knudstrup to his brother Steen Brahe, the transaction completed by mid-1594 (J. R. Christianson, On Tycho's Island / Lord of Uraniborg; cf. Dreyer, Tycho Brahe: Knudstrup was inherited jointly by Otte Brahe's two eldest sons, Tycho and Steen). The edition's recipient:empty is therefore correct: an open letter is addressed to all (for alle), not to a single recipient.
  • Issuer. Tycho Brahe (1546–1601), here styling himself Tygge Brahe thill Knudstrup ("Tyge Brahe of Knudstrup") — the very style whose reservation is the subject of the deed.
  • Steen Brahe of Knudstrup (1547–1620), Tycho's younger brother, royal councillor; by this conveyance he obtained sole ownership of the family seat without awaiting Tycho's death.
  • Knudstrup (Knutstorp, Scania) — the Brahe family's fædrenegård (paternal estate) and sædegård (ancestral noble seat), Tycho's birthplace.
  • Witnesses. Detlev Holck of Højgård (Dethleff Holck thill Høygaardt) and Eske Bille of Ellinge (Eske Biillde til Ellinnge), Danish noblemen requested to co-seal; both named within the body. Only Holck's subscription and seal survive in the closing autograph block as transcribed here.
  • Legal force of the binding clause. The operative sentence carries a single negation governing the whole reservation: att thed alligeuell icke skall komme … thill hinder eller skade i nogen maade = "that it shall nevertheless not be to the hindrance or harm … in any manner." Tycho reserves the honorific style "of Knudstrup" for life but expressly disclaims any property or legal consequence flowing from it against Steen or his heirs. The single negation is reproduced as one; the concessive enndog … alligeuell ("although … nevertheless") pairing is kept intact.
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1594). kienndiß = kendes ("acknowledge"); giør witterligt = gør vitterligt ("make known"); obnne / obne breff = åbne brev ("open letter"); skiødt = skødt ("conveyed," verb of skøde, the deed of conveyance); halffpartt = halvpart ("half-share"); hoffuittgaardt = hovedgård ("principal/manorial estate"); forbeholldett = forbeholdt ("reserved"); liffstiid = livstid ("lifetime"); schriffuis och kalldis = skrives og kaldes ("be written and styled"); førenndt = førend ("before"); affhenndt = afhændet ("alienated, parted with"); eptherthi = eftersom/efterdi ("inasmuch as"); federnne gaardt = fædrenegård ("paternal estate"); arffueligenn thillfaldenn = arveligen tilfaldet ("fallen to [me] by inheritance"); sedegaardt = sædegård ("ancestral noble seat"); beplichter = forpligter ("bind/oblige"); held[s]t — the source reads held with the s of heldst (= helst, "soever") not written; supplied in brackets; for:ne = fornævnte ("aforesaid," the standard chancery abbreviation); alligeuell = alligevel ("nevertheless"); arffuinge = arvinger ("heirs"); hinder = hinder ("hindrance, impediment"); i nogen maade = i nogen måde ("in any manner"); This = des / dess ("thereof," the chancery formula des til ydermere vidnesbyrd, "in further witness whereof"); trøgtt min signete = trykt mit signet ("impressed my seal"); wenligen ombedett = venligen ombedet ("in friendship requested"); besegle = besegle ("to seal").
  • Seal notations. (l.s.) expands loco sigilli, "in the place of the seal" — the editorial marker for where a seal was impressed; egen hand / egen handtt = egen hånd, "[in his] own hand," noting the autograph subscription.
  • Calendar / place. Giffuit vdi Helsingøer thennd x augustj anno Mdlxxxxiiij = "Given at Helsingør (Elsinore), the 10th [day] of August, in the year 1594" (O.S.). The Roman-numeral year Mdlxxxxiiij = 1594; x augustj = 10 August. Body date, catalogue label date, and dateline agree (10 August 1594, Helsingør); no discrepancy.
