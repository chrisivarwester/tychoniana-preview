The original

IMPRESSORIUM AD DECENNIUM PRO TYCHONE BRAHE DANO.

RUDOLPHUS etc. Notum facimus tenore præsentium universis, quod cum nobis humiliter exposuerit syncere nobis dilectus Tycho Brahe Danus, dominus de Knudstrup, se pro singulari instaurandae astronomiæ studio nonnulla mathematica concinnasse opera ac pluribus etiam nunc incumbere, quæ pro communi usu bonoque publico typis in lucem ædere decreverit, vereri autem, ne forte æmuli aliqui ipsum sperato laborum ac sumptuum suorum fructu atque emolumento privare conaturi sint, demisse proinde supplicando, ut privilegio Nostro Cæsareo huiusmodi imposturas avertere clementer dignaremur. Nos sane indemnitati hac in parte suæ benigne consultum cupientes, humilibus eiusdem precibus deesse noluimus.

Ideoque omnibus ac singulis calcographis, bibliopolis ac alijs quamcumque librariam negociationem exercentibus, firmiter inhibemus ac vetamus, ne quis opera illa astronomica seu mathematica, quæ dicti Tychonis Brahe studio et elucubratione in lucem prodierint, per decem annorum spacium a prima cuiuslibet æditionis die computandum intra Sacri Romani Imperij, Regnorumque et Dominiorum Nostrorum hereditariorum fines, in toto vel in parte simili aut alia quapiam figura vel charactere imitari, recudere aliove recudenda dare vel alibi impressa adducere, vendere aut distrahere clam vel occulte præter expressam ipsius voluntatem et consensum ausit. Si quis autem secus faciendo privilegium hoc Nostrum Cæsareum transgredi conatus fuerit, eum non solum libris illis omnibus perperam sic excusis et adductis (quos quidem prædictus Brahe tamquam author eorum vel mandatarius ipsius, ubicunque deprehensos propria authoritate vel magistratus loci illius auxilio sibi vendicare poterit) de facto privandum sed pœna insuper x marcharum auri puri, fisco Nostro Imperiali fraudis vindici et sæpedicti Tychonis Brahe usibus ex æquo pendenda irremissibiliter decernimus mulctandum. Dummodo tamen, pro eo ac pollicetur, huiusmodi libri nihil in se scandalosum vel Sacri Romani Imperij constitutionibus de re litteraria æditis aut orthodoxæ religioni catholice adversum sive in textu sive in præfatione aut alias quoque modo contineant, ideoque idem Brahe terna ad minus cuiuslibet operis exemplaria quamprimum proprijs suis sumptibus ad Cancellariam Nostram Imperialem transmittere teneatur, quatenus privilegio hoc Nostro Cæsareo uti voluerit. Quod si neglexerit, eo ipso privatus et exutus plane censeatur. Mandamus ergo universis et singulis Nostris ac Sacri Romani Imperij Regnorumque et ditionum nostrarum hæreditariarum subditis ac fidelibus dilectis cuiuscunque status, gradus, ordinis, conditionis aut dignitatis exstiterint, tam ecclesiasticis quam sæcularibus, ijs præsertim qui in magistratu constituti vel proprio vel superiorum suorum nomine et loco iuris ac iustitiæ administrationem exercent, ne quenquam privilegium ac interdictum hoc Nostrum Cæsareum impune transgredi, spernere, negligere aut violare patiantur, sed contumaces, si quos compererint, prescripta pœna plecti alijsque idoneis ac opportunis rationibus coerceri curent, quatenus et ipsi eandem mulctam incurrere noluerint. Harum testimonio litterarum, manu Nostra subscriptarum ac sigilli Nostri Cæsarei impressione munitarum.

Datum Pragæ, die 28. martij anno etc. 1586.

PRINTING PRIVILEGE FOR TEN YEARS IN FAVOUR OF TYCHO BRAHE THE DANE.

Rudolf, etc. By the tenor of these presents we make known to all: that whereas Tycho Brahe the Dane, lord of Knudstrup, sincerely beloved of us, has humbly set forth to us that out of his singular zeal for the restoration of astronomy he has composed certain mathematical works, and is even now engaged upon several more, which he has resolved to issue into the light through the press for the common use and the public good — but that he fears lest perhaps certain rivals may seek to deprive him of the hoped-for fruit and profit of his labours and his expenses — humbly petitioning, accordingly, that we would graciously deign by our Imperial privilege to avert impostures of this kind: we, indeed, benignly desiring that provision be made for his protection from loss in this matter, have been unwilling to fail his humble prayers.

And therefore we firmly inhibit and forbid all and each of the printers [calcographers], booksellers, and others carrying on any sort of book-trade whatsoever, that no one dare — for the space of ten years, to be reckoned from the day of the first edition of each [work], within the bounds of the Holy Roman Empire and of our hereditary kingdoms and dominions — to imitate, reprint, or give to be reprinted those astronomical or mathematical works which shall have come into the light through the said Tycho Brahe's study and lucubration, in whole or in part, in a like or in any other form or type, or to bring in [copies] printed elsewhere, or to sell or to dispose of them, secretly or covertly, against his express will and consent. But if anyone, by acting otherwise, shall have attempted to transgress this our Imperial privilege, we irremissibly decree that he is in fact to be deprived not only of all those books thus wrongly printed and brought in (which indeed the aforesaid Brahe, as their author, or his agent, wherever they are caught, shall be able to claim for himself by his own authority or with the help of the magistrate of that place) — but is moreover to be fined a penalty of ten marks of pure gold, to be paid out without remission in equal parts to our Imperial treasury, the avenger of the fraud, and to the uses of the oft-named Tycho Brahe. Provided always, however — in accordance with what he promises — that books of this kind contain nothing scandalous in themselves, nor anything contrary either to the constitutions of the Holy Roman Empire issued concerning literary matters or to the orthodox Catholic religion, whether in the text or in the preface or in any other manner whatsoever; and that therefore the same Brahe be bound, as soon as possible at his own expense, to transmit at least three copies of each work to our Imperial Chancery, in so far as he shall wish to make use of this our Imperial privilege. But if he shall have neglected this, by that very fact let him be deemed plainly deprived and stripped of it. We command, therefore, all and each of our subjects and faithful beloved of the Holy Roman Empire and of our hereditary kingdoms and dominions, of whatever estate, rank, order, condition, or dignity they may be, ecclesiastical as well as secular — and those especially who, set in a magistracy, exercise the administration of law and justice, whether in their own name or in the name and stead of their superiors — that they suffer no one to transgress, spurn, neglect, or violate with impunity this our Imperial privilege and interdict, but that they see to it that the contumacious, if they find any, be punished with the prescribed penalty and constrained by other fitting and opportune means — in so far as they too would not wish to incur the same fine. In testimony of which [we have issued] this letter, subscribed by our hand and fortified with the impress of our Imperial seal.

Given at Prague, the 28th day of March, in the year etc. 1586.

Apparatus

  • Issuer. Rudolf II (1552–1612), Holy Roman Emperor (r. 1576–1612), King of Bohemia and of Hungary, residing at Prague. The edition records the sender as "Rudolf 2." and leaves the recipient field "empty," which is correct: this is not a personal letter but an Imperial privilegium impressorium — a printing-monopoly grant issued in favour of Tycho, the operative parties being the Empire's printers and booksellers, not a named addressee. The header therefore renders the recipient slot as the bracketed function of the instrument.
  • Beneficiary. Tycho Brahe Danus, dominus de Knudstrup = Tycho Brahe (1546–1601), styled by his patrimonial seat Knudstrup (Knutstorp, Scania); syncere nobis dilectus ("sincerely beloved of us") is the standard chancery courtesy-formula for a favoured suppliant, not evidence of personal acquaintance (Rudolf would not become Tycho's patron until 1599).
  • Document type & rubric. IMPRESSORIUM AD DECENNIUM = "a printing-privilege for a ten-year term" (impressorium, the chancery noun for a printing licence/privilege). The grant is registered in the Imperial Reichsregistratur (witness A), hence the formulaic RUDOLPHUS etc. opening and anno etc. 1586 closing, where etc. abbreviates the full Imperial titulature and regnal-year formula of the register.
  • Term of the privilege. per decem annorum spacium a prima cuiuslibet aeditionis die computandum — ten years, counted from the first day of publication of each individual work (not from the date of the grant); a per-title term, characteristic of these privileges.
  • instaurandae astronomiae studio — "zeal for the restoration of astronomy": instauratio astronomiae, Tycho's own programmatic phrase (astronomia instaurata), rendered "restoration" by the edition's convention
  • calcographis — "printers" (chalcographers/typographers); paired with bibliopolis (booksellers) and the catch-all alijs quamcumque librariam negociationem exercentibus (any others in the book-trade).
  • The prohibited acts. imitari, recudere aliove recudenda dare … adducere, vendere aut distrahere — to imitate, reprint, give out to be reprinted, import (copies printed elsewhere), sell, or dispose of; in toto vel in parte … simili aut alia quapiam figura vel charactere = "in whole or in part, in a like or any other form or type [character]." praeter expressam ipsius voluntatem et consensum = "against [i.e. without] his express will and consent."
  • Penalty (negation/legal force verified). The sanction is cumulative, not alternative: non solum … privandum sed poena insuper … mulctandum = "not only to be deprived … but moreover to be fined." The confiscated stock the offender forfeits; the fine is x marcharum auri puri = ten marks of pure gold, ex aequo pendenda = "to be paid in equal parts" between the Imperial fisc (fraudis vindex, "avenger of the fraud") and Tycho's own use. irremissibiliter = "without remission." The author's recovery right — quos … propria authoritate vel magistratus loci illius auxilio sibi vendicare poterit — lets Brahe or his agent seize offending copies by his own authority or with the local magistrate's help.
  • The conditions on Tycho (proviso clause). Dummodo tamen … nihil in se scandalosum … contineant = "provided, however, that [the books] contain nothing scandalous," nothing against the Empire's press-laws (Sacri Romani Imperij constitutionibus de re litteraria) or orthodoxae religioni catholice ("the orthodox Catholic religion") — a notable confessional clause binding the Lutheran Tycho's output, sive in textu sive in praefatione ("in text or preface"). The deposit requirement: terna ad minus cuiuslibet operis exemplaria … ad Cancellariam Nostram Imperialem transmittere = "transmit at least three copies of each work to our Imperial Chancery," at Tycho's own expense — a condition precedent: quatenus privilegio … uti voluerit. Quod si neglexerit, eo ipso privatus … censeatur = "in so far as he shall wish to use the privilege; but if he neglects [it], by that very fact let him be deemed deprived." (Negation count in this clause confirmed: nihil … vel … aut … adversum, one governing negation distributed over the enumerated objects; rendered as a single "nothing … nor … or.")
  • The mandate to officials. ne quenquam … impune transgredi … patiantur, sed … plecti … coerceri curent, quatenus et ipsi eandem mulctam incurrere noluerint — magistrates are not to allow violation and are to see the contumacious punished, "in so far as they too would not wish to incur the same fine" (i.e. on pain of incurring the penalty themselves; the conditional quatenus … noluerint is a veiled threat to negligent officials — its mood is consistent across the parallel clause at the proviso's end and is rendered, not smoothed).
  • Attestation. manu Nostra subscriptarum ac sigilli Nostri Caesarei impressione munitarum = "subscribed by our hand and fortified with the impress of our Imperial seal" — the register's note of the original's authentication.
  • Calendar / place. Datum Pragae, die 28. martij … 1586 = "Given at Prague, 28 March 1586." Denmark and the Empire's chancery practice here are not in tension on the figure as transcribed; the edition label ("1586-03-28 Prag") agrees with the dateline. Treated as Old Style by the edition's convention (the date is given without an n.s. marker; the equivalent N.S. date would be 7 April 1586, but the source does not flag a style and none is asserted here beyond the as-written figure).
