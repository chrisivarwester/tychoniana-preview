The original

GENEROSO ET MAGNIFICO VIRO D. HENRICO RANZOVIO, REGIAe MAIESTATIS DANIAe IN HOLSATIA ET SLESVICIA VICARIO ET CONSILIARIO PRUDENTISSIMO, PRÆFECTO ARCIS SEGEBERGAe, ET DOMINO IN BREDENBERGA AMICO SUO OBSERVANDO.
S.

OBILISSIME et Eruditissime Domine HENRICE RANZOWI, fuit hisce diebus apud me filius tuus GERARDUS RANZOW, Praeses arcis Kroneburgensis, Amicus meus singularis et vicinus exoptatissimus; cumque is prae ceteris nostri aevi Nobilibus Iuvenibus non saltem rerum Mundanarum multiplici et late quaesita Experientia, sed etiam Ingenio et Iudicio praeeminenti valeat, Moribusque insuper admodum laudatis praeditus sit, ipsius conversatione plurimum afficiebar eique tum varias observandis sideribus a me ingenti labore et sumtu constructas Machinas, tum etiam Pyronomici exercitij diversa Artificia lubens ostendi; inter alia vero cum in Typographiam meam, quam hac aestate praeterita parari curavi, illum deducerem, ut Typos et rationem nostram imprimendarum chartarum perspiceret, conquestus sum illi, papyrum impressoriam mihi pro absolutis laboribus edendis deficere, et hyemem obstaculo esse, quominus eam nunc satis opportune nancisci queam; adeo ut ne pro quodam exiguo huius inchoati anni Calendario Astrologico imprimendo mihi charta suppetat, vel alicubi iusto pretio hîc in Dania venalis existat; nedum ut alijs maioribus a me confectis Operibus, quae maximam papyri copiam postulant, sufficiens acquiratur. Subiunxit itaque ille, te Patrem ipsius molendinam habere papyraceam nec dubitare, si negocium hoc tibi constaret, quin lubenter pro Calendario isto excudendo mihi sufficientem Papyrum concessurus sis, suaque sponte (ut est Ingenio bonis conatibus admodum faventi) obtulit, se tibi hoc nomine scripturum, quod et statim hîc praesens praestitit; literasque tibi transmittendas tradidit, quas nunc per proprium nuntium, unum ex meis familiaribus, IOHANNEM BERNSSÖN nomine (cui ut fidem habeas velim) perferendas curo, ut eo certius ad tuam Generositatem mature perveniant, negociumque hoc eo rectius perficiatur; rogoque peramanter ut, si in Officina tua Papyracea reperiatur impressoria Papyrus meis votis conveniens, mihi de ea 20 vel 25 Riss, ut vocant, concedere velis. Pretium solitum tuae Officinae praefecto, quam primum de eo certior factus fuero, lubens transinittam. Continebit Calendarium illud, ubi impressum fuerit, circiter 6 quaterniones, ut ob id 25 Rissij pro 2000 Exemplaribus imprimendis requirantur. Pro reliquis vero maioribus a me absolutis Astronomicis Operibus quam primum, Deo volente, in lucem edendis, multo copiosiorem, quae requiritur, papyrum in hoc ipso Freto Helsingorae facile habiturus sum, cum vernum tempus navigationem consuetam reduxerit. Spero autem tuam Magnificentiam meis defiderijs hac in parte, quantum in te est, interea non defuturam, et dabo operam, ut hoc Officij genus in me non frustra collocatum experiare. Mittam vero brevi literas impressas tibi alijsque Nobilibus et eruditis Amicis inscriptas, quamprimum omnes absolutae fuerint, ut Typos nostros videas, sique illi placuerint, et quid habeas, quod in lucem edere ex Astrologicis rebus decreveris, lubenter ut in mea Officina excudatur, permittam, saltem ut ipse requisitam Papyrum addas. Et si quacunque alia in re tuae Generositati unquam aliquid per me quod gratum sit praestari queat, efficiam ut me facilem et promptissimum habeas. Vale cum charissima coniuge totaque domo, vir Amplissime pariter et Eruditissime, meque tui studiosissimum redamare non graveris.

Datae Uraniburgi in Insula porthmi DaniciHuena die 8. Ianuarij Anno 1585.
Tuae generositati addictiss.
TYCHO BRAHE,
manu propria.

[Postscript — restored from Dreyer, Opera Omnia VII, p. 90; dropped by the working transcription. See Apparatus.]
Si catalogum Astrologicorum tuorum librorum ex amplissima tua Bibliotheca descriptum mihi transmittere non displicuerit, rem feceris longe gratissimam.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the noble and magnificent gentleman Master Heinrich Rantzau, the most prudent viceroy and councillor of the Royal Majesty of Denmark in Holstein and Schleswig, governor of the castle of Segeberg, and lord in Breitenburg, his friend to be esteemed — greeting.

Most noble and most learned Master Heinrich Rantzau, there was with me in these days past your son Gerhard Rantzau, governor of the castle of Kronborg, my singular friend and a most longed-for neighbour. And since he, beyond the other noble young men of our age, is strong not only in a manifold and widely-gathered experience of worldly affairs, but also in pre-eminent talent and judgement, and is moreover endowed with most praiseworthy character, I was greatly taken with his company; and I gladly showed him both the various machines [instruments] I have constructed at vast labour and cost for observing the stars, and likewise the diverse devices of the pyronomic exercise [the chemical art]. Among other things, indeed, when I led him into my printing-house, which I had seen to having set up this past summer, so that he might examine the types and our method of printing sheets, I complained to him that printing-paper was lacking to me for publishing my finished works, and that winter was an obstacle preventing me from now obtaining it conveniently enough — so much so that not even for a certain small Astrological Calendar of this now-beginning year that is to be printed is there paper enough at my disposal, nor is any to be had for sale here in Denmark at a fair price; let alone that sufficient be acquired for the other, greater works completed by me, which demand a very great quantity of paper. He therefore added that you, his father, have a paper-mill, and did not doubt that, if this business were made clear to you, you would gladly grant me sufficient paper for printing that Calendar; and of his own accord (as he is of a disposition very much favouring good endeavours) he offered to write to you on this account, which he did at once, here present; and he gave me the letter to be conveyed to you, which I now have carried by a special messenger, one of my household, John Bernssøn by name (to whom I would wish you to give credence), that it may the more surely reach Your Generosity in good time, and this business be the more rightly accomplished. And I ask most affectionately that, if in your paper-works there be found printing-paper agreeable to my wishes, you would be willing to grant me 20 or 25 reams, as they call them, of it. The usual price I will gladly send to the overseer of your works as soon as I am informed of it. That Calendar, when it has been printed, will contain about 6 quaternions, so that on that account 25 reams are required for printing 2000 copies. But for the remaining greater Astronomical Works completed by me, to be brought into the light as soon as possible, God willing, I shall easily have the much more copious paper that is required here in this very Sound at Elsinore, when the spring season has brought back the customary sailing. I hope, however, that Your Magnificence will not in the meantime fail my desires in this matter, so far as in you lies; and I will take pains that you may find this kind of service not bestowed upon me in vain. And shortly I shall send you, and other noble and learned friends, letters printed [and] inscribed [to you], as soon as they are all finished, that you may see our types; and if they please you, and you have anything among astrological matters that you have resolved to bring into the light, I will gladly permit it to be printed in my workshop — provided only that you yourself supply the requisite paper. And if in any other matter whatsoever anything that is welcome can ever be rendered to Your Generosity through me, I will see to it that you find me obliging and most ready. Farewell, with your dearest wife and your whole house, gentleman both most ample and most learned, and do not be unwilling to return the love of me who am most devoted to you.

Given at Uraniborg, on the island Hven of the Danish strait, on the 8th day of January, the year 1585.
Most attached to Your Generosity,
TYCHO BRAHE,
in his own hand.

[Postscript:] If it should not displease you to send me a catalogue of your Astrological books, copied out from your most ample Library, you will do me a most welcome thing indeed.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Henricus Ranzovius = Heinrich Rantzau (1526–1598), the Danish crown's Statthalter (viceroy/royal governor) in the duchies of Holstein and Schleswig, humanist, astrologer, and one of the great book-collectors and patrons of the age; lord of Breitenburg (Bredenberga) and governor of Segeberg castle. Tycho courted him as a patron and conduit to the wider learned world.
  • Persons named. Gerardus Ranzow = Gerhard (Gert) Rantzau (1558–1627), Heinrich's son, then governor (Praeses) of Kronborg castle at Elsinore — Tycho's near neighbour across the Sound; he later succeeded his father as viceroy. Iohannes Bernssön = Johann (Hans) Bernssøn, a member of Tycho's household (familiaris) sent as the special messenger; otherwise unidentified.
  • Places. arx Kroneburgensis = Kronborg (Elsinore/Helsingør); arx Segebergae = Segeberg castle (Holstein); Bredenberga = Breitenburg (Rantzau's seat in Holstein); Fretum / Helsingorae = the Sound (Øresund) at Elsinore; Uraniburgum in Insula porthmi Danici Huena = Uraniborg on the island of Hven in the Danish strait. The biographies confirm the episode: Gert Rantzau visited Hven and told Tycho his father's paper-mill could supply paper, and Tycho intended to print the calendar in 2000 copies (Christianson, On Tycho's Island).
  • Technical / craft terms. Machinas … observandis sideribus … constructas — Tycho's astronomical instruments (here machinae, rendered "machines [instruments]" per glossary). Pyronomici exercitij … Artificia — "the devices of the pyronomic exercise," i.e. Tycho's chemistry/alchemy ("pyronomy," the fire-art) carried on in the Uraniborg laboratory; glossed rather than modernised. Typographia / Officina / Typos / chartas imprimendas — Tycho's own printing-house at Uraniborg, its types, and the sheets to be printed.
  • Paper terms. papyrus impressoria = printing-paper; molendina papyracea / Officina Papyracea = paper-mill / paper-works; Riss / Rissij ("ut vocant," "as they call them") = the German loanword Ries = ream (a paper quantity, here kept as "reams"); quaterniones = quaternions (gatherings of four sheets / eight leaves). The arithmetic in the letter (≈6 quaternions per copy → 25 reams for 2000 copies) is reproduced as written, not recomputed.
  • The Calendar and the "greater works." Calendarium … Astrologia[e] — a small astrological calendar for 1585; the maiora Astronomica Opera "completed by me … to be brought into the light" are Tycho's projected major publications (the instrument and De mundi volumes), here described prospectively with his own hedge Deo volente ("God willing"), preserved.
  • Formulae. S. = salutem ("greeting"); Vale = "Farewell"; Tuae generositati addictiss. = "Most attached to Your Generosity"; manu propria = "in his own hand." The honorific Generositas / Magnificentia Tua is rendered "Your Generosity / Your Magnificence."
  • Orthographic notes (Source block preserves the printed forms). The salutation word opens OBILISSIME — a dropped/decorative initial [N] for NOBILISSIME ("most noble"), expanded in the translation. transinittam (line: "Pretium … lubens transinittam") is a printer's misprint for transmittam ("I will send"); translated as transmittam. defiderijs = desideriis ("desires"); -ij/-ijs throughout is the period spelling of -ii/-iis. In the dateline DaniciHuena the edition prints Danici and Huena run together without a space; read as Danici Huena ("…of the Danish [strait], Hven").
  • Calendar. die 8. Ianuarij Anno 1585 = 8 January 1585, Old Style (Denmark kept the Julian calendar until 1700). The catalogue label ("1585. 8. januar. Uraniborg") agrees with the dateline; no discrepancy. No date is "corrected."
  • §1 Dreyer collation. Closing postscript, restored from Dreyer VII, p. 90 (dropped by the working transcription).
  • Correction. Latin corrected from 'Calendario Astrologia' to 'Calendario Astrologico' per Dreyer, TBOO VII p.89 (agreement of adjective with 'Calendario'); no change to the English, which already read correctly.
