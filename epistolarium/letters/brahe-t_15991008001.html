The original

MAGNIFICO ET NOBILISSIMO VIRO, D. TYCHONI BRAHE, DOMINO IN KNUDSTORP, MATHEMATICO PRÆSTANTISSIMO, DOMINO AC AMICO S. HONORANDO.
S.

magnifice et nobilissime vir, domine ac amice honorande. Literas tuas Venetijs Bohemorum 28 septemb. datas summa cum voluptate perlegi, cum quod de tua valetudine, tum et quo in loco T. M. subsistat, me certiorem fecerint. Literæ s. cæs. m:tis, domini nostri clementiss., quarum mentionem T. M. facit, senatui nostro non sunt exhibitæ, forsitan incuria tuorum neglectæ; quamprimum autem allatæ et in senatu lectæ fuerint, ex ijsque intellexero, quid tua causa s. cæs. m:tas fieri velit, lubens, quantum in me erit, negotium promouebo. Ferramenta Wandesburgi relicta quod attinet, existimo de ijs nihil certi statui posse, antequam Bredo Ranzouius ad tuas responderit. His paucis T. M. æterni Dei tutelæ commendo, qui eandem diutiss: incolumem conseruet.

Dabantur Hamburgj 8 octob. a:o 1599.
Mag:æ T. studiosiss:

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the magnificent and most noble man, Master Tycho Brahe, Lord in Knudstrup, most outstanding Mathematician, his lord and friend to be honoured. Greeting.
Greeting.

Magnificent and most noble man, lord and friend to be honoured. Your letter, dated at the Venice of the Bohemians [Benátky] on 28 September, I read through with the greatest pleasure, since it made me more certain both about your health and in what place Your Magnificence is residing. The letters of His Sacred Imperial Majesty, our most clement lord, of which Your Magnificence makes mention, have not been presented to our Senate — perhaps neglected through the carelessness of your people; but as soon as they have been brought and read in the Senate, and I have understood from them what His Sacred Imperial Majesty wishes done on your behalf, I will gladly, as much as shall be in me, promote the business. I have admonished Simon von Petkum to make you more certain about the particulars which it concerns you to know, and he has undertaken to write back to you copiously. As regards the ironwork [the iron instruments/tools] left at Wandsbek, I judge that nothing certain can be settled about them before Breide Rantzau has replied to your [letter]. With these few [words] I commend Your Magnificence to the protection of the eternal God, who may preserve you safe as long as possible.

Given at Hamburg, 8 October, in the year 1599.
Most devoted to Your Magnificence,
Vincent Moller, L[icentiate].

Apparatus

  • Sender. Vincent Moller (the Elder, 1527–1599/1600), a Hamburg patrician and Senate figure (the L. after his name = Licentiatus in law); Tycho's local contact during and after his stay near Hamburg. He acts here as Tycho's intermediary with the Hamburg Senate over imperial letters concerning Tycho's affairs.
  • Venetijs Bohemorum — "at the Venice of the Bohemians," a learned by-name for Benátky nad Jizerou (Czech Benátky = "Venice"), Tycho's residence from 1599; rendered with the gloss. Tycho's incoming letter was dated there 28 September.
  • Literae s. caes. m:tis = letters of His Sacred Imperial Majesty (Rudolf II) on Tycho's behalf, which had not reached the Hamburg Senate. Simon a Petkum = Simon von Petkum (Peckius), a diplomat/agent who would write Tycho the particulars. Bredo Ranzouius = Breide Rantzau (1556–1618), the Holstein noble (governor of the Rantzau lands) under whom the Wandsbek matter fell.
  • Ferramenta Wandesburgi relicta — "the ironwork left at Wandsbek": Tycho's heavy iron instruments/fittings left behind at Wandsbek (where he had lodged 1597–98 at Heinrich Rantzau's castle) when he moved on; their fate awaited Breide Rantzau's reply. (Ferramenta = iron tools/instruments; rendered with the gloss.)
  • ⚠ Date. The dateline reads Hamburgj 8 octob. a:o 1599 = 8 October 1599; the identifier (…10 08…) agrees. The edition catalogues it under 8 September — the one-month discrepancy is reproduced, not reconciled; the header follows the as-written 8 October. Hamburg (Lutheran) = Old Style.
