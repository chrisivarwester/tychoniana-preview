The original

DEM DURCHLEUCHTIGEN HOCHGEBORNEN FURSTEN VND HERRN, HERRN VLRICHEN HERTZOGEN ZU MECKELNBURGK, FÜRSTEN ZU WENDEN, GRAFENN ZU SCHWERIN, DER LANDE ROSTOCK VND STARGAARD HERRN, MEINEM GNEDIGEN FÜRSTEN VND HERRN.

DURCHLEUCHTIGER hochgeborner fürst, E.F.G. seindt meine vnterthenige bereitwillige dienste iedertzeit bestes fleißes zuuor. Gnediger furst vnnd herr. Daß E.F.G. mir auff mein junsthin verschinnes monnats julij gegebenes schreibenn dero gnedige antwordt nicht allein vbersendet vnnd ihr mein propositum vnnd furnehmen wollgefallenn lassen, sondern auch so ein herrlich commendationschreiben ahnn die röm. kay. maytt. gnedig communiciret vnnd wiederfahren lassen, darahn haben E.F.G. mir eine besondere gnadt vnnd wolthat bewiesenn, vnnd thue deßhalbenn gegen E.F.G. mich inn aller vnterthenigkeit vnnd zum höchstenn darfur nicht alleine bedanckenn, sondernn erkenne mich auch schuldig, daßelbe vmb E.F.G. nach eusserstem meinem vermugenn iederzeit hinwider inn vntherthenigkeit zuerkennenn.

Dieweil dan auch ab E.F.G. gnedig ahn mich beschehenen schreibenn ich vermercket, daß E.F.G. jhr mein vberschicktes buch de mechanica astronomia lieb vnndt angenehm sein vnnd in dero furstliche bibliotheck aufhebenn vnd stadt findenn lassen, so ist mir solchs zuuemehmenn gantz erfreulichen gewesen, vndt thue E. F. G. daß ander vonn meiner restitutio affixarum stellarum, welches jch auch höchstgedachter röm. kay. maytt. vnterthenigst dediciret, vnterthenig vberschickenn, worahn jch vber die zwanzig jahrlang nebens andern astronomischen sachen mit hochem fleiß [gearbeit] vnndt zugebracht, also das (ohne ruhm zu [melden) in] dieser zeit nie keiner solchen fleiß vnd [muhe hat an] gewendet, dan diese ... minuto vnnd (wo es von ... [du]rch meine große vnnd ... uiret vnnd nachgerech-... astronomi nur den sechsten theill eines gradus verzeichnet vnnd doch bißweilenn vber einen gantzenn gefeilet habenn, bin aber nicht willens, daßelbe so balt, alweiln es mich viell muhe vnnd vnkost gestandenn, inn druck alß publiciren zulassen, sondern es nur manu scriptum, wie dieses, jhrer kay. maytt. zuofferiren.

Langet derhalbenn ahn E.F.G. mein vntertheniges fleißiges bitten, E.F.G. wollen solches nichtweniger alß das vorige von mir gnedig auf- vnndt ahnnehmen vnnd inn dero bibliotecam zum gedechtnuß verwahrenn vnnd aufhebenn lassenn, auch iederzeit mein gnediger furst vnnd her, wie bißhero geschehen, sein vnd bleibenn. Jn solchem gegen E.F.G. mein danckbares gemuedt zuerzeigenn, soll vnnd will jch mich iederzeit vngespart meiner geringfuegigen dienstenn eußersten vermugens vnterthenig erfinden lassenn. Thue hiemit E.F.G. sambt dero hertzvielgeliebten gemahlin inn den schutz vnndt schirm Gottes deß allmechtigen zu langer bestendiger [ge]sundtheit vnnd fürstlicher wolfart getreulich befehlen.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most serene, high-born Prince and Lord, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard, my gracious Prince and Lord.

Most serene, high-born Prince — to Your Princely Grace [be] my humble, ready services at all times, with best diligence, beforehand. Gracious Prince and Lord. That Your Princely Grace not only sent me your gracious answer to the letter I gave last, in the past month of July, and were pleased with my purpose and undertaking, but also so graciously communicated and bestowed such a splendid letter of commendation to the Roman Imperial Majesty — therein Your Princely Grace has shown me a special grace and benefit; and I therefore not only thank Your Princely Grace for it, in all humility and most highly, but also acknowledge myself bound to requite the same toward Your Princely Grace, to the utmost of my ability, at all times, in humility.

And since, too, from Your Princely Grace's gracious letter to me I have noted that Your Princely Grace finds my book de mechanica astronomia, which I sent, dear and agreeable, and lets it be kept and given a place in your princely library — this has been quite a joy for me to learn; and I humbly send Your Princely Grace the other [work], of my restitution of the fixed stars, which I have likewise most humbly dedicated to the aforesaid Roman Imperial Majesty, [and] on which, above twenty years long, alongside other astronomical matters, I have with high diligence [laboured] and spent [my time] — so that (without [boasting] to say it) in this age no one has expended such diligence and [labour]; for these [positions are reckoned] to the minute, and (where it … through my great and … [measured] and recalculated …) whereas [earlier] astronomers noted only the sixth part of a degree, and yet have sometimes erred by a whole one. But I am not willing to let the same be published in print so soon — since it has cost me much labour and expense — but only to offer it in manuscript, like this one, to His Imperial Majesty.

There comes therefore to Your Princely Grace my humble, diligent request, that Your Princely Grace would, no less than the former [book], graciously receive and accept this from me, and let it be kept and preserved in your library as a memorial, and also be and remain at all times my gracious Prince and Lord, as has hitherto been the case. In showing my grateful heart toward Your Princely Grace herein, I shall and will at all times let myself be found humble, and unsparing of my poor services, to the utmost of my ability. I herewith faithfully commend Your Princely Grace, together with your heartily-beloved consort, to the protection and shelter of God Almighty, for long, constant health and princely welfare.

Given at Wandsbek, the 14th of September, the year 1598.
Your Princely Grace's humble and service-willing
[Tycho] Brahe,
with his own hand.

Apparatus

- Recipient. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603), full titulature reproduced (as in brahe-t_15880815001, brahe-t_16010415003, brahe-t_16010604001). The MS is the original letter, explicitly in poor condition (i dårlig stand) — hence the lacunae below.
- Significance — a double-book presentation (census-relevant). This letter documents Tycho's presentation of two of his Wandsbek-press works to the ducal library at Güstrow, in the exile year 1598:
1. mein vberschicktes buch de mechanica astronomia = the _Astronomiae Instauratae Mechanica_ (Wandsbek 1598), already received and "given a place in [Ulrich's] princely library."
2. daß ander vonn meiner restitutio affixarum stellarum = the "restitution of the fixed stars" — i.e. the fixed-star catalogue (the work of the Astronomiae Instauratae Progymnasmata), here sent as the second gift, "likewise dedicated to the Roman Imperial Majesty."
This is a documented presentation/ownership event for a 1598 Wandsbek Mechanica (Duke Ulrich III's copy, into the Güstrow Fürstliche Bibliothek) — directly relevant to the Mecklenburg strand of the Mechanica's documented distribution history.
- The star-catalogue and Tycho's accuracy claim. Tycho stresses he is not yet willing to print the star-restitution (it "cost much labour and expense") but offers it manu scriptum ("in manuscript, like this one") to Rudolf II — the famous practice of presenting the manuscript star catalogue to the Emperor rather than publishing. His accuracy boast: his positions are reckoned "to the minute," whereas earlier astronomers recorded only "the sixth part of a degree" (≈ 10 arc-minutes) and "sometimes erred by a whole degree." (Twenty-plus years of labour — the Hven programme.)
- Damaged passage (flagged; this chapter's convention). The witness is physically damaged; the precision-boast sentence is partly lost: "dan diese … minuto vnnd (wo es von … [du]rch meine große vnnd … uiret vnnd nachgerech-… astronomi nur den sechsten theill eines gradus verzeichnet …". The bracketed words ([gearbeit], [melden) in], [muhe hat an], [du]rch, [ge]sundtheit, and the subscription [iger vnd] di[enstwilliger, Tycho]) are the edition's restorations of legible-by-context gaps; the ellipses (…) mark irrecoverable lacunae, left unfilled in the translation ("[measured]" supplied tentatively in brackets where sense demands a verb). The general sense (minute-precision vs. earlier astronomers' coarser figures) is secure; the exact wording of the broken clause is not.
- Persons / things named. Duke Ulrich III; Rudolf II (röm. kay. Maytt.); the Mechanica and the fixed-star catalogue; Ulrich's hertzvielgeliebte Gemahlin ("heartily-beloved consort") — Anna of Pomerania-Wolgast, Ulrich's second wife (cautiously, not asserted from the text). The July 1598 letter Tycho refers to, and Ulrich's commendation to the Emperor, belong to Tycho's exile-period campaign to secure imperial patronage (cf. the intercession letters of 1598).
- Register / formulae. Deferential chancery German, subject's first person (E.F.G. = Euer Fürstliche Gnaden); subscription mit eigner Hand (= manu propria); the Latin technical titles (de mechanica astronomia, restitutio affixarum stellarum, manu scriptum) embedded in the German by this chapter's convention
