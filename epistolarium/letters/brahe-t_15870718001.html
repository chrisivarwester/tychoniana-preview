The original

THILL ERLIG OCH WELBIURDIG MAND NIELS BILLD THIJL RAUNHOLTT, MIN BESØNDERLIGE GODE WENN DETTE BREFF GANDSKE WENLIG THILLSCHREFFUID.

MIJNN gandske wenlig helßennd nu och altid forsennd med Wor Herre. Kiere Niels Bild, och sønderlige gode wenn, nest en wenligh tacksigelse for alt beuist gode, huilket jeg altid will findis willig och redebon tiill epther formuenn at forschilde, kand ieg eder wenligen icke forholde, att epthersom Christoffher Pepler min fordoms fogit ij Norfiord lhenn ij Norge, dentijd jeg dett haffde af Kong. May. ij forlening, er nu kommen hidt tiill mig fra Norge, saa haffuer hand giffuedt mig tilkiende, huorledis hand endnu haffuer nogen skilld vdstaendis ij Norfiord baade aff kongens restandtz och aff sidt egidt goeds, huormed hand bønderne vndsett haffde, huilckid de nu forhollder hannom, och dertilmed haffuer bygtt nogle hus paa sin egen bekostning oppaa en øde platz, huor ingen huß førre paastodt, ey heller nogen agger heller eng tilligger. Laadendis mig ochsaa der hoß forstaa, huorledis at prousten derudj lenid, som skulle holde kirkens regenskab, er hannom for tong, ij dett hand siger: att de kiercke ombodsmend, som haffuer bygt paa kierkerne, skulle haffue satt deris arbeid for dyrtt och der ijmod wille tage kierkens renntte for leett jnd, som jeg kand forstaa, och att Christoffher skulle dett wederlegge kierckerne jgenn ijmod de breffue, som kiercke ombodsmenddenne haffuer giffuidt hannom, lydendis, att hand jntted haffuer oppebaridt aff kierckens reennte vdenn sin lhøn, och beretter ydermere, att er hand ochsaa befrøchttendis, att ij haffuer hannom mijßtenckt ij dett hand skulle haffue schreffuit nogidt tijll mig om eder, huilked jeg kiendis att hand icke giortt haffuer, menn att jeg aff min egenn goede wille denn tijd jeg spurde hannom nogenn gield der ij leenid tiill att staa, schreff eder til, forseendes mig till eder, som min goede wenn, att hand formijddelst eders hielp och bijstand diß snarere skulle komme thiill rette och nhaa sin betalling. Kiere Niels Bild, er derfor min gandske wenlig bøenn endnu til eder, att ij wille well giøre, som jeg eder thiill troer, och fordre och forfremme samme Christoffher udj diße forneffnde saager till det beste, och hielpe hannom att komme till enn goed ende der vdj, jnngennlunde haffuendis hannom mijstenckt ij nogen maade. Kiere Niels, haffuer jeg gierne sielff willde talidt med eder baade om dette och anditt denn thijd ij ware først neer kommen aff Norghe, menn nu kunde leilighedenn siig jcke saa begijffue. Menn dersom dett ware eder muligt och ij willde haffue denn wmage och besøge mig engang, førrind ij farer thiill Norge jgenn, daa saahe jeg det gandske gierne, oc vdj hues maade jeg kan nogen tijd giøre nogidt for eders skilld, som Eder thijl willge och guode kand vere, will jeg findis gandske och reedebon derthill.

Och will jeg hermed nu och altid haffue eder med alt hues ij well will denn alsommechtigste Gud befallendis.
Datum Vrannieborgh thend 18. julij anno [15]87.
Thyge Brahe
Egen hand.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and well-born man Niels Bild of Ravnholt, my especial good friend, this letter written in all friendship.

My wholly friendly greeting, now and ever, sent with Our Lord. Dear Niels Bild and especial good friend, after a friendly thanksgiving for all the kindness shown me — which I shall always be found willing and ready to requite according to my means — I cannot in friendship withhold from you that, inasmuch as Christoffer Pepler, my former bailiff in the fief of Nordfjord in Norway during the time I held it in fief from His Royal Majesty, has now come hither to me from Norway, he has thereby given me to understand how he still has certain monies outstanding in Nordfjord, both from the king's arrears and from his own goods, with which he had relieved [advanced credit to] the peasants — which they now withhold from him; and that in addition he has built some houses at his own expense upon a waste plot where no house stood before, nor [where] any field or meadow appertains. He has also given me to understand how the provost there in the fief, who was to keep the church's accounts, is too hard upon him, in that he [the provost] says that the churchwardens who built upon the churches are supposed to have set their work too dear and, against that, would enter the church's revenue too low [in the accounts], as I can understand it; and that Christoffer is supposed to make this good to the churches again, against the letters [receipts] which the churchwardens had given him, stating that he has received nothing of the church's revenue beyond his wages. And he reports further that he is also apprehensive that you have held him in suspicion, in that he is supposed to have written something to me about you — which I acknowledge he has not done; but rather that I, of my own good will, at the time when I asked him what debt was standing [outstanding] there in the fief, wrote to you, trusting in you as my good friend, that through your help and assistance he might the sooner come to his right and obtain his payment. Dear Niels Bild, my wholly friendly request to you is therefore still that you will do well, as I trust of you, and to further and advance the same Christoffer in these aforesaid matters to the best [outcome], and help him to come to a good end therein, in no wise having held him in suspicion in any manner. Dear Niels, I should gladly have spoken with you myself, both about this and other things, at the time when you had first come down from Norway, but now the occasion could not so present itself. But if it were possible for you, and you would take the trouble to visit me once, before you go to Norway again, then I should see it most gladly; and in whatever manner I can at any time do anything for your sake that may be to your pleasure and good, I shall be found wholly and readily disposed thereto.

And herewith I shall now and ever commend you, with all that you wish well, to Almighty God.
Given at Uraniborg, the 18th of July, the year [15]87.
Tyge Brahe.
In his own hand.

Apparatus

- Sender. Tycho Brahe (1546–1601), writing from Uraniborg on Hven. The subscription Thyge Brahe / Egen hand ("Tyge Brahe / In his own hand") marks the letter as an autograph; Egen hand = egen hånd, "[in his] own hand."
- Recipient. Niels Billd thijl Raunholtt = Niels Bild til Ravnholt, a Danish nobleman who held crown fiefs in Norway (the surviving MS is a later copy in Langebek's Diplomatarium, so the spelling "Billd/Bild" is the copyist's). Tycho addresses him as a fellow member of the nobility and as a friend, interceding on behalf of a former subordinate.
- Christoffer Pepler — Tycho's former fogit (bailiff/steward) in the Nordfjord fief; the letter is wholly a letter of recommendation/intercession on his behalf. fordoms = fordums, "former / of old."
- Place / fief. Norfiord lhenn ij Norge = the fief (len) of Nordfjord in western Norway. Tycho had held it ij forlening — "in fief / enfeoffment" — from the Crown (Kong. May. = Kongelig Majestæt, "His Royal Majesty," Frederik II); he had been granted the income of Nordfjord len in 1578 and held it until 1587, so this letter coincides with the close of that tenure.
- Document type / rubric. The opening line is the address (superscription) written on the outside/head of the letter, in the chancery majuscule convention, not a separate Latin rubric: "…dette breff gandske wenlig thillschreffuid" = "this letter written in all friendship." (Unlike the Frederik II compass-letter, there is no Latin ut sequitur register-rubric here; the whole document is Danish.)
- Legal / administrative substance (translated with care for negations). Three claims of Pepler's are reported, each preserved with its exact polarity:
1. He has skilld vdstaendis — "monies outstanding" (gæld/skyld udestående) — owed both from kongens restandtz ("the king's arrears," unpaid Crown dues) and from his own goods advanced to the peasants, which they now withhold (forhollder) from him.
2. The provost (prousten, ecclesiastical superintendent of the len), who keeps the church accounts, is for tong ("too hard / too heavy") on him: the dispute is whether the churchwardens (kiercke ombodsmend, church stewards) overcharged for building work and under-entered the church revenue, and whether Pepler must wederlegge ("make good / reimburse") the difference — against the breffue (receipts/letters) the churchwardens gave him stating he took nothing of the church revenue beyond his wages. The double negative force ("nothing… beyond his wages") is reproduced exactly.
3. Pepler fears Niels Bild suspects him of having written something against him to Tycho. Tycho's denial is exact and carries the legal weight of the letter: "huilked jeg kiendis att hand icke giortt haffuer" = "which I acknowledge he has not done" — i.e. Pepler did not write to Tycho about Bild; rather Tycho himself, aff min egenn goede wille ("of my own good will"), wrote to Bild after asking Pepler about debts outstanding in the fief. The closing plea "jnngennlunde haffuendis hannom mijstenckt ij nogen maade" = "in no wise having held him in suspicion in any manner" — three negation-bearing elements (ingenlunde "by no means," mistænkt "suspected," i nogen måde "in any manner") all retained.
- Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1587, via a later copyist). helßennd = hilsen ("greeting"); forsennd = forsendt ("sent"); redebon / reedebon = redebon ("ready, willing"); forschilde = forskylde / gengælde ("requite, deserve in return"); epthersom = eftersom ("inasmuch as"); tilkiende = til kende ("[given] to understand"); restandtz = restance ("arrears"); vndsett = undsat ("relieved, advanced [credit to]"); øde platz = øde plads ("waste / vacant plot"); agger heller eng = ager eller eng ("field or meadow"); prousten = provsten ("the provost"); ombodsmend = ombudsmænd ("stewards, agents"); wederlegge = vederlægge ("compensate, make good"); oppebaridt = oppebåret ("received, collected [revenue]"); befrøchttendis = befrygtende ("apprehensive, fearing"); mijßtenckt / mijstenckt = mistænkt ("suspected"); forseendes mig till eder = forseende mig til eder ("trusting / relying on you"); fordre och forfremme = fordre og fremme ("further and advance"); jnngennlunde = ingenlunde ("by no means"); wmage = umage ("trouble, effort"); leilighedenn = lejligheden ("the occasion"); alsommechtigste = allermægtigste ("Almighty"). Address formulae by the edition's convention: the letter substitutes a friendly noble-to-noble salutation for the royal Wor gunst tilforn.
- Calendar / place. Datum Vrannieborgh thend 18. julij anno [15]87 = "Given at Uraniborg, the 18th of July, the year [15]87" (O.S.; Denmark kept the Julian calendar). Datum "Given" kept by the edition's convention The header date agrees with the edition label (1587. 18. juli. Uraniborg); no discrepancy. The bracketed "15" in anno [15]87 is the editor's expansion of the copy's anno 87.
