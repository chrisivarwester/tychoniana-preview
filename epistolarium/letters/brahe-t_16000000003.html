The original

AD SAC:Æ CÆS:Æ MAIESTA:IS D:NI NOSTRI CLEMENTIS:I MANUS PROPRIAS HUMILLIMA SUPPLICATIO TYCHONIS BRAHE.

ALLERDURCHLAUCHTIGSTER, großmechtigster römischer kayser auch zu Hungern vndt Beheimb königh, allergnedigster herr. Auff Ewer Kay: May: allergnedigstenn vnd zu vnterschiedtlichen mahlen ergangenen befehlich kan ich gleichwohl meine besoldung, so mir nue lenger alß ain jahr restiret, biß dato noch nicht bekommenn, welchs mihr sehr beschwerlich vorfelt vnndt nit wohll mueglichen lenger außzuestehen. Bitte darumb Ewer Kay: May: ich allervnterthenigst, die geruhen allergnedigft zuuerordnen, daß mihr baide die restirende vndt kunfftige besoldung auß Ewer Key: May: herschafften alhie ihn Behemen (wo es dan Ewer Kay: May: allergnedigst gefallig) geraichet, vndt alle quatember richtig gemacht werden möge, auff das ich die guette zeit mit vergeblichen sollicitationibus nebenst großen schaden vndt bekummernußenn (wie bißhero leider geschen) nit lenger zuebringe.

Waß andere mangel betreffendt ihn den sachen, so von Ewer Kay: May: allergnedigst mir von anbegin vndt hernacher bewilliget sindt, aber noch nicht wiederfahren, hab ich dem herrn Baruitio ain kurz verzaichnuß davon vberliefert Ewer Kay: May: meinet wegen vnterthenigst vorzuetragen, vndt bin der genzlichen hoffnung, daß ich auff diß alles mit dem ehesten ein allergnedigste resolution bekommen werde, auff daß ich ainmahl aigentlich wißen möchte, wie ich alhie darahn wehre, vndt ob ich ainige faste vndt bestendige gelegenheit erlangen köntte, mein vorhabendes hohes studium vndt astronomisches wergk Ewer Röm: Kay: May: vnd dem reich zue sondern ruhm, nutz vnd ehren alhie vnterthenigst zuuohlzihen, oder vonnötten wehre andere bequemigkait darzue zue suchen. Ewer Röm: Kay: May: ihn allen vnterthenigstem gehorsamb zue dienen ich mich iederzeit ganz schuldig erkenne. Ewer Röm: Kay: May: allervnterthenigster gehorsambster diener

Tycho Brahe

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the own hands of His Sacred Imperial Majesty, our most clement Lord: the most humble supplication of Tycho Brahe.

Most serene, most mighty Roman Emperor, and King of Hungary and Bohemia, most gracious lord. Notwithstanding Your Imperial Majesty's most gracious command, issued at various times, I still cannot, to this date, obtain my salary, which has now been in arrears for more than a year — which falls very grievous to me, and is not well possible to endure longer. I therefore most humbly beg Your Imperial Majesty that you would most graciously deign to ordain that both the arrears and the future salary be furnished me out of Your Imperial Majesty's lordships here in Bohemia (where it then most graciously pleases Your Imperial Majesty), and be duly settled every quarter [Quatember], so that I may no longer spend the good time with vain solicitations, alongside great damage and troubles (as has hitherto, alas, happened).

As concerns other deficiencies in the matters which were most graciously granted me by Your Imperial Majesty from the beginning and afterward, but have not yet been fulfilled, I have delivered to Master Barvitius a short schedule thereof, to be submitted most humbly to Your Imperial Majesty on my behalf; and I have the entire hope that I shall, on all this, with the soonest, obtain a most gracious resolution — so that I might at last actually know how I stand here, and whether I could attain some firm and lasting situation to carry out here, most humbly, my intended high study and astronomical work, to Your Roman Imperial Majesty's and the realm's special renown, profit, and honour — or whether it were needful to seek other convenience for it. I at all times acknowledge myself entirely bound to serve Your Roman Imperial Majesty in all most humble obedience. Your Roman Imperial Majesty's most humble, most obedient servant,

Tycho Brahe,
with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Document type. A formal petition (Supplicatio) addressed "to the own hands" of Emperor Rudolf II — the Latin superscription Ad Sacrae Caesareae Maiestatis … manus proprias humillima supplicatio Tychonis Brahe. The witness survives in the imperial Familienakten at the Vienna Haus-, Hof- und Staatsarchiv.
  • The unpaid salary. The core grievance of Tycho's Bohemian years: his imperial salary, "more than a year in arrears," despite repeated imperial orders. He asks that arrears and future salary be paid from the imperial domains in Bohemia and settled every quarter (alle Quatember — the quarterly Ember-tide terms), to spare him the "vain solicitations, great damage, and troubles" of chasing the treasury. A candid testimony to the chronic dysfunction of the Rudolfine fisc.
  • Other unfulfilled promises. Tycho refers to a Verzeichnis (schedule/list) of further unmet promises which he has given to Barvitius (Johann Barvitius, the privy secretary; cf. brahe-t_15980626002, …16000507001) to lay before the Emperor; he hopes for a "resolution" so he may know "how he stands," and whether he can pursue his "high study and astronomical work" there — "or whether it were needful to seek other convenience" (a veiled threat to look elsewhere).
  • sollicitationibus / Quatember — Latin/technical terms kept and rendered (solicitations; the quarterly terms). manu pp. = manu propria.
  • Calendar / date. The petition is undated in the source (edition: "Benatky," year 1600, identifier …0000…). Internal content (the year-plus arrears; the Barvitius schedule) fits 1600, during the Benátky residence. Bohemia (imperial) = New Style; no day/month survives.
