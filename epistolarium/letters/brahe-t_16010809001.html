The original

DEM EHRSAMEN VNSERM LIEBEN GETREWEN ANDREÆ MEYERN, RENTMEISTERN, ZUR WISSMAR.

VON Gottes gnadenn Vlrich, hertzogk zu Mekelnburgk etc. Ehrsamer lieber getrewer. Was Eßke Bilde der 10000 tahler halbenn, damit Vnsere vnmündige vettern vnd pflegesohne, die jungen hertzogen zu Mekelnburgk etc., Tycho Brahen verhafft, an Vnseren cammerer Jochim von Stralendorffen abermals gelangen thuet, das hast du aus beyuerwartem seinem originalschreiben nach mehrer lenge zuersehenn.

Nun seind Wir der meynung, es werden die zehen tausend thaler sampt den zinsen, so gemelter Tycho Brahe haben soll, noch beysamen seinn, das sie auff begeren gegenn wiedereinandwortung der außgegebenen verschreibung alßbald außgezehlett werden konnen, wie Wir dan solches newlicher zeit, jnmaßen du aus inligender copey zuuernehmen, von Vns geschriebenn. Weil aber Eßke Bylde vnter andern in seinem schreiben gedenket, das sich gestern abend einer bey jhm zu Rostogk angegeben, so jhm von deinentwegen angemeldet hette, das du obgedachtenn Tycho Brahenn 5000 thaler hinauff nach Prage vbermachet, vnd Wir nicht wißenn können, was es hirumb vor eine gelegenheit hat, alß begeren Wir demnach an dich hiemit gnediglich, das du Vns vngeseumet bey zeigern hieuon eigentlichen vnd gewißen bericht zuschreibest, dan weill Eßke Bilde selbst vmb des geldes willen aus Dennemarken gekommen ist vnd zu Rostogk liegt, konnen die sachen gar keinen verzugk leiden. Vnnd haltenn Wirs auch dafür, weil du Vns das geringste dauon nicht angezeigett, das du das gelt hinauff nach Praag soltest vermachet haben, welches dan auch Vnsers bedünckens vor wiedererlangung der außgegebenen verschreibung nicht fuglich hat geschehen können, es werde die gantze summa noch bey einander seinn, damit es volnkomlich, sintemal es Eßke Bilde auch volnkomlich haben will, außgezehlet werden könne vnd müge. Vnd wirst du zeigern mit der begertenn andwort desto zeitiger wiederumb an Vns abfertigen, damit Wir dieselbe noch vor vnserm auffzuge von hinnen (so ob Gott wil am zukünfftigen dingestage morgen geschehen wirdt) gewiße mügen vberkommen. An dem erstatest du Vnsere gnedige zuuerleßige meynungk.

Datum Güstrow den 9. augustj an:o 1601.

[Postscript — restored from Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, pp. 231–232; dropped by the working transcription. See Apparatus.]
[In schedula:] Post Scripta. Auch ehrsamer lieber getrewer, weil Eßke Bilde itzo zur stedte ist, vnd auf Vnserer vnmundigen vettern vnd pflegesohne vnkosten auf das geldt wartet, vnd Wir Vns erjnnern, das das geldt alhie jn Vnsere renterey verhanden ist, so begheren vnd beuehlen Wir dir demnach hiemit gnediglich, das du dich angelicus briefs erhebest vnd alhie noch vor Vnserm aufzuge bej Vns anlangest, damit Wir nicht allein von dir selbst rechten bericht erfharen, sondern auch die sachen desto ehe jhre richtige maße erlangen, vnd besorgender schade vnd vngelegenheit verhuetet pleiben muge.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable, our dear and faithful Andreas Meier, rent-master, at Wismar.

By the grace of God, Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg etc. Honourable, dear, faithful one: what Eske Bille, concerning the 10,000 thalers with which our under-age cousins and foster-sons, the young Dukes of Mecklenburg etc., stand bound to Tycho Brahe, once again addresses to our chamberlain Joachim von Stralendorf — that you may see at fuller length from his original letter, enclosed herewith.

Now, we are of the opinion that the ten thousand thalers, together with the interest which the said Tycho Brahe is to have, are still on hand together, so that, upon demand, against the surrender of the bond that was issued, they may at once be paid out — as indeed we wrote to you about this recently (as you may gather from the enclosed copy). But since Eske Bille, among other things in his letter, mentions that yesterday evening someone presented himself to him at Rostock, who had reported to him on your behalf that you had remitted 5,000 thalers up to Prague to the aforesaid Tycho Brahe — and we cannot know what the circumstance of this may be — we therefore graciously desire of you herewith that you write us, without delay, by the bearer, an actual and certain report of this; for since Eske Bille has himself come from Denmark for the sake of the money, and lies at Rostock, the matter can suffer no delay whatever. And we also hold it [to be so] — since you have indicated to us not the least of it, that you should have remitted the money up to Prague, which moreover, in our judgement, could not properly have been done before the recovery of the issued bond — that the whole sum is still together, so that it may and can be paid out in full (since Eske Bille, too, wants it in full). And you will dispatch the bearer back to us with the desired answer all the more promptly, so that we may certainly receive it before our departure from here (which, God willing, will take place next Tuesday, tomorrow). In this you fulfil our gracious and trusty intent.

Given at Güstrow, the 9th of August, in the year 1601.

[Postscript:] [On a slip:] Post Scripta. Also, honourable, dear, faithful one: since Eske Bille is now on the spot, and waits for the money at the cost of our under-age cousins and foster-sons, and We recall that the money is here on hand in our treasury, We therefore desire and command you herewith, graciously, that upon [receipt of] the present letter you set out and present yourself to Us here before our departure, so that We may not only learn the true report from you yourself, but also that the matter the sooner attain its right measure, and the apprehended damage and inconvenience remain forestalled.

Apparatus

  • Direction & sender. A ducal letter, from Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg(-Güstrow) (1527–1603), to his own treasury official — the third surviving document of the Tycho/Mecklenburg debt thread, written from the ducal residence at Güstrow. (The edition labels the sender Ulrich 3.) It is included in the Tychonic correspondence because it concerns, and quotes, the recovery of Tycho's loan.
  • Recipient. Andreas Meier, ducal Mecklenburg rent-master at Wismar — the same officer pressed in Tycho's own letters of 27 May and 18 July 1601 (brahe-t_16010527001, brahe-t_16010718001). The Duke, his master, now demands an accounting.
  • The transaction. The 10,000-thaler loan owed Tycho by the young (under-age) Dukes of Mecklenburg, the Duke's vmündige Vettern und Pflegesöhne ("under-age cousins and foster-sons") for whom Ulrich stood guardian. The Duke believes the full sum is in hand, to be paid against the surrender of the bond (Verschreibung) — but Eske Bille has reported a rumour that Meier already remitted 5,000 thalers to Prague, and the Duke demands urgent clarification: a partial remittance before the bond's recovery would be irregular.
  • Persons named.
  • Eßke Bilde = Eske Bille, the Danish nobleman and Tycho's kinsman-agent, who has "come from Denmark for the sake of the money" and waits at Rostock (cf. brahe-t_16010527001).
  • unser Kämmerer Joachim von Stralendorf = Joachim von Stralendorf, the Duke's chamberlain, to whom Eske Bille's latest letter was addressed.
  • Tycho Brahe, the creditor; the junge Herzöge zu Mecklenburg, the under-age debtors.
  • Places. Güstrow (ducal residence, place of issue); Wismar (Meier's seat); Rostock (where Eske Bille waits); Prag = Prague (where Tycho is, and whither the disputed 5,000 thalers were allegedly sent).
  • Register & formulae. The princely first-person plural (Von Gottes Gnaden … Wir … Unsere) and the subject-address unser lieber getreuer ("our dear and faithful one") mark the sovereign-to-servant register, distinct from the burgher-courteous tone of Tycho's own letters to Meier. Datum → "Given." The internal deadline am zukünftigen Dienstag, morgen = "next Tuesday, tomorrow" (the Duke's imminent departure from Güstrow).
  • Calendar. Dateline den 9. Augusti a:o 1601; the edition gives no style marker. As a Lutheran north-German chancery document, Mecklenburg in 1601 was still on the Julian (Old Style) calendar (Protestant Germany kept it until 1700) — to be contrasted with the explicit stylo novo of Tycho's Prague letters in the same thread. The date is reproduced as written; no conversion is performed.
  • §1 Dreyer collation. "[In schedula:] Post Scripta" enclosed slip (Duke Ulrich commands Meier to come at once, the money being in the ducal treasury, Eske Bille waiting), restored from Dreyer XIV pp.231–232 (dropped by the working transcription).
