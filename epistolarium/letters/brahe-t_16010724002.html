The original

DEM ERBARN WOLGEACHTEN VNDT FURNHEMEN ANDRESS MEYER, DIE JUNGE HERTZOGEN ZU MEKELBURG, J: F: G: RENTEMEISTER, MEINEM INSONDERS GUNSTIGEN GUEDTEN FREUNDE F[REUNDLICH] G[ESCHRIEBEN].

MEINEN freundtlichen gruß zuuorn. Freundtlicher lieber rentemester. Kan ich euch freundtlich nicht vorhaldten, daß mihr heudt dato ein schreiben ist behendiget worden, so mihr Berendt Ferste, burger zum Kil, zugesendet hadtt, vndt dieweiln dar kein namen eingeschrieben war, hadt mich doch der briefzeuger berichtet, daß daßelbige brieff von euch keme, vnndt waß dießelbige anlanget, daß ich soldte habenn zu Dobbran, in deß f: g: vorgemack, anlangen laßenn vmme 2000 thaler vnnd derselbige, so dermasßen meinendtwegen hadt angelanget, kein quitung nicht gebragt, so weiß ich nicht von sölche quitung odder 2000 thaler zusagen, habe auch darumme nicht anlangen lasßen, söndern ihr wisßet euch wol zuerinneren, daß ich vor lengst die 10000 thaler, worauff ich ein furstliche obligation habe, habe auffkundigen lasßenn vnndt auff die bestimbte zeidt, alß nemlich auff ostern, mein diener midt des f: obligation hinaußgesenndt daß geldt zu entfhangen, vnndt weiln erß nicht bekhommen hadtt, vndt ihr mich zugeschriebenn, daß eß auff Johannj soldte erlegtt werden, alß habe ich zum vberfluß widderumme mein diener abgefertigtt midt deß f: obligation, vnnd hadt er itzliche thage fur vndt nach Johannj zu Rostock in Joachim Lorman sein hauß gewardtet, vndt weiln daß geldt in d: Lucas Beckmester sein huß zuerlegen auffgekundigt war, hadt erß dem h: doctor wisßen laßen, wohr er zu finden war, ob dar wen kheme, der solche summa außzelen woldte, seindt doch keine nicht gekommen, hadt sich derhalben widderumme nach heim begeben, vnnd weiln ihr schrieben, daß die summa fur Johannj al beisamen ist gewesen, vndt nuhr die entfhenger wardten, so wil ich noch zum dritten mall selbst pershonlich hinaußkommen, vnndt ihr werdet mihr nicht vordenckenn, wo ferne die summa neben all jnterres vnd skaden nicht erlegt wirdt, daß ich dan andre mittel gebrauchen werde. Habe auch diß zum f[reun]dtlich andtwordt nicht vorhaldten khonn[en]. Vnndt thue vnß beiderzeidtz in den schudtz deß Almegtigen empfhelen.

Datum Kopennhagen den 24. julij anno 1601.
Eske Bille

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable, well-respected and distinguished Andreas Meier, rent-master to the young Dukes of Mecklenburg, Their Princely Graces, my especially favourable good friend. Written in friendship.

My friendly greeting beforehand. Friendly, dear rent-master: I cannot, in friendship, withhold from you that today a letter has been handed to me, which Berend Ferste, citizen of Kiel, has sent me; and since no name was written within it, the bearer nonetheless informed me that the same letter came from you. As to what it concerns — that I am supposed to have had application made at Doberan, in the antechamber of His Princely Grace, for 2,000 thalers, and that the man who thus applied on my behalf brought no receipt — I know nothing to say of any such receipt or of 2,000 thalers, nor have I had any application made for it. Rather, you will well remember that I long ago had the 10,000 thalers, on which I hold a princely bond, called in, and at the appointed time — namely at Easter — sent out my servant with the Prince's bond to receive the money; and since he did not get it, and you wrote to me that it should be paid at St. John's [Day], I again, for good measure, dispatched my servant with the Prince's bond, and he waited several days, before and after St. John's, at Rostock in Joachim Lorman's house. And since the money had been appointed to be paid out at Dr. Lucas Beckmeister's house, he let the Lord Doctor know where he was to be found, [to see] whether anyone would come there who would pay out the sum; but none came. He therefore betook himself home again. And since you wrote that the sum was all together before St. John's, and that only the receivers were awaited, I will yet a third time come out in person myself; and you will not think ill of me if — should the sum, together with all interest and damages, not be paid — I then resort to other means. This too I could not withhold, by way of a friendly answer. And I commend us both to the protection of the Almighty.

Given at Copenhagen, the 24th of July, in the year 1601.
Eske Bille

Apparatus

  • Direction & sender. Eske Bille, the Danish nobleman and Tycho Brahe's kinsman-agent, writing in his own person from Copenhagen to the ducal rent-master — the most circumstantial document of the Mecklenburg-debt thread, recounting from the creditor's side what Tycho's letters (brahe-t_16010527001, brahe-t_16010718001) and Duke Ulrich's (brahe-t_16010809001) describe from theirs.
  • Recipient. Andreas Meier, rent-master to the young Dukes of Mecklenburg (J. F. G. = Ihrer Fürstlichen Gnaden, "Their Princely Graces"), at Wismar.
  • The narrative (fills the gaps in the thread).
  • A garbled report had reached Eske Bille via Berend Ferste, a citizen of Kiel, in an unsigned letter — that Bille was to collect 2,000 thalers at Doberan (the Mecklenburg ducal seat at Bad Doberan), in the prince's antechamber, but without a receipt. Bille disavows all knowledge of this.
  • He restates the real claim: the 10,000-thaler princely bond (fürstliche Obligation) was called in long ago; his servant was sent with the bond to collect at Easter, then again "for good measure" around St. John's Day (24 June), waiting at Rostock in Joachim Lorman's house.
  • Payment had been appointed at the house of Dr. Lukas Beckmeister at Rostock; the servant notified him but no payer appeared, and he went home empty-handed.
  • Bille now threatens to come a third time in person, and failing full payment of principal + interest + damages, to "resort to other means."
  • Persons named. Eske Bille (sender); Berend Ferste, Bürger zu Kiel (the source of the false 2,000-thaler report); Joachim Lorman (Rostock householder where the servant lodged); Dr. Lukas Beckmeister (the Rostock doctor at whose house payment was to be made); die jungen Herzöge zu Mecklenburg (the under-age debtors).
  • Places. Kopenhagen (place of issue); Kiel; Doberan (Bad Doberan); Rostock.
  • Language note. The hand is Danish-influenced chancery German: Danish/Low-German orthography and forms surface throughout — skaden for Schaden ("damages"), huß/hauß for Haus, wohr for wo, midt for mit, zuuorn, behendiget, vorhaldten, itzliche, pershonlich. Rendered straight; the spelling is the writer's Dano-German chancery register, not error.
  • Formulae. Burgher-courteous opening meinen freundlichen Gruß zuvor → "my friendly greeting beforehand"; address tag F. G. = freundlich geschrieben ("written in friendship"); Datum → "Given."
  • Calendar. Dateline den 24. Julij anno 1601 at Copenhagen; Denmark kept the Julian (Old Style) calendar until 1700, so this is Old Style — to be contrasted with the New-Style Prague datelines of Tycho's own letters in the same affair (the St. Johannis and Ostern terms are likewise Old Style here). Reproduced, not converted.
