The original

TYCHONI BRAHE EX SORORE NEPOTI CHARISSIMO.
Is, quo vita, salus, via, statque redemptio nostra,
Corporis atque animæ det tibi fausta, precor.

IAM iam literas tuas, Nepos e sorore charissime, de negotijs tuis expediendis apud fabrum ferrarium meum, per famulum tuum accepi, petitionibus tuis quam libentissime satisfacturus, nec officium ullum erga te, quem semper intime dilexi, pollicitationis in me claudier sinam. Armillas, quas, si recte memini, Ptolemaicas appellas, et pro quibus ornandis mihi cupreos circulos misisti, artifici hîc in moletrina ex tuo præscripto ornandos commisi, etiam frementibus et reluctantibus alijs, qui aquam pro conficienda farina nimium exhauriri prætendunt. Sed vereor, ne is iuxta tuam mentem Armillas istas rite parare et levigare norit. Verum, ubi ipsemet me accesseris, rectius de his dispicies. Has scribens, literas Regias accepi, ut ad suam Maiestatem sine mora veniam. Ubi autem a Regia Maiestate liberabor, Lares Huenenses seu potius Uraniburgicos, adeoque te ipsum invisam, tuis Musis thura libando.

His paucis quam feliciter vale.
Datæ Herrisuadi 5 Nonis Maij Anno 1577.

STENO BILDE.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To Tycho Brahe, his most beloved nephew through a sister.
May He in whom are our life, our salvation, our way, and our redemption
grant you what is favourable for body and soul — this I pray.

Just now, my most beloved nephew through a sister, I have received your letter, by the hand of your servant, about getting your business dispatched at my blacksmith's; and I shall meet your requests most willingly, nor shall I let any duty of my promise toward you — whom I have always loved most dearly — be closed off within me. The armillae which, if I remember rightly, you call Ptolemaic, and for the fitting-out of which you sent me copper circles, I have entrusted to the craftsman here at the mill to be fitted out according to your specification — even though others grumble and resist, alleging that too much water is drawn off [thereby], [water] needed for making the flour. But I fear he may not know how to prepare and burnish those armillae duly, in keeping with your intention. Yet when you come to me in person, you will look into these matters more rightly. As I write this, I have received a letter from the King [literae Regiae], [bidding me] come to His Majesty without delay. But when I am freed from the Royal Majesty, I shall visit my household gods of Hven — or rather of Uraniborg — and indeed you yourself, offering incense to your Muses.

With these few words, may you fare most happily.
Given at Herrevad, the 5th [day, reckoned to] the Nones of May, the year 1577.

Steen Bille.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Steno Bilde = Steen (Sten) Bille (1527–1586), Tycho's maternal uncle and the lord of Herrevad Abbey (Herrevadskloster) in Scania. The salutation ex sorore nepoti ("nephew through a sister") fixes the relationship: Steen's sister Beate Bille was Tycho's mother. Steen Bille was the figure who drew the young Tycho into alchemy and metallurgy, on whose Herrevad estate Tycho kept his first observatory and observed the 1572 nova; here, a year after the grant of Hven (1576) and during the building of Uraniborg, he is acting as Tycho's local agent for instrument-fitting.
  • Recipient / place. Tychoni Brahe = Tycho Brahe; Herrisuadi = Herrevad (Herrevadskloster), the former Cistercian abbey in Scania held by Steen Bille. Lares Huenenses seu … Uraniburgicos = "my household gods of Hven — or rather of Uraniborg": Lares (the Roman household deities) stands metonymically for "home/household"; Bille corrects himself to name the rising house, Uraniborg, on the island of Hven.
  • Opening couplet. The two verse lines are an elegiac distich (hexameter + pentameter) of pious well-wishing; kept as verse by this chapter's convention Is, quo … statque redemptio nostra paraphrases Christ as "life, salvation, way, redemption" (cf. John 14:6, via, veritas, vita).
  • Instruments. Armillas … Ptolemaicas = armillae, here specified as "Ptolemaic" — i.e. armillary rings of the Ptolemaic (astrolabon / organon parallacticum) type Tycho was then constructing; rendered "armillae" by this chapter's convention cupreos circulos = "copper circles," the raw graduated rings sent for finishing; ornandos … parare et levigare = "to be fitted out … to prepare and burnish," the dressing and polishing of the metal rings.
  • faber ferrarius / artifex … in moletrina — Bille's "blacksmith" and the "craftsman … at the mill" (moletrina = a water-mill / grinding-mill). The complaint that fitting the instruments "draws off too much water needed for making the flour" indicates the metalwork was being done at the abbey's water-mill, in competition with the milling of grain — a vivid material detail of where early Tychonic instruments were physically made.
  • literae Regiae … ad suam Maiestatem — "a royal letter … to His Majesty": Frederik II of Denmark (r. 1559–88), summoning Bille to court. a Regia Maiestate liberabor = "when I am freed from the Royal Majesty [i.e. from this royal summons / duty]."
  • tuis Musis thura libando — "offering incense to your Muses," a humanist flourish for "paying devout respects to your studies (at Uraniborg)"; cf. the Tychonic sign-off idiom sacris Uraniae lita. Kept as metaphor.
  • Latinity. claudier — archaic/poetic passive infinitive (= claudi), "to be closed off"; rendered without flattening the deliberate archaism. dispicies (from dispicio) = "you will look into / discern," not despicies "you will despise."
  • Dateline crux (date as written vs. catalogue label). The dateline reads "5 Nonis Maij Anno 1577" — literally "5 [to the] Nones of May." The Nones of May fall on 7 May; reckoned in the Roman inclusive manner, a. d. V Nonas Maias (7, 6, 5, 4, 3) = 3 May. The edition label "1577. 3. maj" is therefore consistent with the dateline once the Roman count is resolved, and the header gives 3 May (O.S.). The printed form "5 Nonis" (Arabic numeral + ablative Nonis rather than the regular V Nonas) is a non-classical chancery/transcription rendering of a. d. V Nonas Maias; it is preserved verbatim in the Source block and not normalised. No date is asserted in the header beyond what this reckoning substantiates. (Denmark used the Julian calendar [O.S.] throughout, by this chapter's convention)
  • MS witnesses. A = Vienna, ÖNB cod. Vindob. lat. 10686.7; B = Vienna, ÖNB cod. Vindob. lat. 10686.66 (shelfmarks as given in the source header).
