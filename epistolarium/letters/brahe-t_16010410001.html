The original

DEM DURCHLAUCHTIGSTEN HOCHGEBORNEN FURSTEN VND HERRN, HERRN VLDARICH, HERTZOGEN ZU MECHELLENBURGK, FURSTEN ZU WENDEN, GRAFFEN ZU SCHWERIN, DER LANDE ROSTOCH VND STARGARTTEN HERRN, MEINEM GNEDIGSTEN FURSTEN VNDT HERRN.

DURCHLAUCHTIGSTER hochgeborner furst, gnedigster herr. Ewer Furstl: Gnaden sein meine vnterthenigste, gantzwillige dienste iederzeit hochstes fleißes beuor. Vndt seitenmahl ich itzo ainen aigenen potten inn Dennemarck abgehen laße, hab E: Furstl: Gnaden ich mit dießem meinem geringen schreiben in vnterthenigkait zu ersuchen vnd deroselben vor die vielfaltige mir bezaigte gut vndt wohlthatten zu dancken nitt vmbgehen könnenn.

Waß dan meinem itzigen zustandt betrifft, werden E: Furstl: Gnaden denselben vnlangst auß mainem vorigen schreiben vmbstendiglichen vernomen habenn, darumb hie ferner zu wiederholen vnuonnötten. Dan ihre rom: key: may:, mein allergnedigster herr, nochmahlß (wie vor, ie lenger ie mer) mir mit allen keyßerlichen gnaden (Gott lob) gewogen, vndt haben erst neulich deß gewesen reichs vice-cancellarij Jacobj Curtij l. m. ansehenliche vndt statliche behausung, so ahn ainen hohen ortt eußerst an der königlichen hauptstadt Prag gelegen, vmb zehentausendt thaller zu meinem vnd deß studij astronomicj nutz mildtlichen kauffen laßen, wo alle meine jnstrumenta so ain zeitlang in jhr kay: maytt: lustgarten nahe beim schloß gestanden, itzo conuenientj modo disponirt, auch ain laboratorium pro spagyricis exercitijs angerichtet wirt.

Ferner kan E: Furstl: Gnaden ich vnterthenigst nit verhalten (wie dieselben auch noch gnedigst werden wißen zu entsinen), daß vor ainem halben jahr die summa von zehentausendt reichsthallern, so der jungen herschafft zu Mechlenburgk von mir gutwillig vorgestrecht, deroselben rentmeistern Andreß Meiern durch meinen vettern vndt volmechtigen derer örter Eske Bilden auffkundigen laßen, dan ich dießer örter landtgütter eingekaufft, zu welcher entrichtung ich darzue vervrsacht worden, worein dan E: Furstl: Gnaden damahlß (immaßen sölliches der pilligkeit gemeß) sich gantz gnedigst vndt willfharlich erzaigt, jch auch alhie die leutte darauff vertröstet habe. Anietzo aber werde ich von herrn doctore Gödellman, welchem ich die zehentausendt thaller hauptsumma sampt dem interesse meinentwegen einzuenehmen volnkomblich commission gegeben, glaubwürdig berichtet, daß obgedachter rentmeister nitt allein in der summen, sondern auch deß schon verschienen interesse erstattung weigerlich befunden werde vndt andere große außgaben furwende.

Dieweiln dan E: Furstl: Gnaden alß hochstgedachter jungen herschafft vormund darin gnedichst gewilfharet, sollichs auch ann ihme selbs pillich vndt ich andern leutten darauff zuegesagt, alß gelanget an E: Furstl: Gnaden mein vntherthenigste gantz fleißige pitt, sie wollen nit geschehen laßen, daß meine wort zue nichte gemacht vndt ich dadurch an meinem biß dato wohllhergebrachten nahmen vnd glimff abbruch erleiden, sondern mehrgedachte summa vndt interesse furderlichst, wofern sölliches vber hoffnung noch nitt geschehen, anhero vberkommen muege. Sölliches vmb E. Furstl. Gnaden (wozue dan dero gnedigsten thats ich mich vnterthenig getroste) eußerste mueglicheit gehorsambst zuverdienen, erkenne ich mich stetz schuldig vnd geflißen.

Thue hiemit E: Furstl: Gnaden sampt dero vielgeliebten gemahlen zu glucklicher langwehrender regierung vndt zue deren ferneren furstlichen gnaden mich vnterthenigst vndt getreulich entpfhelen.

Datum Prag, den 10 ap. anno 1601.
Ewer Furstlichen Gnaden vnterthenigster gantz williger
Tycho Brahe
Eigenhn.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most serene, high-born Prince and Lord, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard, my most gracious Prince and Lord.

Most serene, high-born Prince, most gracious Lord. To Your Princely Grace [be] my most humble, wholly willing services at all times, with the utmost diligence, beforehand. And since I am now dispatching a courier of my own into Denmark, I could not pass by approaching Your Princely Grace in humility with this my poor letter, and thanking the same for the manifold kindness and benefits shown me.

As concerns my present state, Your Princely Grace will have learned it in detail, not long ago, from my previous letter, so it is needless to repeat it further here. For His Roman Imperial Majesty, my most gracious lord, is again — as before, the longer the more — favourably disposed toward me with all imperial graces (God be praised); and has but recently graciously had bought, for 10,000 thalers, for my use and that of astronomical study, the imposing and stately house of the late imperial Vice-Chancellor Jacob Curtius — situated at a high place, on the outermost [edge] of the royal capital city of Prague — where all my instruments, which for a while stood in His Imperial Majesty's pleasure-garden near the castle, are now being arranged in a convenient manner, and a laboratory for spagyric exercises is also being set up.

Further, I cannot most humbly conceal from Your Princely Grace (as the same will also still graciously recall) that half a year ago I had the sum of 10,000 Reichsthalers — which was willingly advanced by me to the young lordship of Mecklenburg — given notice on to their rent-master Andreas Meier, through my cousin and plenipotentiary in those parts, Eske Bille; for I have bought landed estates in these parts, for the payment of which I was thereby occasioned [to need it]. Wherein Your Princely Grace then (as is in accordance with fairness) showed yourself quite graciously and compliantly, and I have also reassured the people here on that [basis]. But now I am credibly informed by Lord Doctor Gödelmann — to whom I have given full commission to receive the 10,000-thaler principal together with the interest on my behalf — that the aforesaid rent-master is found refusing not only the sum, but also the repayment of the already-elapsed interest, and pleads other great expenditures.

Since, then, Your Princely Grace — as guardian of the aforesaid young lordship — graciously complied therein, and since it is in itself fair, and I have promised it to other people: there comes to Your Princely Grace my most humble, wholly diligent request, that you would not let it happen that my word be brought to naught, and that I thereby suffer damage to my hitherto well-established name and good repute; but that the oft-mentioned sum and interest may, with all speed — if (against hope) it has not yet happened — be conveyed hither. This, toward Your Princely Grace (to whose most gracious deed I humbly trust), I acknowledge myself ever bound and zealous to deserve, to the utmost of my ability, obediently.

I herewith most humbly and faithfully commend Your Princely Grace, together with your much-beloved consort, to a happy, long-lasting reign, and [commend] myself to your further princely graces.

Given at Prague, the 10th of April, the year 1601.
Your Princely Grace's most humble, wholly willing
Tycho Brahe,
[his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603), full titulature reproduced (as in brahe-t_15880815001, brahe-t_16010604001). The MS is the original dispatched letter in the Schwerin Debita passiva principum ("passive debts of the princes") file.
  • Position in the debt-thread. This 10 April 1601 appeal to the Duke precedes the more pressing one of 4 June 1601 (brahe-t_16010604001) and follows the agents'-level letters (Tycho→Meier 15 Nov 1600; Tycho→Bording; the Eske Bille letter). It conveys the same core: the 10,000-thaler principal lent to the junge Herrschaft (Duke Johann's sons, of whom Ulrich is guardian) was called in half a year ago, via Eske Bille, but rent-master Meier now refuses both principal and elapsed interest, pleading other expenditures; Tycho asks Ulrich to compel payment lest his "word be brought to naught" and his "name and good repute" suffer.
  • New, concrete motive — Tycho's Bohemian estate purchase. Tycho here states plainly why he needs the principal back: "I have bought landed estates in these parts" (dieser Örter Landgüter eingekauft), for whose payment he requires the money — a notable datum on Tycho's late attempt to root himself in Bohemia with land, alongside the imperial house.
  • The Curtius house (cross-ref). Repeats the description of the "new imperial Uraniborg": Rudolf II bought the late Vice-Chancellor Jacob Curtius's house "for 10,000 thalers … at a high place on the outermost edge of Prague," where the instruments (lately in the imperial pleasure-garden near the castle) are being installed and a spagyric laboratory built — consonant with brahe-t_16010415002 (to Sturtz) and brahe-t_16010412001 (to Bording). (Note the coincidence of figures: the Curtius house cost 10,000 thalers, the same nominal sum as the Mecklenburg principal.)
  • Persons named. Duke Ulrich III; Jacob Curtius (the late Vice-Chancellor); Andreas Meier (Mecklenburg rent-master); Eske Bille ("my cousin and plenipotentiary"); Johann Georg Gödelmann (Tycho's commissioned collector); the junge Herrschaft (Duke Johann's sons); Rudolf II; Ulrich's consort (Anna of Pomerania-Wolgast — not named in the text).
  • Calendar. Dateline Prag, den 10 ap. anno 1601; New Style (Prague). Header agrees with the label.
  • Correction. German: 'commisson' corrected to 'commission', per Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, p.219 (translation unaffected — already correctly rendered 'full commission').
