The original

AN TYCHO BRAHEN ZU PRAAG.

Vlrich etc.

VNSERN gonstigen grueß zuuor. Ehrnuester lieber besonder, Wir haben ewer schreiben empfangen vnd was jhr wegen richtiger erlegung der 10000 thaler, damit euch Vnsere vnmundige vettern vnd pflegesohne, die junge hertzogen zu Meckelnburgk etc., verhafft, an Vns gelanget, daraus vernommen. Nun ist nicht ohnedas gegen negst verschienen ostern diejennigen bej denen hochermelter Vnser pflegesohne rentmeister Andr: M[eyer] ewerntsowol auch anderer mehr leute halber geldt besprochen, welche jhm auch darauff gewisse vertrostung gethan, einen bloßen geschlagen. Wir haben aber dagegen in andere wege die versehung gethan, das man baldt hernacher die summa, so jhr haben sollet, zu wege gebracht, welche summa auch bereits vor negst vergangen Johannis baptistae alhie zur stedte gelegen, wie dan auch solch geldt hirselbst noch liegt, vnd hette man es, wan nur dasieder darumb anfurderung geschehen where, gegen wiedereinandtwortung der außgegebenen verschreibung wol mechtig werden konnen. Vnd mugen Wir euch hieneben nicht bergen, das Eßke Bilde vor Johannis einen seiner diener zu Vns gen Dobbran geschicket, vnd sich alda erkundigen laßen, ob das geldt verhanden where, deme Wir wieder anzeigen laßen, weil Wir alle stunde des rentmeisters ankunfft mit dem gelde vermütendt wheren, das er auff Johannis bej Vns wiederumb ansuchens vnd alßdan des geldes gewertig sein solle, es hat sich aber derselbe so weinig auf Johannis alse auch seidt der zeit bej Vns nicht angegeben; where es aber geschehen vnd er hette die außgegebene Verschreibung mit sich gebracht, solte auch gestracks die außzehlung darauff erfolget sein, vnd konnet jhr dahero ewers geldes auch nochmaln wol mechtig werden. Welchs Wir euch hinwieder zu gnediger erklerung nicht wollen verhalten, vnd Wir seind euch mit gnaden geneigt.

Datum Güstrow den 24. julij a:o 1601.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To Tycho Brahe at Prague.

Ulrich, etc.

Our favourable greeting beforehand. Honourable, dear, and especial [friend]: We have received your letter, and have understood from it what you have conveyed to Us regarding the proper payment of the 10,000 thaler with which Our minor cousins and foster-sons, the young Dukes of Mecklenburg etc., are indebted to you. Now, it is not otherwise [than this]: toward the recently-past Easter, those with whom the highly-mentioned rent-master of Our foster-sons, Andreas M[eyer], had bespoken money — on your account, as also for the sake of several other people — and who had given him a certain assurance thereupon, came up empty [struck a blank]. But We, on the other hand, made provision by other means, so that soon afterward the sum which you are to have was brought about — which sum also already lay here in the town [ready] before the recently-past [feast of] John the Baptist [24 June], as indeed such money still lies here; and, had only the demand for it been made here, it could well have been obtained against the return of the issued bond. And We cannot conceal from you herewith that Eske Bille, before [the feast of] John, sent one of his servants to Us at Doberan, and had inquiry made there whether the money was on hand; to whom We had it indicated [in reply] that — since We were every hour expecting the rent-master's arrival with the money — he should, at [the feast of] John, apply to Us again, and then be in waiting for the money; but the same [rent-master] presented himself to Us as little at John['s feast] as he has since that time. But had it happened, and had he brought the issued bond with him, the payment thereon should straightway have followed; and you can therefore still well obtain your money. Which We did not wish to withhold from you, in turn, for [your] gracious information; and We are graciously inclined toward you.

Given Güstrow, the 24th of July, in the year 1601.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603), regent and guardian of the young Dukes of Mecklenburg (here Unsere unmündige Vettern und Pflegesöhne, die jungen Herzöge zu Mecklenburg — his minor cousins and wards).
  • The 10,000-thaler debt. The capital sum of Tycho's Mecklenburg investment: the young Dukes "are indebted to you in 10,000 thaler." The interest (200 reichsthaler) and a 600-thaler instalment appear in the related Mecklenburg documents (the quittance brahe-t_15990110001 to rent-master Andreas Meyer, and Bording's letter brahe-t_16000919001). The Duke explains a payment delay — the original creditors "struck a blank" at Easter; the money has lain ready at Güstrow since before St John's (24 June); but Tycho's agents must present the bond (Verschreibung) to collect.
  • Eske Bille = a member of the Bille family (Tycho's mother's kin), acting as Tycho's agent: he sent a servant to inquire at Doberan whether the money was ready.
  • One of the last letters. Dated 24 July 1601, exactly three months before Tycho's death (24 October 1601); the witness survives, fittingly, in the Schwerin archive series Debita passiva principum ("the princes' liabilities"). The doubled Datum Datum is a scribal slip in the witness, left as printed.
  • Calendar. Güstrow den 24. julij a:o 1601 = 24 July 1601; Mecklenburg (Lutheran) = Old Style. Header agrees with the label.
  • Correction. Restored the sender tag 'Vlrich etc.' ('Ulrich, etc.') beneath the address heading, present in Dreyer (TBOO XIV p.227) but absent from the working transcription; and removed a duplicated 'Datum' in the closing dateline (p.228).
