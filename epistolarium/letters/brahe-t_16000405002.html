The original

IOHANNES KEPLER AD TYCHONEM BRAHE.

ICH Μ. IOHAN. KEPLER, Einer Er: Landt in Steier Mathematicus, bekhenne, demnach Ich mit gnediger Erlaubung wolermelten landtschafft, ein reise in Beheim furgenommen, fürnemlich den Edlen vnd Gestrengen Herrn TYCHO BRAHE, Herrn auf Knudtstrup vnd Vraniburg, wegen Dero Gest. ansehnlicher vnd weitberühempten Astronomischen Instrumente vnd fürhabender bücher zuebesuchen, Das ich zue dem ende den vierten Februarij des 1600 Jahrs allhero gehn Benathka kommen, allda den wollermelten Herrn, gnedig vnd allerfreundtlichst woll empfangen, auch diese gantze Zeitt vber aller müglichkeit nach also tractirt worden, das ich mich hingegen iederzeit, zue aller Vnderthaniger Danckbarkeit schuldig erkhennen.

Demnach auch Ir Gest. alls einen liebhaber aller künsten, vnd denen beygethonen Cultoribus, gnedig gefallen, mir einen gueten theill Irer observationum, sambt andern anhengigen Inventionibus vnd laboribus Astronomicis freywillig mitzuetheilen: vnd Ich aber ietziger Zeitt bösen schädlichen gebrauch anderer leutte nützliche vnd rhuemliche arbeitt eigen nütziger verschlagener weisse zur hinderlichen den rechten Urhabern zueentziehen, vnd anderen rhümberechtig zuezuschreiben, zur gemüeth gefürtt: hab ich eigner sorgfaltigkeit halber, für nützlich vnd rathsamblich geachtet, mit gegenwärtiger meiner eignen handtschrifft vnd vndergedruckten Petschafft dahin mich zuuerpflichten, dass ich alles das, so mir von wolermelten Herrn BRAHE communicirt oder hernacher, in was wege es wolle, communicirt soll werden, in hochster geheim halten. Darmit auch mich andere alss einen liebhaber der Philosophia, gebe vnd offtermelten Herrn BRAHE, alss den rechten authori selbsten iederzeit gefallen würt, vmbgehen handlen vnd dero mich gebrauchen wölle: alles auff treuw vnd glauben, treuwlich vnd vngefehrlich.

In vrkhund meiner handschrifft, vnd angeporn Pitschafft, Geben den 5 Aprilis im 1600 Jah. Benat.

Johannes Kepler to Tycho Brahe.

I, Master Johannes Kepler, Mathematician of an Honourable Province in Styria, acknowledge: that — after I had, with the gracious permission of the well-mentioned [Provincial] Estates, undertaken a journey into Bohemia, chiefly to visit the noble and stern Lord Tycho Brahe, Lord of Knudstrup and Uraniborg, on account of His Lordship's considerable and far-famed astronomical instruments and intended books — I came hither to Benátky, to that end, on the fourth of February of the year 1600; where I was graciously and most kindly well received by the well-mentioned Lord, and, during all this time, was treated according to all possibility [as well as could be], so that I, on the other hand, at all times acknowledge myself bound to all submissive gratitude.

Since, then, it has also graciously pleased His Lordship — as a lover of all the arts, and [a friend] to the devotees [Cultores] attached to them — to share with me, freely, a good part of His observations, together with other associated inventions and astronomical labours: and since I have brought to mind the present evil, harmful practice of [certain] people, of withdrawing other [people]'s useful and praiseworthy work, in a self-interested, cunning manner, to the hindrance of the rightful authors, and of ascribing it to others as though [theirs] by right of renown — I have, for the sake of my own conscientiousness, deemed it useful and advisable, by this my present own handwriting and the seal pressed below, to bind myself thereto: that I will keep in highest secrecy all that is communicated to me by the well-mentioned Lord Brahe, or shall hereafter, in whatever way it may be, be communicated [to me]; so that others, too, may regard me as a lover of Philosophy; and [so] that I will, at all times — as it shall please the oft-mentioned Lord Brahe, as the true author himself — deal, act, and conduct myself, and let him make use of me: all upon trust and faith, faithfully and without guile.

In witness of my handwriting and innate seal, given the 5th of April, in the year 1600. Benátky.

Apparatus

  • A landmark Tycho–Kepler document. This is Kepler's signed declaration of confidentiality — his solemn undertaking to keep Tycho's observations and discoveries secret — drawn up at Benátky on 5 April 1600, during the tense first months of their association. It is the formal pledge that helped patch up the early quarrel between the two men, and it ranks among the most-cited documents of their relationship. The witness survives, naturally, in the Keplerian papers (Vienna, cod. Vindob. lat. 10703 — the same volume preserving Kepler's transcript of Tycho's letter to Hoffmann, brahe-t_16000406001, written the next day, 6 April, on Kepler's return to Prague).
  • Kepler's self-styling. M. Johann Kepler, einer Ehrbaren Landschaft in Steier Mathematicus — "Master Johannes Kepler, Mathematician of an Honourable Province [the Provincial Estates] in Styria" — his then-title as district mathematician of Graz, granted leave to travel to Bohemia "to visit … Tycho Brahe … on account of His Lordship's far-famed astronomical instruments and intended books." He records his arrival "on the fourth of February 1600" and Tycho's kind reception.
  • The substance of the pledge. Tycho having "freely shared a good part of His observations, together with other … inventions and astronomical labours," Kepler — mindful of the "evil practice" of cunningly stripping work from its rightful authors and ascribing it to others (a pointed allusion to the kind of plagiarism-charge then swirling around Ursus and others) — binds himself, "by this my own handwriting and seal," to keep in highest secrecy everything Tycho communicates to him, and to use it only as Tycho, "the true author," shall please. The closing formula auf Treu und Glauben, treulich und ungefährlich ("upon trust and faith, faithfully and without guile") is the binding legal tag.
  • Code-switching. Latin technical/legal nouns embedded in the German — observationum, Inventionibus, laboribus Astronomicis, Cultoribus, Philosophia, authori — kept and italicised, rendered into English.
  • Calendar. Geben den 5 Aprilis im 1600 Jahr. Benat[ky] = 5 April 1600; Bohemia (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
  • Correction. Corrected 'Herrn aus Knudtstrup' to 'Herrn auf Knudtstrup' in Kepler's confidentiality declaration; Dreyer's print (TBOO VIII p.296) uses the standard 'Herr auf X' formula.
