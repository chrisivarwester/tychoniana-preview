The original

DEM DURCHLEUCHTIGEN FÜRSTEN, HERN CHRISTIANO DEM VIERDTEN, ZU DENNEMARGKEN, NORDWEGEN, DER WENDEN VNDT GOTHEN KÖNNIGE, HERTZOGEN ZU SCHLESSWIGK, HOLSTEIN, DER STORMAREN VNDT DITMARSCHEN, GRAFFEN ZUE OLDENBURG VNDT DELMENHORST, UNSERM FREUNDTLICHEM VIELGELIEBTEN HERRN SOHN, ZU JHR: K: W: HÄNDEN.

DURCHLEUCHTIGER könnig, Euer K: W: sey Vnser diensthaffter ehrengruss vndt was Wir der mutterlichen vervandtnuss nach viel mehr leibs vnd guts vermugen, zuuohrn. Freundtlicher hochvielgeliebter herr vetter, sohn, vndt bruder, Wir wollen zu dem Allmechtigen vngezweiffelt hoffen, das E:K:W: sambt dero vielgeliebter gemahlin, Vnser hertzlieben tochter, noch bey guetter leibsgesundheitt vndt allem königlichen Wohlstände gnediglich erhalten werden, wie Wir denn derselben königlichen w:, vndt J:L: solches auß mütterlichen hertzen wünschen thuen.

Vnsern freundtlichen hertzvielgeliebten herrn gemahl vnd kinder wollen E:K:W: noch bey zimblicher leibs vermögenheitt vndt zuestande wissen, allein das wass fur Vnsere person etzliche tage hero vonn dem husten vndt anderer zuefelligen schwacheitt dermassen zugesetzt, daß Wir auch Vnsern gnedigen geliebten herrn vattern, hochlöblicher chriftmilder gedechtnus, wie gerne Wir auch gewolt hetten, die letzte ehr bey dero churfurstl: begrebnus nicht haben leisten können; haben aber dennoch dem Allmechtigen nuhn mehr fur zimbliche besßerung zuedancken, dessen barmhertzigkeit vns sambtlich darbey gnediglich lang erhalten wolle. Es werden auch E:K:W: auß Vnsers hertzlieben hern gemahls an E:K:W: gethanes vorbittlich schreiben, sie haben lassen vndterthenigst vortragen, welcher gestelt dero 1: vmb gnedigste vorschrifft an E:K:W: voer dem erenuehsten Vnserm lieben besonderen Tycho Brahen vndterthenigst angelangt; wann er denn dergleichen auch Vns vmb vorbittschrifft ersucht, vndt Wir niemals vermerckt, daß E:K:W: ihme mitt vngnaden gewogen, auch seine bitt nicht vnzimblich erachten, so gelanget hiemit an E:K:W: Vnser freundtliches bitten, E:K:W: ihn dermasßen mitt gnedigster andtwort wollen versehen lasßen, damitt er seiner geschepsten hoffnung nach im wergk spuren möge, daß diesse Vnsere ihm zue gnaden gemeinte Vorschrift ihm sonderlich zue erlangung seines begehren ersprißlich gewesen, wie Wir denn gar nicht zweiffeln, E:K:W: sich hierumb werden, jhrer angebornen guetigkeitt nach, gegen ihme, so weitt sichs immer der billigkeitt vndt gerechtigkeitt nach (in masßen Wir auch weitters nichts begeren) leiden wil, gnedigst erzeigen. Vndt thuen E:K:W: sambt dero vielgeliebten gemahl, Vnser hertzlieben tochter, zue allen könniglichen wohlergehen, glichlicher regierung vndt guetter bestendig gesundheitt dem Allmechtigen ganz mutterlich beuehlen.

Datum Cöln an der Sprew, den 4. februarij anno 98.
Vonn Gottes gnaden Katharina geborne marggraffin
auch churfurstin zue Brandenburgk,
inn Preußen hertzogin.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the Most Serene Prince, the Lord Christian the Fourth, King of Denmark, Norway, the Wends and the Goths, Duke of Schleswig, Holstein, of the Stormarn and the Dithmarschen, Count of Oldenburg and Delmenhorst, our friendly, much-beloved Lord Son — into His Royal Majesty's [own] hands.

Most Serene King, [be] to Your Royal Majesty Our dutiful and honourable greeting, and, according to the bond of motherhood, whatever much more We are able [to render] of body and goods, beforehand. Friendly, most heartily beloved Lord cousin, son, and brother: We will hope, undoubting, to the Almighty, that Your Royal Majesty, together with your much-beloved consort, Our heart-dear daughter, are still graciously preserved in good bodily health and in all royal well-being — as We do indeed wish the same to that same Royal Majesty and to Her Beloved Grace out of a motherly heart.

Our friendly, most heartily beloved Lord consort and children Your Royal Majesty may know to be still in tolerable bodily capacity and condition; save only that, as for Our own person, for some days past the cough and other incidental weakness have so afflicted [Us], that We were also unable to render the last honour to Our gracious beloved Lord father — of most laudable, devout-in-Christ memory — at his electoral funeral, however gladly We should have wished [to do so]; yet We have nonetheless now to thank the Almighty for a tolerable improvement, whose mercy may graciously preserve us all therein long. Your Royal Majesty will also [learn] from Our heart-dear Lord consort's letter of intercession addressed to Your Royal Majesty — which they had most humbly submitted — in what manner there had most humbly arrived [a request] for a most gracious letter of recommendation to Your Royal Majesty on behalf of the most honourable, Our trusty and well-beloved Tycho Brahe; since he has then likewise besought Us also for a letter of intercession, and We have never noted that Your Royal Majesty was disposed toward him with disfavour, and do also not deem his request unreasonable — there reaches Your Royal Majesty herewith Our friendly request, that Your Royal Majesty would have him furnished with such a most gracious answer that he, according to the hope he has conceived, may perceive in the deed that this Our recommendation, intended for his benefit, has been especially fruitful to him for the attainment of his desire; as indeed We do not at all doubt that Your Royal Majesty will, according to your inborn kindness, show yourself herein most graciously toward him, so far as it may ever admit according to equity and justice (in which measure We also desire nothing further). And We commend Your Royal Majesty, together with your much-beloved consort, Our heart-dear daughter, to the Almighty for all royal welfare, fortunate governance, and good lasting health, quite as a mother [does].

Given at Cölln an der Spree, the 4th of February, in the year [15]98.
By the grace of God, Katharina, born Margravine,
also Electress of Brandenburg,
Duchess in Prussia.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Katharina of Brandenburg-Küstrin (1549–1602), Electress of Brandenburg, wife of Elector Johann Georg of Brandenburg. She was the maternal grandmother of Christian IV: her daughter Anna Catharina of Brandenburg (1575–1612) married Christian IV in 1597. The kinship triple in the salutation ("herr vetter, sohn, vndt bruder" — "Lord cousin, son, and brother") is conventional inter-dynastic courtesy address, not literal; she calls the queen "Vnser hertzlieben tochter" ("Our heart-dear daughter"), which is literal.
  • Recipient. Christian IV of Denmark and Norway (r. 1588–1648). The titulature in the address-block is the standard Danish royal style: King of Denmark, Norway, the Wends and Goths; Duke of Schleswig, Holstein, Stormarn and Dithmarschen; Count of Oldenburg and Delmenhorst.
  • Subject — the Tycho intercession. The letter is a Vorbitte / Vorschrift (a letter of intercession + recommendation) on behalf of Tycho Brahe, here styled "dem erenuehsten Vnserm lieben besonderen Tycho Brahen" ("the most honourable, Our trusty and well-beloved Tycho Brahe" — lieben besonderen is the German chancery formula glossed at the edition's convention Vester lieber besonder → "Our trusty and well-beloved"). It coordinates with a parallel intercession from her husband the Elector ("Vnsers hertzlieben hern gemahls … vorbittlich schreiben"). Dating (4 Feb. 1598) and content place it amid the deteriorating relations between Tycho and the Danish court that culminated in his departure from Denmark in 1597; the Brandenburg electoral couple are lobbying the young king on his behalf. Tycho had evidently solicited the intercession himself ("wann er denn dergleichen auch Vns vmb vorbittschrifft ersucht").
  • Negation count. The body contains exactly three negations, all preserved: (1) "die letzte ehr … nicht haben leisten können" — "were unable to render the last honour" [at her father's funeral]; (2) "Wir niemals vermerckt, daß E:K:W: ihme mitt vngnaden gewogen" — "We have never noted that Your Royal Majesty was disposed toward him with disfavour"; (3) "auch seine bitt nicht vnzimblich erachten" — "do also not deem his request unreasonable" (a single negation governing the litotes unzimblich "unreasonable"; rendered as one "not," not doubled). The parenthetical "in masßen Wir auch weitters nichts begeren" — "in which measure We also desire nothing further" — is a fourth negative element (nichts), preserved. None of these reverse a command; the controlling speech-act is a request ("Vnser freundtliches bitten"), and the conditional logic is straightforward (since X, and since not-Y, and not-Z → therefore We request), so no Possible-grade ambiguity arises.
  • The qualifying clause "so weitt sichs immer der billigkeitt vndt gerechtigkeitt nach … leiden wil" — "so far as it may ever admit according to equity and justice" — is a deferential limiting clause: the Electress asks the king to favour Tycho only insofar as equity and justice permit, explicitly adding that she desires nothing beyond that. The hedge is preserved, not sharpened into an unconditional plea.
  • Her father's funeral. "Vnsern gnedigen geliebten herrn vattern, hochlöblicher chriftmilder gedechtnus … dero churfurstl: begrebnus" — Katharina's father was Margrave Johann (Hans) of Brandenburg-Küstrin (d. 1571); "chriftmilder" is a chancery spelling of christmilder ("devout/charitable in Christ," a memorial epithet for the deceased), rendered "devout-in-Christ memory." She reports being kept by illness from attending the funeral — apparently a recent re-interment or commemorative obsequy, since the date of her own letter (1598) post-dates the Margrave's death by decades; the text does not specify the occasion, and it is left unresolved rather than supplied.
  • Court-German orthography (Frühneuhochdeutsch / chancery, c. 1598). Representative forms: vndt / vnd = und; vff-type v/u alternation throughout (vermugen, vermögenheitt); könnig / könniglichen = König(lichen); zuuohrn = zuvorn ("beforehand," the address-formula,); gnediglich / gnedigst = gnädiglich / gnädigst; thuen / thuen … beuehlen = tun / befehlen; besßerung, ersprißlich, lasßen, masßen show ß/ss alternation; Sprew = Spree; marggraffin = Markgräfin; churfurstin = Kurfürstin. husten = "cough."
  • Abbreviations expanded in sense. E:K:W: = Euer Königliche Würden ("Your Royal Majesty/Dignity"); J:L: = Ihre Liebden ("Her Beloved Grace," conventional address for a princess — here the queen); dero 1: — an abbreviation in the witness, most plausibly dero Liebden ("their Beloved Grace," i.e. the Elector); read as the source of the humble request, but the contracted "1:" is editorially uncertain and is flagged here rather than silently expanded.
  • Calendar. "den 4. februarij anno 98" = 4 February 1598. Brandenburg and Denmark both kept the Julian calendar (Old Style) in 1598 (Protestant territories did not adopt the Gregorian reform until 1700), so the dateline is O.S.; no conversion is applied. The catalogue label ("1598. 4. februar. Cölln an der Spree") agrees with the dateline.
  • Place. Cöln / Cölln an der Sprew = Cölln an der Spree, the twin town of Berlin on the Spree (seat of the Brandenburg court; later merged into Berlin), not Cologne on the Rhine.
  • MS witness. The Opera Omnia text rests on the transcript (Afskrift) in Langebek's Diplomatarium, Rigsarkivet, Copenhagen — i.e. a later copy, not the original sent letter; readings such as dero 1: should be weighed with that transmission in mind.
