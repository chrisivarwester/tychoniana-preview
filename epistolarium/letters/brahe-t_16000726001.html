The original

[TYCHO BRAHE AD ANONYMAM].

EDLE viel ehr- und tugentsame insonders gönstige vielgeliebte fraw schwegerin, derselben sein vnsere sämptliche freundtwillige dienste vnd alles gutten zuvor. Vnd kan deroselben hiemit freundtlichen nitt pergen, das vns der ochße, so der herr schuager zu vnserer notturfft aingekaufft, vns wohl zu handen kommen. Thuen vns dero darumb gehabten mühe zum höchsten vnd fleißigsten bedancken, wollens auch vff alle vorfallende gelegenheit eußerstes vermögs nach zu verschulden nitt vnterlaßen.

Was nuhn das verhörte gutt betrifft, das der herr schuager vnd fraw schuegerin vns zu gefallen in bestallung haben, deran geschiht vns gleichßfals zum höhesten ahngenehmsten gefallen. Wollen auch gegen gemeldte zait, alsnemblich vmb Bartholomei, daßelbe zu besehen ainen von vns abfertigen, allerdinges hierumb vnd schließlichen beschaid zu macchen.

Für die bieren thuen wir vns gleichßfals zum höchsten bedancken.

Vns hiemit göttlicher almacht getreulich entpfhelendt.

Jn eill, Prag den 26. julii a:o 1600

English translation machine translation, Probable

[Tycho Brahe to an unnamed lady.]

Noble, very honourable and virtuous, especially favourable, dearly-beloved lady sister-in-law: to her be all our friendly-willing services and all good, beforehand. And we cannot herewith, in friendly wise, conceal from her that the ox, which the Herr Schwager [brother-in-law] bought for our need, has come well to our hands. We thank her most highly and diligently for the trouble had therein, and will not fail to requite it, to the utmost of our ability, on every occasion that may arise.

Now, as concerns the property under discussion, which the Herr Schwager and the Frau Schwägerin hold in charge [under arrangement] to oblige us — that too is to us a most agreeable pleasure of the highest sort. And toward the said time — namely, about [the feast of] St Bartholomew — we will dispatch one of our [people] to inspect the same, in order to settle this matter altogether and conclusively.

For the pears we likewise give our highest thanks.

Herewith faithfully commending ourselves to God's Almighty [protection].

In haste, Prague, the 26th of July, in the year 1600.

[Postscript:] The messenger has in all respects been paid his messenger's-fee.

Apparatus

  • Document type. A short family/domestic letter (a draft, Kladde, in the Basel autograph collection), with no recipient named — the edition titles it ad anonymam, "to an unnamed [woman]." She is addressed as Frau Schwägerin (lady sister-in-law / kinswoman by marriage); her husband is the Herr Schwager (brother-in-law).
  • Content. Wholly domestic: Tycho acknowledges receipt of an ox bought for him by the brother-in-law; thanks for pears; and a property/estate matter (das verhörte gut … in Bestallung) that the couple are managing on his behalf, which he will send someone to inspect around St Bartholomew's Day (24 August).
  • verhörte gut — flagged: literally "the heard-of / discussed property," i.e. the estate or goods previously spoken of and now "in Bestallung" (under arrangement/commission). The exact referent is not recoverable from this isolated draft; rendered "the property under discussion." Bestallung here = arrangement/charge, not "appointment."
  • Register. Family German in the courteous first-person plural (wir/uns/unsere); the cascade of honorifics (Edle, ehr- und tugendsame, gönstige, vielgeliebte) is the standard address to a gentlewoman. Preserved.
  • Calendar. Prag den 26. julii a:o 1600 = 26 July 1600; Prague (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
  • §1 Dreyer collation. One-line closing postscript, restored from Dreyer XIV, p. 209 (dropped after the dateline "Jn eill, Prag den 26. julii a:o 1600").
