The original

ERLLIG OCH WELBYURDICH MANDT THYGGE BRADE TILL KNUDSTRUP, HØFFUITZMANDT PAA BENACSIA, MIN KIERRE NESTSSØSCHINDT BARN OCH SYNDERLIG GODE WEN, GANTZ WENLIG THILLSCHREFFUITT.

KIERE Thygge och min kiere morßøfterßenn, Gudt vere althidt huoß thig medt sin helig andt och beuare thig medt altt, huis thig kiert er, fra alltt ont. Jegh thacker thig gandske vennligen och gierne for aaltt beuist erre och gott, huilcket att forschyllde ieg vill althidt findis villige y huis mader meg mouligt er. Kiere Thygge, ieg haffuer stackit sidenn bekomit thinn schriffuelße, och theraff formerker thinn leglighedts gode thillstandt ther vde i thendt landtz egn, huilcken er mig som Gudt ßaa kiertt att høre, som du vaarst min egenn sønn heler broder. Och vill ønsche aff Gudt allermegtigste, att thet maa bliffue jo lenger jou beder. Sammelethis, kiere morßøsterßen, som samme thin schriffuelße ochsaa formelder om dj pendinge, ieg thig skylldig er, atu thenom thill thenne omslag maate bekomme, saa schall Gudt vide, att ieg helder end giernne (som och billichtt vaar) ville samme thinn begiering eptherkomme, thersom thet vaar mig mouligt. Menn nu kandt ieg thenom saa hastig och snnart jcke thill vege bringe, aff thend aarßage jegh nu vdj thenne thillkomindis vgge schall giffue kong. maytt. 6000 daller paa min sallige broder Peders vegnne, och thersom thet icke hafde verit, skulle du for:ne thinne pendinge giernne haffue bekommit, och beder thig ther for, kiere Thygge och kiere mor[sø]sterßøn, paa thet vennligst, du ville haffue gedultt medt for:ne pendinge thill nu jull førstkomindis ett aar, schall Eske Billde thenom paa thine vegnne, vden alldt vider forhalling heller vndschylding, bekomme. Dog renten er aarligen och endnu thette aar ochsaa bettalidt. Och ther som ieg i nogen mader kandt thienne thig igenn, skaltu i[n]genn thuill haffue paa, ieg giør jou giernne som ieg och pligthig er. Nytt att schriffue theg thill, er her inthet synderligt. Thij huadt vdj Thydschland sig thilldrager, vedst thu beder endt vij j diße lande; thij penninge haffuer affthackidt alle vore pentzionierer, som vj schulle haffue kundschaff aff, saa vell som och ander, vij sider saa secker och frij som en fougell vnder garnedt. Hertug Kaarell aff Suerig thiranißer grußammb, haffuer nu faaedt aldt Findlandt indt och ombragt och ladit rete alle dij adell ther y landit. Jeg frygter Erick Spaare och hanns paartie ville snart følge epther, huilckett Gudt aff vennde.

Kongen aff Pollenn ßende 8 schibbe met 800 mand her igenom sundit, schulle indthage Elsborige, paa huilcke johan Gyldenstiern vaar admierall. Hoffuidtschibbit, som admierall vaar paa, bleff vnder Halldlandtz side medt mieste paartenn skiøtt, huilkedt vij nu lader opthage och bierge oss thill beste, thet andit schibb, nest admierallen, bleff och med thop och thagell; the ander ere kommen thillbage hidt thill Kiøbenhaffuen, ville gierne bliffue her vdj vintherlege (thij the thør icke løbe hiemmb, hertug Kaarell er thenom for i vegenn met 12 schibe); the haffuer huercken att bide eler brydde; erre beuilgidt at maa bliffue her i byen paa nogen thidlang att there for thierris pendinge, huilcket erre saare faa; the haffuer soldt och selger endnu dagelig aff thierris krudt och laadt for pendinge thill vnderholding, doch thet vill icke naa lanngt. Thet er vell snucket verheffthig folck, men the haffuer icke thet, the kunde schyulle thennom vdj.

Jacop Sieffelltt haffuer Gudt kaldit nu for xiiij dage forledenn, epther hand haffde giortt thuende sin døthers brøllup med Niels Schrammb och Hans Lindernou thill Gaffuenøe. Huis andit nytt her er, som icke synderligt haffuer paa sig, thet ved thin sønn thig vell adt berete; thij vill ieg nu icke lennger forhinder thig met thenne min schriffuelße, men vill beffale dig met huiß thig kiertt er thendt gode Gud udj volldt.

Ex Kiøbenhaffuen thendt 3 decembris a:o 99.
Axell Gyldenstern
med egen hand.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and well-born man Tyge Brahe of Knudstrup, captain at Benátky, my dear first cousin and especial good friend, [this] most amicably written.

Dear Tyge, and my dear cousin [mother's-sister's-son], may God ever be with you with his Holy Spirit, and preserve you, with all that is dear to you, from all evil. I thank you quite amicably and gladly for all the honour and good shown me, which to repay I will always be found willing in whatever way is possible for me. Dear Tyge, I have a short while ago received your letter, and from it note the good state of your circumstances there in that region — which is as dear to me to hear (God knows) as if you were my own son or brother; and I will wish from God Almighty that it may grow the better the longer. Likewise, dear cousin, as your same letter also makes mention of the money I owe you, that you might receive it at this Umschlag — God knows I would more than gladly (as was also fair) comply with that request of yours, if it were possible for me. But now I cannot bring it to hand so quickly and soon, for the reason that I, in this coming week, must give the Royal Majesty 6,000 dalers on behalf of my late brother Peder; and had it not been for that, you should gladly have received your aforesaid money. And I ask you therefore, dear Tyge and dear cousin, most amicably, that you would have patience with the said money until next Christmas a year hence, [and] Eske Bille shall receive it on your behalf, without all further delay or excuse — though the interest is paid yearly, and this year too. And if I can serve you in any way in return, you shall have no doubt [of it]; I do it gladly, as I am also bound. As for news to write you, there is nothing special here; for what passes in Germany you know better than we in these lands — for money has dismissed all our pensioners [informants], from whom we should have intelligence, as well as others: we sit as secure and free as a bird under the net. Duke Charles of Sweden tyrannizes cruelly: he has now taken all Finland, and put to death and executed all the nobility there in the land. I fear Erik Sparre and his party will soon follow — which God avert.

The King of Poland sent 8 ships with 800 men here through the Sound, [meaning] to take Älvsborg, on which Johan Gyldenstierne was admiral. The flagship, on which the admiral was, was for the most part shot up off the Halland side — which we now have salvaged and recovered for our benefit; the second ship, next the admiral's, [was taken] too, with top and tackle [and all]. The others have come back here to Copenhagen, [and] would gladly stay here in winter quarters (for they dare not run home — Duke Charles is in their way with 12 ships); they have neither [bread to] bite nor [aught to] brew; they are permitted to stay in the city for some time, [living] on their money, which is very little; they have sold, and still daily sell, of their powder and shot for money for their keep — but it will not reach far. They are indeed neat, valiant folk, but they have not that wherein they might shelter themselves.

Jacob Sehested God has now called [hence] a fortnight ago, after he had made the weddings of two of his daughters — with Niels Skram and Hans Lindenov of Gavnø. Whatever other news there is here, which is nothing special, your son will well know to report to you; therefore I will not now hinder you longer with this my letter, but will commend you, with all that is dear to you, into the good God's keeping.

From Copenhagen, the 3rd of December, the year [15]99.
Axel Gyldenstierne,
with my own hand.

Apparatus

- Sender & kinship. Axel Gyldenstierne (1542–1603), Danish Rigsraad councillor and Statholder of Norway (1588–1601) — Tycho's maternal cousin, addressed as min kiere morsøstersøn ("my dear mother's-sister's-son") and, in the address, næstsøskendebarn (first cousin): their mothers were Bille sisters. He addresses Tycho as "captain at Benátky" (the imperial castle). The MS is the original letter.
- Significance — a Danish kinsman's news-letter (December 1599). Two strands:
1. A private debt. Axel owes Tycho money (which Tycho's letter had asked to receive at the Umschlag); he cannot pay now, since "in this coming week" he must give the King 6,000 dalers on behalf of his late brother Peder, and begs delay until next Christmas a year hence, when Eske Bille will collect on Tycho's behalf — "though the interest is paid yearly." (Tycho's web of Danish loans, like the Mecklenburg one.)
2. Scandinavian war-news. A vivid bulletin of the Polish–Swedish war and the Swedish succession crisis:
- Duke Charles of Sweden (later Charles IX) "tyrannizes cruelly," having taken Finland and executed its nobility (the 1599 reduction of Finland / Åbo executions); Axel fears Erik Sparre (the Swedish statesman) "will soon follow" — as indeed he did, in the Linköping Bloodbath (1600).
- The King of Poland (Sigismund III Vasa) sent 8 ships, 800 men through the Sound to take Älvsborg, under admiral Johan Gyldenstierne; the flagship was shot to pieces off Halland (and salvaged by the Danes), a second ship taken, the rest stranded at Copenhagen — unable to sail home (Duke Charles blocking with 12 ships), destitute, selling their powder and shot to live: "neat, valiant folk, but they have not that wherein they might shelter themselves." A striking eyewitness of the stranded Polish squadron.
3. Family news. The death of Jacob Sehested (a fortnight earlier), after marrying two daughters to Niels Skram and Hans Lindenov of Gavnø.
- Persons named. Axel Gyldenstierne (sender); his late brother Peder Gyldenstierne; Eske Bille (to collect Tycho's money); Duke Charles of Sweden; Erik Sparre; King Sigismund III of Poland; Johan Gyldenstierne (the Polish-service admiral); Jacob Sehested and his daughters' husbands Niels Skram and Hans Lindenov; Tycho's son (Tycho the Younger, "your son … will report").
- Idiom (kept and glossed). "vi sidder så sikker og fri som en fugl under garnet" — "we sit as secure and free as a bird under the net" (i.e. heedless of imminent danger); "hverken at bide eller brygge" — "neither [bread] to bite nor [aught] to brew" (utterly without provisions).
- Calendar. Dateline Ex Kiøbenhaffuen thendt 3 decembris a:o 99; Old Style (Denmark). Header agrees with the label.
- Place-name. Benacsia = Benátky; Elsborige = Älvsborg (the Swedish fortress at the Göta mouth); Halland, Sundet (the Sound).
