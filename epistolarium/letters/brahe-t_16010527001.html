The original

DEM EHRNUESTEN VND ACHTPARN ANDRESS MEIERN, FÜRSTLICHEM MECKLENBURGISCHEN RENTMEISTERN, MEINEM BESONDERN GUTEN FREUNDT. WISSMAR.

MEINE freundtwillige dienste zuvorn. Ehrnuester, achtpar, lieber herr rentmaister, insonders gueter freundt. Ewer schreibent von Wißmar, den 18. maij dieses lauffenden jhars datiert, ist mir wohl behendiget, aber das vorige, wovon ihr meldet, so wegen der fürgewandten verhindernuß dero 10000 thaller hauptsummen vnd zinsen erlegung ahn mich ergangen sein soll, hab ich bis dato nitt gesehen, geschweig die darinnen angezogene vrsacchen für erheblich ahnnehmen können. Vnd seitenmahl obgedachte summa zu rechter zeit vnsers hieruber auffgerichteten contracts auffgekündiget, vnd ihr auch (wie dan ahn ihm selbs pillich) dieselbigen ahngenohmen, hab ich die anderen leutten zugesagt vnd versprocchen, dan mir ohnedas vnleidlich, die zinse nitt zu rechter vnd gepüerlicher zeit zue entpfhangen, wie dan auch dieselbige von Rostogk anhero zu bringen kain mittel noch rhat weiß. Zu deme auch hatt mein blutsverwanter Esche Bilde meinentwegen ain thail deßelbigen geldes anderen leutten versprocchen, vnd wollen noch können ewerer ex postfacto angereugter vrsacchen halben vnser wort nitt wiedernehmen. Gelanget derowegen ahn euch mein abermähliges, ernstliches pitten vnd begehren, das ihr nochmahls dazu thuen wollen, damit mir ohne ainigen weittern verzugk zum allerlengsten innerhalb nechstkünfftigen s. Joannis thagk, wie ihr offtgedachtem Esche Bilden zuegeschrieben, mehrgedachte hauptsumma sampt dem interesse richtich erlegt werde.

Hieran thuet ihr was der pilligkait vnd meiner aigentlichen resolution gemeß, jhm fal ihr nit wollet, das ich die bürgen in schaden zue bringen, vnd bej jhr: f: g: hertzogk Vldricchen, meinem gnedichsten fürsten vnd herren, micch derentwegen zue beklagen verursaccht vnd genotdrenget werde.

Söllichs hinwiederumb auff zu tragendem fall vmb euch vnd die ewerige zu verschülden, bin ich iederzeit willig vnd geflißen. Euch hiemit göttlicher almacht entpfhelendt.

Datum Prag den 27. maij a:o 1601.
E: dienst- vnd freundtwilliger
Tycho Brahe
egenhn.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and worshipful Andreas Meier, princely Mecklenburg rent-master, my especial good friend. Wismar.

My friend-willing services beforehand. Honourable, worshipful, dear Lord rent-master, especial good friend: your letter from Wismar, dated the 18th of May of this current year, has duly been handed to me; but the earlier one, of which you make mention — which is said to have come to me concerning the alleged hindrance to paying out those 10,000 thalers of principal sum and interest — I have to this day not seen, much less been able to accept the reasons cited therein as weighty. And seeing that the above-mentioned sum was called in at the proper time of our contract drawn up over it, and that you too (as is indeed only fair in itself) accepted the same, I have pledged and promised it to other people; for it is in any case intolerable to me not to receive the interest at the proper and due time, just as I also know neither means nor counsel for bringing the same hither from Rostock. To this is added that my blood-relative Eske Bille has, on my behalf, promised a part of that same money to other people, and we neither will nor can take back our word on account of the reasons you have alleged after the fact. There comes to you, therefore, my renewed and earnest request and demand, that you will once more see to it that, without any further delay, at the very latest within the coming St. John's Day — as you wrote to the oft-mentioned Eske Bille — the said principal sum together with the interest be duly paid out to me.

In this you do what accords with fairness and with my actual resolve, if you would not have me caused and constrained to bring the sureties into loss, and on that account to lodge a complaint with His Princely Grace Duke Ulrich, my most gracious Prince and Lord.

To deserve this in return at your hands, should occasion arise, on behalf of you and yours, I am at all times willing and assiduous. Herewith commending you to divine omnipotence.

Given at Prague, the 27th of May, in the year 1601.
Your service- and friend-willing
Tycho Brahe
[in his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender & context. Tycho Brahe at Prague, five months before his death. This is the earlier of the two surviving Tycho→Meier letters on the Mecklenburg loan: 27 May 1601 (this) → 18 July 1601 (brahe-t_16010718001). Read together with Duke Ulrich's own letter to Meier of 9 August 1601 (brahe-t_16010809001), the three documents track the increasingly pressed recovery of a 10,000-thaler loan (principal + interest) that the ducal house of Mecklenburg owed Tycho.
  • Recipient. Andreas Meier, ducal Mecklenburg rent-master at Wismar (see brahe-t_16010718001 apparatus). Tycho has received Meier's letter of 18 May but not an earlier one Meier claims to have sent pleading a "hindrance" to payment — and rejects the excuse in any case.
  • Persons named.
  • mein Blutsverwandter Esche Bilde = Eske Bille, a Danish nobleman and kinsman of Tycho acting as his financial agent in the matter; he has already re-promised part of the money to third parties, so the word given cannot be retracted. (In the 9 August ducal letter, Eske Bille has come from Denmark and "lies at Rostock" to collect.)
  • J. F. G. Herzog Ulrich = Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg(-Güstrow), the prince to whom Tycho threatens to complain if payment fails.
  • Places. Wismar; Rostock (whence Tycho sees no way to have the interest conveyed); Prague.
  • The deadline. nechstkünftiger S. Joannis Tag = the coming St. John's Day (24 June 1601) — the term Meier had himself named to Eske Bille; the alternative Tycho holds over him is proceeding against the Bürgen (sureties).
  • Formulae & abbreviations. Burgher register (ehrnvest und achtbar). Subscription E: dienst- vnd freundtwilliger = Euer [Ehren] dienst- und freundwilliger; egenhn. = eigenhändig ("in his own hand," autograph attestation — the German counterpart of manu propria).
  • Calendar. Dateline den 27. Maij a:o 1601 stylo novo — explicitly New Style; the internal S. Joannis Tag is read consistently with the document's stated usage. Reproduced, not converted.
  • Correction. Removed 'stylo nouo' from the closing dateline, and the corresponding 'New Style' from the translation; Dreyer's print (TBOO XIV p.222) has no such phrase in the letter's own closing line.
