The original

DEM EHRNUESTEN VNSERN BESONDER LIEBEN TYGE BRAHEN AUFF WIEN.

Vlrich etc.

VNSERN günstigen grüs zuuor. Ehrnuester besonder lieber, nachdem Wir in Vnser ankunfft alhie befunden, das das druck pappiers, so dieß orts fur euch bestellet worden, funff vnd funfzigk reiß fertigk gewesen, weil sich gleich zugetragen, das meister Peter, der vhrmacher, auch alhie bej Vns angelangt vnd sich vernemen lassen solch pappier euch zuzubringen, so haben Wir jhm dasselb zustellen lassen, auch mit der fhur bis naher Warnmunde befördert. Das es sich aber mit Verfertigung desselben was verzogen, berichtet der meister, hat daran gemangelt, das er kejne form gehapt, so darzu gerecht gewesen, sondern hat an die formen, nach denen er das regal papier machet, noch sonderliche leisten sezen müssen, aufdas sich die grosse dieß eurs druck papiers darein recht schicken wollen, welchs denn woll zeit wegk genommen. Wir haben jhm dem meister die bezalung dafür entrichtet, wollen euch damit verehret haben, des versehens jhr werdet es nicht anders dan gnedigk vnd woll gemeint von Vns vermercken.

Da jhr Vns auch ein exemplar von eurem Opere Astronomice, wenn jhr damit fertigk, zustehen lassen wolte, thettet jhr Vns daran zu gemeinem gefallen, dan ob Wir woll die kunste nicht verstehen, mochten Wir doch gleichwoll Vnsere liberrj damit zu eurem gedechtnis gern zieren. Seindt Wir vmb euch in gnaden, damit Wir euch auch ohn das sonders woll gewesen zu beschulden gneigt.

Actum Grabauw den 15t. augustj anno 88.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable, our especially well-beloved Tyge Brahe at Hven.

Ulrich, etc.

Our gracious greeting beforehand. Honourable, especially well-beloved, [we write] inasmuch as on our arrival here we found that the printing paper which had been ordered for you in this place was [now] ready, five-and-fifty reams of it; and since it happened just then that Master Peter the clockmaker had likewise arrived here with us, and let it be known that he would bring such paper to you, we had it handed over to him, and forwarded it too by the carrier as far as Warnemünde. But that there has been some delay in the making of it — so the master reports — was owing to this: that he had no mould [form] suited to the purpose, but had to fit special battens onto the moulds by which he makes royal paper, so that the size of this printing paper of yours might be properly accommodated to them, which indeed took up a good deal of time. We have rendered the master his payment for it, [and] wish hereby to have made you a present of it, in the expectation that you will take it from us in no other sense than as graciously and well meant.

If, moreover, you would let us have a copy of your astronomical work [Opus Astronomicum], when you have finished it, you would thereby do us a common pleasure; for although we do not, to be sure, understand the arts [of astronomy], we should nonetheless gladly adorn our library with it, in your memory. We remain graciously disposed toward you, being besides this inclined to deserve especially well of you.

Done at Grabow, the 15th of August, the year [15]88.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg[-Güstrow] (1527–1603), reigning at Güstrow; a Lutheran prince and patron of learning, and maternal grandfather of King Christian IV of Denmark. The MS is the duke's own Correspondenz mit Gelehrten at Schwerin; the witness is a Kladde — a chancery draft/minute, not the dispatched fair copy. Vlrich etc. abbreviates the full ducal titulature, kept abbreviated as in the draft.
  • Address line / place. AUFF WIEN — not Vienna but "at Hven" (the island Hven / Ven, where Uraniborg stood); the chancery clerk's spelling of the Danish island name. Rendered "at Hven" and flagged, since Wien in isolation invites the false reading "Vienna." Actum Grabauw = "Done at Grabow" (Grabow in Mecklenburg), O.S. — the dateline kept by this chapter's convention
  • The paper gift — the substance of the letter. druck pappier = printing paper; funff vnd funfzigk reiß = fifty-five reams (Rieß / ream, the standard counting unit of paper). The duke is supplying paper for Tycho's Uraniborg press as a princely gift (verehren "to make a present of"), having paid the maker himself. This documents the well-attested practice whereby Tycho drew large-format paper for his self-printed works from German princely papermills.
  • The papermaking detail. form = the papermaker's mould (the wire frame on which the sheet is couched); regal papier = royal paper, a large sheet-size; leisten = battens / strips fixed to the standard moulds to enlarge the sheet to the oversized format Tycho required. The delay is technical: existing royal-paper moulds had to be re-fitted to yield Tycho's larger Druckpapier sheet. Rendered descriptively; no instrument-glossary term applies.
  • The carrier. meister Peter, der vhrmacher = "Master Peter the clockmaker", who happened to be travelling to Hven and undertook to convey the paper; vhrmacher (= Uhrmacher) "clockmaker/horologist." Warnmunde = Warnemünde, the port at the mouth of the Warnow near Rostock — the shipping point toward Hven. fhur / mit der fhur befördert = "forwarded by the carrier / by waggon-carriage."
  • The requested book. exemplar von eurem Opere Astronomice — a copy of "your astronomical work [Opus Astronomicum]," to be sent wenn jhr damit fertigk ("when you have finished it"). The work is not named; in August 1588 Tycho's press had just issued De mundi aetherei recentioribus phaenomenis (the second volume of the Progymnasmata series), and the Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata proper was still in progress — so the singular "work in progress" is left unspecified rather than identified. liberrj = Liberei / Librarei, "library" (the ducal book-collection); zu eurem gedechtnis = "in your memory / as a memorial of you." The duke's disclaimer ob Wir woll die kunste nicht verstehen ("although we do not understand the arts") is preserved, not smoothed.
  • Register & formulae (chancery German, princely Wir). VNSERN günstigen grüs zuuor = "Our gracious greeting beforehand"; the princely Wir / euch and the address Ehrnuester besonder lieber ("Honourable, especially well-beloved") are kept. Orthography is chancery Frühneuhochdeutsch: v/u for u/v (Vnser, vhrmacher, zuuor), -tz/-zk (funfzigk, fertigk), bej / kejne for bei/keine, jhm/jhr for ihm/ihr. des versehens = "in the expectation/confidence that." zu beschulden gneigt = zu verschulden geneigt, "inclined to deserve [well] of," an idiom of princely favour.
  • Code-switch. Latin nouns are embedded in the German body by this chapter's convention: Opere Astronomice (ablative of Opus Astronomicum), exemplar, and the dating rubric Actum … anno 88 — preserved and italicised on first use.
