The original

GENEROSO ET NOBILI VIRO D:NO TYCHONI BRAHE, DOMINO HEREDITARIO IN KNUDTSTRUP, AMICO SUO INPRIMIS HONORANDO.
Generose et nobilis vir, amice inprimis honorande.

QUOD ad Generos:tis T: litteras, quas jam ternas accepi, aliquanto serius respondeam, non mea culpa factum est. Nam Claudius Mulæus, cui priores traditas Generos:tas T. scribit, ipsemet mihi non adfuit, nec responsum ullum a me petijt. Alteras m. Christianus Longomannus me absente hic in aula reliquit, id quod Generos:tas T: ex adiecta hisce schedula, manu eius scripta, cognoscet. Quare ut me excusatum habeat, maiorem in modum rogo. Jnterim tamen quam posteriores ad manus meas pervenerunt, sexcentos illos thaleros, qui juniorum principum Megapolensium nomine Generos:ti T: debentur, Rostochium ad d. Lucam Backmeisterum, affinem meum, uti Generos:tas T: voluit, perferendos curavi. Egi quidem diligenter cum quæstore, ut in moneta aurea, quod Generos:tas T: cupit, solutio fieret, sed monetam eam in tanta summa conficere non potuit. Quare ut eo quoque nomine Generos:ti T: excusatus sim, itidem rogo. Chyrographum fidei meæ creditum Generos:ti T: remitto, nam alterum, quod m. Christianus Longomannus d. Backmeistero tradidit, quæstor retinuit. Quod reliquum est Generos:tem T: divini numinis protectioni commendo et officia mea eidem defero promptissima.

Datæ Gustrovij 19. die mensis septemb. stylo veteri anno 1600. Gener: T: studiosiss:
Jacobus Bordingus

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the generous and noble man, Master Tycho Brahe, hereditary lord in Knudstrup, his friend especially to be honoured. Generous and noble man, friend especially to be honoured.

That I answer Your Generosity's letters — of which I have now received three — somewhat late, has not happened by my fault. For Claude Muler, to whom Your Generosity writes the first were delivered, did not himself present himself to me, nor did he seek any answer from me. The second, Master Christian Longomannus left here at the court in my absence — as Your Generosity will learn from the note attached hereto, written in his hand. Wherefore I ask, in the greater degree, that you hold me excused. Meanwhile, however, as soon as the latter [letters] came to my hands, those six hundred thaler which are owed to Your Generosity in the name of the junior Princes of Mecklenburg, I had conveyed to Rostock, to Master Lucas Backmeister, my kinsman, to be delivered — as Your Generosity wished. I dealt diligently, indeed, with the treasurer, that the payment be made in gold coin, as Your Generosity desires; but he could not make up that coin in so great a sum. Wherefore, that I may be excused to Your Generosity on that score also, I likewise ask. The bond entrusted to my good faith I return to Your Generosity; for the other, which Master Christian Longomannus delivered to Master Backmeister, the treasurer retained. As for what remains, I commend Your Generosity to the protection of the divine Power, and offer the same my most ready services.

Given at Güstrow, the 19th day of the month of September, old style, in the year 1600.
Most devoted to Your Generosity,
Jacob Bording.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Jacob Bording (Bordingus), an official at the ducal court of Mecklenburg at Güstrow; affinis (kinsman by marriage) of Lucas Backmeister of Rostock.
  • The Mecklenburg debt. This documents the 600 thaler owed Tycho "in the name of the junior Princes of Mecklenburg" (juniorum principum Megapolensium — Megapolis = Mecklenburg) — the principal of which the 200 reichsthaler of interest in Tycho's quittance (brahe-t_15990110001, to the Mecklenburg rent-master Andreas Meyer) were a part. Bording has had the sum conveyed to Lucas Backmeister at Rostock, per Tycho's instruction; he apologises that the treasurer (quaestor) could not assemble so large a sum in gold coin (moneta aurea) as Tycho wished, and returns one of the two bonds (chirographa).
  • Couriers. Claudius Mulaeus = Claude Muler (the household member of brahe-t_15980627001), to whom the first letters were entrusted but who never delivered them; Christianus Longomannus = Longomontanus, who left the second batch at Güstrow and gave the matching bond to Backmeister.
  • quaestor rendered "treasurer" (the ducal fiscal officer); chirographum = a handwritten bond/IOU.
  • Calendar. Gustrovij 19. die … septemb. stylo veteri … 1600 = 19 September 1600, Old Style (stylo veteri explicit; Lutheran Mecklenburg kept the Julian calendar). Header agrees with the label.
