The original

OTTAVIO CAURIAN AD TYCHONEM BRAHE.

HERR FRIDERICH ROSENKRANTZ hat sich bei Jr. Fürstl. Durchl. Erzherzog MATHIAS zu Osterreich mit Irer Kay. Mt. Interceßion, ertzeiget vnd eingestelt, vnd alberait von Ir Durchl. zuesag bekhommen, vnnd derselben aufwarter nach seinem begern vnndergestellt vnnd vnnderhaltten zuwerden. Weiln aber Ir Mt. Resolution wegen Irer Durchl. veldtzug etwas spät hinauß geschicht worden, vnnd entzwischen herr SEYFRIDT VON KHOLONITSCH, obrister yber 1000 Pferdt, fortgetzogen, so hat Er mit Irer Durchl. vorwissen vnnd gnedigsten bewilligung, sich vnnder denselben gestelt, vnnd ist ietziger zeit vor Papa.

Das deme also sei, hab Ich OTTAUIO CAURIANI, dem Herrn TYCHONI BRAHE, so mich derwegen alhie zu Praag ersuecht, vnnd hieuon wiffenschafft begert, deß zu zeugnuß mein Handschrifft hierundergestelt.

Datum Praag Stilo nouo den 2. Augusti Anno 1600.
OTTAUIO CAURIAN,
manu propria subscripsi.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Ottavio Cauriani to Tycho Brahe.

Herr Friedrich Rosenkrantz presented and introduced himself to His Princely Highness Archduke Matthias of Austria, with the intercession of His Imperial Majesty, and has already received from His Highness the promise to be admitted and maintained, according to his desire, as one of His [Highness's] attendants. But since His Majesty's resolution concerning His Highness's campaign was sent out somewhat late, and in the meantime Herr Seyfried von Kollonitsch, colonel over 1000 horse, had moved off, he [Rosenkrantz] — with His Highness's foreknowledge and most gracious consent — placed himself under that same [Kollonitsch], and is at present before Pápa.

That this is so, I, Ottavio Cauriani, have set my signature hereunder, as testimony, for Herr Tycho Brahe — who sought me out here at Prague on that account, and desired knowledge of it.

Given at Prague, New Style, the 2nd of August, in the year 1600.
Ottavio Cauriani,
I have subscribed with my own hand.

Apparatus

  • Document type. A signed attestation / certificate (Zeugnis), furnished at Tycho's request, documenting the whereabouts and military engagement of Friedrich Rosenkrantz.
  • Sender. Ottavio Cauriani (Caurian), an Italian secretary/agent at the Imperial court in Prague; he certifies the matter "with his own hand" (the Latin subscription manu propria subscripsi closing a German body).
  • Subject. Friedrich Rosenkrantz (1571–1602), the Danish nobleman who, after a notorious scandal in Denmark, entered Imperial military service against the Ottomans. Through the intercession of Emperor Rudolf II he was promised a place among the attendants of Archduke Matthias (1557–1619, the Emperor's brother and commander on the Hungarian front); but, the formal resolution arriving late and the cavalry of colonel Seyfried von Kollonitsch ("over 100 horse") having already marched, he attached himself to Kollonitsch's troop and is now before Pápa (the Hungarian fortress, scene of the 1600 siege/mutiny). Tycho — Rosenkrantz's kinsman — evidently sought this certificate to document the young man's honourable service.
  • Register. Imperial chancery German (Ihr Fürstl. Durchl., Ihr Kay. Mt.); titulature preserved. aufwarter = attendant/courtier; obrister yber 100 Pferdt = colonel commanding 100 horse; veldtzug = (military) campaign.
  • Calendar. Stilo novo … 2. Augusti 1600 = 2 August 1600, New Style (explicit; Prague, imperial). Header agrees with the label.
  • §1 Dreyer collation. Dreyer (Opera Omnia VIII p. 330) reads obrister yber 1000 Pferdt — colonel over 1000 horse (the working transcription dropped a zero). Corrected in Source and translation.
  • Correction. Restored the dropped zero in the troop count 'obrister yber 1000 Pferdt' (the transcribed original had '100 Pferdt'), per Dreyer, TBOO VIII p.330. The published English already correctly read '1000 horse'; only the German the transcribed original required correction.
