The original

ERLIG OCH WELBYRDIIGE MANNDT TYGGJ BRADE TILL KNUDSTRUP, HØFFUIDTZMANDT PAA VRANNIBORGH, GANDTZE YDMYGELIGENN TILLSCHREFFUITT.

MYNN gandske ydmygelige helsßenn nu och altidt forsenndt medt Wor Herre. Kierre Tyggi Brade, thacker ieg ethers fromhedt for alt ere och gott, som y meg giort och beuist haffuer, for huilcket ieg effter minn formoue gandtze giernne will findis ethers fattige thienner till att giørre och lade alt huis ieg widt ethers fromhedt kandt haffue ere och gott aff. Kierre Tyggi och guode juncker, giffuer ieg ethers welbyrd[ig]hedt wenligen att wiide, om thj breffue, som eders fromhedt meg antuordit haffuer, denom ere huer well thill throer hande komit, som thj war tillschreffuit (Gud werre loffuit). Sameledis, frome juncker, som ethers welbyrdighedt well dragis tiill minnde, att ieg paa ethers wegnne schulle giøre min beste och forhørre ether om enn from, erlige personne, som kunde well stande ethers fromhedtz lehnn och befalling Nordfiordt forre, och som well widste landzenns leilighedt och skicke, huorfor ieg haffuer giffuit thenne breffuißer ethers welbyrdighedtz willie tilkiennde. Om saa kunde werre, attj wille betrou hanom eders lehnn, tha thuiller ieg jntedt ther paa, att samme personn, Morttenn Bertilßønn, skall io bliffue och werre eders fromhedt huldt och trou y alle maade, saa y nest Gudtz hielp skall hanom jche andit skylde, end thet som erligt och christeligt skall werre. Och thuiller ieg iche heller paa, att hand skall forsøme seg y huis maade, som handt widt eders welbyrdighedtz sager och bestelling kandt kome till gaffn och beste vdj nogenn maader. Ther for, gunstige juncker, haffuer ieg paa eders guode behagh forskreffuet samme Morttenn Bertilßønn till ether, saa ethers fromhedt kanndt sielffuer widere handle och talle medt hanom. Och om ieg widste, att handt war iche throu och guodt, tha skulle ieg jche forschriffue hanom tiill eders fromhedt; thi handt haffuer thiennt min hustrue[s] fader, Michell Eskelßønn, fogitt y Halsnø Kloster, y 9 aar som en erlig och trou karll y all maade, saa handt maa fritt skyude sinn skodtzmaall tiill hanom igienn, om behouff giøris. Frome gunstige juncker, er minn ydmygelige bønn till ethers welbyrdighedt, om saa er, att ethers fromhedt iche sielff hagde hans tienniste behouff, y tha wille wel giøre, som enn from manndt, och werre hanom behielpelig huos enn ethers fromhedtz guode wenner, som haffuer forlening her i landit, att handt motte fange nogenn pladtz. Thj hanndt widt well landzens leilighedt. Och will ieg iche lenger bemøde ethers fromhedt medt thenne minn schriffuelße, menn altidt giernne giøre huis ethers fromhedt kand werre till thienniste och guode, huilcke Gudt allemectigste kiennde. Jeg will nu och altidt haffue ether euindelige beffallindis.

Schreffuit vdj Halsnø closter then 2 aprillis anno 96.
Ethers ydmyge thienner altidt
Matzs
Jørgensßøn.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and well-born man Tyge Brahe of Knudstrup, governor at Uraniborg, written all most humbly.

My wholly humble greeting, now and always conveyed with Our Lord. Dear Tyge Brahe, I thank your worthiness [fromhedt] for all honour and good that you have done and shown me, for which, to the best of my ability, I will most gladly show myself your poor servant, to do and to leave undone all whereby I may, with your worthiness, have honour and good. Dear Tyge and good squire [juncker], I make known to your well-bornness in friendly wise that the letters which your worthiness entrusted to me have each duly come into faithful hands, as they were addressed (God be praised). Likewise, good squire, as your well-bornness will well recall, that I, on your behalf, was to do my best and make inquiry for you after an upright, honest person who could well preside over your worthiness's fief and command of Nordfjord, and who knew well the country's condition and order — wherefore I have made known to this letter-bearer your well-bornness's wish [willie]. If it might be that you would entrust your fief to him, then I doubt nothing thereof: that this same person, Morten Bertelsen, shall indeed remain and be loyal and faithful to your worthiness in every way, so that, next to God's help, you shall charge him with nothing other than what shall be honest and Christian. And I doubt no more either that he shall be negligent in any way wherein he, in your well-bornness's affairs and business, can come to profit and advantage in any manner. Therefore, gracious squire, at your good pleasure I have written the same Morten Bertelsen to you, so that your worthiness may yourself deal and speak further with him. And if I knew that he were not faithful and good, then I should not commend him to your worthiness; for he has served my wife's father, Michel Eskelsen, bailiff [fogitt] at Halsnøy Abbey, for nine years as an honest and faithful fellow in every way, so that he may freely refer his testimonial [skodtzmaall] back to him, if need be. Good gracious squire, my humble plea to your well-bornness is, if it be so that your worthiness yourself had no need of his service, that you would then do well, as an upright man, and be helpful to him with one of your worthiness's good friends who has an enfeoffment [forlening] here in this land, that he might obtain some place. For he knows well the country's condition. And I will not trouble your worthiness longer with this my writing, but will always gladly do what may be of service and good to your worthiness, as Almighty God knows. I will now and always have you eternally commended [to God].

Written at Halsnøy Abbey, the 2nd of April, the year [15]96.
Your humble servant always,
Mads
Jørgensen.

Apparatus

  • Correspondent (sender). Matzs Jørgensßøn = Mads Jørgensen Huus (the surname is supplied from the edition/edition heading, not from the letter's own subscription, which signs only "Mads Jørgensen"). He writes from Halsnøy Abbey in western Norway, evidently in some administrative or household capacity connected to the bailiff Michel Eskelsen, whose daughter he had married. He is otherwise a minor figure; this commendatory letter is his sole appearance in the Brahe correspondence.
  • Recipient. Tyge (Tycho) Brahe of Knudstrup, here addressed by his Danish style and titled høffuidtzmandt paa Uraniborg ("governor / captain at Uraniborg"). At this date (1596) Tycho still held Hven and his crown fiefs; the letter assumes he has fiefs (lehn) at his disposal to bestow, including Nordfjord in Norway.
  • Document type. An autograph letter of recommendation (commendation) for the bearer, Morten Bertelsen, who is to deliver it in person ("this letter-bearer," thenne breffuißer) and then treat with Tycho directly. MS witness A is the autograph at Basel (Universitätsbibliothek, Autographensammlung, Vol. 23, fol. 1).
  • Person recommended. Morttenn Bertilßønn = Morten Bertelsen, the bearer, proposed as administrator of the Nordfjord fief; vouched for as having served nine years under the writer's father-in-law.
  • Person (referee). Michell Eskelßønn = Michel Eskelsen, fogitt y Halsnø Kloster = bailiff (Da. foged) at Halsnøy Abbey; the writer's wife's father, under whom Morten Bertelsen had served.
  • Places. Halsnø Kloster / Halsnøy = Halsnøy Abbey (Halsnøy kloster), the former Augustinian monastery on the island of Halsnøy in Sunnhordland, western Norway, by then a secularised crown estate administered by a bailiff. Nordfiordt = Nordfjord, a district and crown fief in western Norway (Bergenhus region). Knudstrup = Knutstorp (Knudstrup), the Brahe family seat in Scania. Vranniborgh = Uraniborg on Hven.
  • fromhedt — literally "piety / probity"; here, as a chancery courtesy-title for an addressee below royal rank, it functions as an honorific abstraction roughly equivalent to "your worthiness / your good self." Rendered "your worthiness" throughout for consistency; it is not the religious term "piety" in this register.
  • welbyrd[ig]hedt / welbyrdighedt — "well-bornness," the courtesy-abstraction for a welbyrdig (well-born, noble) addressee; rendered "your well-bornness." The first occurrence is contracted in the source (welbyrdhedt); the missing syllable is restored in brackets.
  • juncker — "squire / young lord" (Ger. Junker), a respectful address for a nobleman; kept.
  • lehn / forlening / befalling — lehn = fief / fiefdom (a crown estate held in administration); forlening = enfeoffment (the grant of such a fief); befalling = "command / charge" (here the office of administering the fief). The letter turns on Tycho's power to grant the Nordfjord fief to a deputy.
  • fogitt — bailiff (Da. foged), the resident administrator of a crown estate.
  • skodtzmaall — a testimonial / character-reference (Da. skudsmål), the formal vouching a servant could call upon from a former master; "he may freely refer his testimonial back to him" = Morten may appeal to Michel Eskelsen as a referee.
  • Negation / conditional logic (checked). Three negated clauses carry the letter's force and are reproduced one-for-one: (1) "om ieg widste, att handt war iche throu och guodt, tha skulle ieg jche forschriffue hanom" — a double-negative conditional: "if I knew he were not faithful and good, then I should not commend him" (both negations preserved; the sense is a guarantee, not a doubt). (2) "y nest Gudtz hielp skall hanom jche andit skylde, end thet som erligt … skall werre" — "you shall charge him with nothing other than what shall be honest" (litotes of trust, kept). (3) "om saa er, att ethers fromhedt iche sielff hagde hans tienniste behouff" — the contrary-to-expectation conditional governing the closing plea: "if it be so that your worthiness yourself had no need of his service, [then] you would … be helpful to him [in placing him] with one of your good friends." The mood of hagde (past subjunctive) is hypothetical/polite; rendered "had no need" as a courteous conditional, not as an assertion that Tycho lacks the need.
  • Emphatic io. "skall io bliffue och werre … huldt och trou" — the particle io (≈ "indeed, assuredly") intensifies the assurance; rendered "shall indeed remain and be loyal and faithful."
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1596, German-influenced). Heavy -ff- for -v- (haffuer = haver, breffue = breve, giffuer = giver), w- for v- (Wor = Vor, will = vil, widt = ved), th- (thacker = takker, thill = til), sch-/-ß- (tillschreffuitt, helsßenn, schriffuelße), attj = at I ("that you," plural-polite), denom/hanom = dennem/hannem ("them/him"), io = jo, vdj = udi ("in/at"). The orthography is German-coloured chancery Danish — the kind that misleads automatic language detection (the heuristic tagged it da, correctly here).
  • Calendar / place. Schreffuit vdj Halsnø closter then 2 aprillis anno 96 = "Written at Halsnøy Abbey, the 2nd of April, the year [15]96" (O.S.; Denmark-Norway kept the Julian calendar until 1700). The dateline date (2 April 1596) agrees with the catalogue label; no discrepancy.
  • Correction. Corrected the English's reversed subject/object: Mads made Tycho's own wish known to the letter-bearer Morten Bertelsen, not the bearer's mind known to Tycho (Dreyer, TBOO XIV p.98).

1 defect found:
- mistranslation (medium). The Danish syntax "giffue nogen noget tilkiennde" = "make something known TO someone": "thenne breffuißer" (this letter-bearer) is the dative recipient, and "ethers welbyrdighedtz willie" (your well-bornness's will/wish, genitive-marked) is the thing made known. The direction is reversed in translation: Mads is telling the letter-bearer (Morten Bertelsen) what TYCHO's wish is, not reporting the bearer's mind to Tycho. The published English swaps subject and object, attributing the possessive "willie" to the bearer instead of to Tycho and inverting who informs whom. At: “wherefore I have made this letter-bearer's mind [willie] known to your well-bornness”.

Repaired. Verified against Dreyer TBOO XIV pp.97-99; the sentence appears on p.98, lines 8-11, reading "...huorfor ieg haffuer giffuit thenne breffuißer ethers welbyrdighedtz willie tilkiennde," matching the transcribed original exactly. The Danish dative construction confirms the audit's reading and the English has been corrected accordingly. No other defects found on these three pages.
