The original

[TYCHO BRAHE AD JOHANNEM TAUCHMANNUM]

MEIN freundliche grueß mit wunschung alles guttes zuuor. Erbar vnd vornehmer Hans Tauchman, inbesonder gutter freundt. Eur schreiben, den letsten decemb. forgangen jahr datirt, hab ich mit meinem son Tychoni wohll emptfangen, vnd wie jhr daein förmeldet, daß jhr demselbigen meinen son die 20 thaller, so dem brandenburgiske mathematico Mullero deputirt, solltet haben fölgen lass[en], so bericht er, daß ihr daßselbige woll habt thun wollen, aber ehr hette es nicht begehrt, das es also ist nachgeblieben, seithero eur brieff ist geschrieben worden. Jch hab auch bishero nichs vornummen von demselbigen mathematico Mullero, weiß auch nicht, ob er zu kommen gesint oder nicht. Könnet jhr derowegen vom selbigen gelt nehmen, waß jhr fur mihr außgelegt fur bottelohn vnd brieffe, das vbrige behalten biß auf seiner ankombst oder weitteren bescheidt. Last mich auch wissen, ob da kein brieffue sein ankommen von Lubech vnd Hamburgk oder auch von Wittenbergk; mich wundert, daß eur schwager vnd mein gutter freundt m. Jostelius mihr in so lange zeitt nichts zuschreibt, vnd kain beskeid lest bekommen, woruon ich ihmme zuuor habe zugeschreiben; verstendigt mich derowegen, wie jhme gehet, vnd ob er wiederumb sich befreien will. Jch will in diese tage wieder ein schreibent lassen ahn ihme abgehen, vnd so baldt er daauff antwortet, so verschicket den brieff gen Prag an dem vbersten postmeister, so wehrd ich den wohll bekommen. Vnd thu euch hiemit sambt eure liebe hausfrauue, die von mihr vnd von meinen haußfrauwen folck soll freundlich gegrussen sein, in Gott deß allmechtigen befellen.

Datirt auis dem keyserligen slottz Benattky ihn lanth zu Behemen den 22 thag martij anno 1600.

English translation machine translation, Secure

[Tycho Brahe to Johann Tauchmann.]

My friendly greeting, with the wishing of all good, beforehand. Honourable and distinguished Hans Tauchmann, especially good friend. Your letter, dated the last of December of the past year, I have well received with my son Tycho; and as you therein report that you should have let my said son have the 20 thaler that were deputed to the Brandenburg mathematician Müller — so he reports that you indeed wished to do the same, but that he had not requested it, so that it thus remained undone, since your letter was written. I have also so far heard nothing from the said mathematician Müller, nor do I know whether he intends to come or not. You can therefore take, from that same money, what you laid out for me for messenger-fees and letters, [and] keep the rest until his arrival, or [until] further instruction. Let me also know whether no letters have arrived there from Lübeck and Hamburg, or also from Wittenberg; I wonder that your brother-in-law, and my good friend, Master Jöstel, writes me nothing in so long a time, and lets [me] get no answer to what I wrote him before; inform me, therefore, how he fares, and whether he wishes to marry again. I will, in these days, let a letter go off to him again; and as soon as he answers it, send the letter to Prague, to the supreme postmaster, [and] so I shall surely receive it. And I herewith commend you, together with your dear housewife — who shall be kindly greeted by me and by my housewife's household — to God the Almighty.

Dated from the Imperial castle Benátky, in the land of Bohemia, the 22nd day of March, in the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Johann (Hans) Tauchmann, a contact (at or near Wittenberg) and the brother-in-law of Melchior Jöstel; here handling money and forwarding for Tycho.
  • Content. Business and news: the 20 thaler earmarked for "the Brandenburg mathematician Müller" (an unnamed Müller in Brandenburg service) remained undisbursed (Tycho's son Tycho not having claimed them); Tauchmann may reimburse himself for messenger- and letter-costs and keep the rest pending Müller's arrival. Tycho asks for any letters from Lübeck, Hamburg, or Wittenberg, and — affectionately — after Jöstel (his silence; "whether he wishes to marry again," sich wiederum befreien = to remarry).
  • A nice cross-link. Tycho instructs that Jöstel's reply be sent "to Prague, to the supreme postmaster" (an dem obersten Postmeister) — the very imperial post-prefect whose unreliability Herwart warned against in brahe-t_16000411001 (11 April 1600, three weeks later). Tycho here still trusts the Prague post.
  • mein Sohn Tycho = Tycho Brahe the Younger, the courier; meines Hausfrauen Volk = "my wife's household/people."
  • Calendar. keyserligen Schloss Benátky … den 22 Tag Martij 1600 = 22 March 1600; Bohemia (imperial) = New Style. Header agrees with the label. (A draft, Kladde.) The bracketed lass[en] is an editorial completion of a damaged spot.
