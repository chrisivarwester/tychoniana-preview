The original

[AD RECTOREM UNIVERSITATIS HAUNIENSIS].

MAGNIFICE domine rector. Efterdi jeg formercker, att j mett eders assessoribus icke wilde bevilge mig att maa fange att see, hues j giffue mig aff eders consistorio beskreffuen, førend det bliffuer beseglet, endog ieg icke dett aff eder begierede i nogen ond mening, men snarer till dett beste, andre vitløfftighed och uleyglighed att forekomme, saa kand och wil ieg hertill nu intet andet suare, end j kunde derudi effterfølge eders egen betenkning och mening, og lade mig mett det første fange beskreffuet og beseglet, hvis j vilde unde mig, saa at ieg icke nu lenger skal bliffue opholden, med hvilcket ieg faaer paa denne tid att lade mig nøye. Begierendis derhos, att alle eders acta, hues denne min sag angaaer, maa bliffue paa huer side undertegnet, og siden tillbeseglet og velforvaret, indtil videre beskeden, efftersom behoff giøris, og att der motte bliffue tegnet paa denne skrifft, att den er laest for eder.

Actum Kiøbnehaffn 31. martii 1596.
Thyge Brahe
met egen haand.

English translation machine translation, Probable

[To the Rector of the University of Copenhagen.]

Magnificent lord rector. Since I perceive that you, together with your assessors, would not grant me leave to be allowed to see what you give me set down in writing from your consistory before it is sealed — even though I did not request this of you with any bad intent, but rather for the best, to forestall further prolixity and inconvenience — I therefore can and will answer nothing else to this now than that you may in this matter follow your own judgement and opinion, and let me at the very first have [it] set down in writing and sealed, [namely] whatever you would grant me, so that I shall not now be held up any longer; with which I must for the present let myself be content. Requesting moreover that all your acts touching this my case may be subscribed on each page, and thereafter sealed up and well kept safe, until further notice, as the need shall arise; and that there be noted upon this writing [of mine] that it has been read out before you.

Done at Copenhagen, 31 March 1596.
Tyge Brahe
with [his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender / subscription. Thyge Brahe … met egen haand = "Tyge Brahe … with [his] own hand" — the autograph subscription formula (rendered by the edition's convention conventions for manu propria).
  • Recipient. Addressed to the office of the Rector of the University of Copenhagen (Universitas Hauniensis) and the consistorium (the academic senate of professors). The bracketed Latin heading [AD RECTOREM UNIVERSITATIS HAUNIENSIS] — "To the rector of the University of Copenhagen" — is an editorial supplied title (square brackets as printed in the edition of record), not part of the original letter body; reproduced as printed.
  • Context. Written in the spring of 1596, in the months before Tycho's relations with the Danish establishment broke down and he left the kingdom (1597). The letter concerns a legal/administrative matter (denne min sag, "this my case") in which Tycho had asked to inspect the consistory's written record before it was sealed; the rector and assessors refused, and Tycho here concedes the point while insisting on procedural safeguards (each page subscribed, the whole sealed and kept, and a note that the document was read out to him). No specific person is named; the identification rests at the level of the office.
  • Latin code-switches (within a Danish body). Magnifice domine rector — "Magnificent lord rector," the standard formal academic address to a university rector; kept and translated. assessoribus (abl. pl.) = "assessors" (the consistory members sitting in judgement). consistorio = "consistory" (the university senate). acta = "acts / records" (the official minutes). Actum = "Done / Given" (the dating formula, the edition's convention). Each is a learned Latin technical noun embedded in the Danish, in the chancery manner described in the edition's convention
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1596). Efterdi = "since, inasmuch as"; formercker = formærker ("perceive, observe"); j = I ("you," pl./polite); icke = ikke ("not"); wilde bevilge = ville bevilge ("would grant"); fange att see = "get to see / be allowed to see"; hues / hvis = "what / that which"; beskreffuen / beskreffuet = beskrevet ("written down, set down in writing"); beseglet = "sealed"; endog = "even though, although"; vitløfftighed = vidtløftighed ("prolixity, protracted trouble"); uleyglighed = ulejlighed ("inconvenience"); forekomme = "forestall, prevent"; betenkning = "deliberation, judgement"; opholden = opholdt ("held up, delayed"); lade mig nøye = "let myself be content"; Begierendis derhos = "requesting moreover"; huer side = "each page/side"; undertegnet = "subscribed, signed below"; tillbeseglet = "sealed up"; velforvaret = "well preserved/kept safe"; indtil videre beskeden = "until further notice/instruction"; behoff giøris = "need arises, is required"; motte = måtte ("might"); tegnet paa = "noted/marked upon"; laest for eder = "read [out] to/before you."
  • Negation check. The Danish carries four negations, all preserved in the translation and none added: (1) icke wilde bevilge — "would not grant"; (2) icke dett … begierede i nogen ond mening — "did not request … with any bad intent"; (3) intet andet suare — "answer nothing else"; (4) icke nu lenger skal bliffue opholden — "shall not now be held up any longer." The opening Efterdi … icke wilde bevilge …, saa kand och wil ieg … intet andet suare, end … is a single long causal-conditional period ("Since … would not grant …, I therefore can and will answer nothing else than that …"); its logic is concessive resignation, not a counter-demand, and is rendered as such.
  • Calendar / place. Actum Kiøbnehaffn 31. martii 1596 = "Done at Copenhagen, 31 March 1596" (O.S.; Denmark kept the Julian calendar until 1700). The dateline supplies Copenhagen, although the edition label records the place as s.l. (sine loco, "without place"); the as-written place is followed here, the catalogue's s.l. noted. The dateline date (31 March 1596) agrees with the catalogue date; no discrepancy.
  • Correction. Dropped the unsupported 'for others' from '...to forestall further prolixity and inconvenience for others'; the Danish 'andre' means 'further/additional', not 'for other people'. Verified from the letter's own Danish wording; the supplied Dreyer pages did not contain this letter's text.
