The original

DEM DURCHLEUCHTIGSTEN HOCHGEBORNEN FURSTEN VNDT HERRN, HERRN VLDRICHEN, HERTZOGEN ZU MECKLENBURGK, GRAFFEN ZU SCHWERIN, DER LANDE ROSTOCH VNDT STARGART HERRN, MEINEM GNEDICHSTENN HERRN.

DURCHLAÜCHTIGSTER hochgeborner fürst, gnedichster herr. Ewer Fürst: Gnaden sein meine vnterthenigste gantzwillige dienste iederzeit höchstes fleißes bevor. Vndt soll deroselben abermahl vnterthenigster gueter wohlmainung nit pergen, welchergestallt ich zwar vngezweiffelte hofnung gefast gehabt, es wurde von Ewer Fürst: Gnaden rentmeistern Andres Meyern vnserem wegen dero 10000 thalern auffgerichteten contracts vnd auffkundigungesbrieff (inmaßen sie zue rechter zeit beschehen) zufolge mihr sölliche summa sampt dem interesse gegen vergangenen ostern ohne ainige fernere verzugerung dageraicht vndt gefolget sein. Weiln aber damalß wegen anderer von obgemeldten h: rentmeistern angereugter hohen vndt notwendigen außgaben sölliches nitt geschehen können (wiewohl zuvor die auffkundigung ohne ainigen bedencken ahngenohmen vndt ich meine sacchen alhie darnach gerichtet, auch andere leutte darauff vertrostet, vnd dardurch in keinen geringen schaden gerahten), so hab dennoch E: F: G: zue vnterthenigsten diensten vnd meinem schaden vngeacht ich biß das gegen vergangen s: Johan: der hauptstuel sampt dem interesse richtig erlegt vndt mir zuegestelldt würde (wie dan mehrgedachter h: rentmeister mir abermahl schriftlich versprocchen) mich gedulde[t]. Weiln dan dießer ander termin abermahl lengst verfloßen, alß nehme ich in kainen zweiffell, E: F: G: werden eß durch den rentmeistern schon dahin gedirigirt vnd gebracht haben, daß söllich geldt schon richtig verhanden, damit ich die burgen einzuemahnen nitt genotdrenget werde. Vndt bin derowegen eine gewiße pottschafft alßpaldt derer örtter daßelbige anhero zuholen abzuefertigen ihn willens.

Gelanget derowegen ahn Ewer F: G: mein vnterthenigste gantzfleißige pitt, sie geruhen den gnedichsten befehlich vnd anordnung zue thuen, damit derselbige, sopaldt ehr anlangen wirdt, nit damit auffgehalten, sondern ehist sich sampt dem gelde wiederumb anhero zue verfüegen gefurdert werden muege.

Diß vnd andere gut vnd wohlthatten vmb E: F: G: gehorsambst zue verdienen, will ich mir stetz zum höchsten angelegen sein laßen. Ewer F: G: sampt dero vielgeliepten gemahlin hiemit göttlicher allmacht vnd zue dero fernern gnaden mich vnterthenigst vndt getreulich entpfhelendt.

Datum Prag den 18. julij anno 1601.
Ewer Fürstlichen Ghnaden vnterthenigster gantz williger
Tycho Brahe

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most illustrious, high-born Prince and Lord, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard, my most gracious Lord.

Most illustrious, high-born Prince, most gracious Lord. To Your Princely Grace, my most humble, wholly willing services with the highest diligence, at all times beforehand. And I should not again conceal from the same, in most humble good intention, in what manner I had indeed conceived an undoubted hope that, by Your Princely Grace's rent-master Andreas Meier — pursuant to the contract drawn up over the 10,000 thalers and to the notice-of-recall (inasmuch as these took place at the proper time) — that sum, together with the interest, would be handed over and paid to me against the past Easter, without any further delay. But since at that time, on account of other high and necessary expenditures cited by the aforesaid lord rent-master, this could not be done (although the recall had previously been accepted without any hesitation, and I had ordered my affairs here accordingly, and reassured other people upon it, and thereby came to no small loss), I nonetheless — for Your Princely Grace's most humble service, and notwithstanding my loss — held my patience, until against the past St. John's [Day] the principal together with the interest should be duly paid and delivered to me (as the oft-mentioned lord rent-master again promised me in writing). Since, then, this second term too has long since passed, I have no doubt that Your Princely Grace will already have so directed and brought it about, through the rent-master, that such money is now duly to hand — so that I be not constrained to dun the sureties. And I am therefore minded to dispatch a trusty messenger at once to those parts to fetch the same hither.

There comes therefore to Your Princely Grace my most humble, wholly diligent petition, that you would deign to give the most gracious command and order, that the same [messenger], as soon as he arrives, be not held up with it, but be furthered to betake himself back hither with the money as soon as possible.

This, and other good deeds and benefactions, to deserve most obediently of Your Princely Grace, I will ever let be of the highest concern to me. Your Princely Grace, together with your much-beloved consort, I herewith most humbly and faithfully commend to divine omnipotence and to your further graces.

Given at Prague, the 18th of July, in the year 1601.
Your Princely Grace's most humble, wholly willing
Tycho Brahe
with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender, recipient & register. Tycho Brahe at Prague, escalating the Mecklenburg-debt matter from the rent-master to the sovereign: this is the princely-register companion to the burgher-register letter sent the same day to the rent-master, brahe-t_16010718001 (Tycho→Meier, 18 July 1601). The recipient is Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg(-Güstrow) (1527–1603), given his full titulature — Herzog zu Mecklenburg, Graf zu Schwerin, der Lande Rostock und Stargard Herr. The deferential first/second-person forms (Eure Fürstliche Gnaden, "Your Princely Grace") mark the subject-to-prince register, against the peer-to-peer tone of the Meier letters.
  • The matter (now fully cross-linked). The 10,000-thaler loan (principal + interest), recalled by contract; promised first against Easter, then against St. John's Day (24 June) — both terms now lapsed. Tycho, professing patience and loss, asks the Duke to command that his coming messenger be paid out promptly, so he need not proceed against the sureties (Bürgen). The thread is now complete across five documents: Tycho→Meier (27 May, this 18 July), Tycho→Duke Ulrich (this), Eske Bille→Meier (24 July), Duke Ulrich→Meier (9 Aug).
  • Persons named. Andreas Meier, the rent-master; the Bürgen (citizen-sureties); dero vielgeliebte Gemahlin = the Duke's consort (Elisabeth of Denmark, daughter of Frederik II — herself a connection to Tycho's Danish world).
  • Place. Prag = Prague.
  • Formulae & abbreviations. E: F: G: = Eure Fürstliche Gnaden ("Your Princely Grace"); Hauptstuhl = the principal (capital sum); man: pprj: = manu propria ("with his own hand," autograph). Datum → "Given."
  • Calendar. Dateline den 18. Julij anno 1601; New Style (Tycho's Prague usage; cf. the explicit stylo novo of the same-day Meier letter). The internal terms (Ostern, St. Johannis) read on the same reckoning. Reproduced, not converted.
  • Correction. Latin: 'versfüegen' corrected to 'verfüegen' per Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, p. 227.
