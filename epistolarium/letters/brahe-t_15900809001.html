The original

THYGE BRAHE FICK BREFF NOGEN PENDINGE ANRØRENDIS VT SEQUITUR

Sophia etc.

WOR gunst thilforn. Wider, att ßom j ether haffuer att erindre om thett thußinnd daller, Wij naadigst ether haffue med forstrachtt, huilke wdj thenne neruerendis herredag her wdj Koldinng schulle werrid Oß erlagdtt och bethald, och j wnnderdannigst erre begierrendis formedelst gott folck dag och reßpitt med ßamme pending paa nogen wgger epther Michaelis førstkommendis, tha erre Wij naadigst ther med thillfridtz och wille ether ther med bedage thill fiorthen dage epther for:ne Michaelis førstkommendis. Bedendis ether therforre och wille, attj thill thend thiid wisseligen och wden ald widere forhalling och forßømmelse retther ether epther att erlegge och bethalle Oß for:ne summa pendinge, j wdj ßaa maade thill Oß schyldig er, att Wij thill ßamme thiid wist kunde komme thill Wor bethalling, anßehendis att Wij ßidenn pindtzdag, thill huilken thid the schulle werrid bethald, nu thill for:ne bestempthe dag epther Michaelis aff gode wilge och naadigst benegenhed haffue med ßamme Wor bethalling forthøffuid, attj therforre retther ether ßielff, ßaa Wij thill for:ne thermin wden widere ophold bliffuer hoffuedßummen med renthen fornøyett och thilstilledtt, ßaa framptt Wij jcke schulle foraarßagis wed anndre middell baade hoeß ethers forloffuere och ether att ßøge Wor bethallinng, therßom ther med widerre bliffuer forhalled.

Ther med etc.
Actum Koldinghus thennd 9. augustij anno 1590.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Tyge Brahe received a letter concerning certain monies, as follows:

Sophie, etc.

Our favour beforehand. Know that, as you have to recall concerning the one thousand daler with which We have graciously advanced you, which were to have been paid down and settled to Us at this present assembly of the realm [herredag] here at Kolding — and [as] you do most humbly request, through good people, a day and respite with the same monies for some weeks after the coming Michaelmas — then We are graciously content therewith, and will indulge you therein until fourteen days after the aforesaid coming Michaelmas. Asking you therefore, and desiring [it], that against that time you assuredly, and without any further delay and neglect, dispose yourself to lay down and pay Us the aforesaid sum of money in which you are in such manner indebted to Us, so that at the same time We may surely come to Our payment — considering that We have, ever since Pentecost (by which time they were to have been paid), now [extended] to the aforesaid appointed day after Michaelmas, out of good will and gracious favour, deferred the same Our payment; so that you therefore [may] dispose yourself, so that by the aforesaid term, without further delay, We are satisfied and have handed over to Us the principal sum together with the interest — inasmuch as We should not be obliged, by other means, to seek Our payment both from your sureties and from you, if it be hereby further delayed.

Therewith, etc.
Done at Koldinghus, the 9th of August in the year 1590.

Apparatus

  • Issuer. Sophie of Mecklenburg (1557–1631), dowager queen of Denmark, widow of Frederik II (d. 1588) and mother of the still-minor Christian IV; she resided at Nykøbing and was active in financial administration. Here she acts as a creditor calling in a loan to Tycho. The opening Sophia etc. abbreviates her full royal style.
  • Recipient. Tyge Brahe (the Danish form of Tycho's name), styled simply by name in the register rubric.
  • Document type. A chancery-register entry (Dronning Sophies tegnelser — the queen dowager's register of outgoing letters). The Latin rubric "…NOGEN PENDINGE ANRØRENDIS ut sequitur" ("concerning certain monies, as follows") heads the Danish body — a code-switch preserved by this chapter's convention
  • Substance. A demand for repayment of a 1,000-daler loan (thett thußinnd daller) that the queen had advanced to Tycho. The original term was Pentecost (pindtzdag); she has already deferred it once to the present herredag at Kolding, and now grants a final extension to fourteen days after Michaelmas (29 September O.S. → c. 13 October O.S.). The principal (hoffuedßummen, "the principal sum") plus interest (renthen) is to be repaid by that term, on pain of recourse against both Tycho and his sureties (forloffuere).
  • herredag — a Herredag, the assembly of the Council of the Realm and high nobility (sometimes a supreme-court session); here convened at Kolding. Rendered "assembly of the realm."
  • forloffuere — "sureties / guarantors" (those who stood bond for Tycho's loan); the threat of seeking payment from them as well as from Tycho is the operative legal pressure.
  • Negation count. The body contains exactly one negation, in the closing conditional: ßaa framptt Wij jcke schulle foraarßagis … therßom ther med widerre bliffuer forhalled — "so that We should not be obliged [to seek payment by other means] … if it be hereby further delayed." This is a courteous-but-firm threat: unless you pay, so that she need not pursue sureties. The conditional's force (a veiled warning of enforcement) is preserved; the single negation is reproduced exactly once.
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1590). WOR gunst thilforn = Vor gunst tilforn ("Our favour beforehand," the royal address-formula, this chapter's convention); Wider = Vider (imperative "Know"); ßom j ether haffuer att erindre = som I eder haver at erindre ("as you have to recall"); forstrachtt = forstrakt ("advanced, lent"); wdj = udi ("in"); neruerendis = nærværende ("present"); schulle werrid = skulle været ("were to have been"); erlagdtt och bethald = erlagt og betalt ("paid down and settled"); wnnderdannigst = underdanigst ("most humbly"); formedelst = "by means of, through"; dag och reßpitt = dag og respit ("a day [of grace] and respite"); wgger = uger ("weeks"); førstkommendis = førstkommende ("next-coming"); thillfridtz = tilfreds ("content"); bedage = "to grant a term/respite to"; fiorthen dage = fjorten dage ("fourteen days"); wden ald widere forhalling och forßømmelse = uden al videre forhaling og forsømmelse ("without any further delay and neglect"); retther ether epther = retter eder efter ("dispose / conform yourself to"); for:ne = fornævnte ("aforesaid," the standard chancery abbreviation); anßehendis = ansehendes ("considering"); ßidenn = siden ("since"); bestempthe = bestemte ("appointed, fixed"); benegenhed = bevågenhed ("favour, goodwill"); forthøffuid = fortøvet ("delayed, tarried"); ßielff = selv ("yourself"); thermin = termin ("term, term-date"); ophold = "delay"; fornøyett = fornøjet ("satisfied, contented"); thilstilledtt = tilstillet ("delivered, handed over"); ßaa framptt = såfremt ("inasmuch as / provided that"); foraarßagis = foraarsages ("be caused / obliged"); middell = midler ("means"); hoeß = hos ("with, at the hands of"); ßøge = søge ("to seek / sue for").
  • Calendar / dating. The festival anchors are Old Style: pindtzdag = Pentecost (Whitsun), in 1590 (O.S.) 24 May; Michaelis = Michaelmas, 29 September (O.S.); "fourteen days after Michaelmas" ≈ 13 October (O.S.). Dates are flagged, not converted. Actum "Done/Given" kept by this chapter's convention
  • Place / date crux. The edition label records the place as "København (Copenhagen)", but the source's own dateline reads Actum Koldinghus … 9. augustij anno 1590 — Koldinghus, the royal castle at Kolding (Jutland). The header follows the as-written dateline (Kolding) and appends the catalogued place by this chapter's convention The body confirms Kolding internally (herredag her wdj Koldinng, "this assembly of the realm here at Kolding"), so the catalogue's "Copenhagen" is an error or refers to the register's archival home, not the place of issue. The discrepancy is recorded, not reconciled. The date itself (9 August 1590) is not in dispute.
