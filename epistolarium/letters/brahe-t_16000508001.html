The original

DEM EDELEN, GESTRENGEN VNDT EHRENUEHSTEN GEORGEN VONN WOLFRAMSDORF, ERBSESSEN VFF VOLLMERSHAIN ETC., FURSTLICHEN SECHSISCHEN CAMMER IUNGKHERN ZUE TORGAW, MEINEM INNSONDERS GUNSTIGEN VNDT GUETEN FREUNDT.

EDLER, Gestrenger, Ehrenuehster Innsonders gönstiger vndt guetter Freundt, Dem Herrn sein meine willige geflissene dienste hier wiederumb nach Vermögen zuuor. Vndt wann es demselben glücklich vndt wohl ergienge, wehre es mihr gantz lieb vndt angenehm zuerfahren. Dess Herrn an mich gethanes schreiben, sub dato Torgaw den 3 Martij, habe ich vonn dem H. Doctore IESSENIO wohl empfangen, durch welchen ich auch den herrn hienwieder mit einen schreiben freundtlich zuersuchen nicht hab vndterlassen können. Belangende meinen vndt der Meinigen Zuestandt alhier inn Konnigreich Behemen, ist derselbige vndter Ihr Kays. May. Vnsers allergnedigsten herrn, gnedigsten schutz vndt Gottlob inn guetten wesen, wie der herr mitt mehren Vmbstanden vonn dem obgedachten herrn Doctore IESSENIO, vnserer beider gueten freundt, vernehmen kan. So ich alhier dem herrn im Kaysserlichen hof oder sonsten etwas dienstlich sein kondte, wil ich allzeitt nach meinen Vermögen willig darzue erfunden werden. Von Newer Zeittung weiss ich nichts sonderlichs zueschreiben; allein das man sagt, das der Türck sich gar starck, vndt noch mehr als zuuor, ruste. Vnser gnedigster Kaysser ist noch nicht vonn Pilsen gen Prag wieder ankommen; Man verhoffet aber, das Ihr Maiestet mit dem ehrsten wieder alda sein werde; vndt seindt die Muscowiterischen gesandten, als mann vermeinet, auch dahien gefodert, alda ihr gentzliche abfertigung zueerlangen. Mann ist auch etzlicher anderer gesandten der deutschen Fürsten alda Vermuthen. Diss habe ich also dem herrn vff sein schreiben wiederumb zuerkennen geben wollen. Vndt thue vns hiermitt sambtlichen dem Allmechtigen Gott inn seinen Vätterlichen Schutz vndt beschirmung trewlich befehlen.

Datum vff dem Kayss. Schloss Benatky den 8 Maij Anno 1600.
D. H.
Allzeitt Dienstwilliger

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the noble, gallant and honourable Georg von Wolframsdorf, hereditary lord of Vollmershain etc., Electoral Saxon chamber-junker at Torgau, my especially favourable and good friend.

Noble, gallant, honourable, especially favourable and good friend: my willing and assiduous services to the gentleman beforehand, here again, as far as is in my power. And were it to go happily and well with him, it would be wholly dear and agreeable to me to learn it. The gentleman's letter to me, dated Torgau the 3rd of March, I duly received from Doctor Jessenius, through whom I have likewise not failed to address the gentleman in turn in friendly fashion with a letter. Concerning the state of myself and mine here in the Kingdom of Bohemia: the same is, under the most gracious protection of His Imperial Majesty, our most gracious Lord, and God be praised, in good condition — as the gentleman may learn at greater length, with fuller circumstances, from the aforesaid Doctor Jessenius, the good friend of us both. Should I here be able to be of any service to the gentleman at the Imperial court or otherwise, I will at all times be found willing thereto according to my power. Of fresh news I know nothing particular to write; only that it is said the Turk is arming very strongly, and yet more than before. Our most gracious Emperor has not yet returned from Pilsen to Prague; but it is hoped that His Majesty will be there again at the earliest; and the Muscovite envoys, as is supposed, have also been summoned thither to obtain their final dispatch. There is also expectation there of several other envoys of the German princes. This, then, I wished to make known to the gentleman in return upon his letter. And herewith I faithfully commend us all together to Almighty God, into His fatherly protection and shelter.

Given at the Imperial castle of Benátky, the 8th of May, in the year 1600.
[In the gentleman's service]
Ever ready to serve

Apparatus

  • Sender. Tycho Brahe, at the Imperial castle of Benátky nad Jizerou (Latin Benatecum; the edition's convention), where he was installed in 1599–1600 as imperial mathematician to Rudolf II before moving to Prague.
  • Recipient. Georg von Wolframsdorf, Erbsasse auf Vollmershain, fürstlich-sächsischer Kammerjunker zu Torgau = Georg von Wolframsdorf, hereditary lord of Vollmershain and chamber-junker (Kammerjunker) to the Elector of Saxony, resident at Torgau. He recurs as Cubicularius of the Saxon Elector in the contemporaneous letter to Jessenius (brahe-t_16000507002), where Tycho encloses a letter "to the noble Lord Georg von Wolframsdorf" — i.e. this very letter, dispatched through Jessenius.
  • Persons named.
  • H. Doctor Jessenio = Johannes Jessenius a Jessen (Jan Jesenský, 1566–1621), physician and anatomist, professor at Wittenberg, the mutual friend who carried correspondence between Tycho and the Saxon court and who performed the celebrated public anatomy at Prague in June 1600.
  • Ihr Kays. May. / unser gnädigster Kaiser = Emperor Rudolf II, Tycho's imperial patron.
  • Places & events (the "news"). Pilsen = Plzeň, where Rudolf II's court had withdrawn (in part to escape plague at Prague); his awaited return to Prague is reported as imminent. der Türck … rüstet sich = the Ottoman armament of the Long Turkish War (1593–1606). die Muscowiterischen Gesandten = the Muscovite (Russian) envoys at the Imperial court awaiting their Abfertigung (formal dismissal/dispatch); gesandten der deutschen Fürsten = envoys of the German princes.
  • Epistolary formulae. The chancery opening Dem Herrn sein meine willige geflissene Dienste … zuvor ("my willing assiduous services to the gentleman beforehand") is the courteous-equal register (Wolframsdorf is addressed in the third person as der Herr, "the gentleman," throughout). The subscription D. H. — Allzeitt Dienstwilliger is read as Des Herrn … allzeit dienstwilliger ("[the gentleman's] … ever ready to serve"); the signature proper is not written out in this draft-register text.
  • Calendar. Dateline den 8 Maij Anno 1600; the edition marks the letter n.s. (New Style), consistent with Tycho's shift to Gregorian dating in Imperial service. Reproduced as written, not converted. The recipient's letter is dated Torgaw den 3 Martij (Saxony being a Lutheran territory still on the Julian calendar in 1600 — Protestant Germany did not adopt the Gregorian reform until 1700); the two datelines therefore sit on opposite sides of the 10-day gap, but no conversion is performed.
  • Correction. Corrected the non-word 'meme' to 'meine' in the opening salutation; Dreyer's print (TBOO VIII p.317) reads 'meine' clearly.
