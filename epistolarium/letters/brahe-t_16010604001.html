The original

DEM DURCHLAUCHTIGSTEN HOCHGEBORNEN FURSTEN VND HERRN, HERRN VLDARICH, HERTZOGEN ZU MECHELLENBURGK, FURSTEN ZU WENDEN, GRAFFEN ZU SCHWERIN, DER LANDE ROSTOCH VNDT STARGARDEN HERRN, MEINEM GNEDIGSTEN FURSTEN VNDDT HERRN.

DURCHLÄUCHTIGSTER hochgeborner fürst, gnedichster herr. E: Fürstl: Gnaden sein meine vnterthenigste gantzwillige dienste iederzeit höchstes fleißes bevor. Vndt mag deroselben abermahl auß hochdrengenden vndt erheblichen vrsachen vnterthenigst nit pergen, wie dan auch E: F: Gnaden zweiffelsohn sich nochmahls gnedigst werden wißen zu entsinnen, daß die summa von 10000 thaller hauptstuel sampt dem interesse, so der jungen herschafft zue Mechelburgk von mir gehorsambst vndt guttwillig vorstreckt, ich wegen meiner sonderbaren notturfft vndt ahnligens zu rechter zeit vnsers hierumb auffgerichteten contracts auffgekundiget. Worein dan Ewer F: G: (in maßen es der pilligkait gemeß) so paldt solchs deroselben gehorsambst vorgetragen, gahr kein bedencken gehabt, sondern dero rentmeistern Andreas Meiern ahn stundt die gnedigste ahnordenung gethan, obgemelte summam vndt interesse gegen vergangenen ostern ohne ainigen verzuegk inhabe[r]n meines brieffes richtig wiederumb zueerstatten. Worauff auch (seitemahl sollichs geldt von Rostoch gehn Prag zue bekommen mir bißanhero fast beschwerlich gefallen) ich eß zue meinem vndt den meinigen besten vndt frommen andern leutten alhie, zum theil auch mein blutsverwandter Esche Bilde anderßwo meinentwegen zuegesagt vndt versprochen.

Nun aber wirdt mir von gedachtem E: F: G: rentmeistern wieder meinen verhoffen zuegeschrieben, daß anderer außgeben halben sollichs geldt noch nicht erleget vndt bezahlt werden könne, welchs zwar mir, weiln E: F: G: gnedigster zuesag zufolge ich gegen vergangenen ostern mich mit andern deßwegen eingelaßen, schmertzlich vndt frembd vorkhumpt.

Gelanget derowegen ahn E: F: G: mein vnterthenigste gantz fleißige pitt, E: F: G: wollen nit gescheen laßen, daß mihr ahn meinem biß dato wohlhergebrachten nahmen etwaß abgehen, vndt ich wieder mein wort handeln muße, sondern der gnedigsten befehlich thuen, daß mir zum allerlengster innerhalb nechstkunfftigen s: Johannis tagk mehrgedachtes geldt ohne fernere verzugerung gefollget werden muege.

Hirahn erzaigen E: F: G: mir ein große gnadt, vndt ich binß meiner geringfügigen personen nach iederzeit in vnterthenigster danckparkeit zue erkennen gantzwillig vndt geflißen. Dieselben hiemit sampt dero vielgeliepter gemahlin zu langwahrender gluchklicher regierung göttlicher almacht gehorsambst vndt getreulich entpfhelendt.

Datum Prag den 4 junij a:o 1601.
Ewer Furst: Gnaden vnterthenigster gantzwilliger
Tycho Brahe
manu pprj:

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most serene, high-born Prince and Lord, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard, my most gracious Prince and Lord.

Most serene, high-born Prince, most gracious Lord. To Your Princely Grace [be] my most humble, wholly willing services at all times, with the utmost diligence, beforehand. And I cannot, out of highly pressing and weighty causes, once again most humbly conceal from the same — as also Your Princely Grace will doubtless once more graciously be able to recall — that the sum of 10,000 thalers of principal, together with the interest, which was obediently and willingly advanced by me to the young lordship of Mecklenburg, I called in, on account of my special need and concern, at the proper time of our contract drawn up over this matter. Wherein Your Princely Grace (as accords with fairness), as soon as it was obediently laid before the same, had no hesitation at all, but at once gave to your rent-master Andreas Meier the most gracious order to repay duly the aforesaid sum and interest by the past Easter, without any delay, to the holder of my bond. Whereupon, too (since to bring such money from Rostock to Prague has hitherto fallen out quite burdensome for me), I have pledged and promised it — for my own and my people's best and benefit — to other people here, and in part also my blood-relative Eske Bille has, on my behalf, pledged and promised [it] elsewhere.

But now I am written to by Your Princely Grace's said rent-master, contrary to my expectation, that on account of other expenditures such money cannot yet be laid out and paid — which indeed comes to me as painful and strange, since, in accordance with Your Princely Grace's most gracious promise, I had by the past Easter committed myself to others on that account.

There comes therefore to Your Princely Grace my most humble, wholly diligent request, that Your Princely Grace will not let it happen that anything be lost to my hitherto well-established name [credit], and that I must act against my word, but [will] give the most gracious command that, at the very latest within the coming St. John's Day, the oft-mentioned money may be paid over to me without further delay.

In this Your Princely Grace shows me a great favour, and I am, according to my humble person, at all times wholly willing and zealous to acknowledge it in most humble gratitude. Herewith commending the same, together with your much-beloved consort, to the protection of Almighty God for a long-lasting and happy reign, obediently and faithfully.

Given at Prague, the 4th of June, in the year 1601.
Your Princely Grace's most humble, wholly willing
Tycho Brahe,
with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603), with his full titulature reproduced (Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard) — exactly as in Tycho's invitation letter to the same Duke of 15 April 1601 (brahe-t_16010415003). The MS is the original dispatched letter (Originalbrev), filed at Schwerin under the apt rubric Debita passiva principum — "the passive debts [debts owed] of the princes."
  • Position in the debt thread. This is Tycho's direct personal appeal to the Duke in the 10,000-thaler affair, written from Prague just under five months before his death. It sits between the appeal-to-the-go-betweens (Tycho→Bording, 12 April, brahe-t_16010412001; Tycho→rent-master Meier, 27 May, brahe-t_16010527001) and the later pressing of Meier (18 July, brahe-t_16010718001) and the ducal replies (24 July, brahe-t_16010724001; Ulrich→Meier, 9 August, brahe-t_16010809001).
  • The transaction. Tycho had advanced 10,000 thalers of principal (Hauptstuhl) plus interest to the junge Herrschaft zu Mecklenburg — the young lordship/junior dukes of Mecklenburg — and had lawfully called it in at the contract term. Duke Ulrich had ordered his rent-master Andreas Meier (at Wismar) to repay it by the past Easter to the bearer of Tycho's bond. Relying on that promise, Tycho had re-pledged the money onward to creditors at Prague, and his kinsman Eske Bille (Esche Bilde) had likewise re-promised part of it elsewhere — so the rent-master's now pleading "other expenditures" threatens Tycho's word and credit (mein wohlhergebrachter Name).
  • The deadline. innerhalb nechstkünfftigen S. Johannis Tag — within the coming St. John's Day, 24 June 1601 — the same term named in the 27 May letter to Meier. The remittance difficulty von Rostock gen Prag (getting Baltic money to Bohemia) is the recurring practical snag of the whole affair.
  • Persons named. Duke Ulrich III of Mecklenburg; the junge Herrschaft (junior dukes of Mecklenburg); Andreas Meier, ducal rent-master at Wismar; Eske Bille, Tycho's Danish kinsman and financial agent. The Duke's vielgeliebte Gemahlin ("much-beloved consort") is Anna of Pomerania-Wolgast, Ulrich's second wife (m. 1588) — named cautiously, not asserted from the text.
  • Register & orthography. Deferential chancery Early New High German in the subject's first person to a prince: E: F: G: (Euer Fürstliche Gnaden, "Your Princely Grace") throughout; period spellings (vndt, pitt, pilligkait, gnedigst, vorstreckt, zueerstatten). Subscription manu propria (manu pprj:) marks the autograph. One slip in the witness rubric — E: E: G: for E: F: G: — is the clerk's, not significant.
  • Calendar. Dateline den 4 Junij a:o 1601 at Prague; New Style, per Tycho's imperial-service usage. Reproduced, not converted.
  • Correction. Latin: 'E: E: G:' corrected to 'E: F: G:' per Dreyer, Opera Omnia XIV, p. 224.
