The original

TYGGE BRAHE FICK BREFF NOGEN PENDINGE ANRØRENDIS VT SEQUITUR

Sophia etc.

WOR gunft thilforn. Wider, att ßom j ether haffuer att erinndre om thett thußinnd daller, Wij ether for nogen thiid ßiden naadigst med forstragdtt haffuer och thill ßist forgangen omßlag schulle werrid erlagd och bethald, Wij dog ßamme thiid paa ethers wnderdannigst anfordring haffue ether naadigst bedaged thill thend dag epther pindtzdag ßist forganngen, paa huilken herredagen ßamme thid wor berammid, att the wden widere forhalling ßamme thiid schulle erleggis, haffde och icke annditt Oß thillformoditt, ennd ßligtt ethers løffthe schulle werre schied. Och epther att Oß nu paahennger nogen pendings wdgifft, ßaa Wij thennum therfor haffue fornøden att bruge, bede Wij ether och begierre, attj wille thilthencke, att ßamme summa pendinge wden widere forhalling nu wdj thenne thilstandendis herredagh her wdj Koldinng med renthen kand bliffue Oß erlagd och bethald. Annßehendis att Wij offuer thennd ßist berampthe thiid, thill huilkenn the schulle werrid bethald, nu nogen thid aff gode wilge och naadigstt benegennhed haffue forthøffuedtt, wille Oß ther forre anmode, att the nu wden widere forhallinng bliffuer Oß epther ethers ßiste løffte thilstillett, saa framptt Wij wed anndre middell icke schulle foraarsagis att ßøge Wor bethallinng; j wille ochßaa med thett første Oß thilbiude, huor thill Wij Oß maa forlade, att Wij ther epther kunnde wide Oß att retthe.

Ther med etc.
Actum Kolding thend 18. julij anno 1590.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Tyge Brahe received a letter concerning certain monies, as follows:

Sophie, etc.

Our favour beforehand. Know that, as you have [cause] to recall concerning that thousand daler with which we graciously advanced you some time ago, and which were to have been paid and discharged at the last past omslag [settlement-term], we did nevertheless at that same time, upon your most humble request, graciously grant you a respite to the day after the Whitsun last past, on which the Diet [herredag] was at that same time appointed, [so] that they should without further delay be paid at that time; and [we] had expected nothing else of us than that such a promise of yours should have been kept. And since there is now incumbent upon us a certain outlay of money, so that we have need of it therefor, we ask and desire you that you will take heed [to it], that the same sum of money may now, without further delay, in this approaching Diet here in Kolding, be paid and discharged to us together with the interest. Considering that we have over the last appointed time, by which they were to have been paid, now for some while out of good will and gracious benevolence forborne, we wish therefore to entreat you that they now without further delay be delivered to us according to your last promise, lest we should be caused by other means to seek our payment; you will also at the first offer yourself to us, upon which we may rely, [so] that we may thereafter know how to govern ourselves.

Therewith, etc.
Done at Kolding, the 18th of July, the year 1590.

Apparatus

  • Issuer. Sophia = Sophie of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1557–1631), queen consort of Frederik II and, after his death in April 1588, queen dowager. As the source's sender field records, this is "Dronning Sophie / Queen Sophie." She acts here as a creditor of the crown's household, calling in a loan she had advanced to Tycho.
  • Document type. A chancery-register entry (Dronning Sophies tegnelser, the queen dowager's register). The Latin rubric "…NOGEN PENDINGE ANRØRENDIS ut sequitur" ("concerning certain monies, as follows") heads the Danish body — a code-switch preserved by the edition's convention Sophia etc. and Ther med etc. are the register clerk's standard abbreviations of the full intitulatio and closing protocol.
  • Recipient name. The rubric names the recipient Tygge Brahe (= Tyge Brahe, the Danish form of Tycho); the body addresses him throughout with the courteous plural j / ether ("ye / you").
  • Place discrepancy (flagged, not reconciled). The edition label gives the place as København / Copenhagen, but the dateline reads "Actum Kolding" and the body itself locates the coming Diet "her wdj Koldinng" ("here in Kolding"). The internal evidence is doubly consistent for Kolding (the Jutland royal residence, Koldinghus); the header therefore leads with the as-written place and appends the catalogued place. The label Copenhagen is treated as a catalogue datum, not corrected within the text. Kolding = the town of Kolding in Jutland, seat of Koldinghus castle.
  • Sum and loan. thett thußinnd daller = "that thousand daler"; forstragdtt (= forstrakt) = "advanced, lent." Sophie had lent Tycho 1,000 daler, due at the previous omslag and then deferred.
  • omßlag (= omslag) — the omslag, the semi-annual term (especially the snapsting/omslag at Viborg) on which loans, interest, and accounts fell due in Danish financial practice; kept in the original and glossed "settlement-term."
  • herredag(en) — the herredag (the Danish royal council-court / Diet of the rigsråd, convened by the crown); rendered "Diet" with the term flagged on first use.
  • pindtzdag (= pinsedag) — Whitsun (Pentecost). The deferral ran thill thend dag epther pindtzdag ßist forganngen — "to the day after Whitsun last past" — i.e. Whit Monday of the recent Whitsuntide; the source gives no civil-calendar figure and none is supplied.
  • Negations (verified count = 2, reproduced exactly). (1) haffde och icke annditt Oß thillformoditt, ennd ßligtt ethers løffthe schulle werre schied — rendered "[we] had expected nothing else … than that such a promise of yours should have been kept": one negation carried by icke … annditt … ennd ("nothing else … than"). The clause is a courteous reproach — the queen had assumed the promise would be honoured. (2) saa framptt Wij wed anndre middell icke schulle foraarsagis att ßøge Wor bethallinng — rendered "lest we should be caused by other means to seek our payment": saa fremt … icke is the conditional-negative "in case / lest … not," a veiled threat of other (legal/distraint) recovery. Both negations are reproduced; neither is added or dropped.
  • Conditional / legal force. The operative clause saa framptt Wij wed anndre middell icke schulle foraarsagis att ßøge Wor bethallinng functions as a polite-but-pointed warning: pay now per your promise, so that we are not driven to recover the debt "by other means." The mood is conditional-hortatory; the threat is implicit, and is kept implicit (not sharpened into an explicit legal threat).
  • Orthography (chancery Danish, c. 1590). WOR gunft thilforn = Vor gunst tilforn ("Our favour beforehand," the address-formula, the edition's convention; the long-s gives gunft for gunst); Wider = imperative vider ("know ye"); attj = at I ("that ye"); ether = eder ("you/your"); naadigst = nådigst ("most graciously"); wnderdannigst anfordring = underdanigst anfordring ("most humble request"); bedaged = bedaget ("granted a respite/term"); erlagd och bethald = erlagt og betalt ("paid and discharged"); forhalling = forhaling ("delay"); renthen = renten ("the interest"); forthøffuedtt = fortøvet ("forborne, waited"); thilstillett / thilstillet = tilstillet ("delivered, made over"); saa framptt = såfremt ("provided that / in case"); att retthe (wide Oß att retthe) = at rette ("to govern/conduct ourselves").
  • Calendar / place. Actum Kolding thend 18. julij anno 1590 = "Done at Kolding, the 18th of July, the year 1590" (O.S.; Denmark kept the Julian calendar). Actum "Done/Given" kept by the edition's convention
