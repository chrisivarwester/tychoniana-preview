The original

DEM EDELEN GESTRENGEN EHRENVESTEN HERREN TYCHO BRAHENN AUFF KNUTSTORFF ERBSASSEN, RÖMISCHER KEYSERLICHER MAIESTATASTRONOMO,MEINEM GROSSGUNSTIGEN HERREN.

S.

GENEROSE et doctissime D. TYCHO, misit ante paucas septimanas responsum ad priores tuas literas SIMON a PETKUM. Illud, cum priorem aliquem habere non potuerim, per hunc FRANCISCUM SCHROTERUM Sundensem, Chymicarum rerum studiosum, qui nescio qua de causa iter Pragam instituerat, ad te amandare volui, spero recte redditum iri. De fornacibus quoque advehendis videbo quid efficere potero. Nondum enim quicquam præter eas Hamburgo advectum est, propter nimiam Albis exundationem. Quia vero moris est, ut amici amicis novarum narrationum communicatione gratificentur, mitto D. TYCHONI cuiusdam LL. Doctoris DETHARDI HORSTIJ Witebergæ agentis prognosticon de eventu belli Turcici, sane gratissimum, utinam etiam verissimum. Impingunt nostri Theologi et vani vates omnes in eundem quo Pharisæi et Apostoli olim lapidem, ut vaticinia de CHRISTO eiusque et Ecclesiæ regno et victorijs, politica Phrasi pronunciata, politice intelligant, cum Ecclesia novi Testamenti nullam habeat promissionem politiæ et politici regni ut veteris Testamenti, usque ad CHRISTUM, et CHRISTUS ipse dicat: Regnum meum non est de hoc mundo, item: In mundo afflictionem habebitis, in me autem TANTUM pacem. Stultum quoque hoc est, quod omnes vaticinium de GOG et MAGOG interpretantur de regno Turcico, cum id loquatur historice de victorias Israëlitarum ante CHRISTUM, allegorice vero, ut Apocalypsis IOHANNIS eum declarat, de persecutoribus Ecclesiæ in toto orbe terrarum adventu CHRISTI extremo tollendis, cap. 20 vs. 8: Exibit SATHANAS ut seducat gentes, quæ sunt in QUATUOR angulis terræ, τὸν Γὼγ καὶ τὸν Μάγωγ, qui circumierunt castra Sanctorum et urbem dilectam. Sed descendit ignis a DEO e cœlo, qui devoravit eos etc. Simile delirium est, quod hic interpres idolum vastans interpretatur de MAHOMETE, cum ipse filius DEI dicat Matth. 24 intelligendum esse DANIELEM de signo præcessuro finalem Politiæ Iudaicæ, inprimis templi destructionem, Ideo monet: Cum videritis abominationem vastatricem stantem in loco sancto, ut dictum est a DANIELE propheta tum qui in Iudæa est, fugiat ad montes etc. Ridiculum denique plane est somnium, quod metuunt, ne DANIEL mentiatur, cum definit Monarchias quatuor, si Turca inchoaret quintam Monarchiam, cum prima fuerit Monarchia iam Assyriorum, iam Chaldæorum, in secunda iam in Babylonia, iam in Persia dominabantur Monarchæ, in tertia singuli Duces ALEXANDRI constituebant sibi peculiarem Monarchiam, in quarta alia erat Orientis, quam dudum rapuit Turca, alia Occidentis, quam hactenus mordicus retinet Papa, et vicinorum regulorum potentia ad suam triplicem coronam tuendam simplici credulitate et armis abutitur. Ante vero illa tempora quam varios habuit Monarchas, quorum post annum CHRISTI 480 nemo Romanus aut Italus fuit, quando AUGUSTULUS ultimus purpuram abiecit, eamque induit ODOACER Sax. Post illum anno 495 CHRISTI THEODORICUS Ost-Gotthus dictus Veronensis, inde 574 Longobardorum Reges usque ad CAROLUM Magnum Francum, qui cœpit Imperator Romanorum dici 800 CHRISTI. Unde reliqui Germani titulum, Papa imperium habet hactenus. Et sic digiti pedum in DANIELIS Colosso varie discriminantur sine inchoatione quintæ Monarchiæ, et metuimus, ne Turca sit quintus Monarcha; qui iamdiu est Monarcha Orientis. Sed hæc delirant, qui historiarum sunt ignari, et qui amant, ipsi sibi somnia fingunt. DEUS tueatur hospitia Ecclesiæ, servet verbum suum et confundat consilia Turcæ et Papæ, propter gloriam nominis sui.

Bene in Domino vale.
18 Aprilis nostro 1601.
G. N. T.
addictiss.
GEORGIUS ROLLENHAGEN.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the noble, gallant, and honourable Lord Tycho Brahe, hereditary lord of Knudstrup, Astronomer to the Roman Imperial Majesty, my greatly favourable Lord.

Greeting.

Generous and most learned Lord Tycho: Simon von Petkum sent, a few weeks ago, a reply to your earlier letter. That [reply], since I could have no earlier [carrier], I wished to dispatch to you by this Franz Schröter of Stralsund, a student of chemical matters, who for some cause I know not had undertaken a journey to Prague; I hope it will be duly delivered. Concerning the furnaces also, to be conveyed, I will see what I can effect; for nothing yet but they has been brought from Hamburg, on account of the excessive flooding of the Elbe. And since it is the custom that friends gratify friends by sharing news, I send Lord Tycho a prognostication on the outcome of the Turkish war by a certain Doctor of Laws Dethard Horst, active at Wittenberg — most welcome indeed; would it were also most true.

Our Theologians, and all the vain seers, stumble on the same stone as the Pharisees and the Apostles once did: that they understand politically the prophecies about Christ, and his and the Church's kingdom and victories — pronounced in political phrase — whereas the Church of the New Testament has no promise of a polity and political kingdom, as the Old Testament had up to Christ; and Christ himself says, "My kingdom is not of this world," and again, "In the world you shall have affliction, but in me ONLY peace." This too is foolish, that all interpret the prophecy of Gog and Magog of the Turkish kingdom — whereas it speaks historically of the victories of the Israelites before Christ, but allegorically (as John's Apocalypse declares it) of the persecutors of the Church throughout the whole world, to be destroyed at Christ's final coming — chapter 20, verse 8: "Satan will go out to seduce the nations which are in the FOUR corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, who surrounded the camp of the Saints and the beloved city. But fire came down from God out of heaven, which devoured them," etc. A similar madness it is, that this interpreter expounds the "desolating idol" of Mohammed — whereas the Son of God himself says, in Matthew 24, that Daniel is to be understood of the sign that should precede the final [end] of the Jewish polity, especially the destruction of the temple; therefore he warns: "When you see the abomination of desolation standing in the holy place, as was said by Daniel the prophet, then let him who is in Judaea flee to the mountains," etc. Finally, it is a plainly ridiculous dream that they fear lest Daniel lie — when he defines four Monarchies — if the Turk should begin a fifth Monarchy: whereas the first Monarchy was now of the Assyrians, now of the Chaldeans; in the second the Monarchs ruled now in Babylonia, now in Persia; in the third Alexander's individual generals each set up his own Monarchy; in the fourth, one [Monarchy] was of the East, which the Turk long ago seized, another of the West, which the Pope hitherto retains tooth and nail, abusing the power of the neighbouring princelings — by their simple credulity and by arms — to defend his triple crown. And before those times, how many and various Monarchs it had! — of whom, after the year of Christ 480, none was a Roman or an Italian, when Augustulus, the last, cast off the purple, and Odoacer the Saxon put it on; after him, in the year of Christ 495, Theodoric the Ostrogoth, called of Verona; then, from 574, the Kings of the Lombards, up to Charlemagne the Frank, who began to be called Emperor of the Romans in [the year] 800. Whence the Germans have kept the title, [while] the Pope has held the [imperial] power hitherto. And thus the toes of the feet in Daniel's Colossus are variously distinguished, without the beginning of a fifth Monarchy — and [yet] we fear lest the Turk be a fifth Monarch, who has long been Monarch of the East! But these things they rave who are ignorant of the histories; and those who are fond [of such fancies] fashion dreams for themselves. May God protect the dwellings of the Church, preserve his word, and confound the counsels of the Turk and the Pope, for the glory of his name.

Fare well in the Lord.
18 April, by our [style], 1601.
Most devoted to Your Generous Nobility,
Georg Rollenhagen.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Georg Rollenhagen (1542–1609), rector of the Magdeburg school (Altstädtisches Gymnasium), Lutheran humanist and poet (author of Froschmeuseler), Tycho's "especial friend" and Magdeburg agent (cf. brahe-t_16000404001, brahe-t_16001009001). The German address styles Tycho "Astronomer to the Roman Imperial Majesty."
  • The practical opening. Rollenhagen forwards a reply from Simon von Petkum (Tycho's Hamburg factor; cf. brahe-t_16000422001) by the hand of Franz Schröter of Stralsund (Sundensis), "a student of chemical matters" bound for Prague; reports that Tycho's furnaces (the alchemical fornaces) are the only thing yet brought from Hamburg, the Elbe flooding having stalled the rest; and encloses, as news, a prognostication on the Turkish war by the Wittenberg jurist (LL. Doctor = Legum Doctor) Dethard Horst — "would it were as true as it is welcome."
  • Significance — a learned anti-apocalyptic, anti-Roman polemic. The body is a vigorous humanist critique of the literal-political reading of Biblical prophecy then rampant in war-anxious Germany. Rollenhagen argues:
  • Theologians err like "the Pharisees and Apostles" in taking the prophecies of Christ's kingdom politically, though "My kingdom is not of this world" (John 18:36) and "in the world you shall have affliction, but in me only peace" (John 16:33).
  • Gog and Magog (with the Greek τὸν Γὼγ καὶ τὸν Μάγωγ preserved) is not the Turk but, historically, Israel's pre-Christian wars, and allegorically (per Revelation 20:8–9) the Church's persecutors destroyed at the Last Day.
  • The "abomination of desolation" (Daniel; Matthew 24:15–16) is not Mohammed but the sign of the destruction of the Jewish temple.
  • Daniel's four monarchies (the dream-statue of Daniel 2) do not leave room for a Turkish "fifth": he sketches the sequence — Assyrian/Chaldean → Babylonian/Persian → the Diadochi (Alexander's generals) → the divided fourth (East, seized by the Turk; West, held by the Pope) — and runs the Western imperial succession from Romulus Augustulus (476/"480") and Odoacer, through Theodoric the Ostrogoth and the Lombard kings, to Charlemagne (800) and the German emperors, "the Pope [holding] the [imperial] power hitherto." The "toes of Daniel's Colossus" are merely the divided kingdoms, "without the beginning of a fifth Monarchy." "These things they rave who are ignorant of the histories."
  • A pointed anti-papal Lutheran edge throughout (the Pope's "triple crown," "tooth and nail," abusing princes' credulity), closing: "God … confound the counsels of the Turk and the Pope."
  • Scripture & figures identified. John 18:36; John 16:33; Revelation 20:8–9 (Gog and Magog); Matthew 24:15–16 (abomination of desolation, citing Daniel); Daniel 2 (the four-monarchy statue with feet/toes of iron and clay). Historical figures: Romulus Augustulus, Odoacer ("the Saxon"), Theodoric the Ostrogoth ("of Verona" = Dietrich von Bern), the Lombard kings, Charlemagne.
  • Greek. τὸν Γὼγ καὶ τὸν Μάγωγ — "Gog and Magog"; kept verbatim in the source block, translated in the body.
  • Persons named. Georg Rollenhagen (sender); Simon von Petkum; Franz Schröter of Stralsund; Dethard Horst (Wittenberg jurist); plus the Scriptural/historical figures above.
  • Calendar. Dateline "18 Aprilis nostro 1601" — nostro [stylo], "by our [Old] style": Lutheran Magdeburg kept the Julian calendar (the nostro explicitly marks the O.S./N.S. divide from Tycho's imperial New Style). No place in the dateline (edition s.l.); "[Magdeburg]" supplied as the evident origin (Rollenhagen's seat). Header marked [O.S.].
