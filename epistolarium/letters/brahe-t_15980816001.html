The original

COPIA LITERARUM ILLUSTRISSIMI PRINCIPIS, DOMINI VDALRICI DUCIS MEGAPOLENSIS AD IMPERATOREM.

ALLERDURCHLEUCHTIGSTER, großmechtigster und unuberwindtlichster römischer kayser. E: Kay: Majtt: ßeindt meine allerunterthenigste gehorßamste bereitwillige dienste jederzeit zuvor. Allergnedigster herr, E: Rom: Kay: Maj:tt gebe ich hiemit allerunterthenigst zuerkennenn, daß gegenwertiger der ehrnveste Tycho Brahe mit seinem gantzen haußweßen auß seinem vaterlandt, dem königreich Dennemarcken, gezogen, nicht aber darumb alß ob er da nicht woll hette bleiben mugenn, oder auch in einiger ungnade bey der Kön: Maj:stt daßelbst ßein ßolte, sondernn alleinn aus denn urßachen, nachdem er vermercket undt vernommenn, daß die astronomische sachen, die er inn ßo viel iahren mit großer mühe undt unkost alda angerichtet undt getribenn, wie vor beschehen, wieder ßein verhoffenn nicht geachtet noch befürdert werden mögen, darumb er sich selbst willig inn Teutschlandt weitere und beßere gelegenheit dazu zu suchen begebenn. Unndt ob ich woll derowegenn, alß er sich inn meiner stadt Rostock aufgehaltenn, an die Kön: M: zu Dennemarcken, meinen freundtlichen hertzvielgeliebten herrn sohn, ein freundtlich unndt fleißigs erinnerunges schreibenn gethan, dieße astronomische sachen dero königreich zu ehren zu restituiren unndt zu unterhaltenn, weil aber darauff nun einn ganzes iahr verfloßenn, unnd ich nicht spühren könnenn, daß er revocirt werde, unndt gleichwoll solche herrliche kunst, an sich selbst werth, daß sie unterhalten unndt promoviret werde unnd großer potentatenn patrocinii woll bedarff, unnd schade, das dieß so weit numehr gebrachte werch inn ein abnehmenn kommen solte, dan ich bekennenn muß, das mir dergleichenn instrumenta mathematica, alß ich bey ihm auff der insull Huen im königreich Dennemarcken gesehenn, an andern örtern niemalß vorkommenn: Alß habe ich nebenst andern vor guth angesehenn, nachdem es im reich Dennemarcken nicht mehr geschehenn kann, das sie irgendt im Teutschlandt gebürlichen unterhalten unnd foviret würde, wie dießelbige vonn alters hero allezeit von vielen königen unndt potentaten nach außweißung der löblichen exempel, so E: Kay: Maj:stt auß den historien, mehr dann gnugsamb bekandt, lieb unndt in würdenn gehaltenn worden, unndt weil dann E: Kay: Maj:st, alß daß oberhaubt deß heiligenn römischenn reichs, ein ßonderliche zuneigung zu offtberürter kunst tragen, so habe ich demnach nicht unterlaßen können, E: Röm: Kay: Maj:st ermelten Tychonem Brahen mit derselbigenn weitberümbtenn kunst allerunterthenigst zu recommendiren, unndt bitte E: Röm: Kay: Maj:st darauff unterthenigstes höchstes fleißes, sie wolle es nicht anderß dann gnedigst vermerckenn, unndt sich mehrgedachtes Tychonis Brahen unndt seines herrlichen studii allergnedigst annehmenn unndt ihm alle kayserliche guthe befurderung widerfahrenn lassenn. Daß binn ich umb E: Kay: Maj:st (ßo ich damit göttlicher almacht zu aller glücksehligen wolfart, derselbenn aber mich auch zu fernern kayserlichen gnaden, allerunterthenigst unnd getreulich befehle) jederzeit unterthenigst unnd gehorsambst zuverdienen willig unnd geflissenn.

Datum Gustrow den 16. augusti anno 1598.
E: Röm: Kay: Maj:ts unterthenigster unnd gehorsamer furst
Ulrich
herzog zu Meckelnburgk.

English translation machine translation, Secure

Copy of the letter of the Most Illustrious Prince, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, to the Emperor.

Most serene, most mighty, and most invincible Roman Emperor. To Your Imperial Majesty [be] my most humble, most obedient, and ready services at all times, beforehand. Most gracious Lord: I herewith most humbly give Your Roman Imperial Majesty to know that the present honourable Tycho Brahe has departed, with his whole household, from his fatherland, the kingdom of Denmark — not, however, as though he could not well have remained there, or should be in any disgrace with the Royal Majesty there, but solely for the reasons that, after he had noted and learned that the astronomical matters which he had over so many years set up and pursued there, with great labour and expense, could — contrary to his hope — no longer be valued nor furthered as before, he therefore willingly betook himself into Germany to seek further and better opportunity for it. And although I therefore, while he stayed in my city of Rostock, sent to the Royal Majesty of Denmark, my dear and heartily-beloved Lord son, a friendly and earnest letter of reminder, that he restore and maintain these astronomical matters to the honour of his kingdom — yet, since a whole year has now elapsed thereupon, and I have not been able to perceive that he is recalled; and since such a splendid art is in itself worthy that it be maintained and promoted, and has good need of the patronage of great potentates; and [since it would be] a pity that this work, now brought so far, should fall into decline — for I must confess that the like mathematical instruments, such as I saw with him on the island of Hven in the kingdom of Denmark, have never come before me in any other place — therefore I, together with others, have deemed it good that, since it can no longer happen in the realm of Denmark, it be fittingly maintained and fostered somewhere in Germany; as the same [art] has from of old always been held dear and in honour by many kings and potentates, according to the showing of the praiseworthy examples more than sufficiently known to Your Imperial Majesty from the histories. And since Your Imperial Majesty, as the head of the Holy Roman Empire, bears a special inclination toward the oft-mentioned art, I could not therefore omit most humbly to recommend the said Tycho Brahe, with that same far-famed art, to Your Roman Imperial Majesty; and I ask Your Roman Imperial Majesty thereupon, with most humble and utmost diligence, that you would take it in no other than the most gracious way, and most graciously take up the oft-mentioned Tycho Brahe and his splendid study, and let all imperial good furtherance befall him. For which I am, toward Your Imperial Majesty (whom I herewith most humbly and faithfully commend to divine omnipotence for all blessed welfare, and myself to your further imperial graces), at all times most humbly and obediently willing and zealous to deserve well.

Given at Güstrow, the 16th of August, the year 1598.
Your Roman Imperial Majesty's most humble and obedient prince,
Ulrich,
Duke of Mecklenburg.

Apparatus

- Significance — the imperial commendation itself. This is the copy (Copia literarum) of Duke Ulrich III's letter recommending Tycho to Emperor Rudolf II — the "splendid letter of commendation to the Roman Imperial Majesty" that Tycho thanks Ulrich for in brahe-t_15980914002 (14 September 1598). Written from Güstrow, 16 August 1598, during Tycho's stay at Rostock, it is a cornerstone document of the campaign that brought Tycho to the imperial court (cf. the later imperial-establishment letters: brahe-t_16000106001, brahe-t_16000324001).
- Sender. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603). He addresses Christian IV of Denmark as "meinen freundtlichen hertzvielgeliebten Herrn Sohn" ("my dear, heartily-beloved Lord son") — a courtesy kinship-term: Christian IV was in fact Ulrich's grandson (Ulrich's daughter Sophie of Mecklenburg married Frederik II; Christian IV was their son). The dynastic-courtesy force is kept literal with this note, by the audit's rule (no silent "correction").
- The argument Ulrich makes to the Emperor.
1. Tycho left Denmark neither in disgrace nor for inability to stay, but solely because his astronomical work "could no longer be valued nor furthered as before" — the diplomatic framing of the rupture (consonant with Tycho's own account, e.g. brahe-t_16000323001).
2. Ulrich had reminded Christian IV (during Tycho's Rostock stay) to "restore and maintain" the work "to the honour of his kingdom" — but a year passed with no recall.
3. First-hand testimony: "the like mathematical instruments, such as I saw with him on the island of Hven, have never come before me in any other place" — Ulrich had personally visited Tycho on Hven and seen the instruments. A valuable independent witness to the Uraniborg installation.
4. The appeal to Rudolf II as "head of the Holy Roman Empire," himself inclined to the art, with the exempla of kings and potentates who patronised astronomy "from the histories" — and the request that the Emperor take Tycho and his study under imperial patronage and furtherance.
- Persons named. Ulrich III of Mecklenburg (sender); Rudolf II (addressee); Christian IV of Denmark ("Herr Sohn" = grandson); Tycho Brahe; the island of Hven; Tycho's stay at Rostock.
- Register / formulae. High imperial-petition German (Allerdurchlauchtigster … unüberwindlichster römischer Kaiser; E: Kay: Maj:tt = Euer Kaiserliche Majestät). Latin terms embedded (restituiren, promoviret, patrocinii, recommendiren, instrumenta mathematica) by the edition's convention The heading Copia literarum marks this as the archival copy in Langebek's Diplomatarium.
- Calendar. Dateline Güstrow den 16. Augusti anno 1598; Lutheran Mecklenburg kept the Julian (Old Style) calendar. Header agrees with the label.
