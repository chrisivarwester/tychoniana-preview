The original

NOBILI AC CLARISSIMO AMICO NOSTRO HONORANDO DOMINO TYCHONI BRAHE.
Nobilis et clarissime Domine.

QUIS typographus scriptum hoc cuius exemplar nobis D.V. communicauit et cuius authorem se Nicolaus Reimarus Vrsus Dietmarsus esse fatetur absque consensu nostro in hac urbe impresserit, scire non possumus. Sed ipsum inuestigari et, si repertus fuerit, ut alijs in exemplum debito modo in eundem animadvertatur et exemplaria, si quae penes eundem sunt, auferantur curabimus. Cum autem praememoratus Vrsus sub jurisdictionem illustrissimi d. mareschalci aulici pertineat, poterit apud ipsum D. V:ra instare ac sollicitare, quo eidem Vrso ex officio isto demandetur, ut et typographum indicet et exemplaria, si quae habet, exhibeat. Nos qua ratione a nobis fieri potest ac debet rem iuvare non intermittemus.

Datum Pragae in nostra archiepiscopali residentia, die 27 julii a:o MDC.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To our noble and most distinguished friend, to be honoured, Master Tycho Brahe.
Noble and most distinguished Lord.

Which printer printed in this city, without our consent, this writing — a copy of which Your Lordship has communicated to us, and whose author Nicolaus Reimarus Ursus the Dithmarsian confesses himself to be — we cannot know. But we shall see to it that he himself be investigated, and, if he be found, that he be punished in due manner, as an example to others, and that the copies, if any are in his keeping, be taken away. But since the aforementioned Ursus pertains to the jurisdiction of the most illustrious Lord Court-Marshal, Your Lordship will be able to press and solicit with him, so that it may be enjoined upon that Ursus, from that office, both to indicate the printer and to produce the copies, if he has any. We, in what manner it can and ought to be done by us, will not fail to aid the matter.

Given at Prague, in our archiepiscopal residence, on the 27th day of July, in the year 1600.

Apparatus

  • Sender. Zbyněk Berka of Dubá (Zbyněk Berka z Dubé, 1551–1606), Archbishop of Prague (1592–1606) — the Berka Zbyněk of the edition.
  • Subject — the Ursus affair. Nicolaus Reimarus Ursus Dietmarsus = Nicolai Reimers Bär, called Ursus, of Dithmarschen (c. 1551–1600), Imperial Mathematician at Prague and Tycho's bitter adversary, whom Tycho accused of plagiarising his geoheliocentric world-system. The "writing" (scriptum) is the defamatory anti-Tycho tract (the Tractatus / De astronomicis hypothesibus … defensio), printed at Prague; Tycho had sent the Archbishop a copy and was pursuing its suppression. The Archbishop disclaims knowledge of the printer, promises to investigate and confiscate, and directs Tycho to the secular authority that actually has jurisdiction over Ursus.
  • mareschalcus aulicus = the Court Marshal (Obersthofmarschall), the imperial-court officer under whose jurisdiction Ursus (as an imperial servant) fell; the Archbishop's ecclesiastical authority could not reach him directly.
  • Witness. The sole witness is the draft (Kladde) in the Archbishop's Archive at Prague — i.e. the chancery retained copy, hence the editorial first-person plural (nos … curabimus).
  • Calendar. die 27 julii MDC = 27 July 1600; Prague (imperial / Catholic) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
