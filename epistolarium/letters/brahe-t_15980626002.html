The original

AN BARUITIUM, JHRER KAY: MAYTT: GEHEYMEN SECRETARIUM.

Ernst etc.

WAS Wir jhrer kay: maytt: wegen des weitberuhmbten Tychonis Brahe vnterthenig zuschreiben, das hastu ab dem einschluss zu[v]ernehmen. Jst darauf Vnser gnedigst gesinnen an dich, wan du vernehmen wurdest, das solche schreiben jhrer kay: maytt: vberantworttet werdenn, das du pro uiribus daran vnd darob seyst, damit dieser ehrlicher man balt bescheidt auf seine æquissimas prætensiones haben möge, dann diese zeugnuss Wir ihme geben können, das ehr unicus restaurator hujus laudatissimæ scientiæ, vnd das Vnser patria Germanica jhme selbst nit allein congratuliren, sondern apertis ulnis et brachijs zu empfahen geflissen sein soll. Vndt weil sein personn vnd studia dermassen per uniuersum orbem beruhmbt, auch vnter andern dem gewesenen vicecansellario Kurzio gar woll bekandt gewest, so achten Wir vor vnnöttig, dir sein personn weiter zu recommendiren. Allein haben Wir dir auch zu erkennen geben wollen, das Wir jhme Tychoni Brahen, tum propter conformia studia tum propter eius insignia erga artem mathematicam merita, mit sondern gnaden zugethan sein, alsso das Wir Vnß zu dir gnedigst versehen, du werdest jhnen auch von vnserntwegen bey j: kay: maytt: gehorsamblich zurecommendiren nit vnterlassenn, vnndt Wir bleiben dir mit gnaden gewogen.

Luttich den 26. iunij 98.
Ernst, ertzbischoff zu Cöllenn.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To Barvitius, His Imperial Majesty's secret secretary.

Ernst, etc.

What we are humbly writing to His Imperial Majesty concerning the far-famed Tycho Brahe, that you will gather from the enclosure. Thereupon it is our most gracious desire of you that, when you perceive that such writings are delivered to His Imperial Majesty, you be, to the best of your powers [pro viribus], at it and over it, so that this honourable man may soon have a decision upon his most equitable claims [aequissimas praetensiones]; for this testimony we are able to give him: that he is the sole restorer of this most praiseworthy science [unicus restaurator huius laudatissimae scientiae], and that our German fatherland [patria Germanica] should not only congratulate itself on him, but should be zealous to receive him with open arms and embrace [apertis ulnis et brachiis]. And since his person and studies are so renowned throughout the whole world [per universum orbem] — and were, among others, very well known to the late vice-chancellor Kurz [Curtius] — we deem it unnecessary to recommend his person further to you. Only, we wished also to make known to you that we are devoted to him, Tycho Brahe, with especial grace — both on account of [our] conformable studies and on account of his signal merits toward the mathematical art [tum propter conformia studia tum propter eius insignia erga artem mathematicam merita] — so that we most graciously trust in you that you, on our behalf also, will not fail obediently to recommend him to His Imperial Majesty; and we remain graciously inclined toward you.

Liège, the 26th of June, [15]98.
Ernst, Archbishop of Cologne.

Apparatus

  • The third Cologne letter of 25–26 June 1598. With brahe-t_15980625002 (to Tycho) and brahe-t_15980625001 (to Rantzau), this completes Elector Ernst of Bavaria's coordinated push: here he instructs the imperial secretary to expedite Tycho's suit once the Elector's memorial reaches the Emperor.
  • Recipient. Johann Anton Barvitius (Barvitius, c. 1555–1620), Geheimsekretär (privy secretary) to Rudolf II — the official who controlled access to the Emperor's desk. Ernst presses him to ensure Tycho gets a prompt favourable Bescheid (decision) on his aequissimae praetensiones ("most equitable claims," i.e. for a post and stipend).
  • The praise. unicus restaurator huius laudatissimae scientiae — "the sole restorer of this most praiseworthy science" (Tycho's instauratio astronomiae, per glossary); the patria Germanica should receive him "with open arms" (apertis ulnis et brachiis).
  • vicecancellarius Kurzius = Jakob Kurz von Senftenau (Jacob Curtius), the late Imperial Vice-Chancellor (d. 1594), who had known Tycho's work (and who reappears as Herwart's intimate friend in brahe-t_16000908001).
  • Code-switching. A German chancery letter saturated with Latin technical/legal tags (pro viribus, aequissimas praetensiones, unicus restaurator …, apertis ulnis et brachiis, per universum orbem, conformia studia, insignia … merita); each is rendered into English and flagged as the Elector's own Latin within the German. tum propter conformia studia — "both on account of [our] conformable/like studies": Ernst, himself astronomically inclined, claims a shared pursuit.
  • Calendar. Lüttich [Liège] den 26. iunij 98 = 26 June 1598; Liège/Cologne (Catholic) = New Style. Header agrees with the label.
