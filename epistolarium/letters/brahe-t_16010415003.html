The original

DEM DURCHLAUCHTIGSTEN HOCHGEBORNEN FURSTEN VNDT HERRN, HERRN VLDARICH, HERTZOGEN ZU MECHELLENBURGK, FURSTEN ZU WENDEN, GRAFFEN ZU SCHWERIN, DER LANDE ROSTOCH VNDT STARGARTTEN HERRN, MEINEM GNEDICHSTEN FURSTENN VNNDT HERRNN.

DURCHLÄUCHTIGSTER hochgeborner fürst, gnedigster herr. Ewer Furstl: G: sein meine vnterthenigste gantzwillige dienste iederzeit höchstes fleißes bevor.

Vndt soll deroselben vntherthenigst nitt pergenn, daß ich aine von meinen vielgeliepten töchtern mit nahmen Elizabet Brahe ainem westpfhälischen edelman Franzen Tengnagel zum Campe ehelich versprochen, auch nechst göttlicher hülff zwischen ostern vndt pfhingsten nechstkünfftich ihre hochzeit zu macchen vorhabens bin. Vndt dieweil nit allein die röm: kayß: may:, vnsern allergnedichsten herrn, ich allervnterthenigst sampt etzlichen deroselben vornehmen vndt hochweißen rhätten, sondern auch etzliche fürsten in Deutschlandt, baid eccleßastici vndt politici status, welche ich weiß, mir in gnaden bewogen zu sein, dazue zu beruffen in willens, hab auch solliches Ewer F: G: alß meinem iederzeit gnedichsten fürsten vndt herrn gehorsambst zu verstendigen vnd sie darauff zu laden nitt vmbgehen können. Nitt daß ich begehren kan oder darff, daß weder die andre fürsten noch E: F: G: ainen von denseinigen sölliche aine weitte reiße anhero schicken solte, sondern ihm fahll etwa dieselbe ainigen von ihren leutten alhie ahm hoffe hetten, daß von E: F: G: wegen der alsdan diesen nuptialem actum sua præsentia cohonestieren möchte.

Woran nur zum hohen ahngenehmen vnd gnedichsten gefallen geschehe, vnd bins meinem geringen vermög nach stets vnterthenigst vndt danckparlich zu erkennen gantz willig vnd gefließen. E: F: G: sampt dero vielgeliepten gemählin göttlicher allmacht abermahl vnterthenigst vnd getreulich empfhelendt.

Datum Prag auß der Curtianiske hauße den 15 dag aprilis anno 1601 stylo nouo.
Ewer F: G: vntherthenigster ganz williger
Tycho Brahe
Eigenhn.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the most serene, high-born Prince and Lord, Lord Ulrich, Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard, my most gracious Prince and Lord.

Most serene, high-born Prince, most gracious lord. To Your Princely Grace be my most humble, entirely willing services at all times, of the highest diligence, beforehand.

And [I] shall not most humbly conceal from the same, that I have betrothed one of my much-beloved daughters, by name Elisabeth Brahe, to a Westphalian nobleman, Frans Tengnagel to Camp, and intend, next with God's help, to make their wedding between Easter and Pentecost next coming. And since I am minded to summon to it not only the Roman Imperial Majesty, our most gracious lord — most humbly — together with several of the same's distinguished and most wise councillors, but also several princes in Germany, both of the ecclesiastical and the political estate, who I know are graciously inclined toward me: I have also not been able to omit obediently to inform Your Princely Grace, as my ever most gracious Prince and Lord, of such, and to invite you thereto. Not that I can, or may, desire that either the other princes or Your Princely Grace should send one of [your] people so long a journey hither; but [rather] in case the same [you] should perhaps have any of your people here at court, [so] that [one of them] might then, on Your Princely Grace's behalf, honour this nuptial act with his presence [nuptialem actum sua praesentia cohonestieren].

Whereby would happen [something] only to [my] high agreeable and most gracious pleasure; and I am, according to my slight ability, ever most humbly and thankfully entirely willing and assiduous to acknowledge [it]. Commending Your Princely Grace, together with your much-beloved consort, once again most humbly and faithfully to God's Almighty [protection].

Given Prague, from the Curtius house, the 15th day of April, in the year 1601, new style.
Your Princely Grace's most humble, entirely willing,
Tycho Brahe,
[his] own hand.

Apparatus

  • Recipient. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow (1527–1603) — the same Duke who, three months later, writes to Tycho about the 10,000-thaler debt (brahe-t_16010724001). His full Mecklenburg titulature (Duke of Mecklenburg, Prince of the Wends, Count of Schwerin, Lord of the lands of Rostock and Stargard) is reproduced. The witness is in the Schwerin Familien-Akten: Brahe.
  • A wedding invitation — companion to brahe-t_16010404001. The second of Tycho's surviving invitations to the wedding of his daughter Elisabeth Brahe to Frans Tengnagel ("a Westphalian nobleman, Frans Tengnagel to Camp"), to be held at Prague between Easter and Pentecost 1601 (cf. the invitation to Holger Rosenkrantz, brahe-t_16010404001, 4 April 1601). Tycho says he is inviting the Emperor and his councillors, and "several princes of Germany, both ecclesiastical and political"; he tactfully asks the Duke only to let a courtier already at Prague represent him and "honour this nuptial act with his presence" — not to send anyone the long journey.
  • nuptialem actum sua praesentia cohonestieren — a Latin code-switch within the German (kept and italicised, by the edition's convention): "to honour the nuptial act/ceremony with his presence."
  • Place. aus der Curtianischen Hause = the Curtius house at Prague (the former mansion of Vice-Chancellor Jakob Curtius), Tycho's residence — the same venue named in brahe-t_16010404001.
  • Calendar. Prag … den 15 dag aprilis anno 1601 stylo novo = 15 April 1601, New Style (explicit); Prague, imperial. Header agrees with the label.
