The original

ERLIG VELBYRDIG QVINDE, METTE HANS MULIS, BOENDE PAA NISLEFFGAARD, MIN TILFORLADENDE SYNDERLIGE GODE VEN, DETTE VENLIGEN TILHÆNDE.

MIN gantske wenlig hilsen nu och altidt forsendt med Wor Herre. Kiere Mette Mulis, tilforladendis synderlige gode wen. Nest min gantske wenlig och store taksigelse for alt megit beuist gode, huilckit at forskylde ieg stedse wil findis aff min yderste formue gantske willig och redebon till vdi alle de maader, ieg nogen tid kand giøre nogit, som eder til aere och tieniste kand were. Och skal j ochsaa haffue saa megen stor aere och tak for eders gode skriffuelse, forn ieg bekom fran eder, der wii ware till Witenberig, det war mig saa megit kiert, at ieg fick eders breff, och kunde forfare, horledis eder och eders kiere mand med eders liide. Och skulde ieg wel for lang tid siden haffue skreffuet eder til udigen, mens ieg haffuer siden slet ingen wisse budt kunde fangit till eder; derfore kunde ieg icke lade, at ieg jo nu skulde skriffue eder till udigen, effterdi at mig stedis nu saa wisse budt ind udi Danmarck med eders kiere søn Clauß Mule och min broder Tyge, hwilcke min kiere fader haffuer sendt did ind i landet till hans slecht och gode wenner, hans aerende att bestille. Kiere Mette, anlangendis woris leylighedt her i fremmede land, saa maae j gantske wenligen wide, att baade min kiere fader, moder, och wii sydskende liider, Gud were loffuit, alle well. Gud allermechtigste vnde os altidt de samme tidende fra eder och alle eders att høre och spørge, dett er os gantske kiert att fornemme. Och er min kiere fader om St. Hans dags tiide med min kiere moder och os alle dragen fran Wittenberig och op till Prag till den romske keyserlig mayestat effter hans mayestats naadigst begiering, och er hand der well bleffuen antagen och naadigst forsynit, saa att hand lader sig well nøye. Och haffuer keyserlig mayestat vndt hanum ett aff sine slotte, liggendis i lante Bemen 5 mile fran Prag, att boe paa, huilckit hans mayestat lod hannum iblant tre aff sine slotte selff vduelle. Och haffuer hans mayestat naadigst foraerit min kiere fader tho tusend gylden, och loffuit hannum til aarlig pension tre tusend gylden først att begynde med. Saa att hans mayestat er min kiere fader med all keyserlig gunst och naade well beuogen. Wor Herre giffue widere lycke och benedidelse. Kiere Mette, dette kunde ieg icke lade att giffue eder vdi en god mening tilkiende, helst effterdi ieg weed, att j er woris gode wen och gierne seer, att det gaaer os wel, for huilcken eders gode willie j skal were paa det allerhøyeste betacket och wii aff woris ringe formue vdigien forskylde wil. Disligeste, kiere Mette, maa j ochsaa wenligen wide, att min kiere fader loffuet och tilsagde eders kiere søn Claus Mule, att dett første Gud vil, hand kommer fran Danmarck til os vdigien, da will hand forskaffe hannum med en første vdi Italien paa sin bekostning och gierne forhielpe hannum til det beste, huor hannum mueligt kand were, baade for eders och hans egen skyld. Og vdi alle de maader min kiere moder och wii syskend kand giøre nøget, som eder eller hanum eller nogen aff eders til willige och tieniste kand were, wille wii det aff woris yderste formue hiertens gierne giøre, huilcket och i alle maade baade aff eder och hanum wel forskyldt er. Kiere Mette, wil ieg icke nu lenger opholde eder med denne min widtløfftige schriffuelse, thii ieg tencker well, att eders søn Claus Mule schriffuer eder all widere beskening til om min kiere faders och woris leylighedt, och ochsaa eders svoger doctor Niels Krag, thii min fader haffuer selff schreffuit hanum widtløfftelig nock til der vd om. Och will ieg her med nu och altidt haffue eder den euige alsommechtigste gode Gudt, med all langvarendis sundhed, lycke och benedidelse, med alt huis eder kiert er, til siel och liff befalit. Och lader min kiere fader, moder, syskende och ieg hilse eders kiere mand och eder med alle eders kiere børn, med mange m. maal m. gode netter.

Datum paa det keyserlig slot Benatica den 25. septembris stilo novo, anno d:ni 1599.
Magdalene Brahe
med min egen handt.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable, well-born woman Mette, [wife of] Hans Mule, dwelling at Nislevgård, my trusted especial good friend, [this] amicably to hand.

My quite friendly greeting, now and always, conveyed with [the blessing of] Our Lord. Dear Mette Mule, trusted especial good friend. Next [after] my quite friendly and great thanks for all the much kindness shown, which to repay I shall always be found — to my utmost ability — quite willing and ready, in every way I can ever do anything that may be to your honour and service. And you shall also have so much great honour and thanks for your good letter, which I received from you when we were at Wittenberg; it was so dear to me that I got your letter, and could learn how you and your dear husband, with yours, fare. And I should well, long ago, have written to you again, but I have since been able to get no sure messenger to you at all; therefore I could not forbear writing to you again now, since so sure a messenger is now available to me [going] into Denmark — with your dear son Claus Mule and my brother Tyge, whom my dear father has sent there into the country, to his kin and good friends, to attend to his business. Dear Mette, concerning our circumstances here in a foreign land, you may quite amicably know that both my dear father, mother, and we siblings fare, God be praised, all well. May God Almighty grant us always to hear the same tidings from you and all yours; it is quite dear to us to perceive [them]. And my dear father, about St. John's Day [24 June], with my dear mother and us all, moved from Wittenberg up to Prague, to the Roman Imperial Majesty, at His Majesty's gracious request; and he has there been well received and graciously provided for, so that he is well content. And the Imperial Majesty has granted him one of his castles, lying in the land of Bohemia, 5 miles from Prague, to dwell in — which His Majesty let him himself choose from among three of his castles. And His Majesty has graciously presented my dear father with two thousand gulden, and promised him as annual pension three thousand gulden to begin with — so that His Majesty is well disposed toward my dear father with all imperial favour and grace. May Our Lord give further fortune and blessing. Dear Mette, this I could not forbear to give you to know, in good intent, especially since I know you are our good friend and gladly see that it goes well with us — for which good will you shall be most highly thanked, and we will repay it according to our humble ability. Likewise, dear Mette, you may also amicably know that my dear father promised and assured your dear son Claus Mule that, the first [moment] God wills it — [when] he comes back from Denmark to us — he will provide for him with a prince in Italy at his own expense, and gladly help him to the best he can, both for your sake and his own. And in every way my dear mother and we siblings can do anything to the willing service of you, or him, or any of yours, we will do it most heartily, to our utmost ability — which is also in every way well deserved both by you and by him. Dear Mette, I will not now detain you longer with this lengthy letter of mine, for I think your son Claus Mule writes you all further news of my dear father's and our circumstances, and also your brother-in-law Doctor Niels Krag, for my father has himself written to him fully enough about it. And herewith I will, now and always, commend you to the eternal, almighty good God, with all long-lasting health, fortune, and blessing, with all that is dear to you, in soul and body. And my dear father, mother, siblings, and I bid greet your dear husband and you, with all your dear children, with many a thousand-thousand-thousand good nights.

Given at the imperial castle Benátky, the 25th of September, New Style, in the year of the Lord 1599.
Magdalene Brahe,
with my own hand.

Apparatus

- Sender — Magdalene Brahe. Magdalene Brahe (b. 1574), Tycho's eldest daughter, writing in her own hand from Benátky. A rare and valuable autograph letter from within Tycho's familia (and one of few surviving in a daughter's voice).
- Recipient. Mette [Kotte], wife of Hans Mule, of Nislevgård — the mother of Claus Mule (Claudius Mulenus / Nicolaus Mulaeus), Tycho's household-member and agent (cf. brahe-t_15980910001, brahe-t_16000919001), and (per this letter) kinswoman of Niels Krag.
- Significance — a prime source for Tycho's imperial settlement. Written just three months after the move, the letter states the terms of Rudolf II's patronage with unusual concreteness:
- The family moved from Wittenberg to Prague "about St. John's Day [24 June 1599]" at the Emperor's request, and Tycho "is well content."
- The Emperor granted Tycho one of his castles in Bohemia, 5 miles from Prague — which Tycho was let choose from among three (i.e. Benátky, chosen over two others).
- The Emperor gave Tycho 2,000 gulden outright and promised an annual pension of 3,000 gulden "to begin with."
This is one of the earliest and most direct testimonies to the famous 3,000-gulden imperial salary and the choice of Benátky — the more authoritative for coming from inside the household.
- The Claus Mule / Italy promise. Magdalene relays that Tycho promised to send Mette's son Claus Mule to a prince in Italy at his own expense, once Claus returns from Denmark — a glimpse of Tycho's patronage of his servants (cf. the parallel Italian plans for his own son, brahe-t_16000504001).
- Persons named. Magdalene Brahe (sender); Mette [Kotte] and her husband Hans Mule of Nislevgård; Claus Mule (their son, co-courier with Tyge to Denmark); Tyge (Tycho the Younger, Magdalene's brother); Tycho Brahe ("my dear father"); Kirsten Jørgensdatter ("my dear mother," unnamed); Niels Krag (Doctor Niels Krag, Mette's svoger — kinsman/brother-in-law; cf. brahe-t_15980628001, brahe-t_16010311001); Rudolf II.
- Calendar. Dateline Benatica den 25. Septembris stilo novo, anno d:ni 1599; explicitly New Style (imperial). Header agrees with the label.
- Correction. English corrected from 'your kinsman Doctor Niels Krag' to 'your brother-in-law Doctor Niels Krag', restoring the precise sense of Danish 'svoger' (Dreyer, TBOO XIV p.153).

1 defect found:
- mistranslation (medium). Danish 'svoger' specifically denotes 'brother-in-law'; the English broadens this to the generic 'kinsman', losing a precise, verifiable family relationship in a letter that is otherwise a careful genealogical/family report. At: “your kinsman Doctor Niels Krag”.

Repaired. Verified 'eders svoger doctor Niels Krag' against the Dreyer page image (TBOO XIV p.153); the Danish source text was already correct, so only the English translation required correction. No uncertainty remains in this letter.
