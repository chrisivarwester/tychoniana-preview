The original

AN ESSKE BILDEN ZU KOPENHAGEN

Vlrich etc.

VNSERN gunstigen grues zuuor. Ehrnuester lieber besonder. Wir seind von dem erbarn Vnser cammerer vnd lieben getrewen Jochim von Stralendorff berichtet worden, was jhr wegen der 10000 thaler, damit Vnsere vnmundjge vettern vnd pflegesohne, die junge hertzogen zu Meckelnburgk, dem auch ernuesten Vnsern lieben besondern Tycho Brahen verhafft, vnd welche so auf negst verschienen ostern betagt gewesen, an jhn gelanget. Nun wißen Wir Vns dieser schuldtfurderung vnd das auf negst verschienen ostern das geldt hette erlegt werden sollen, woll zuerjnnern, haben auch von deßwegen bereits vorlengst hochermelter Vnserer pflegesohne rentmeister Andreas Meyern verwarnet, darzu verdacht zusein, das er auf rechte gesetzte zeit zur zahlung gelangen konte, jnmaßen Vns dan auch ferner nicht vnbewust ist, das er zu dero notturfft geldt besprochen, so jhm auch gegen die negstverschienen ostern gewisse zugesaget, aber auf die letzte stunde jhm allererst wieder ein bloßen geschlagen worden, dahero es sich dan verursachet, das er seines teils auf bestimbte zeit zu der versprochenen zahlung nicht gelangen können. Jedoch ist er noch in fleißiger bemuhung, das er mit dem ehisten geldt zuwege bringen muge, vnd verhoffen Wir numehr auch teglich von jhm diese gute erklerung, das er werde rath geschaffet haben. Vnd weill die sachen itzterzeltermaßen geschaffen, alß gesinnen Wir demnach an euch hiemit gantz gnediglich, jhr wollet euch wegen gedachtes Tycho Brahen noch eine kleine vnd geringe zeit gedulden, so seindt Wir des gnedigen erbietens, die versehung zuthun, das alßdan mit dem schleunigsten die wirckliche bezahlung erfolgen soll, dan Wir auf itzo mehrgedachten rentmeister derentwegen von newen wiederumb erjnnerung vnd auch starcken beuehll gethan. Habens euch immittelst zu gnediger erklerung nicht verhalten wollen, vnd Wir seind euch mit gnaden vnd allen gutten woll gewogen.

Datum Dobbran den 17. junij a:o 1601.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To Eske Bille at Copenhagen.

Ulrich, etc.

Our favourable greeting beforehand. Honourable, dear, and especial [friend]. We have been informed by the honourable Our chamberlain and dear faithful [servant] Joachim von Stralendorff of what you have conveyed to him regarding the 10,000 thaler, with which Our minor cousins and foster-sons, the young Dukes of Mecklenburg, are indebted to the likewise-honourable Our dear and especial [friend] Tycho Brahe, and which fell due at the recently-past Easter. Now, We well remember this debt-claim, and that the money should have been paid at the recently-past Easter; and We have therefore already long since warned the rent-master of Our highly-mentioned foster-sons, Andreas Meyer, to be intent thereon, that he might come to the payment at the rightly-set time — just as it is also not further unknown to Us that he bespoke money for that need, which was also certainly promised him toward the recently-past Easter, but at the last hour was once again struck a blank; whence it then came about that he, for his part, could not come to the promised payment at the appointed time. However, he is still in diligent effort to bring the money about with the soonest; and We now also daily expect from him this good declaration, that he will have arranged the means. And since the matters stand in the manner just told, We accordingly desire of you herewith quite graciously that you would, on account of the said Tycho Brahe, have patience yet a little and short time; [and] so We are of the gracious offer to make the provision, that then, with the speediest, the actual payment shall follow — for We have now again, on that account, given the oft-mentioned rent-master fresh reminder and also strong command. We did not wish to withhold [this] from you meanwhile, for [your] gracious information; and We are graciously, and with all good, well inclined toward you.

Given Doberan, the 17th of June, in the year 1601.

Apparatus

  • Sender & recipient. Ulrich III, Duke of Mecklenburg-Güstrow, writing not to Tycho but to Eske Bille at Copenhagen — Tycho's agent/kinsman (of the Bille family; cf. brahe-t_16010724001, where Eske Bille's enquiries at Doberan are mentioned). The witness is among the Schwerin Debita passiva principum records.
  • The same 10,000-thaler debt. This letter (17 June 1601) is part of the same dossier as brahe-t_16010724001 (24 July 1601, Ulrich to Tycho): the 10,000-thaler debt of the young Mecklenburg Dukes to Tycho, due at Easter 1601 but unpaid because the rent-master Andreas Meyer's arranged financing "was struck a blank" at the last hour. The Duke asks Eske Bille (and through him Tycho) for patience and renews his command to Meyer. The chamberlain Joachim von Stralendorff is the intermediary who relayed Bille's message.
  • einen bloßen geschlagen — idiom, "came up empty / the promise failed" (as in brahe-t_16010724001).
  • Calendar. Dobbran [Doberan] den 17. junij a:o 1601 = 17 June 1601; Mecklenburg (Lutheran) = Old Style. Header agrees with the label.
