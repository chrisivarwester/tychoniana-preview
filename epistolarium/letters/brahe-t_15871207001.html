The original

ERLIG OCH VELBYRDIG MANDT HEINRICH BELOFF THILL SPÖTTROP KONGL. MAYT. BEFALNINGS-MANDTT PAA SKIFFHUSZ MIN KERE SWOGER OCH SYNDERLIG GODE VEN GANSKE VENLIG THILSKREFFUEN.

MEINEN freuntlichen grues mitt wunschung alles guettes alzeitt beforr. Edler, Ehrnvester, freundtlicher lieber Schwager vnd besonder vortraweter freundt. Neben Dancksagung fur vielfeltige erzeigete wolthaten, kan ich dir freundtlicher wolmeinung nicht verhalten, das ich dein schreiben habe entpfangen vnd darinne ein Copie des Durchleuchtigen Hochgebornen Fursten vnd Herren, Hertzog VLRICHS zu Mechelburg an dir geschreibene breiffs, worauss ich erfahre, das ihr furstliche Gnade begehrett von mir gnedichlich zu wissen, welcher meines erachtens von den beiden Prognosticatoribus TOBIA MOLLER vnd ANDREA ROSA dem Zeill neher zutreffet, ihn dem daß der eine ihn diesem zukunfftigen 88 Jhar den Regenten des Jhars Iovem vnd Venerem, der ander Saturnum vnd Martem setzet, darahn sie nicht alleine keins theils einig sein, sondern wie ihr Furstliche Gnade schreibet, gahr widerwertiger meinung haben. Dan der eine macht beide beneficos Planetas, der ander beide maleficos (wie sie die Astrologi nennen) zum Regenten im selbigen Jhar, welchs gar contrarie bedeuttung bringett. Hierauff kan ich dir freundlicher meinung nicht bergen, das wiewoll ich in die Astrologische Sachen, welche bedeuttung auß dem gestirn herholen vnd weißagunge tractiren, mich nicht gerne einlasse, dieweill darauff nicht vhill zu bawen ist, Sondern allein die Astronomiam, welche den wunderlichen lauff des gestirns erforschett, in einen gewissen vnd rechtmeßigen ordnung zu bringen mich etzliche Jhar her bemuhe, dan darahn kan durch rechtgeschaffene Instrumenten nach Geometrisch vnd Arithmetisch grundt vnd gewisheit die eigentliche warheitt durch langwirigen fleiß vnd arbeitt gefunden werden, So habe ich doch nach ihrer Furstlicher gnaden begerung beide Prognostica, die du mihr zuschickest (die ich doch nicht, wie du gemeinett hast, zuvor gehatt habe, dan ich niemals pflege solche practicen wider zu kauffen, noch zu lesen, ne bonas horas male collocem) durchgesehen, den mangel, worahn es hafftett, das sie so widerwertige Iudicia stellen, daraus zu suchen, vnd befinde, das sie in ihre Rechnung gar vnterscheidliche fundament gebraucht haben. Dan der eine, nemlich MOLLERUS, bawett sein Calculation auf des hocherfarnen COPERNICI rechnung, der ander, ROSA, auff die alte durch des Königs ALPHONSI in Hispanien liberalitet gemachte Tabeln, die man darumb die Alphonsinische nennett. Hirauß kömpt es, das der eine den anfang des Jhars in æquinoctio verno setzet ahm 10 tag Martij bey Neun vhr nach mittage, der ander ahm selbigen tag, aber vmb 2 Stunden nach der vorgehende Mitternacht, daß also zwischen beide ihre rechnung schier 19 gantze Stunden verlauffen, in welchen der himmel sich gar vil vorendern thutt, vnd kan gar ein ander Astrologisch Iudicium darauß fallen, eben so wol als wen dar ein gantzes Jhar oder noch mehr zwischen wehre: Das darumb nicht zuvorwundern ist, das diese beide Astrologi in domino Anni nicht vberein stimmen, weil sie den auß der Figura Cœli introitus Solis in Arietem, wan daß vorbemelte æquinoctium vernum geschicht, pflegen herholen. Wiewol es auch lichtlich geschehn kan, das wan sie schon gleichmeßige Tabeln vnd rechnungen volgeten, das sie gleichwoll in Dominis Anni vnd ihren gantzen weissagungen gar widerwertige meinungen können fürgeben, das darauß leichtlich zu probiren ist, daß wan man hundertt der Prognosticken lisset, so befindett sich doch gahr selten, das einer mitt dem andern concordierit, dan sie bawen nicht alle auff gleichformige grundt ihn ihren Iudicijs vnd haben vntherscheidliche proceß vnd ahnleitungen. Es sein auch diese Astrologische weissagungen wie ein cothurnus, den man kan auff ein jeder Bein ziehen, gros vnd klein, wie man will. Darumb ich auch niemals dar von ettwas Sonderlichs gehalten habe. Das ich aber Kong. May. meinen Gnedigsten Herren jhärlich ein solches Astrologisch Prognosticon untherteniglich zustelle, mus ich in dem nach ihre May. befell vnd willen thun, wiewoll ich selbst nicht vill darauf halte vnd nicht gerne mitt solchen zweiffelhafftigen prædictionibus vmbgehe, darin man die eigenttliche warheitt nicht durchauß erforschen mag, wie sonst in Geometria vnd Arithmetica, darauff die Astronomia durch hulff der vleißigen observation ihm lauff des Himels gebawet wirtt. Dennoch dieweill ihr Furstliche Gnade gnedichlich begertt von mihr zu wissen, welchen von den beiden ich beifellig sey, was den dominis Anni also widerwertiger weiß von ihnen gestellet thutt ahnlangen, So kan ich hierauff nicht anders sagen, dan das ihrer beiden rechnung vnd iudicium gehtt auß ein vormeinten vnrichtigen grundt. Dan weder die Alphonsinischen, noch die Copernianischen Tabeln, welchen sie folgen, geben den justen lauff der Sonnen, wie er ahn sich selbst am Himmel geschichtt, vnd ist hierinne kein geringe vnterscheidt, wie auß meiner eigenen Restitution vnd vornewrung in Rechnung des lauffs der Sonnen zusehen ist, welche ich auf etzliche vorgehende Jharen durch große vnd rechtmeßige Instrumenten augenscheinlich vom Himel selbst her ab, durch fleißige observation vnd warnemungen genomen habe, auß welchen sich befindet, das des Jhars Anfang in æquinoctio verno, darauß die Astrologi ihre vrtheil nehmen, geschicht ahm 1O tag Martij 8⅔ stunde nach der vorigen Mitternacht, welchs bey 7 stunden speder ist, als ANDREAS ROSA gesetzt hat, und 12 stunden fruer, als TOBIAS MOLLERUS meinett, darauß den vhil ein andere Constitution des himels zur Zeitt des æquinoctij einfeltt, als ein jetzlicher von ihnen gefunden hat, worauff auch ein ander vrtheill folgett, vnd auch wol andere domini Anni, wie sie es nennen (darauß doch nicht vhil zuholen ist), mögen gesetz werden. Was aber mene meinung sey ahnlangende Astrologische gitzung vber dis kunfftige 88 Jhar, habe ich Königl. May. meinen gnedigsten Herren ihn einen geschriebenen Prognostick vnterteniglich auffgezeignett, welchs ich auch ihre Furstliche Gnade gerne wolte vnterteniglich mitteilen, aber ich habe keine vberige Exemplar dar von, wan ihr Furstliche Gnade lesset bey ihr May. darumb ahnlangen, wird ihr Furst. Gna. wol ein abschrifft dar von bekomen. Ich bin darinne gentzlich nicht der meinung, daß solche gahr große vorenderunge in diesen negstkunftigen Jhar vorhanden sein, als die Astrologi auß etzlichen altten reimen, die sie den Regiomontano zumessen, furgeben, dan ich befinde im Constitution des Himels keine Sonderliche vrsachen darzu, sondern achte, das dis kunfftige Jhar wird den vorgehenden gleichmeßig sein, vnd in zimlichen guten wesen, in allen Sachen, sich erzeigen, aleine wo zuuor krieg vnd vneinigkeitt aufferwecktt ist, dar möchte es wol ettwas weitter einreißen. Vnd kan ihr Furstliche gnade meine meinung vom Astrologischen iudicio vber das gantze Jhar auß vorbemelten Prognostico, welchs ich Köng. May. meinen gnedigsten herren vnterteniglich habe zugesteltt, weitter erfahren. Dis habe ich auff dein guttwilliges schreiben vnd beger nicht wöllen vntherlassen zu antworten. Bitte gar deinstlich, du wollest vnbeschwert sein vnd mitt erster gelegenheitt ihr Furstliche Gnade hierauff meine antwortt vnd meinunge vnterteniglichen von meinett wegen zuvorstehn geben. Worin ich sonst ihre Furstliche gnaden zu willen vnd gefallen sein kan, bin ich alzeitt mit aller deinstlichkeitt vnterteniglich erböttig.

Lieber Schwager, nach dem ich so gutte gelegenheitt habe, kan ich nicht vnterlassen, dich in einer Sachen, darahn mihr vhil gelegen ist, vnd darinne du mihr wol itzunder konnest behulfflich sein, zu besuchen, vnd gebe dir darumb gantz freundtlich zuwissen, das nach dem ich itzunder in meiner eigenen alhie angerichten Druckerey ein Astronomisk Buch lasse außgehn, welchs zimlich groß wird fallen, vnd mihr baldt Druckpapir darzu vonnötten mangeln wirt, vmb wes wegen ich meine botschafft auff vhil örten außgehabt, vnd gleichwol keines der größe, wie zu dem wercke vonnöten, bekommen können, habe ich darumb ergistern widerumb im Land zu Mechelburg dem lassen nachstellen, dieweill ich vormercke, das alda zu Graubow vnd Neustat zwey gutte papir Mölen vorhanden sein, vnd habe die beide Ambtleuthe ahn den selbigen örtern, nemlich IOHAN HELDORP vnd STELLAN WAKENITZ, zugeschrieben, das sie wolten mihr so vhil zugefallen sein, vnd den papirmachern alda befehl geben, daß sie mihr mitt dem Allerersten etzliche ballen Druckpapir nach der größe des vbergeschichten forms wolten forfertigen, welchs ich dan zu danck ihnen will lassen bezahlen, so baldt ich es holen lasse. Aber die weil ich diesen beiden Amptleutten villicht mach vnbekantt sein, ist mein gantz fleißige bitte ahn dir, du woltest so woll thun vnd ihnen von meinet wegen hierumbe auch zuschreiben, das sie mihr darinne wolten zugefallen sein, vnd du wollest auch so woll thun vnd einen von deinen gutten bekanten ahm Furstlichen hoffe zuschreiben, das er bey meinen Gnedigsten Hern Hertzog VLRICH wolte von meinett wegen vnthertenigen ahnlangung thun, das auß ihrer Furstlich Gnaden authoritet vnd befehl alles möchte mitt dem befurderlichsten zugehn, das ich mitt erster Muglichkeit könte ein 14 oder 15 Ballen papir alda auff ihr Furstliche Gnaden papirmöllen beckomen, nach solcher größe und gestalt, wie ich dir hier neben ein proba schicke. Ich will gerne bezalen, was die papirmeister darfur haben sollen. Vnd du woltest darneben auch lassen vnterteniglich befördern, das ihr Furst. Gn. wolte mihr gnedichlich furgunnen, wan das papir fertig vnd bezalt ist, das es möchte von einen Amptman zum andem biß gen Rostock gefurett werden, vnd alda den Hern Doctori HENRICO BRUCÆO vberlübert, das ichs von dannen mitt dem allerersten schiff möchte hierein beckommen. Wan du mihr hierinne in der gestaltt zu gefallen sein woltest, wie ich auch ahn deinem gutten willen keinen zueifel trage, vnd mihr dis zuwege bringen, beide bey ihr Furstlichen Gnaden selbst, vnd auch bey den Amptleutten, thetest du mir einen sonderlichen deinst vnd wolgefallen dar mitt. Dan mihr gahr viell daran gelegen ist, das ich mitt dem ersten solchs papir in einer gutten menge beckomen möchte. Vnd so ich in einiger sachen von meinen vormuegen dier jemals widerumb zu deinst und danckbarckeitt sein kan, wil ich alzeitt mehr dan guttwillig gefunden werden, vnd thue dich hiermitt sambt deiner lieben haußfrawen dem Almechtigen Gott befehlen. Du wollest auch meiner lieben Mutterschuester Fraw Maren und deiner lieben Hausfrawe, ihrer tochter, von meinett wegen vhil gutternacht sagen.

Datum Vraniborg den 7 Decemb. Anno etc. 87.
Tui amantissimus
TYCHO BRAHE, Manu propria.

English translation machine translation, Secure

To the honourable and well-born man Henrik Below of Spøttrup, His Royal Majesty's prefect at Skivehus, my dear brother-in-law and especially good friend, written in all friendliness.

My friendly greeting, with the wish of all good things, at all times beforehand. Noble, honour-firm, friendly dear brother-in-law and particularly trusted friend. Besides my thanks for the manifold kindnesses you have shown, I cannot withhold from you, in friendly good will, that I have received your letter and in it a copy of the letter written to you by the Most Serene, High-born Prince and Lord, Duke Ulrich of Mecklenburg, from which I learn that His Princely Grace desires graciously to know from me which of the two prognosticators, in my judgement, comes nearer the mark — Tobias Möller and Andreas Rosa — in that the one sets Jupiter and Venus as the rulers of this coming year [']88, the other Saturn and Mars; in which they are not only in no part agreed, but, as His Princely Grace writes, hold quite contrary opinions. For the one makes both the beneficent planets [beneficos Planetas], the other both the malefic ones [maleficos] (as the astrologers call them) ruler in the selfsame year, which brings quite a contrary signification. On this I cannot conceal from you, in friendly intent, that — although I do not gladly involve myself in astrological matters, which fetch their significations out of the stars and treat of soothsayings, since not much is to be built upon them, but for some years now have laboured solely to bring astronomy, which investigates the wondrous course of the stars, into a sure and well-grounded order, for therein the proper truth can be found, by long-lasting diligence and labour, through rightly-fashioned instruments on a geometric and arithmetic foundation and certainty — I have nonetheless, at His Princely Grace's request, gone through both the prognostica that you sent me (which, however, I had not, as you supposed, had before, for I never am wont to buy such practica back, nor to read them, lest I spend good hours ill [ne bonas horas male collocem]), in order to seek out from them the defect in which it lies that they set down such contrary judgements [Iudicia]; and I find that they have used quite different foundations in their reckoning. For the one, namely Möller, builds his calculation upon the reckoning of the highly-experienced Copernicus; the other, Rosa, upon the old tables made through the liberality of King Alfonso in Spain, which one therefore calls the Alfonsine. From this it comes that the one sets the beginning of the year at the vernal equinox on the 10th day of March at about nine o'clock in the afternoon, the other on the same day, but at about 2 hours after the preceding midnight, so that thus between the two reckonings nearly 19 whole hours run by, in which the heaven alters very much, and a quite different astrological judgement can fall out from it, just as much as if a whole year or more lay between: so that it is not therefore to be wondered at that these two astrologers do not agree on the ruler of the year [domino Anni], because they are wont to derive it from the figure of the heaven [Figura Coeli] at the entry of the Sun into Aries, when the aforesaid vernal equinox occurs. Although it can also easily happen that, even were they to follow tables and reckonings that matched, they could nonetheless put forth quite contrary opinions in the rulers of the year [Dominis Anni] and in their whole soothsayings — which is easily to be proved from this, that when one reads a hundred of the prognostica, it is yet found very seldom that one agrees [concordierit] with another, for they do not all build upon a uniform foundation in their judgements and have different procedures and methods. These astrological soothsayings are also like a buskin [cothurnus] that one can draw onto any leg, big and small, as one will. For which reason, too, I have never held anything special of them. But that I yearly submit such an astrological prognosticon humbly to His Royal Majesty, my Most Gracious Lord — in that I must act according to His Majesty's command and will, although I myself hold not much of it and do not gladly deal with such doubtful predictions [praedictionibus], in which one may not thoroughly investigate the proper truth, as otherwise in geometry and arithmetic, upon which astronomy is built by the help of diligent observation in the course of the heaven. Nonetheless, since His Princely Grace graciously desires to know from me to which of the two I incline — as concerns the rulers of the year [dominis Anni] thus set down by them in contrary manner — I can say nothing else upon this than that the reckoning and judgement of both of them proceeds from a supposed faulty foundation. For neither the Alfonsine nor the Copernican tables, which they follow, give the true [justen] course of the Sun as it occurs in itself in the heaven; and herein is no small difference, as is to be seen from my own restoration [Restitution] and renewal in the reckoning of the course of the Sun, which I have, over several preceding years, taken visibly down from the heaven itself by large and rightful instruments, through diligent observation and observings; from which it is found that the beginning of the year at the vernal equinox, from which the astrologers take their judgement, occurs on the 10th day of March, 8⅔ hours after the previous midnight, which is about 7 hours later than Andreas Rosa has set, and 12 hours earlier than Tobias Möller supposes; from which then a very different constitution of the heaven falls out at the time of the equinox than each of them has found, upon which also a different judgement follows, and indeed other rulers of the year [domini Anni], as they call them (out of which, however, not much is to be fetched), may be set down. But what my opinion may be concerning the astrological estimate over this coming year ['] 88, I have humbly noted down for His Royal Majesty, my most gracious Lord, in a written prognosticon, which I would also gladly impart humbly to His Princely Grace; but I have no spare copy of it. If His Princely Grace will make application to His Majesty for it, His Princely Grace will well obtain a transcript of it. Therein I am altogether not of the opinion that such very great changes are at hand in this next-coming year, as the astrologers put forth out of certain old rhymes which they ascribe to Regiomontanus; for I find in the constitution of the heaven no special causes for it, but reckon that this coming year will be like the preceding ones, and will show itself in fairly good condition in all things — only, where war and discord have been stirred up before, there it might well break in somewhat further. And His Princely Grace can learn more fully my opinion of the astrological judgement over the whole year from the aforesaid prognosticon which I have humbly submitted to His Royal Majesty, my most gracious Lord. This I would not omit to answer upon your kindly letter and request. I beg quite dutifully that you would be unburdened [by it] and at the first opportunity convey to His Princely Grace, upon this, my answer and opinion humbly on my behalf. Wherein I can otherwise be at His Princely Grace's will and pleasure, I am at all times humbly ready with all dutifulness.

Dear brother-in-law, since I have so good an opportunity, I cannot omit to call upon you in a matter that much concerns me, and in which you can well be of help to me just now; and I therefore make known to you quite in friendliness that, since I am now letting an astronomical book go forth from my own printing-house set up here, which will turn out fairly large, and I shall soon lack printing-paper for it — on which account I have had my errand out in many places, and yet could obtain none of the size as is needed for the work — I have therefore the day before yesterday again had inquiry made for it in the land of Mecklenburg, since I observe that there at Grabow and Neustadt two good paper-mills are to be found; and I have written to the two officials at those same places, namely Johan Heldorp and Stellan Wakenitz, that they would do me so much favour and give order to the papermakers there that they should at the very first prepare for me several bales of printing-paper according to the size of the form sent over [to them], which I will then thankfully have paid to them as soon as I have it fetched. But since I am perhaps still unknown to these two officials, my quite diligent request to you is that you would be so good as to write to them too on my behalf about this, that they would do me the favour therein; and that you would also be so good as to write to one of your good acquaintances at the Princely court, that he would on my behalf make humble application to my Most Gracious Lord Duke Ulrich, that out of His Princely Grace's authority and command everything might proceed with all furtherance, so that I might at the first possibility obtain there at His Princely Grace's paper-mills some 14 or 15 bales of paper, of such size and form as I send you a sample [proba] herewith. I will gladly pay what the paper-masters are to have for it. And you would besides also have it humbly furthered that His Princely Grace would graciously grant me, when the paper is finished and paid for, that it might be carried from one official to another as far as Rostock, and there delivered over to Master Doctor Heinrich Brucaeus, so that from there I might obtain it in here by the very first ship. If you would be willing to do me favour in this manner herein — as I bear no doubt of your good will — and bring this about for me, both with His Princely Grace himself and also with the officials, you would do me a special service and pleasure thereby. For it much concerns me that I might at the first obtain such paper in a good quantity. And if in any matter within my power I can ever again be of service and gratitude to you, I will at all times be found more than willing; and I herewith commend you, together with your dear housewife, to Almighty God. You would also, on my behalf, say many a goodnight to my dear mother's-sister Mistress Maren and to your dear housewife, her daughter.

Given at Uraniborg, the 7th of December, in the year etc. [']87.
Your most loving,
Tycho Brahe, with his own hand.

Apparatus

  • Sender / hand. Tycho Brahe, writing from Uraniborg on Hven. The subscription Manu propria ("with his own hand") asserts an autograph signature.
  • Recipient. Heinrich Beloff / Henrik Below (Below of Spøttrup, d. 1606), Danish nobleman, here styled Kongl. Mayt. Befalningsmandt paa Skiffhusz — His Royal Majesty's lensmand (prefect/governor) at Skivehus (Skive, Jutland). Repeatedly addressed as Schwager — brother-in-law in the period's loose sense (a relation by marriage); the close kin link is made explicit at the close, where Tycho greets meiner lieben Mutterschuester Fraw Maren ("my dear mother's-sister Mistress Maren") and deiner lieben Hausfrawe, ihrer tochter ("your dear housewife, her daughter") — i.e. Below's wife is the daughter of Tycho's maternal aunt, making them cousins-by-marriage. Spøttrop / Spøttrup and Skiffhusz / Skivehus kept as the period forms with modern glosses.
  • Address-line language. The opening superscription is in chancery Danish (ERLIG OCH VELBYRDIG MANDT … MIN KERE SWOGER … GANSKE VENLIG THILSKREFFUEN, "to the honourable and well-born man … my dear brother-in-law … written in all friendliness"); the letter body is Early New High German. The code-switch is preserved by the edition's convention
  • Occasion (per Dreyer, Opera Omnia and Tycho Brahe, p. 155). Duke Ulrich of Mecklenburg — father of Queen Sophie of Denmark, hence Tycho's interest — had procured two prognostica for 1588 and, finding them irreconcilable, asked Below to have Tycho adjudicate. Duke Ulrich III of Mecklenburg(-Güstrow) (1527–1603); Hertzog VLRICH zu Mechelburg / Mecklenburg. Ihr Furstliche Gnade = "His Princely Grace" (the Duke); Kong. May. / Königl. May.* = His Royal Majesty (Frederik II of Denmark), Tycho's patron, to whom he owed an annual prognostication.
  • The two astrologers. Tobias Möller (Tobia Moller / Mollerus) and Andreas Rosa (Andrea Rosa), authors of competing printed prognostica for 1588. Tycho's diagnosis: Möller computed on Copernicus's numbers (COPERNICI rechnung), Rosa on the Alfonsine Tables (die Alphonsinische, "made through the liberality of King Alfonso [X] in Spain"); their equinox times differed by nearly 19 hours, hence the contrary "rulers of the year."
  • Astrology — technical terms. Prognosticon / Prognostica / practica / practicen — yearly astrological forecasts. Regenten des Jhars / dominus Anni / domini Anni → ruler(s) of the year (the planet(s) governing an annual revolution), a term Tycho flags as their jargon (wie sie es nennen, "as they call them"). beneficos Planetas / maleficos → the beneficent / malefic planets (Jupiter, Venus vs. Saturn, Mars); kept as Latin in source, glossed. Figura Coeli → the figure of the heaven (the horoscope chart cast for the moment of the Sun's introitus … in Arietem, the Sun's entry into Aries at the vernal equinox). Iudicium / Iudicia / iudicio → judgement(s) (the astrological verdict). Astrologische gitzung → rendered "astrological estimate/conjecture"; gitzung (cf. Gissung, conjecture/guess) is a non-standard chancery form, read as "estimate."
  • Astronomy. aequinoctium vernum / aequinoctio verno → vernal equinox. Restitution vnd vornewrung → restoration and renewal (of the Sun's theory) — Tycho's restitutio / instauratio astronomiae programme; here applied to his corrected solar tables. Instrumenten … nach Geometrisch vnd Arithmetisch grundt → instruments … on a geometric and arithmetic foundation. observation vnd warnemungen → "observation and observings" (Wahrnehmungen, observations/percepts), the doubled term kept.
  • The solar-equinox figures (Tycho's central technical claim). The two almanac-makers placed the 1588 vernal equinox on 10 March, one "at about nine o'clock in the afternoon [p.m.]," the other "at about 2 hours after the preceding midnight" — c. 19 hours apart. Tycho's own observationally-grounded value: 10 March, 8⅔ hours after the previous midnight — "about 7 hours later than Andreas Rosa has set, and 12 hours earlier than Tobias Möller supposes." (The 8⅔ h figure is printed 1O with a capital-O for the numeral in the source's "1O tag Martij"; reproduced as printed and read as "10th day of March.")
  • Regiomontano … altten reimen — the "old rhymes ascribed to Regiomontanus": the notorious prophetic verses on 1588 as a year of vast upheaval (the "octogesimus octavus … annus mirabilis" prophecy widely circulated under Regiomontanus's name). Tycho discounts it, finding "no special causes" in the heaven, expecting an ordinary year — except where war is already kindled, which "might break in somewhat further."
  • cothurnus — a buskin (the loose-fitting tragic actor's boot that fits either foot); a stock simile for something accommodated to any view. Tycho: the prognostica are "like a buskin one can draw onto any leg." Latin term kept, glossed.
  • The book / paper request. ein Astronomisk Buch … in meiner eigenen … Druckerey — "an astronomical book [from] my own printing-house": the volume Tycho was preparing on the press at Uraniborg, requiring large quantities of Druckpapir (printing-paper) of a specific form (sheet size), of which he encloses a proba (sample). He seeks 14–15 bales from the ducal paper-mills at Grabow (Graubow) and Neustadt (Neustat) in Mecklenburg, via the officials Johan Heldorp and Stellan Wakenitz (Ambtleuthe / Amptleutte — bailiffs/officials), with the finished paper to be relayed biß gen Rostock (to Rostock) and there delivered to Heinrich Brucaeus (Hern Doctori HENRICO BRUCAeO, the Rostock professor, the edition's convention) for shipment to Hven.
  • Latin code-switches in the German body (kept in source, glossed in translation): ne bonas horas male collocem ("lest I spend good hours ill"); beneficos Planetas / maleficos; contrarie; Iudicia / iudicio; Figura Coeli, introitus Solis in Arietem, aequinoctium vernum, domino/domini/dominis Anni; concordierit, proceß; praedictionibus, observation, Geometria / Arithmetica, Astronomia, Constitution, Restitution; the subscription Tui amantissimus … Manu propria.
  • Calendar / place. Datum Vraniborg den 7 Decemb. Anno etc. 87 = "Given at Uraniborg, 7 December [15]87" — Old Style (Denmark kept the Julian calendar until 1700). The catalogue label ("1587. 7. december. Uraniborg") agrees with the dateline; no discrepancy. The internal date 10 March / equinox for "this coming 88 year" is likewise O.S. — note that on the Julian calendar the vernal equinox fell near 10 March, consistent with the figures discussed; not converted.
