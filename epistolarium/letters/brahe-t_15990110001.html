The original

JCH Tycho Brahe, erbgesessenn zu Knudtstrup im reiche Dennemarckenn vnnd kön: maytt: alda ambtman auf Vraniburgk, vrkunde vnd bekenne hiemit inn crafft dieses, daß jch vonn dem ehrnuesten vnnd achtbarnn Andreaß Meyernn, furstlichenn meckelburgischenn junger herrschafft verordenten rentmeister, zwey hundert reichsthaller zinße, so erst auf schieristkommendenn monnat aprilis dieses neun vnndt neuntzigstenn jahrs betaget sein wurden, heut dato auff rechnung entpfangenn habe. Thue derowegenn gedachtenn herrnn rentmeister solcher woll entpfangenenn zwey hundert thaller, alß werenn dieselbigenn auf obberurtenn termin erleget vnnd betzalet gentzlichenn wie es zu rechte am krefftigstenn vnnd bestendigstenn geschehenn solle, quitirenn vnnd loßsagenn. Zu mehrer vrkundt hab jch diese quitanz mit eig[enen handt] vnterschriben vnnd mein angeborn pitzschafft hierauff gedruckt.

Actum Wittemberg denn zehenden tag januarij anno 99.
Tycho Brahe
eigenhd.

(l.s.)

English translation machine translation, Secure

I, Tycho Brahe, hereditary lord at Knudstrup in the kingdom of Denmark, and His Royal Majesty's bailiff [Amtmann] there at Uraniborg, attest and acknowledge hereby, by force of this [document], that I have today, on account, received from the honourable and respectable Andreas Meyer — appointed rent-master of the princely Mecklenburg junior lordship — two hundred reichsthaler of interest, which were to fall due only in the next coming month of April of this ninety-ninth year. I therefore acquit and release the said Master rent-master of these two hundred well-received thaler, as though the same had been fully discharged and paid at the aforementioned term — as may, in law, be done in the most effective and binding manner. In further attestation I have signed this quittance with my own hand, and pressed my hereditary seal hereupon.

Done at Wittenberg, the tenth day of January, in the year [15]99.
Tycho Brahe,
in his own hand.

(Place of the seal.)

Apparatus

  • Document type. A quittance (Quitanz) — a formal receipt-and-release for money received, in legal chancery German.
  • Issuer. Tycho Brahe, styling himself erbgesessen zu Knudstrup (hereditary lord of Knudstrup) and the King of Denmark's Amtmann (bailiff) at Uraniborg — the titles he still claimed in early 1599, during his self-imposed exile (he had left Hven in 1597 and was at Wittenberg before proceeding to Bohemia).
  • Recipient / payer. Andreas Meyer, rent-master (Rentmeister, treasury officer) of the "princely Mecklenburg junior lordship" (fürstliche Mecklenburgische junge Herrschaft) — i.e. the household of the young Dukes of Mecklenburg. The 200 reichsthaler are interest (Zinse) on a sum Tycho had on loan/deposit there, paid in advance of its April term. The piece survives among the Schwerin treasury records (Renterei-Akten), confirming the Mecklenburg financial tie.
  • angeborn pitzschafft = "innate/hereditary seal" (angeborenes Petschaft), the family signet. (l.s.) = locus sigilli, "place of the seal" (editorial mark for where the wax seal stood). The bracketed [enen handt] in mit eig[enen handt] is an editorial restoration of a damaged spot in the original; left as printed.
  • Calendar. Wittemberg … zehenden tag januarij anno 99 = 10 January 1599; Wittenberg (Lutheran Saxony) kept the Julian / Old Style calendar. Header agrees with the label.
  • Correction. German 'aus obberurtenn termin' corrected to 'auf obberurtenn termin' per Dreyer, TBOO XIV p.150; the English clause 'paid from the aforementioned term' corrected to 'paid at the aforementioned term' to follow Dreyer's reading.

1 defect found:
- text error (low). Dreyer's page reads 'auf obberurtenn termin' (paid at/for the aforementioned term, standard German quittance idiom) where the transcribed original has 'aus' (from/out of); read at this resolution with reasonable but not total confidence. The the published English ('paid from the aforementioned term') follows the transcribed original's 'aus' rather than Dreyer's 'auf', but the substantive content of the quittance — Tycho acquitting Andreas Meyer of the 200-thaler payment — is unaffected either way. Dreyer prints: alß werenn dieselbigenn auf obberurtenn termin erleget vnnd betzalet gentzlichenn.

Repaired. Verified visually against the Dreyer page image, TBOO XIV p.150 (letter no. 158). The single text error (aus/auf) is confirmed as printed and both the German and its English rendering are corrected accordingly. No other reading on this page or the adjoining pp.149, 151 was found to diverge from the transcribed original. Nothing remains uncertain.
