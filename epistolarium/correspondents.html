4Correspondents

One entry per party to the correspondence, as the Familia register identifies them: 138 entries — 130 persons, 4 bodies and 4 groups of unnamed recipients — reached through 149 name-forms of the edition, which are printed beneath each name. The summary table gives the extent of each exchange in both directions, its span, its languages and how its documents survive; the entries below it list each exchange in order with the abstracts and the reply chain. Letters between two other people are indexed under both parties, and the one address naming two men (Valkendorf and Friis) counts under both entries, so the column sums exceed the number of documents. Who these people were is Familia’s to say; the name links there.
