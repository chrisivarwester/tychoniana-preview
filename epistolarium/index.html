Epistolarium — The Letters

The complete correspondence of Tycho Brahe — all 509 known letters, 1568–1601, in parallel original text and English, with an index of correspondents, a map of the exchange, and its year-by-year rhythm.

Tycho's correspondence as geography. Where written traces both his own moving address — Copenhagen and Rostock, then the island of Hven, and in exile Wandsbek, Wittenberg, Benátky and Prague — and the reach of the network writing to him, from Kassel and Augsburg to Edinburgh and Bologna. Switch to where the manuscripts survive to see how they re-converged into a few great archives.
The index of the correspondence, as the register of a printed edition would give it: every correspondent, the extent of the exchange in each direction, its span, its language, and how many of its letters survive with a manuscript witness. Click a column heading to sort; click a row to open that correspondence in the Reader. Letters between third parties are indexed under both correspondents.
Letters per year, 1568–1601, split by direction. The shape is a biography: a thin trickle through the student years, the long Hven plateau of the great scientific correspondences, then the exile explosion — the year 1600 alone carries more letters than any three Hven years, as Tycho wrote relentlessly to secure his position in Prague. Click a year to read its letters.
