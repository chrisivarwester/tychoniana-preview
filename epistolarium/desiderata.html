  2. Correspondences known to be lost — from Norlind 1970:
    • No letter survives from 1569 or 1570 (Norlind 1970, p. 174).
    • Tycho's letters to Anders Sørensen Vedel, some fifty of them, burned with Bishop Christen Worm's library in the Copenhagen fire of 1728; Vedel's letters to Tycho are lost (Norlind 1970, pp. 174–175).
    • Holger Rosenkrantz's papers burned at Fredrikshald in 1826; his exchange with Tycho survives only in Jacob Langebek's eighteenth-century transcripts, now in Rigsarkivet (Norlind 1970, pp. 173–174).
    • Letters to and from Tycho are known to lie unpublished in the Herwart and Rosenkrantz papers (Norlind 1970, p. 176).
  3. Translations not collated against Dreyer's pages — 51 documents (§10), 47 of them in vol. VI, which tekstnet does not paginate; the pages have since been located by search (§6) and the collation can now be run. Settled by running it.
  4. No human review of the English — all 508 translations are machine-made and machine-checked. Settled by a Latinist's sampled review, beginning with the 54 not graded Secure.
  5. Words where the two transcriptions of tekstnet's text disagree — 80 documents, 1664 words in all, listed on each letter page; settled word by word against Dreyer's page.

  7. The Marburg convolute — HStAM Best. 3 II Nr. 1070 holds originals of the Hven–Kassel exchange of 1585–1592; which letters it holds has not been read letter by letter from the Arcinsys images, so no record cites it as a witness. Settled by reading them.
  8. The unmarked witness lines — 236 of 475 lines say nothing about what the manuscript is; most Vienna and Basel lines are originals or drafts, but the edition does not say so and the repository catalogues have not been checked line by line.
  9. The press flag — 218 documents are flagged as concerning Tycho's printed books by the editor's reading, with no per-letter work recorded; settled by recording the work (and its Typographica entry) on each.
  10. Places not keyed to Loca — 36 of 61 places of writing are not in Tycho's own gazetteer, because he was never there; they are located here from the edition's datelines with coordinates of the modern town.
  11. A record-level concordance to Early Modern Letters Online — EMLO catalogues the same printed corpus as 505 letters; the two have not been matched record by record.
  12. The right to republish tekstnet's texts — the terms under which the DSL's texts stand here beside an English translation are to be stated in §12 once the DSL has been asked.
