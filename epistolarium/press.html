9Letters concerning the press

The documents that touch the making, gift, sale or acquisition of Tycho’s printed books — the paper and the press on Hven, the privileges, the presentation copies, the bookseller, the plates. The flag is the editor’s, set by reading the letters; no per-letter work is recorded, so the table cannot be grouped by book, and the criterion is a desideratum (§11). The books themselves are Typographica’s.
