Across All Twelve — views, frontier & method

Five ways to work across every domain at once: the cross-cutting views that re-project the whole archive onto one timeline or one map; the research frontier of open questions and known gaps; and the method for reading the evidence by hand.

Views — see across all twelve
Chronologia
Timeline
Dated events from 1546 to the present — works, instruments, sites, and correspondence frequency — on one chronological axis.
Dispersio
Where the copies are
Where the surviving copies of Tycho’s works are held today — of the 230 recorded copies, those located in institutions from Scandinavian libraries and Vienna to North American research collections.
The research frontier
Quaestiones
Research questions (5 open · 25 resolved)
Puzzles arising across all twelve domains — the 5 still open, and the record of the 25 already resolved.
Lacunae
The gaps (174)
What is still missing: 36 lost items — works, instruments, sites, and images; 51 copies whose identification is graded possible (C) or doubtful (D); 87 books from his library known to have existed but not yet found.
Methods — reading the evidence
Manus
Reading the hand — the Graphonomicon
How to tell whether a page is in Tycho’s own hand — 80 catalogued specimens of his hand, a guide to his letterforms, and 10 features that separate his hand from his assistants’ and contemporaries’.
