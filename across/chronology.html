2General chronology Chronologia

The same year across every kind of thing. The first figure is the life, 1546 to 1601: the bands are the eight periods of the itinerary as Loca divides it, the diamonds its documented journeys, and the lanes below carry, year by year, the editions printed, the instruments in use, the sites standing, the observation volumes in progress, the years of the Uraniborg diary, the likenesses made, the letters written and the episodes of reception that began in his lifetime. The second figure is the afterlife, from 1601 to the present, drawn to scale: the reception episodes, the dated fates of the instruments and the printings of 1602 to 1610. Every mark links to its record. The tables that follow are the figures’ twins: the afterlife by date, and the year index of the life, one row per year, in which every cell is a link.
