Every record in full, in the order of the catalogue: the edition's title and the full Latin heading where the record carries it, the English title, the form, the date with its basis and its certainty, the addressees and the occasion, the summary, the transmission and the first printing, the place in Dreyer's Opera omnia and in the bibliographies, the sections with their authors, the relations to other records, the apparatus counts, the references with what each supports, the open questions, and the links to the text, to the edition, to the printed witnesses and to the persons and places in the other pillars. Texts of forty lines or fewer are printed here entire; the text page remains the citable locus. A record not yet researched shows the fields it has and says so.
