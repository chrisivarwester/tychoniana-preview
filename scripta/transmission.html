Tycho never collected his poems, and the texts here reached us by different roads: printed inside his own books, printed separately at his order, printed by others in his lifetime, first printed after his death, or in manuscript until Dreyer and Ræder printed them. The five channels are Zeeberg's account of the transmission as this catalogue partitions it; each record is placed under one channel on the evidence stated beside it, and the records whose channel is not yet established are listed last. Where the printed witness survives as a book, the Typographica entry and the census of its copies are linked; where a print is lost, the Typographica entry that records the loss.
