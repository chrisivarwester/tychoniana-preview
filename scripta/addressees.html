The persons index inverted from the records: everyone a text addresses, is dedicated to, was commissioned by, is cast for, commemorates or speaks in the voice of, with the texts under each name. A name links into Familia where Familia has a record; the persons without one are named without a link and listed in §10. The authors of the sections not by Tycho are indexed here too. Zeeberg's reading of the poetry as a social practice — verse written for a patron, a friend, a prince, an occasion, and circulated in print or in manuscript to a known circle — is what this index makes visible; it is his argument, not a taxonomy of this catalogue.
