Nicolao Kaasio

To Niels Kaas

Magnifico, amplissimo nobilissimoque viro, sapientia, doctrina omnimodaque virtute praeeminenti, D. Nicolao Kaasio, domino de Taarup, Regiae Maiestatis Daniae cancellario et consiliario prudentissimo, utilitatis et tranquillitatis publicae suasori conservatorique fidelissimo, amico suo inprimis observando (short title: Nicolao Kaasio; Nielsen's supplied title: Carmen ad Nic. Kaasium)

Elegy · 1585 (secure) · Hven · Niels Kaas (addressee)

Printed New Year verse epistle of 288 verses, dated Uraniborg 1 January 1585, to Niels Kaas, chancellor of Denmark, in which the muse Urania sings Kaas's praises. Her epiphany reprises the 1573 elegy of De nova stella; her tale of exile through the lands that failed astronomy, ended at last on Hven, is Tycho's private version of the translatio Musarum; an episode modelled on Ovid's Invidia makes Kaas the saviour of Uraniborg's establishment; and Tycho closes by inviting him to visit Uraniborg and its instruments when spring comes.
