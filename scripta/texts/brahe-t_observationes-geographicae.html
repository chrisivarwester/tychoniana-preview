Observationes geographicae

Geographical observations

Survey · 1598 (the edition) (the edition's) · Hven

Tables of geodetic sightings taken on the island of Hven — azimuths and horizontal angles measured from Uraniborg and other stations to towns around the Sound, among them Copenhagen, Malmö, Lund, Helsingborg and Elsinore.

Latin: the edition's text (Zeeberg & Gredal Jensen 2019) on Dreyer, Opera omnia, tom. V, pp. 294–300; the edition ↗.
