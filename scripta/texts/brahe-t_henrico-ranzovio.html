Henrico Ranzovio

To Heinrich Rantzau

Nobilissimo et amplissimo viro, sapientia et eruditione inprimis excellenti, D. Henrico Ranzovio, potentiss. Regis Daniae in Holsatia vicario et consiliario, praefecto arcis Segebergae et domino in Bredenberga etc., astrologiae omniumque liberalium disciplinarum excultori et promotori laudatissimo, amico suo plurimum colendo

Elegy · 1585 (secure) · Hven · Henrik Rantzau (addressee)

Verse epistle of 114 verses to Heinrich Rantzau, the Danish king's governor in Holstein, praising his book on the kings and princes who cultivated astrology and describing Uraniborg and its instruments, with letter-like greetings to Rantzau's wife and children; printed at Uraniborg on 1 March 1585 — months after the other Uraniborg press poems — and sent to Rantzau with a printed view of the building. The original print survives, and from it Zeeberg's edition corrects three misprints in the Opera omnia text.
