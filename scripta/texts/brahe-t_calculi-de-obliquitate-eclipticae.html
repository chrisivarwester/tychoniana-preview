Calculi de obliquitate eclipticæ

Calculations on the obliquity of the ecliptic

Working notes · 1600 (probable) · Exile

Working notes of 1600 on the obliquity of the ecliptic, in which Tycho re-computes solstitial observations made at Prague in 1416 by 'Doctor Syndel', excerpted from a manuscript of the old Prague Collegium communicated to him by M. Bacchatius, and finds the obliquity unchanged after 200 years.

Latin: the edition's text (Zeeberg & Gredal Jensen 2019) on Dreyer, Opera omnia, tom. V, pp. 228–229; the edition ↗.
