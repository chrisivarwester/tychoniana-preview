Epistolae astronomicae

Astronomical letters

Printed correspondence · 1596 (probable) · Hven · Moritz, Landgrave of Hesse (dedicatee), Wilhelm IV, Landgrave of Hesse (correspondent), Christoph Rothmann (correspondent)

Tycho's printed volume of astronomical correspondence with Landgrave Wilhelm IV of Hesse-Kassel and his court astronomer Christoph Rothmann, printed at Uraniborg in 1596, dedicated to the young Landgrave Moritz, and including a description with woodcuts of Uraniborg and Stjerneborg. Six honorific poems by learned contributors accompany the volume, and Tycho's own memorial poem on Wilhelm IV grants the dead Landgrave an apotheosis through astronomy.

Latin: the edition's text (Zeeberg & Gredal Jensen 2019) on Dreyer, Opera omnia, tom. VI, pp. 4–341; the edition ↗. The volume's non-letter parts are given here; its letters are listed with their Epistolarium records.
