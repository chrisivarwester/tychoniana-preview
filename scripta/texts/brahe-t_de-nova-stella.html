De nova stella

On the new star

De nova et nullius aevi memoria prius visa stella, iam pridem anno a nato Christo 1572. mense Novembri primum conspecta, contemplatio mathematica

Treatise · 1573 (secure) · Early · Johannes Pratensis (addressee of the dedicatory letter)

Tycho's report on the new star first seen in November 1572 — his first book, printed at Copenhagen by Lorentz Benedicht in 1573 — published with a prefatory exchange of letters with his friend Johannes Pratensis, an opening poem by Hans Frandsen and a closing one by Anders Sørensen Vedel: guarantees from university and church for the noble author. The book also carries Tycho's own elegy to Urania, in which the muse appears and gives him the new star as his calling — the beginning of the allegory that runs through his poetry for the rest of his life.
