Ante stellas restitutas

Before the restored stars

Poem · 1598 (the edition's) · Exile · Rudolf II (dedicatee), Matthias, Archduke of Austria (dedicatee of a presentation copy)

Dedicatory poem to Emperor Rudolf II prefacing the 1598 manuscript star catalogue, whose opening line was varied in the presentation copies given to other princes (Archduke Matthias, the Doge of Venice, Duke Heinrich Julius, Friedrich Wilhelm of Saxony) — the same exchangeable-opening device used in the presentation verse of the Mechanica.

Latin: the edition's text (Zeeberg & Gredal Jensen 2019) on Dreyer, Opera omnia, tom. IX, pp. 212–212; the edition ↗.

Section 1

IX 212A 11r ANTE STELLAS RESTITUTAS.

HOS quoque Stelliferos capias, RUDOLPHE, labores, Quos operosa diu noxque diesque dedit: Debuerant equidem, quibus orti fulgere in oris, Et jubar hinc imos spargere ad antipodas: 5Ni Superis aliter visum, qui fata gubernant, VRANIÆque aliter, quæ sua sacra fovet, Nescia contemni, fraudari, odia invida, noxas, Censurasque rudes, sorditiemque pati: Plurima quæque alias fanda atque infanda tulisset, 10Regna nisi mallet nunc peregrina sequi, Nec peregrina tamen: Cœlum tota undique tellus Suspicit, Æthereis invigilatque vijs. Prosperiora igitur Cœlestia Numina spondent: Tanta nec ingratis sunt peritura locis.
