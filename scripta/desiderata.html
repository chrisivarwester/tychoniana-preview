What this catalogue does not hold, by class. First, Tycho's writings outside the edition's selection: the three pieces of Dreyer's Carmina the edition omits, the verse Dreyer prints inside the other volumes of the Opera omnia, the printed books and the star catalogue — each with its locus and its home in this portal where it has one. Then the work that remains on the records themselves.

The work that remains on the records

rights Written permission from Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab for the republication of the edition's Danish translations, textual notes and commentary. Until it is given, the pages carry the edition's Latin and link to the translations and the notes at the source.
attribution The translator of the Mechanica's Danish instrument chapters (sections 4–27, 32–33), reproduced by the edition in a pre-1948 orthography; the local copy names no one. The Danish Mechanica of Ræder, Strömgren and Strömgren (Nordisk Astronomisk Tidsskrift, 1948–1955) is the likely source and should be confirmed.
research Full records — summary, occasion, addressee, printing, transmission, references, open questions, grade — for the 28 texts that carry only their identifying fields. The 29 researched records set the standard.
text The Latin of Occasio et argumentum, which the edition prints only in Danish; Dreyer prints it at TBOO IX 324–326 from cod. Vindob. lat. 10686. A transcription from the page images would make the record a Latin–Danish pair.
text Dreyer's text of every work here, from the page imagess of the Opera omnia, as a second witness beside the edition's text — the public-domain fallback should the edition's permission be refused.
apparatus The edition's key to its witness sigla, which the harvest did not capture; sigla are listed per text as they occur, with a meaning only where the note itself gives one.
concordance The entries of the Epistolae astronomicae that could not be joined to an Epistolarium letter on their date alone (listed in §5); each wants a hand join.
concordance The letters of Jerónimo Muñoz appended to the preface for the second volume have no Epistolarium record; the authorship of the letter dated 6 December is to be settled against TBOO VII 395–403.
bridge The Kronborg inscriptions have no site record in Loca; the bridge waits on one.
bridge Horoscopi has no per-item anchors; the three nativities, the almanac preface and the fragment link to the pillar page only.
bridge Persons named on these pages who have no Familia record: Niels Kaas, Jacob Ulfeldt, Hans Frandsen, Lauge Beck, Rudolphus Caukerchius, Jerónimo Muñoz, James VI, the six poets of the Epistolae astronomicae, Tycho's children.
text The verse Dreyer prints inside the other volumes (§10 a) is outside the edition; the pieces inside the letters are read in the Epistolarium, the rest only in the Opera omnia.
text The edition transmits the figures of the 1573 almanac, the nativity charts and the Mechanica's woodcuts as placeholders; the woodcuts are seen in Instrumenta, the rest only in the scans.
