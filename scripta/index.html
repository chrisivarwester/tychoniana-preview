Scripta — Tycho’s Writings

The reading room. 57 works by Tycho Brahe from the critical edition of the Danish Society for Language and Literature — the occasional poems, the verses carved into Uraniborg’s stone, the Copenhagen oration, horoscopes and alchemical recipes, and three of the printed books — two readable here in full, the third (the Epistolae astronomicae) linked page by page at the source. Ten works carry Peter Zeeberg’s modern Danish translation beside the Latin; one, the 1591 Flemløs preface, is in Tycho’s own Danish. This is the edition’s selection, not the whole oeuvre — De mundi aetherei and the Progymnasmata are not in it, and the correspondence lives in the Epistolarium.

Typographica treats these works as printed books; Epistolarium as a correspondence network; here you read them.

Texts and Danish translations from the critical edition prepared by Peter Zeeberg and Finn Gredal Jensen for Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab, 2019 · tekstnet.dk.
