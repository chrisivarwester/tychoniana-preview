Familia — Tycho’s Circle

The 189 people documented around Tycho Brahe — 152 parties to the surviving correspondence (Tycho himself among them), 33 documented associates of the household, press, and archive (10 are both), and 14 known from craft, service, or scholarship. Of the 33 associates, 27 were Tycho’s contemporaries; 6 are the earlier and later owners, editors, and scholars through whom his books and papers passed.
