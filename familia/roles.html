4Roles

The register re-projected by function. A person may carry several roles — Longomontanus is assistant, scribe and correspondent — and appears under each; the group counts therefore exceed the number of entries. The vocabulary is the store’s own; the grouping into eight kinds of relation is editorial and is defined in the table.
