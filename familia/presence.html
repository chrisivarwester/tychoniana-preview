8Presence

Who was physically with Tycho, and when, on the two registers that record it: the meteorological diary kept on Hven, whose arrivals and departures the Diarium models, and the reconstructed itinerary of Loca, whose stays name the people engaged at each. Both joins are shown by person; the Diarium join is by the reviewed identifier each visitor entry carries, the Loca join by the name forms each record lists.
