6Seats

Where the circle operated: the documented seat or affiliation of each person — the court, the university, the press, the island — never the birthplace. Each seat carries the basis on which it was assigned. The map draws the seats on the borders of c. 1600; the table beneath it is the same data by place. Persons with no confidently documented seat are counted and are not placed.
