5Chronology

The dated entries as lifespans against Tycho’s own (1546–1601), in the three bands the era rule defines: the predecessor dead before his working life began, the contemporaries, and the afterlife of the editors, owners and scholars. A bar runs from birth to death; a diamond marks a person known only by a death year, so that no lifespan is invented. The table beside the chart is the same data sortable; the undated majority is counted, not hidden.
