The 1901 exhumation (the tercentenary)

1901LegacyBrecep-exhumation-1901

For the three-hundredth anniversary of his death the Týn grave was opened and the remains examined by the anthropologist Jindřich Matiegka, whose certificate of authenticity was sealed in with the bones — the first scientific look at Tycho's body, which fixed in the literature the question of the prosthetic nose and the green stain it left on the skull.
