8Sources

Every source the register cites, with its full reference, what it contributes to this edition, the records that cite it, and an open-access or catalogue link where one was verified. A source with no link is one for which no open copy could be found; the gap is stated, not hidden. The evidence grades of §1 are the register’s, not the sources’: a grade B record may rest on an excellent study the compilation has simply not read at the page.

  26. The 2010 exhumation project (Aarhus University with the National Museum, Prague)
    The Danish–Czech exhumation of Tycho Brahe's remains from the Týn church, Prague, led by Jens Vellev (Aarhus University) with the National Museum in Prague and Charles University, November 2010; the results announced in Prague on 15 November 2012, excluding mercury poisoning. The analyses themselves are published as Rasmussen et al. 2013, Kučera et al. 2017 and Kacki et al. 2018 (below). The 1901 opening is known through the same project's account of what the tomb contained.
    Contributes The 2010 exhumation as a project — who, when, what was sampled, and when the results were announced.
    Cited by 3 records: The grave in the Týn Church, Prague; The 1901 exhumation (the tercentenary); The 2010 exhumation & the death question
    Open copy the project page, Aarhus University
