6The threads on one time axis

The five threads are the register’s own classification of what was received: the data, the science, the sky, the estate and the life. They are not exclusive by nature — the Historia coelestis is both a printing of the data and an event in the science — and each episode is assigned to the thread it advances most; the assignment is stated in the record and can be disputed. The figure lays the episodes on one time axis by thread: a dot is an episode at its date, a bar the years an episode ran, an open-ended bar one that continues. Every mark links to its record, and the tables beneath give the same information in the order the marks appear.
