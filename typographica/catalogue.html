One row per edition or issue, in the order of the presses and the years: the two Copenhagen printings, the Uraniborg press, Wandsbek, and the posthumous printings of 1602–1610. The columns give the kind of printing event, the shop and printer, the format, and the copy figures read from the census at this build; the key column is the edition's stable identifier, and the Nielsen column its number where it has one. Sort by any column; filter by period, kind, author or whether the edition is censused; the count line states how many rows are shown. The table is downloadable as CSV.
