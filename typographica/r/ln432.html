Astronomiae instauratae mechanica
[black:] TYCHONIS BRAHE | [red:] ASTRONOMIÆ | [black:] INSTAVRATÆ | [red:] MECHANICA | [woodcut, the author's emblem] | [red:] WANDESBVRGI | [black:] ANNO | [red:] CIↃ IↃ. IIC. | [black:] Cum Cæsaris & Regum quorundam Privilegiis. |

Nielsen's description
A new edition in a wholly new setting, printed for Hülsius at Nuremberg in 1602 from Tycho's original woodblocks and copperplates. Hülsius was a publisher, not a printer; the material assigns the book, like the Epistolae title-quire, to Christoph Lochner. Libraries catalogue it as 1598 when the title-leaf is wanting (Nielsen 1921, p. 212).
Note
Not a re-issue of the 1598 sheets: Nielsen establishes it as a complete new reprint, set afresh in type though printed from Tycho's original woodblocks and copperplates. Norman 320 = this issue. Libraries routinely catalogue it as “1598” when the title-leaf is wanting.

Nielsen’s record and the apparatus

Collation
42 leaves. Signatures: ):(⁴ A⁶ B⁴–F⁴ G⁶ H⁶ (Nielsen 1921, p. 205; the formula in the Dansk Bibliografi entry as transcribed for this chapter does not sum to the stated leaf count). Variable line-count. Type-area: 265 × 178 mm. Catchwords. Roman and italic. Every page enclosed in a border (assembled from flowers and rules). Initials, friezes and vignettes. Woodcuts and copper-engravings. Title-leaf and Bl. 2a printed in red and black.
Contents
Bl. 1a title; 2–3 dedication to Emperor Rudolf II (dated pridie Calend. Jan. 1598 [= 31 Dec 1597]); 4a a poem by Holger Rosenkrantz; 4b an engraving; Bl. 5a (sign. A) 'Explicatio fabricae et usus' — the instrument descriptions; Bl. 25b a brief notice of further instruments not yet engraved; Bl. 27b on what Tycho has so far achieved and what remains; Bl. 32–35a letters to the author (Curtius, Magini); Bl. 37a (sign. H) the appendix on the architecture of the observatory; Bl. 42b the colophon: 'IMPRESSVM WANDESBVRGI IN ARCE RANZOVIANA … PROPRIA AVTHORIS TYPOGRAPHIA opera Philippi de Ohr Chalcographi Hamburgensis Ineunte ANNO M.D.IIC.' In most copies a portrait of Tycho is pasted onto Bl. 1b.
Production
Printed at Wandsbek early in 1598 by the Hamburg printer Philip de Ohr (who from 1597 used the Binder family's material). Nielsen's type-analysis shows the body-type, initials, friezes, vignettes and flower-borders are de Ohr's or Binder's; of Tycho's own material only the instrument-woodblocks and his printer's marks are in the book — the press itself, however, was his, carried from Hven. Hasselberg's census (Vierteljahrsschrift der Astronomischen Gesellschaft 39, 1904) lists 33 copies, to which Nielsen added two more, at Danzig and Kiel; most of the surviving copies are illuminated, and most have Gheyn's engraved portrait pasted onto the title-verso.
Copies, as described
The first edition; the Nuremberg reprint of 1602 is entered as its own edition below. Most copies are illuminated and carry de Gheyn's engraved portrait pasted to the title-verso; a large group are presentation copies with Tycho's autograph dedication (the surviving copies, below). Hasselberg listed thirty-three copies in 1904, Nielsen added two.

Editorial note
Dedicated to Rudolf II, dated 31 December 1597. 18 of the 22 instrument-plates are printed from the existing Uraniborg woodblocks; the 4 new ones (including the great mural quadrant with Tycho's portrait, and the great celestial globe) are copperplate engravings cut in Germany. The colophon's propria authoris typographia is true as far as it goes — the press was Tycho's own — and it names Philip de Ohr on its face; what it obscures is how much of the finished book is Hamburg work. Re-issued as a complete new reprint by Hülsius (Nuremberg 1602, printed by Christoph Lochner; = Norman 320), which is catalogued as '1598' where its title-leaf is lost.
