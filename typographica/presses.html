Who printed what, where, and on what evidence. Eight shops printed the twenty-nine editions and issues: two Copenhagen jobbing printers, Tycho's own press on Hven, the same press at Wandsbek with Philip de Ohr's type, an attempt at Wittenberg that issued nothing, the press at Prague completed by the Schumann shop, Christoph Lochner at Nuremberg for the publisher Hülsius, and Gottfried Tampach at Frankfurt. The Uraniborg printers are known by three names, each attested by a single document. Four printings of which nothing is preserved are entered on Nielsen's authority.
