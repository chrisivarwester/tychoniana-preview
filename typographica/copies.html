The copies of the censused editions, as their censuses count them at this build. The first table sets the twenty-four censuses side by side with the five uncensused issues; the second lists the holders of located copies; the third the presentation and dedication copies — a table generated from the census records themselves, by the rule that a copy is entered when its provenance chain records Tycho's autograph dedication or its census label calls it a presentation copy. Every copy links to its census record; none is described here.
