4Index of persons

One entry per person, across every form in which the scribes wrote the name — Soph. B., Domina S. B., Sophia Braa, Tychonis soror are one sister. The folding follows an alias table that is part of the published dataset; a form that matches no entry in it stands alone under its own name. Unnamed persons and groups (“two Scottish students”, “a nobleman sent by Heinrich Rantzau”) are gathered at the end. Each entry lists its notes by date, opening the record in §3, and links to the Familia pillar where the person is in the household prosopography.
