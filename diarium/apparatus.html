9The apparatus of the 1876 edition

The 1876 edition’s own apparatus, in the editors’ words: the footnotes under the diary text, by which F. R. Friis and H. F. Rørdam identified the abbreviated names, dated a death from another source, or marked where one scribe’s hand gives way to another; the five corrigenda printed after the text; and, drawn out of the footnotes, the changes of hand — the nearest thing to a scribal history of the codex until the manuscript itself is examined.
