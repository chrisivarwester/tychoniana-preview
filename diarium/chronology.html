7Chronology

The notes by year and month. The unevenness is the diary’s own: the scribes of 1590 and 1591 noted arrivals almost weekly, those of 1587 and 1588 rarely, and the months in which the weather record itself lapses (§8) are months in which nobody was noting anything.
