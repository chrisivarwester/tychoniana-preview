Meteorologica — the weather over Hven

Uraniborg kept two daily registers. One recorded who climbed the hill (the Diarium of visitors); this one recorded what the sky did overhead, nearly day by day, from October 1582 to April 1597 — one of the earliest continuous weather journals in Europe. Among the cloud and frost it logs, 45 times, the chasmata: the northern lights.

✦ The chasmata — Tycho's aurora record

"Chasmata" (also casmata, kasmata) is the diary's word for the aurora borealis — literally "chasms" or "gapings" of light in the northern sky. Long before the aurora was understood, Tycho's staff noted each occurrence with its date and form — 45 nights across the sixteen years. Click any to read the entry.
