Dreyer printed the observations in volumes X–XIII of the Opera Omnia, year by year, each year in the subject order of the fair copies — Sun, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury, fixed stars — with the comets of 1577–1596 at the end of vol. XIII and the indices in vol. XV. The table below gives each volume's years, printed extent, the codices it was set from and its page images at the Internet Archive. The register that follows is one row per page that the 2026 reading reached, by volume and year: its printed page linked to the page image, the body observed, the codex it was set from and the reading's summary of the page. 976 of the 1,850 pages of volumes X–XIII are here, and 55 pages of apparatus; the rows are a sample, and every filter states its count against the whole. The rows that carry no body are pages of reductions, tables or mixed entries.
