Resources 11

  1. Alvin — platform for digitised cultural heritage (Swedish university libraries)
    Alvin, Uppsala University Library, Waller Ms dk-00231 (alvin-record:16945). — iiif repository · public
    Contributes Hosts the catalogue record (and image) of the 10 Nov 1598 Dresden 'PLATO' album leaf — Uppsala UB, Waller Ms dk-00231, record 16945 — one of the seven-leaf Wittenberg series. The route to a citable digital surrogate of that specimen.
    Open copy alvin-portal.org
  2. Det Kgl. Bibliotek (Royal Danish Library) — soeg.kb.dk catalogue + digital collections
    Det Kgl. Bibliotek, København (soeg.kb.dk). — catalogue database · public
    Contributes Holds and catalogues the largest cluster of Tycho autograph and partly-autograph manuscripts: the Hven observation logs (GKS 311, 312, 1825, 1826), the autograph horoscopes (GKS 1822 Prince Ulrik, GKS 1823 Duke Hans), the autograph receipt (NKS 4898), and the 1586 Diarium astrologicum autograph-dedication copy (LN 1170). Each has a stable permalink.
    Open copy soeg.kb.dk
  3. Early Modern Letters Online (EMLO) — Correspondence of Tycho Brahe
    Cultures of Knowledge / Bodleian Libraries, Early Modern Letters Online — Correspondence of Tycho Brahe. — catalogue database · public
    Contributes The systematic source for the LETTER corpus' witness/repository metadata — distinguishes surviving autograph ORIGINALS (TBOO 'A' siglum) from drafts/copies ('B') and records holding repositories. This is what's needed to harvest correspondence-register specimens at scale beyond the 3 now in store; the local tekstnet.dk jsonl carries text but NOT the A/B siglum, so the harvest is gated on EMLO's per-letter witness field.
    Open copy emlo.bodleian.ox.ac.uk
  4. Edward Rosen, 'Don't Give Tychon What Isn't Brahe's' (Kwartalnik Historii Nauki i Techniki 26/3-4, 1981, 549–564)
    Rosen, E. (1981). Nie daj Tychonowi, co nie jest Brahego. Kwartalnik Historii Nauki i Techniki, 26(3-4), 549–564 (trans. E. Hilfstein). — scholarly project · public
    Contributes Rosen's corroboration of the Gingerich–Westman reattribution of the De revolutionibus marginalia from Tycho to Paul Wittich. Supplies the Jakob Monau attestation (23 Oct 1600) authenticating the Wittich comet leaves in KB GKS 1826, paleographic plates of both hands (Ryc.1 Tycho / Ryc.2a–e Wittich, same codex/leaf; Ryc.4 Vatican Ottob. lat. 1902; Ryc.5a–g Prague Cimelia), the Wittich library-dispersal narrative (sister in Wrocław → Monau → Tycho), and the edition correction (Prague 14 B 16 = Basel 1566; Nuremberg 1543 = Vatican Ottob. lat. 1902). Also withdraws the Newskaja claim that the Leningrad/Edinburgh De rev. carries Tycho's hand (it is Reinhold's).
  5. Harald Mortensen, ‘Tycho Brahe-Autografer i Stambøger’, Cassiopeia XIV (1953), pp.94–100
    Cassiopeia 14 (1953), s.94–100 — the prior systematic catalogue of Tycho's stambog autographs — bibliography
    Open copy search.worldcat.org
  6. Jon Jacobsen Venusinus — 13 facsimiled autographs 1579–1606 (Fund og Forskning 60, 2022, plates)
    FoF60/2022 (Andersen) pp.28–51 — table + 13 facsimile plates; V3 KB NKS 396g 8°; V4 Heidelberg UB Hs.487; V8 Lund UB — facsimile archive
    Open copy tidsskrift.dk
  7. Manuscriptorium — European digital library of manuscripts (Czech National Library aggregator)
    Manuscriptorium, National Library of the Czech Republic. — iiif repository · public
    Contributes Aggregates digitised manuscripts from the Czech National Library, Strahov, and the Národní muzeum — i.e. the institutional homes of Pillars 1-4 (VI F 44, DG IV 25, AG XI 56, III-a-18/1693), the autograph status disputed Tabulae Sinuum (VI E 9), the Bruno Acrotismus (15 K 22), and the two debunked trig pamphlets (NK 14 C 20, NK XII A 28). The single best route to IIIF surrogates of the Prague pillar corpus.
    Open copy manuscriptorium.com
  8. Owen Gingerich, An Annotated Census of Copernicus' De revolutionibus (Brill, 2002)
    Gingerich 2002 — per-copy census of De revolutionibus annotations; the four Wittich copies + the Tycho-misattribution corrections — catalogue database
    Open copy search.worldcat.org
  9. Public HTR models for early-modern humanist-italic Latin cursive (Transkribus / eScriptorium survey)
    READ-COOP / Transkribus public models registry. — htr model · registered
    Contributes Surveys the publicly available HTR models that could bootstrap transcription of Tycho's prose hand and the familia scribal hands. As of this build NO Tycho-specific per-hand model exists; generic early-modern Latin / humanist-cursive models would need fine-tuning. Registered to mark the gap honestly: the v1 Graphonomicon ships a grounding pack (atlas + feature model), NOT a trained per-hand model (§7.6 opt-in deferred).
    Open copy readcoop.eu
  10. Repertorium Alborum Amicorum (RAA) — Stammbuch index, FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg
    W.W. Schnabel (ed.), Repertorium Alborum Amicorum (RAA), Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg. — catalogue database · public
    Contributes The largest international album-amicorum index, catalogued down to the individual ENTRY. The instrument for locating the unidentified host albums of the FIVE detached Wittenberg leaves (25 Oct 1598; 16 Apr, 8 May, 29 May, 14 Jun 1599) — searching for Tycho-entry records or for the Jessenský/Peucer-household album owners could re-unite leaves with their original Stammbücher.
    Open copy raa.gf-franken.de
  11. tekstnet.dk — Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (DSL) Tycho Brahe text corpus
    Tycho Brahe, ed. P. Zeeberg & S. Gredal Jensen, DSL (tekstnet.dk), after J.L.E. Dreyer, Opera Omnia. — transcription corpus · public
    Contributes Zeeberg / Gredal Jensen digital edition of the Dreyer Opera Omnia text (509 letters + 56 works), with cross-referenced 1598 foliation. Supplies the diplomatic/normalised TEXT against which the manu-propria letter subscriptions (Scultetus, Savile, Rothmann) and dated formulae can be read — the apparatus layer for the correspondence register. NOT a hand resource (no images), but the authoritative transcription backbone.
    Open copy tekstnet.dk
