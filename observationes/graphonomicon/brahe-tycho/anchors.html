  1. Vienna, ÖNB, Cod. 10689/29 — 'Iudicium astrologicum huius geniturae', autograph nativity-judgment draft — 1500-1599; wholly autograph; One of the Vienna Tychonis-Brahe Nachlass papers (Cod. 10689; Dreyer TBOO X notes these bundles 'contain autograph material' used to edit Tycho's Works & Letters). A full-res hand-triage (IIIF surrogate, 17 canvases @ ~5857x8685px): the judgment is a WORKING DRAFT in Tycho's rapid humanist-italic autograph hand — heading 'Iudicium Astrologicum huius geniturae', then a nativity chart/positions table and the judgment under rubric sections 'De Complexione', 'De Amicis et Inimicis', 'De Itinere'. Diagnostic features all match the secure pillars: single-storey cursive a, uncial looped d, long-s + round final-s, looped -que abbreviation, dense macron suspensions, steep rightward slant, rapid ductus — plus AUTHOR-STYLE strike-throughs and interlinear corrections (img 0006, 0009), i.e. composition not transcription. Same genre and hand as the autograph Latin judgment in GKS 1823. Sampled canvases 0006 + 0009 show a single consistent autograph hand; a full leaf-by-leaf confirmation of all 4 leaves is pending. A register-symmetric, feature-by-feature comparison of the judgment prose ('De Amicis et Inimicis') against the SECURE pillar GKS 316 autograph prose ('De occasione interruptarum observationum & discessus mej') supports the same hand: 22 concordances — 19 of them confirmed on a second audit pass — against 4 discordances, all four on LABILE traits (baseline-drift direction, line-spacing, pen-pressure, minim regularity), plausibly genre- or speed-driven, with NO discordance on letterform construction. Two highly distinctive concordances carry the case: the non-closing, leftward-curling g descender, and the curved, hook-shaped (rather than barred) abbreviation marks. The secure/A grade rests on CONVERGENCE — (i) provenance: this is literally a leaf of Tycho's own Nachlass (Cod. 10689), bought with his effects and mined by Dreyer to edit Tycho's Works; (ii) full-diagnostic paleographic match to the pillars; (iii) the draft-with-corrections character (a composer's, not a copyist's, hand); and (iv) the register-symmetric comparison against a secure pillar — a corroboration GKS 316 itself never received. CAVEATS (ENFSI BPM-FHX-01): a comparison at these sample lengths is SUPPORTING evidence, not a forensic identification; the run is modest (~85 words combined) with no overlapping capitals; and unlike GKS 306 there is no independent NAMED-AUTHOR anchor (the geniture is unnamed) — the case is provenance + paleography + comparison, not authorship documentation.
  2. Copenhagen, KB, GKS 1822 kvart — Horoscope of Prince Ulrik (1579), wholly in Tycho's hand — 1579; wholly autograph; KB catalogues it as written in Tycho's own hand; green velvet binding.
  3. Copenhagen, KB, NKS 4898 kvart — Tycho's autograph receipt — 1580-04-13; wholly autograph; KB catalogues it as a genuine autograph receipt signed in Tycho's own hand.
  4. Prague, Strahov Monastery, DG IV 25 — Sebald Plan album, Tycho's entry (Uraniborg, 1591) — 1591; wholly autograph; Šíma–Valeška Pillar 2; only pillar ~10 yrs earlier than the rest, confirms hand stability across decades. RAA locates Tycho's entry at fol. 58v (1574_plan;114)
  5. Copenhagen, KB, GKS 306 folio — 1000-star catalogue presentation copy to Christian IV (Wandsbek 1598); autograph preface to Rudolf II — 1598; partly autograph; Deluxe parchment presentation copy of the 1000-star catalogue 'Stellarum octavi orbis inerrantium accurata restitutio' (Wandsbek 1598), dedicated to King Christian IV of Denmark. A hi-res surrogate hand-triage (a KB surrogate at ~9125x13293px, far above the 800px elib facsimiles) delineates FOUR layers, only ONE autograph: (1) the 'AD AVGVSTISSIMVM IMPERATOREM RVDOLPHVM SECVNDVM... TYCHONIS BRAHE PRAEFATIO' (front-matter ff. ~img 0010-0018, a multi-page Latin prose address to Emperor Rudolf II) is Tycho's autograph rapid humanist-italic prose — single-storey a, uncial looped d, long-s + round final-s, dense macron suspensions, the looped -que abbreviation, steep ~18-22 deg rightward slant, flourished terminals. It is FIRST-PERSON authorial ('a nobis... exponuntur et demonstrantur', 'in normam ipsi coelo eas exaequavimus', 'Constitui itaque') and carries author-style carets/insertions (img 0011), not the clean ductus of a transcription. Direct comparison against the secure pillar GKS 316 'Ad Daniam Elegia' (autograph, Wandsbek Oct 1597, same months) matches across the diagnostic set. (2) The star-catalogue TABLES (constellation by constellation, img ~0020-0051) and the precession-canon + RA/declination tables (img ~0052-0058+) are a deliberately rounder, slower calligraphic book-hand — a scribe/famulus fair copy, NOT Tycho's running hand. (3) The gilt dedication to Christian IV (img 0007) and the illuminated title (img 0008) are display calligraphy/illumination ('TYCHO BRAHE' on the dedication is built-up gold capitals, NOT an autograph signature). (4) The arched portrait with 16 noble quarterings (img 0009) is a hand-coloured engraving. NB the cursive canon-usage rubrics in the lower-left of the precession tables (img 0019/0052/0055) are in the same cursive as the preface and are a probable autograph annotation of the scribal tables. INVERSION of the Mechanica presentation-copy pattern: there the calligraphic body carries only an autograph signature; here the display is calligraphic but the substantive PREFACE is autograph. A register-symmetric, feature-by-feature comparison of the preface prose against the secure GKS 316 autograph prose (the f.131v departure narrative, 'De occasione interruptarum observationum & discessus mej') returns the strongest band: it supports the same hand, on 25 concordances — 21 of them confirmed on a second audit pass — against 3 discordances, all three on LABILE instrument- and session-dependent traits (pen-pressure contrast, nib width, baseline-drift direction). The two most distinctive concordances carry the case: the leftward-looping uncial d, and the curved, high-placed, concave-up suspension marks; with them run an aligned semi-cursive ductus, habitual pen-lifts before tall d and long-ſ, and an overlapping abbreviation repertoire (nasal macrons, -que, -um, -runt). Caveat (ENFSI BPM-FHX-01): a comparison at these sample lengths is SUPPORTING evidence, not a forensic identification, and no shared capitals were testable. The A grade rests on the CONVERGENCE: (i) Tycho is the author of the preface (first-person address to the Emperor in his own presentation copy), (ii) author-style drafting carets, (iii) full-diagnostic-set paleographic match to the secure pillar, and (iv) the corroborating register-symmetric comparison — not on that comparison alone.

  7. Album leaf, Wittenberg 25 Oct 1598 — 'Suspiciendo despicio' (Tycho's device) — 1598-10-25; wholly autograph; seven-leaf Wittenberg series; Sotheby's catalogued as 'feuillet détaché... liber amicorum'; opening leaf of the 236-day dated sequence
  8. Uppsala UB, Waller Ms dk-00231 — album leaf, Dresden 10 Nov 1598, 'PLATO' (with angel-and-armillary miniature) — 1598-11-10; wholly autograph; seven-leaf Wittenberg series; retains album-context (folio '127'); Waller collection (acquired before 1953)
  9. Venice, Biblioteca Nazionale Marciana, H24 — Mechanica (1598) dedicated to the Republic of Venice, autograph signature 'manu propria' — 1598-1601; calligrapher's body, autograph signature; Šíma–Valeška Pillar 5 (added from Truffa); explicit 'manu propria' makes the signature's autograph claim self-asserted
  10. Prague, Národní muzeum, VI F 44 — Tyge Brahe album, Tycho's holograph entry (Wittenberg, 28 Feb 1599) — 1599-02-28; wholly autograph; explicit Filio TYCHoni primogenito scripsi + dated/located; Šíma–Valeška Pillar 1 — 'as certain as can be made'
  11. private collection — album leaf, Wittenberg 16 Apr 1599, 'PALINGENIUS' (the house reference specimen for the 1599 hand) — 1599-04-16; wholly autograph; Weiss 1930 BSB Munich handwriting authentication; seven-leaf series. No diaeresis visible on the y in this scan.
  12. Album leaf, Wittenberg 8 May 1599 — St Paul, I Cor 13:10 — 1599-05-08; wholly autograph; seven-leaf Wittenberg series; Sotheby's catalogued as liber-amicorum leaf 'à Wittenberg'. Serves as our secure reference in the cross-decade (vs 1588 Rothmann) and Duodo-sixth-pillar comparison tests.
  13. Album leaf, Wittenberg 14 Jun 1599 — the farewell leaf ('quo Witebergā discessi') — 1599-06-14; wholly autograph; seven-leaf Wittenberg series; closing bookend (most pronounced signature paraph); explicit 'the day on which I departed from Wittenberg'
  14. Křivoklát Castle (Národní muzeum), III-a-18/1693 — Mechanica (1598) dedicated to U.D. Pruskowski, autograph signature (12 Mar 1601) — 1601-03-12; calligrapher's body, autograph signature; Šíma–Valeška Pillar 4; one of the latest dated Tycho holographs (~7 months before death). 19th-c. restoration shifted positions of magistri/stabuli/administranti
