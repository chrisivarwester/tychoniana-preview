5The register × life-stage grid and the hand-variants

Each writing register crossed with each period of the life. A populated cell is a characterised hand-variant, graded, with the number of specimens grounding it; a number without a link is specimens in that cell for which no feature model has yet been written; a dot is a genuine gap in the surviving record. The hand-variants follow, each with its feature model.

Correspondence hand (autograph subscription) — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Bvar-correspondence-hven
Letters from Hven: the engrossed body was almost always written by a familia copyist (Longomontanus most often); only the closing line, the subscription 'Tycho Brahe manu propria' (sometimes 'Tuus' first), and internal autograph corrections are Tycho's hand. The discrimination question for any 'Tycho letter' is always: how much is autograph vs signature-only? The cell now also holds the largest dated batch of SENT autograph originals: the five Marburg Kassel-correspondence letters (HStAM 3 II Nr. 1070; to Wilhelm IV 1586/1588/1589/1592 and to Rothmann 1592), where the bodies were read as Tycho's own prose hand in the surrogate but the extent (whole holograph vs familia-body + 'manu propria' subscription) is flagged to confirm in-hand.
Cell correspondence · Hven / Uraniborg maturity · script: humanist italic cursive
Ductus and instrument no tremor (good pen control to the end); generally stable baseline, slight downward sag at line-ends in fast specimens; confident rapid execution — marked thick/thin contrast — flexible broad-edge quill; signature more pronouncedly shaded than prose (slant 5–10° (typical 7°); abbreviation moderate; iron-gall brown, broad-edge quill)
Distinctive features autograph subscription contrasts in ink/ductus with the copyist body (medium)
Variation within the cell Autograph extent ranges from subscription-only to subscription + marginal corrections; the body is a discriminator hand, not Tycho's. Now also includes three KB/Rigsarkivet-held SURVIVING ORIGINALS (Birgitte Bølle 1586 Da.; Poul Madsen 1589 La.; Anders Christensen 1591 Da.) recovered from the Dreyer-apparatus witness harvest — the Danish personal letters are candidates for wholly-holograph execution.

Fol. 68); Letter to Poul Madsen (Bishop of Zealand), Uraniborg, 16 Dec 1589 — surviving original (Copenhagen, Rigsarkivet, Konsistoriums arkiv, KA 188); Letter to Anders Christensen, Uraniborg, 21 Jun 1591 — surviving original (Copenhagen, Rigsarkivet, Konsistoriums arkiv, KA 185); Letter to Landgrave Wilhelm IV of Hesse-Kassel, Uraniborg, 1 March 1586 — autograph original (Marburg); Letter to Landgrave Wilhelm IV, Uraniborg, 15 August 1588 — autograph original (Marburg); Letter to Landgrave Wilhelm IV, Uraniborg, 21 February 1589 — autograph original (Marburg); Letter to Christoph Rothmann, 12 February 1592 — autograph original (Marburg); Letter to Landgrave Wilhelm IV, Uraniborg, 20 September 1592 — autograph original (Marburg)

Correspondence hand (autograph subscription) — Exile / late (1597-1601)

Bvar-correspondence-exile
Letters of the exile years, surviving as sent originals in recipients' archives across Germany, Sweden and Denmark. Same Hven convention (familia-secretary body + autograph 'manu propria' subscription), now dispersed by Tycho's wandering. Includes a surviving original of the Hessen correspondence (Moritz, Marburg 1597); the Wilhelm IV / Rothmann exchange likewise survives as sent autograph originals at Marburg (HStAM 3 II Nr. 1070 — the Hven-period correspondence, ~5 holograph letters 1586–1592), besides the Vienna copybook copies + the 1596 print.

Album-amicorum / inscription prose hand (the gold standard) — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Avar-dedication-inscription-hven
The prose gold-standard: album-amicorum entries (sententia + scripsi + place + date, no named recipient) and inscriptions. The densest dated autograph in the corpus is the 1598-99 Wittenberg seven-leaf series. This is our preferred prose comparator, chosen by closest calendar date.
Cell dedication inscription · Hven / Uraniborg maturity · script: humanist italic cursive
Ductus and instrument no tremor (good pen control to the end); generally stable baseline, slight downward sag at line-ends in fast specimens; confident rapid execution — marked thick/thin contrast — flexible broad-edge quill; signature more pronouncedly shaded than prose (slant 5–10° (typical 7°); abbreviation light; iron-gall brown, broad-edge quill)
Distinctive features uncial d, ascender loops/curls leftward at top (medium); sigma-form round final-s + long-s medial (the combination) (medium); display-cursive capital T with looped ascender (signature line) (high)
Variation within the cell Wide, generous spacing with horizontal rules in formal album entries vs tighter in fast notes of the same period — normal within-writer genre variation, not a counter-attribution.

Proportions ascender to x-height ≈ 0.8, descender ≈ 0.25 — Cited from the running-prose consensus of the observation-log (GKS 316, ~0.82/~0.31) and documentary (GKS 1823, ~0.81/~0.20) registers — both Tycho's running humanist italic at a different resolution — NOT independently measured on a dedication leaf. Documentary / administrative hand — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Avar-documentary-administrative-hven
Receipts, horoscopes, and testamentary/financial acknowledgements — fully autograph documentary prose. Includes the 1590 Dibner Uraniborg fragment (earliest dated autograph prose), the autograph horoscopes (GKS 1822 Prince Ulrik, GKS 1823 Duke Hans), and the KB receipt (NKS 4898).
Cell administrative document · Hven / Uraniborg maturity · script: humanist italic cursive
Ductus and instrument no tremor (good pen control to the end); generally stable baseline, slight downward sag at line-ends in fast specimens; confident rapid execution — marked thick/thin contrast — flexible broad-edge quill; signature more pronouncedly shaded than prose (slant 14–28° (typical 20°); abbreviation light; iron-gall brown, broad-edge quill)
Proportions ascender to x-height ≈ 0.8, descender ≈ 0.2 — Measured per isolated line on GKS 1823 g0040 (1583 judgment prose): body:x-height ~2.07, ascender:x ~0.81, descender:x ~0.20, x-height ~15px. The short-descender / tall-ascender asymmetry matches the 1597 observation-log specimen (~0.82/~0.31), confirming it as a stable hand habit across 14 years and two registers. Ratios are resolution-independent (held at 15px here vs 12px in GKS 316).
Distinctive features large display signature closing the document (high); stable baseline, no tremor, full prose (light abbreviation) (low)
Variation within the cell Spans formal horoscopes (green-velvet presentation objects) and rapid receipts; absolute size varies with surface, proportions stable.

Documentary / administrative hand — Exile / late (1597-1601)

Bvar-documentary-administrative-exile

Marginal / annotation hand (candidate, mostly autograph status disputed) — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Dvar-marginalia-hven
The hardest register: most Brahe-context marginalia are NOT his (familia, calligraphers, or — in De revolutionibus — Wittich). The two specimens here are Šíma–Valeška's 'cannot exclude' philosophically-loaded inscriptions (Tabulae Sinuum, Bruno Acrotismus). The default prior is non-attribution until proven against the pillars.
Cell marginalia · Hven / Uraniborg maturity · script: humanist italic cursive
Ductus and instrument no tremor (good pen control to the end); generally stable baseline, slight downward sag at line-ends in fast specimens; confident rapid execution — marked thick/thin contrast — flexible broad-edge quill; signature more pronouncedly shaded than prose (slant 5–10° (typical 7°); abbreviation moderate; iron-gall brown, broad-edge quill)
Distinctive features philosophically-loaded short Latin/Greek register typical of Tycho's in-margin comments (low)
Variation within the cell Both specimens are autograph status disputed (grade D); no secure marginalia exemplar exists, so this cell's feature model is provisional.

Observation-log hand (autograph interventions) — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Bvar-observation-log-hven
The Diarium / observation logs are PRIMARILY familia (Longomontanus, Flemløse, Morsing, Gellius): the tabulated distance/time observations are engrossed in a neat upright clerk-hand. Tycho's autograph appears as (a) corrections, marginal computations, headings, date markers — visible by ink/ductus shift — AND (b), newly confirmed from the GKS 316 facsimile, as SUSTAINED autograph PROSE: the 'DE OCCASIONE INTERRUPTArum Observationum & discessus mei' first-person narrative of the April 1597 departure from Hven (f.131v, image TYCH0009) is a whole-leaf autograph in Tycho's rapid humanist-italic prose hand — not merely an intervention. The KB GKS series (1825/1826 'mostly', 311/312 'partly', 316 'partly') is the located corpus. Never assume a tabular Diarium folio is autograph; the autograph is the prose + the interventions.
Cell observation log · Hven / Uraniborg maturity · script: humanist italic cursive
Ductus and instrument no tremor (good pen control to the end); generally stable baseline, slight downward sag at line-ends in fast specimens; confident rapid execution — marked thick/thin contrast — flexible broad-edge quill; signature more pronouncedly shaded than prose (slant 16–28° (typical 22°); abbreviation heavy; iron-gall brown, broad-edge quill)
Proportions ascender to x-height ≈ 0.8, descender ≈ 0.3 — Measured per isolated line on GKS 316 f.131v (TYCH0009): body:x-height ~2.12, ascender:x ~0.82, descender:x ~0.31, x-height ~12px. DIAGNOSTIC: descenders are SHORT relative to ascenders (~0.3 vs ~0.8) — the same asymmetry the 1583 documentary specimen shows (~0.20 vs ~0.81), so it is a hand habit, not register/resolution noise. Ratios (not absolute px) are the portable metric per §7.3; they hold across the 12px (this) and 15px (GKS 1823) x-heights, confirming resolution-independence.
Distinctive features autograph headings/date-markers/computations distinguished from familia body by ink + ductus shift (medium)
Variation within the cell Heavy abbreviation here vs light in the formal registers is genre-driven; and most of the log is NOT Tycho's hand at all — the variant models only his interventions.

Presentation / signature hand — Hven / Uraniborg maturity (1576-1597)

Avar-presentation-formal-hven
The presentation register: the autograph signature added to printed presentation copies (d:dt: / dono dedit / dono misit / manu propria above the name) and the rarer FULLY-autograph dedications (Rothmann 1588, candidate Duodo 1600) written in the same display-cursive as the signature. The signature is markedly larger and more flourished than surrounding text.

Distinctive features display-cursive capital T, prominent looped ascender + elongated bar (high); classical double-bow capital B, slightly enlarged (medium); flourished final -e of Brahe with downward sweep (medium)
Variation within the cell Signature-only on calligraphic presentation copies (pillars 3-5) vs whole dedication in the fully-autograph cases (Rothmann/Duodo) — a genre split, not two hands.
