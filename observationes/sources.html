The works and records this edition rests on, each with what it contributes and how often the register cites it. Dreyer's Prolegomena to Opera Omnia X is the principal witness for the codices; Verbunt & van Gent's tables are the text of record for the star catalogue; the two libraries' catalogues and the Vienna Tabulae codicum supply the extents and titles.

  1. Dreyer, Tycho Brahe (1890)
    J. L. E. Dreyer, Tycho Brahe: a picture of scientific life and work in the sixteenth century (Edinburgh, 1890).
    Contributes the narrative of the observing programme and its assistants
    Cited by 17 citations
  2. Dreyer (ed.), Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia (1913–29)
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, 15 vols (Copenhagen, 1913–1929).
    Contributes the printed edition of the observations (vols X–XIII, 1923–26) that this register indexes
    Cited by 4 citations
  3. Royal Danish Library catalogue (soeg.kb.dk)
    Det Kgl. Bibliotek, the discovery catalogue (soeg.kb.dk): the records of the seventeen Copenhagen volumes, with their permalinks.
    Contributes the catalogue records and permalinks of the seventeen Copenhagen volumes
    Cited by 21 citations
    Open copy soeg.kb.dk
  4. Brøndsted, 'Et ukendt digt af Tycho Brahe' (1962)
    Mogens Brøndsted, 'Et ukendt digt af Tycho Brahe', Fund og Forskning i Det kongelige Biblioteks samlinger 9 (1962), 71–78.
    Contributes the farewell elegy to Denmark in Codex G (GKS 316), read in Scripta
    Cited by Codex G
    Open copy tidsskrift.dk (open access)
  5. Austrian National Library catalogue (search.onb.ac.at)
    Österreichische Nationalbibliothek, the Primo catalogue (search.onb.ac.at; record permalinks data.onb.ac.at/rec/AC…): the records of the twenty Vienna volumes.
    Contributes the catalogue records of the twenty Vienna volumes
    Cited by 21 citations
    Open copy search.onb.ac.at
  6. Dreyer, Opera Omnia X — Prolegomena Editoris
    J. L. E. Dreyer, prolegomena to Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia X (the manuscript history & the split archive).
    Contributes Dreyer's own English account of his method and of the printing, 1913–1926
    Cited by 1 citation
  7. tekstnet.dk — DSL critical edition
    Tycho Brahe's works & 350 letters, ed. P. Zeeberg from Dreyer Opera Omnia, Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab 2019–21 (Carlsbergfondet).
    Contributes the critical texts of Tycho's works, to which the nova and the elegy are bridged
    Cited by the bridges to Scripta
    Open copy tekstnet.dk
  8. Dreyer, Opera Omnia X — Prolegomena (codex descriptions, Codices A–V)
    J. L. E. Dreyer, 'Prolegomena editoris' to Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia X (Copenhagen, 1923): the codex-by-codex description of the Copenhagen autograph protocols (Codices A–P = GKS 1824–1832 / 306–318) and the Vienna apographa (Codex V = Cod. Vindob. lat. 10657–10675), with the editorial collation rule (Copenhagen original always followed; Vienna used only where the original is incomplete, marked 'From Cod. V'). An English working translation was made for this edition.
    Contributes the codex-by-codex description of the archive (pp. XII–XXV), the editorial rule (p. XXV) and the history of the books (pp. I–XI); the pillar's principal witness
    Cited by 66 citations
    Open copy archive.org (vol. X; the Prolegomena open the volume)
  9. Bartholin, Specimen recognitionis (1668)
    Erasmus Bartholin, Specimen recognitionis nuper editarum observationum astronomicarum Tychonis Brahe (Copenhagen, 1668), 48 pp. 4°; 36 pp. of detailed collation of the 1582 observations as printed in Curtz's Historia Coelestis against Tycho's autograph manuscripts — the first published KB↔print collation and the methodological precedent for the year-by-year apparatus.
    Contributes the first collation of the fair copies against the archetypes (the year 1582)
    Cited by 2 citations
  10. Tabulae codicum, ÖNB vol. VI (1873)
    Tabulae codicum manu scriptorum praeter graecos et orientales in Bibliotheca Palatina Vindobonensi asservatorum, vol. VI: Cod. 9001–11500 (Vienna, 1873), p. 218: entry '10657–10675 … Tycho Brahe, Observationes astronomicae annorum 1582–1592 et 1594–1601 factae Vraneburgi in insula Huena', with per-volume leaf counts I–XIX (216, 173, 239, 250, 369, 406, 296, 214, 264, 257, 147, 144, 284, 199, 168, 154, 181, 259, 152).
    Contributes the leaf counts and contents of Cod. 10657–10675 as the Vienna catalogue of 1873 gives them
    Cited by 19 citations
    Open copy archive.org
  11. Carman & Recio, 'Appendix ad Observationes anni 1593' (2022)
    Christián C. Carman & Gonzalo L. Recio, 'Tycho Brahe's Appendix ad Observationes anni 1593 and the date of Brahe's theory of Mars, the prototype for Kepler's vicarious hypothesis', Journal for the History of Astronomy 53.3 (2022), 239–265 — uses the 1593 material that survives only on the KB side after the Vienna fair-copy was lost.
    Contributes the use of the 1593 material that survives only on the Copenhagen side
    Cited by 1 citation
    Open copy doi
  12. Rosen, 'Don't Give Tychon What Isn't Brahe's' (1981)
    Edward Rosen, 'Don't Give Tychon What Isn't Brahe's' (Polish: 'Nie daj Tychonowi, co nie jest Brahego', trans. E. Hilfstein), Kwartalnik Historii Nauki i Techniki 26/3-4 (1981), 549–564 — corroborates the Wittich reattribution, prints the Jakob Monau attestation (23 Oct 1600) + facsimile plates of Tycho's and Wittich's hands, and withdraws the Newskaja claim on the Leningrad/Edinburgh De rev. (it is Reinhold's).
    Contributes the Wittich hand in the comet book (Codex N, ff. 56–58) and its discriminator
    Cited by 1 citation
  13. Graphonomicon of Tycho Brahe — the Paul Wittich discriminator
    Graphonomicon of Tycho Brahe (Tychoniana), the Paul Wittich discriminator: the located Wittich specimens, Jakob Monau's attestation of 23 October 1600, and the Basel-1566 / Nuremberg-1543 edition correction.
    Contributes the specimen-level study of Tycho's hand, to which the autograph codices are bridged
    Cited by 1 citation
    Open copy the Graphonomicon
  14. Dreyer (ed.), Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia III (1916)
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, vol. III, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer (Copenhagen, 1916): the 1000-star catalogue + the Annotationes editoris (manuscript list p. 411; Cod. 10707 autograph dedication p. 417; the Appendix printed from Cod. 10707 alone, pp. 379–389).
    Contributes the 1,004-star manuscript catalogue (pp. 333–373) and Dreyer's notes on its witnesses (pp. 411–417)
    Cited by 2 citations
    Open copy archive.org
  15. Truffa, 'The First Printed Edition of Tycho's 1004 Star Catalogue' (2002)
    G. Truffa, 'The First Printed Edition of Tycho Brahe's 1004 Star Catalogue', in Tycho Brahe and Prague: Crossroads of European Science (Frankfurt: H. Deutsch, 2002), pp. 310–322 — census of the surviving star-catalogue apographs (incl. the ÖNB copies) and the 1604 Pifferi print.
    Contributes the census of the surviving manuscript copies of the star catalogue
    Cited by the star table
  16. Verbunt & van Gent, 'Three editions of the star catalogue of Tycho Brahe' (2010)
    F. Verbunt & R. H. van Gent, 'Three editions of the star catalogue of Tycho Brahe', Astronomy & Astrophysics 516 (2010) A28 — machine-readable collation; based on Dreyer's TBOO III Copenhagen base-text (GKS 306).
    Contributes the keyed, Hipparcos-identified text of the three editions of the star catalogue (CDS J/A+A/516/A28) — the witness the 777-star table is built from
    Cited by 1 citation
    Open copy doi · the data files
  17. Dreyer (ed.), Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia IV (1922)
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, vol. IV, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer (Copenhagen, 1922): De mundi aetherei recentioribus phaenomenis (1588), Book II — the comet of 1577, its day-by-day places (ch. III, pp. 38–61) and Tycho's daily table of its motion (pp. 79–80).
    Contributes the text of record for the comet's positions, sign glyphs included
    Cited by 1 citation
    Open copy archive.org (University of Toronto copy)
  18. Dreyer (ed.), Tychonis Brahe Opera Omnia II (1915)
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, vol. II, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer (Copenhagen, 1915): Astronomiae instauratae progymnasmata (1602), Book I — the 777-star catalogue (pp. 258–280, with Dreyer's notes pp. 451–452) and the new star of 1572 (the seven determinations and the adopted place, pp. 363–364).
    Contributes the text of record for the star catalogue and the nova's adopted place
    Cited by the star table and the nova
    Open copy archive.org (University of Toronto copy)
  19. Opera Omnia X–XIII, XV — archive.org facsimiles
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, vols X–XIII (the observations, 1923–1926) and XV (indices and addenda, 1929), University of Toronto copies digitised by the Internet Archive; every page image addressable by leaf, the printed-page assertions read from each item's scandata.
    Contributes the page images behind every row of the printed-edition index

    Open copy archive.org (vol. XI; the others by number)
