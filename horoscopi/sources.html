  1. Christianson 1970
    John Robert Christianson, 'Tycho Brahe's Facts of Life', Fund og Forskning 17 (1970), pp. 21–28.
    Contributes The Stephanius notes, copied from Tycho's lost nativity of himself, giving the birth moment 14 December 1546, 10.47 a.m.
    Cited by 1 row: Tycho's nativity of himself — lost
    Open copy tidsskrift.dk
  2. Dreyer 1890
    J. L. E. Dreyer, Tycho Brahe: A Picture of Scientific Life and Work in the Sixteenth Century (Edinburgh: Adam & Charles Black, 1890).
    Contributes The Leipzig book of themata genethliaca and Peucer's nativity (p. 21); the 1566 eclipse prognostication (p. 26); the 1577 nativity and the royal title in its copies (p. 145 with n. 1); the Ulrik and Hans volumes described (p. 153); the king's letter of 24 September 1587 asking for the usual almanac, none of which survives (p. 156).
    Cited by 5 rows: Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583); The Leipzig book of nativities, with Caspar Peucer's geniture — lost; The annual almanacs for Frederik II — lost
    Open copy Internet Archive
  3. Friis 1876
    Tyge Brahes meteorologiske Dagbog, holdt paa Uraniborg for Aarene 1582–1597, ed. F. R. Friis, with the meteorological summary and French résumé by Poul la Cour (Copenhagen: Kgl. Danske Videnskabernes Selskab, 1876).
    Contributes The editor's statement (Forord, p. I) that the diary, Vienna Cod. 10718, was written on Hven by Tycho's assistants and shows no trace of his own hand.
    Cited by 1 row: The Uraniborg weather diary, 1582–1597
    Open copy Royal Danish Academy
  4. Gigas 1900
    Emil Gigas, 'Nogle Bøger fra Tycho Brahes Bogbinderi', Bogvennen (Copenhagen, 1900), nos. 10–11.
    Contributes The presentation bindings of GKS 1822 and GKS 1823: green velvet, gilt edges, the lily ornaments — products of the Uraniborg bindery.
    Cited by 2 rows: Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583)
  5. KB catalogue
    Det Kgl. Bibliotek, Copenhagen: the manuscript catalogue (soeg.kb.dk), read record by record in September 2026, and the library's digitised facsimile of GKS 1823.
    Contributes Titles, shelfmarks, extents, bindings and acquisition notes of the ten Copenhagen witnesses; the record for GKS 1821 that the previous register had not traced; the facsimile of Prince Hans's horoscope.
    Cited by 4 rows: Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583); The astrological and meteorological diary for 1573
    Open copy soeg.kb.dk
  6. Nielsen 1931
    Lauritz Nielsen, Dansk Bibliografi 1551–1600 (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1931–33), nos. 606, 607 and 1170.
    Contributes The bibliographical descriptions and holding libraries of Flemløs's Astrologia (Danish no. 606, German no. 607) and Morsing's Diarium for 1586 (no. 1170).
    Cited by 2 rows: Morsing's Diarium astrologicum et meteorologicum for 1586, the first book from the Hven press; Flemløs's Astrologia (1591), the Danish book of weather signs from the Hven press
  7. Oestmann 2002
    Günther Oestmann, 'Tycho Brahe's Attitude towards Astrology and his Relations to Heinrich Rantzau', in J. R. Christianson, A. Hadravová, P. Hadrava and M. Šolc (eds), Tycho Brahe and Prague: Crossroads of European Science (Frankfurt am Main: Harri Deutsch, 2002), p. 87.
    Contributes The court obligation — the annual almanac and the royal nativities — and the observation that the Copenhagen copies of the 1577 nativity style the prince 'serenissimus rex Christianus IV', a title he did not hold before 1588.
    Cited by 2 rows: Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); The annual almanacs for Frederik II — lost
  8. Opera Omnia I
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. I (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1913).
    Contributes The edition of the three princely nativities (pp. 179–280: headnotes pp. 180, 211 and 251, the editor's notes p. 314), of the Astrologiae meteorologicae fragmentum (pp. 176–178, notes p. 313) and of the Diarium for 1573 (pp. 75–130, notes pp. 311–312); with De nova stella and its preface on diaries (pp. 34–44) and the oration of 1574 (pp. 143–173). Dreyer's witness lists, his statements on which parts are in Tycho's hand, and his verdict on the horoscopes are the published authority this edition rests on.
    Cited by 8 rows: Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583); The astrological and meteorological diary for 1573; Fragment of a treatise on weather astrology; The annual almanacs for Frederik II — lost; De nova stella (1573): the astrological judgment on the new star and the preface on diaries; The oration on the mathematical disciplines (1574): the defence of astrology
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  9. Opera Omnia III
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. III (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1916).
    Contributes The appendix to the star catalogue for Rudolf II, printed from Cod. 10707 alone (pp. 379–389); the list of the catalogue's manuscripts (p. 411); Tycho's autograph dedication to Coraduz (p. 417).
    Cited by 1 row: The appendix to the star catalogue keyed to Rudolf II's nativity (1600)
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  10. Opera Omnia IV
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. IV (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1922).
    Contributes The German tract on the comet of 1577 (pp. 381–396) with the editor's note on its two manuscripts (p. 511); the two commentaries on the comet of 1585 (pp. 397–414) with the notes assigning their drafts to the fascicles of Cod. 10689 (pp. 512–515).
    Cited by 3 rows: The German tract on the comet of 1577, with its astrological signification; The two commentaries on the comet of 1585, with their astrological conjectures; 'Quae genera eventuum portendat crinita a. 1572' — Cod. 10689/41–46, fol. 21r–24v
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  11. Opera Omnia IX
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. IX (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1927).
    Contributes The weather diary re-edited from Cod. 10718 (pp. 1–146) and the draft preface for the 1586 almanac (pp. 149–150).
    Cited by 3 rows: The Uraniborg weather diary, 1582–1597; Morsing's Diarium astrologicum et meteorologicum for 1586, the first book from the Hven press; The draft preface for the 1586 almanac
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  12. Opera Omnia VII
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. VII (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1924).
    Contributes Tycho to Caspar Peucer, Uraniborg, 13 September 1588 (p. 137): the Leipzig book of nativities of about 1564, Peucer's geniture noted in it, and Tycho's account of setting the predictions aside.
    Cited by 2 rows: The Leipzig book of nativities, with Caspar Peucer's geniture — lost; Tycho to Caspar Peucer, 13 September 1588: the Leipzig nativities
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  13. Opera Omnia VIII
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. VIII (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1925).
    Contributes Ludolf Riddershusen's questions on nativities and violent deaths (pp. 217–218) and Tycho's reply of 1600 on the nativities sent to him (p. 304).
    Cited by 2 rows: Ludolf Riddershusen to Tycho, 16 December 1599: questions on nativities; Tycho to Riddershusen, 20 April 1600: on the nativities sent to him
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  14. Opera Omnia X
    Tychonis Brahe Dani Opera Omnia, ed. J. L. E. Dreyer, tom. X (Copenhagen: Gyldendal, 1923), Prolegomena editoris.
    Contributes Dreyer's description of the three Vienna bundles of Tycho's papers, codices 10686, 10688 and 10689 (p. XXV).
    Cited by 2 rows: Draft nativity of Frederik II; Judgment of an unnamed nativity
    Open copy HathiTrust catalogue record for the set
  15. tekstnet.dk
    Tycho Brahe: Latinske tekster, ed. Peter Zeeberg et al., Det Danske Sprog- og Litteraturselskab (tekstnet.dk).
    Contributes The modern critical text, with Danish translation, of the three princely nativities, the weather-astrology fragment and the Diarium for 1573 — read here through Scripta.
    Cited by 5 rows: Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583); The astrological and meteorological diary for 1573; Fragment of a treatise on weather astrology
    Open copy tekstnet.dk
  16. The Hand (Graphonomicon)
    Tychoniana, the Graphonomicon of Tycho Brahe's hand: the specimen files for GKS 1822, GKS 1823 and Cod. 10689/29 — facsimile autopsies of June 2026 with their stated confidence. The portal’s own authority, named as such wherever it raises a claim.
    Contributes The reading that the Latin of GKS 1823 is in Tycho's italic and its German in a different, Kurrent, hand (confidence B); the reading that Cod. 10689/29 is a working draft in his hand (confidence A); the verified facsimile and IIIF links.
    Cited by 3 rows: Nativity of Prince Ulrik (1579); Nativity of Prince Hans (1583); Judgment of an unnamed nativity
    Open copy the Graphonomicon
  17. Zeeberg 1994
    Peter Zeeberg, Tycho Brahes »Urania Titani«: et digt om Sophie Brahe, Renæssancestudier 7 (Copenhagen: Museum Tusculanum, 1994), with the appendix by Ove von Späth, pp. 311–321.
    Contributes The critical edition of the poem and von Späth's reconstruction of the four nativities its allusions imply — the birth data he had, the moments he chose, and the charts he computed (p. 313 on Tage Thott and on Tycho's own recorded birth time).
    Cited by 6 rows: Tycho's nativity of himself — lost; Urania Titani (1594): the poem in which four nativities are set; Titan — Erik Lange; Urania — Sophie Brahe (two charts for her two candidate birth dates); Tage Thott; Apollo — Tycho Brahe
  18. ÖNB catalogue
    Österreichische Nationalbibliothek, Vienna: the manuscript catalogue records (data.onb.ac.at), whose descriptions of the Tycho fascicles are taken from the Tabulae codicum manu scriptorum (1864–1893) and, for Cod. 10707 and Cod. 10932, from Unterkircher's Datierte Handschriften (1969–1976); with the library's digital viewer and IIIF manifests. Read record by record in September 2026.
    Contributes The unit, extent, language, binding and provenance of every Vienna witness; the attribution of Cod. 10689/29 to Tycho and of Cod. 10689/36 to a pupil; the digitised objects.
    Cited by 11 rows: Draft nativity of Frederik II; Nativity of Prince Christian, the later Christian IV (1577); Judgment of an unnamed nativity; Reply to an astrological question of the Emperor; The German tract on the comet of 1577, with its astrological signification; The astrological and meteorological diary for 1573; Fragment of a treatise on weather astrology; The appendix to the star catalogue keyed to Rudolf II's nativity (1600); 'Quae genera eventuum portendat crinita a. 1572' — Cod. 10689/41–46, fol. 21r–24v; Urania Titani (1594): the poem in which four nativities are set; A pupil's draft of a nativity in German — Cod. 10689/36
    Open copy search.onb.ac.at
